# Changing Employers (Transfer of Services)

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Since the Labor Reform Initiative, private-sector expat workers can move between employers electronically through the **Qiwa** platform. In MHRSD's description, the transfer of services ("job mobility") service lets a worker move to a new employer **without the current employer's approval, subject to the regulations and conditions** checked by the system.

## Who this applies to

- Expat employees in the private sector with a valid work permit and an Iqama, and a documented contract on Qiwa.
- The Labor Reform Initiative allows transfer **at the end of the contract** without employer consent, and during the contract **subject to notice and specific conditions**.
- **Domestic workers** use separate rules and the Musaned platform.
- Workers with an "absent from work" report have been covered by separate, time-limited MHRSD initiatives; check Qiwa for any that currently apply.

## What you need

- A job offer from a new employer registered on Qiwa.
- Access to your individual Qiwa account.
- Your current contract details (type: fixed-term or indefinite; end date; notice period).

## Steps

1. **Review your current contract on Qiwa:** note its type, end date and the notice period recorded when it was created. Qiwa calculates notice on that basis.
2. **The new employer sends a job offer** to you through Qiwa.
3. **Accept or reject the offer** from your Qiwa individual account.
4. **Your current employer is notified** of the transfer request.
5. **The system verifies the requirements** (contract status, notice, permits and other regulatory conditions).
6. **On approval,** your record is updated, a new work permit is issued under the new employer and your Iqama details follow.
7. **Serve your notice period** where required, and settle end-of-service entitlements with your previous employer.

## Fees and timing

- Transfer fees, if any, are paid by the employer and shown on Qiwa. A 2025 MHRSD initiative for absent-from-work cases required the new employer to pay outstanding permit fees.
- Processing times and conditions are shown on Qiwa at the time of the request. We did not find an official fixed timeline to quote.

## Common questions

**Do I need a release letter (NOC) from my current employer?**
Under the Qiwa transfer service, the current employer is notified rather than asked for approval, provided the regulatory conditions are met. Qiwa will show if a condition blocks your transfer.

**What happens to my end-of-service award?**
Your previous employer owes your end-of-service award under the Labor Law; resignation can reduce it depending on years of service. See the employment-rights guide.

**Can I change jobs during probation?**
Conditions during the contract, including probation, are checked by Qiwa. Ask the new employer to confirm eligibility on the platform before you resign.

**Will my profession change?**
Profession changes are a separate MHRSD/Qiwa service handled by the employer.
