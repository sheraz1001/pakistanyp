# Government Digital Services for Expats

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Most government procedures for residents in Saudi Arabia are done online. Knowing which platform handles what saves time and helps you avoid unofficial sites.

## Who this applies to

- All residents with an Iqama, and in some cases their sponsors and employers.
- Visitors use a smaller set of services (for example the MOFA visa platform and visitor health insurance).

## What you need

- Your Iqama and a Saudi mobile number registered in your name.
- An **Absher Individuals** account; many other platforms rely on it or on **Nafath**, the national single sign-on.
- The official apps downloaded only from the official app stores or links on the platforms' own websites.

## Which platform does what

| Platform | Run by | Typical uses for expats |
|---|---|---|
| **Absher Individuals** | Ministry of Interior | Your digital Iqama, family members' services, visas for dependants, traffic services, driving licence |
| **Absher Business / Muqeem** | Ministry of Interior | Used by employers for workers' Iqamas and visas |
| **Qiwa** | MHRSD | Employment contracts, job transfers, work permits |
| **Nafath** | National single sign-on | Approving logins to government and other platforms |
| **Tawakkalna** | SDAIA | National app bringing together government services and documents |
| **my.gov.sa** | Unified national platform | Directory of all government services and their official pages |
| **Sehhaty** | Ministry of Health | Health appointments, virtual clinics, vaccinations |
| **Ejar** | Real Estate General Authority | Documented rental contracts |
| **MOFA visa platform** | Ministry of Foreign Affairs | Family visit and other visas |
| **Noor** | Ministry of Education | School registration |
| **Musaned** | MHRSD | Domestic worker contracts |

## Steps

1. **Create your Absher account** after your Iqama is issued, using the official Absher website or app.
2. **Activate Nafath** where prompted, so you can approve logins to platforms such as Qiwa and others that use the national single sign-on.
3. **Install Tawakkalna** to keep digital documents in one place.
4. **Use my.gov.sa** to find the correct official service page before starting any procedure.
5. **Keep your contact details updated** on Absher so you receive notifications and verification codes.

## Fees and timing

- Creating accounts on Absher, Nafath and Tawakkalna is done through the official channels; service fees, where they apply, are shown on each service page and paid through official channels such as SADAD.
- Sehhaty appointment booking is free, according to MOH.

## Common questions

**Someone offers to "process" my Absher request for a fee. Is that safe?**
Never share your login details or Nafath approval codes with anyone. Approve a Nafath request only if you started the login yourself.

**How do I know a site is official?**
Saudi government sites generally use the .gov.sa domain or the platforms' own domains linked from my.gov.sa. Search my.gov.sa for the service and follow its link.

**I can't receive the SMS code.**
Check that your mobile number is registered in your name (CST "My Numbers") and updated in Absher.

**Is there one phone number for help?**
Each platform has its own support channels listed on its website; for health questions, MOH's 937 line is available.
