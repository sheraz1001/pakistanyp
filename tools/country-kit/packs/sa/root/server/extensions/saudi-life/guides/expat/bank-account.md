# Opening a Bank Account

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Banks in Saudi Arabia are licensed and supervised by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA). SAMA's Account Opening Rules set out what every bank must collect from expatriate customers. Individual banks may ask for more, so check with the bank you choose.

## Who this applies to

- Expat residents holding an Iqama.
- Newly arrived workers who do not yet have an Iqama (with restrictions, see below).
- Visitors may be able to open accounts using a visitor ID under SAMA's updated rules; ask the bank.

## What you need

Under SAMA's rules for expatriates, the bank must obtain:

- A copy of your **Iqama**, the Iqama number and its expiry date.
- Your **address in Saudi Arabia** and your **home-country address**.
- Banks may ask for additional documents under their own policies; check the list with your chosen bank.

## Steps

1. **Choose a SAMA-licensed bank** and compare account types and fees on its official website or app.
2. **Apply in the bank's app or at a branch.** SAMA's rules allow remote (online) account opening for Iqama holders, with identity verified against reliable independent sources. Remote opening is not allowed if you already have an account at the same bank.
3. **Submit the documents** the bank requests and accept the account agreement.
4. **Activate digital banking** and register your salary account with your employer for payroll (Wage Protection).
5. **Keep your Iqama data updated** with the bank whenever you renew it.

## Fees and timing

- Account fees are set by each bank and listed in its fee schedule.
- **Before you have an Iqama:** SAMA's rules cap transfers or cheques at SAR 10,000 for expatriates in the first three months after arrival, and the bank must tell you that the account will be frozen if you do not submit your Iqama within three months ([SAMA Rulebook](https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/20013-expatriates-saudi-arabia)).
- **Complaints:** under SAMA's Complaint Management System, banks must handle a complaint at the first level within a maximum of 5 business days ([SAMA Rulebook](https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/samacares-announcement-0)).

## Common questions

**Can I open an account fully online?**
Yes, if the bank offers it. SAMA's remote-opening rules cover Iqama holders.

**My account was frozen. What should I do?**
SAMA's rules require banks to inform customers about freezing and the conditions that trigger it, for example not submitting an Iqama within three months of arrival. Ask the bank for the reason and update your documents; if it is not resolved, file a complaint.

**How do I complain about a bank?**
Contact the bank first; banks must provide channels such as a free phone line, website, app and branches, and give you a reference number. If unresolved, escalate to SAMA Consumer Protection (toll-free 8001256666, listed on SAMA's site).

**Do I need to close my account when leaving Saudi Arabia?**
See the leaving-Saudi-Arabia guide and ask your bank about closure or keeping the account for final settlements.
