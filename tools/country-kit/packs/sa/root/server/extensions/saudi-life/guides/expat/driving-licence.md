# Getting a Saudi Driving Licence

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Driving licences in Saudi Arabia are issued by the General Department of Traffic (Muroor) under the Ministry of Interior. Appointments, payments and renewals are handled through **Absher**.

## Who this applies to

- Residents holding a valid Iqama who want to drive in the Kingdom.
- Holders of a **valid foreign or international licence recognised by the traffic department** may be exempt from the driving test, according to the Absher issuance service. Whether your licence is recognised depends on the issuing country and is decided by Muroor; check Absher or a traffic department before booking.
- Others must train and pass tests at a licensed driving school.
- Visitors follow separate rules for driving on a foreign licence; check the official traffic department guidance.

## What you need

From the Ministry of Interior's published requirements for replacing a foreign licence:

- The original foreign licence plus a certified Arabic translation.
- A copy of your Iqama and passport.
- Personal photos as specified.
- An identification letter from the sponsor (for non-Saudis), where requested.
- A medical examination (eye test and any other checks required), usually arranged through the driving school or traffic department.
- Payment of the required fees.

## Steps

1. **Check eligibility** in Absher: whether your foreign licence can be replaced directly or you need full training.
2. **Book an appointment** with a driving school or traffic department through Absher; walk-ins may not be accepted.
3. **Complete the medical check** and submit documents at the appointment.
4. **Take the required test** (or training course, if you are not exempt).
5. **Pay the licence fee** through SADAD; the amount and licence duration options are shown in Absher.
6. **Collect the licence** or use the digital version in Absher/Tawakkalna. Renew online through Absher before it expires.

## Fees and timing

- Licence issuance and renewal fees are shown in Absher at payment and depend on the licence type and validity period. The my.gov.sa service cards for issuance and renewal list current service costs; check them before paying.
- Traffic fines registered on your record must usually be paid before issuance or renewal.
- Driving-school training and test fees are set by the school and shown when you book.

## Common questions

**Is my home country's licence recognised?**
Recognition is decided by the traffic department. Lists circulate online, but only Absher or Muroor can confirm your case.

**How long can I drive on my foreign licence after getting an Iqama?**
Rules for new residents have changed over time, and secondary sources disagree. Check the current rule with Muroor or Absher.

**Can I renew my licence online?**
Yes. Renewal is available through Absher after paying fees and any outstanding traffic fines.

**Do I need a separate licence for a motorcycle or heavy vehicle?**
Yes. Licence categories differ; the type you apply for is selected in Absher.
