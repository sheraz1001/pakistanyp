# Renting a Home (Ejar)

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Residential leases in Saudi Arabia are documented on **Ejar**, the electronic rental network supervised by the Real Estate General Authority (REGA). An Ejar contract uses a standard form and gives both landlord and tenant an official record of the lease.

## Who this applies to

- Expat residents renting an apartment, villa or other residential unit from a landlord or through a licensed real-estate broker.
- Employees whose housing is provided by the employer should check whether the employer holds the lease.

## What you need

- Your Iqama and a Saudi mobile number registered in your name.
- The property details and the landlord's or broker's details.
- Agreed rent, payment schedule and any extras (such as parking or cleaning), which should be written into the contract as fixed amounts.

## Steps

1. **Find a property** through a licensed broker or the landlord directly. Ask the broker for their brokerage licence.
2. **Agree on terms** (rent, payment schedule, duration, deposit, maintenance responsibilities).
3. **Register the contract on Ejar.** The landlord or broker normally creates it; REGA states that tenants can also request registration, and if a landlord refuses, a tenant can request registration and complain to REGA.
4. **Approve the contract** from your side through the Ejar notification and keep the electronic copy.
5. **Pay rent** as set in the contract and keep receipts.
6. **Renewal:** contracts are deemed automatically renewed unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry, subject to exceptions.
7. **Moving out:** give notice as the contract requires, document the property's condition and settle any deposit.

## Fees and timing

- **Ejar registration fee:** shown on the Ejar platform at registration. Official listings we checked showed different amounts, so check Ejar directly.
- **Renewal fees** fall on the landlord; if unpaid, the contract still renews and the fee is recorded as the landlord's debt.
- **Brokerage fee:** capped at 2.5% of one year's rent per unit; the parties agree who pays ([Ejar FAQ](https://www.ejar.sa/en/faq/434422)).
- **Riyadh rent freeze:** for properties within Riyadh's urban boundaries, landlords cannot raise the total rent in existing or new contracts for five years from 25 September 2025, and a vacant unit with a previous lease cannot be let above its last contract rent, with limited exceptions ([REGA](https://rega.gov.sa)). In Riyadh, a landlord may refuse renewal only in specific cases. Outside Riyadh, rent is set by agreement.
- **Undocumented paper contracts** can be registered as a rental incident on Ejar for a fee shown on the platform.

## Common questions

**Do I need an Ejar contract?**
An Ejar-documented lease is the official record of your tenancy and protects both parties. If the landlord refuses to register it, you can request registration yourself and complain to REGA.

**Can my Riyadh landlord increase the rent at renewal?**
Not while the five-year freeze applies to properties in Riyadh's urban boundaries. Report violations to REGA.

**Who pays the broker?**
The tenant or landlord, as agreed, within the 2.5% cap.

**Are building or owners'-association fees part of the rent?**
No. REGA's FAQ says owners'-association fees are not part of the lease and are handled through the Mullak platform.
