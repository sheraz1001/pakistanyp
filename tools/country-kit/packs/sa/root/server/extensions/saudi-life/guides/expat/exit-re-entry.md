# Exit and Re-entry Visa

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Residents of Saudi Arabia need an exit and re-entry visa to travel abroad and come back on the same Iqama. It is issued electronically by the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) through Absher and Muqeem.

## Who this applies to

- **Employees:** the employer issues the visa through Absher Business or Muqeem. Jawazat has also enabled some residents to issue their own re-entry visa through Absher Individuals where the employer allows it.
- **Family members and domestic workers:** the sponsoring resident issues the visa through Absher Individuals.
- Visas can be **single** (one trip) or **multiple** (several trips within the validity period).

## What you need

- A valid Iqama and a passport with enough remaining validity; the platform checks the minimum required and will refuse the request if validity is too short.
- The traveller must be **inside Saudi Arabia** when the visa is issued.
- Outstanding traffic fines must normally be paid first.
- For employees: no "absent from work" report on the record.

## Steps

1. **Ask the right person:** your employer (employees) or your sponsor (family members and domestic workers). Sponsors sign in to Absher Individuals and open the visas section.
2. **Select the visa type** (single or multiple) and the duration, or the return date.
3. **Pay the fee** shown on the platform through SADAD or the listed payment method.
4. **Print or keep the visa** confirmation. You can check status on the Absher "Exit/Re-entry Visa Status" service.
5. **Travel and return** before the visa expires.
6. **If you are delayed abroad,** your employer or sponsor can extend the visa from inside the Kingdom through Absher or Muqeem after paying the extension fee.

## Fees and timing

- **Base fee:** the unified national platform's service card for issuing an exit and re-entry visa lists a service cost of SAR 200 ([my.gov.sa](https://www.my.gov.sa)). Longer durations and multiple-trip visas cost more; the exact total is shown in Absher before payment.
- **Extension from abroad:** the my.gov.sa listing for extending a visa for sponsorees outside the Kingdom via Muqeem shows a service cost of SAR 200 ([my.gov.sa](https://my.gov.sa/en/services/269426)). Confirm the amount for your case on the platform.
- **Refunds:** Jawazat has stated that fees for cancelled exit and re-entry visas are not refunded.
- **Conversion:** Jawazat has said a re-entry visa cannot be converted into a final exit visa while the holder is outside the Kingdom.

## Common questions

**What if I don't return before the visa expires?**
The visa expires and your status may be affected. Rules on re-entry after an unused visa have changed in the past, so check Absher or contact Jawazat before assuming you can return on a new visa.

**Can my employer refuse to issue a re-entry visa?**
Employees can discuss leave and travel with the employer; disputes about leave rights fall under the Labor Law and Qiwa/MHRSD channels.

**Do I need the visa printed?**
The visa is electronic. Carry your passport and keep the confirmation available.

**Can the visa be issued while I am abroad?**
No. It must be issued while you are in the Kingdom; from abroad, only extension of an existing visa is possible.
