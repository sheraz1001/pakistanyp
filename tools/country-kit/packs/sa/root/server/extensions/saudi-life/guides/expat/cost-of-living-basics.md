# Cost of Living Basics

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Living costs in Saudi Arabia vary widely by city, housing type and family size. Rather than quoting price lists that go out of date, this guide explains the main **cost factors** and where to find current official figures.

## Who this applies to

- Expats planning a move to Saudi Arabia or budgeting for a family.
- Residents comparing a job offer's allowances against real costs.

## What you need

- Your job offer or contract, showing salary and any housing, transport or schooling allowances.
- A list of household members (dependants affect fees, insurance and school costs).

## Steps

1. **Check inflation and price trends** on GASTAT. Annual consumer price inflation was 1.8% in August 2026, with the housing, water, electricity, gas and fuels group up 3.9% year on year, the largest contributor ([GASTAT](https://stats.gov.sa/en/w/news/214)). GASTAT's homepage shows a September 2026 figure of 1.9%; the October release is scheduled for 15 November 2026.
2. **Budget for VAT:** the standard VAT rate is 15% on most goods and services, with some supplies zero-rated ([ZATCA](https://zatca.gov.sa)).
3. **Estimate housing:** rent is usually the largest cost. In Riyadh, rents within the urban boundary are frozen for five years from 25 September 2025 ([REGA](https://rega.gov.sa)). Brokerage is capped at 2.5% of one year's rent. Elsewhere, rent is set by agreement. See the renting guide.
4. **Factor in schooling:** public-school places for non-Saudis are limited; private and international school fees are set by each school under MOE fee rules.
5. **Check who pays government fees:** under the Labor Law the employer pays the worker's Iqama and work-permit fees. **Dependants fees** are paid by the resident; MOI's published schedule set them at SAR 400 per dependant per month from July 2020 (confirm in Absher).
6. **Confirm health insurance:** employers must insure workers and eligible dependants under CHI rules; check co-payments in your policy.

## Fees and timing

| Item | Official position | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 15% standard rate | ZATCA |
| Inflation (Aug 2026) | 1.8% annual | GASTAT |
| Riyadh rents | Frozen 5 years from 25 Sep 2025 | REGA |
| Brokerage | Max 2.5% of one year's rent | Ejar |
| Dependants fee | SAR 400/month per dependant (from July 2020; confirm) | MOI |
| Iqama and work-permit fees | Paid by employer | Labor Law |

Utility tariffs, transport fares and school fees are published by each provider; check their official sites.

## Common questions

**Is VAT charged on healthcare and medicines?**
According to ZATCA guidance, private healthcare services are subject to VAT at 15%, while qualifying medicines and medical equipment are zero-rated.

**How much should a family budget per month?**
It depends on city, housing and schooling. Use the factors above and current listings rather than generic averages.

**Are dependants fees charged monthly?**
They are collected in advance for the period of the Iqama issuance or renewal, according to MOI.

**Where can I see official price data?**
GASTAT publishes the monthly Consumer Price Index and other price statistics on stats.gov.sa.
