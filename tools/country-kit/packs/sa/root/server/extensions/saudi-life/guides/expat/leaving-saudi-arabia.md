# Leaving Saudi Arabia (Final Exit)

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Residents who are leaving the Kingdom permanently need a **final exit visa**, issued electronically by the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat). After leaving on a final exit, your Iqama is cancelled.

## Who this applies to

- Employees whose contract has ended or who have resigned (the employer issues the visa through Absher Business or Muqeem).
- Family members and domestic workers (the sponsor issues it through Absher Individuals).
- Workers in the 90-day trial period who have not yet received an Iqama have a separate final exit service.

## What you need

From official Jawazat and Muqeem service conditions:

- An Iqama with **at least 30 days of validity**. If it has 30–60 days left, the visa lasts only as long as the Iqama; with 60 days or more, the visa is valid up to 60 days ([SPA – Jawazat](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2240177)).
- A passport with sufficient remaining validity (the platform checks the minimum).
- You must be **inside the Kingdom** when the visa is issued.
- Fingerprints on record (required for persons aged 15 and over, per Muqeem).
- Traffic fines paid, and no valid unused visa already on the record.

## Steps

1. **Settle work matters:** agree your last working day and notice, and confirm your end-of-service award and final salary with your employer. Contract termination is recorded on Qiwa.
2. **Request the final exit visa** from your employer (employees) or issue it through Absher Individuals for family members and domestic workers.
3. **Use the visa within its validity**; Muqeem conditions state it must be used within two months.
4. **Housing:** end your lease on Ejar according to the contract's notice terms and settle your deposit.
5. **Utilities and phone:** close utility accounts and cancel or transfer mobile lines registered in your name; check them with CST's "My Numbers".
6. **Bank:** ask your bank how to receive final payments before closing or keeping your account.
7. **Family:** make sure dependants also have final exit visas (or a new sponsor) before you leave.
8. **Vehicle:** transfer or deregister any vehicle registered in your name through Absher.

## Fees and timing

- Jawazat has stated that issuing a final exit visa through Absher, Absher Business and Muqeem is a **free** electronic service; other conditions such as paid traffic fines still apply.
- End-of-service award: see the employment-rights guide; it is calculated under the Labor Law.

## Common questions

**Can I convert my exit and re-entry visa to final exit while abroad?**
No. Jawazat has said this conversion is not possible while the holder is outside the Kingdom.

**Can I cancel a final exit visa if I find a new job?**
Cancellation rules are shown on Absher and Muqeem. A job change is normally handled through the Qiwa transfer service instead of a final exit.

**My Iqama has less than 30 days left. What now?**
The Iqama must be renewed before a final exit visa can be issued.

**What happens to my health insurance?**
Under CHI rules, cover ends when the insured person leaves the Kingdom permanently, so plan any medical appointments before departure.
