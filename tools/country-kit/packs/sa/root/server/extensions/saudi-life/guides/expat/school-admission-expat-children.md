# School Admission for Expat Children

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Expat children in Saudi Arabia can attend **public (government) schools**, **private schools** following the Saudi curriculum, or **international schools** that teach foreign curricula. All are licensed or supervised by the Ministry of Education (MOE), and registration in government and private schools runs through the **Noor** system.

## Who this applies to

- Children of residents with **valid legal residency** (Iqama).
- **Public schools:** MOE states that non-Saudi children are admitted within a cap of 15% (set by a Cabinet resolution), applied at district or governorate level, and only where this does not require additional teachers, classrooms or leased buildings. Non-Saudis are placed in vacant seats after Saudi students have registered, with priority rules that favour children of government-sector contractors.
- **International schools:** licensed by MOE to serve expatriate communities with non-Saudi curricula, from pre-school to secondary level.

## What you need

- The child's Iqama (or residency details) and passport.
- Original academic documents from previous schooling abroad, attested as required, so the school can equate them and place the child in the right grade. MOE lists country-specific attestation requirements for some nationalities.
- Vaccination records and other documents the school requests.

## Steps

1. **Decide on the school type** (public, private or international) and check availability in your area.
2. **For public and private schools,** register through the **Noor** system during the registration window MOE announces. Requests go to schools where registration is active, and the principal accepts or rejects them.
3. **For international schools,** apply directly to the school; confirm it holds an MOE licence.
4. **Submit documents** and complete any placement assessment the school requires.
5. **Get previous certificates equated** where needed so the child is placed in the correct grade.
6. **Transfers between schools** use MOE's school-transfer e-service.

## Fees and timing

- **Private school tuition** is subject to MOE's Rules Governing Tuition Fees for Private Schools, under which fee increases are reviewed by a committee ([MOE](https://www.moe.gov.sa/en/education/generaleducation/pages/nsfeesrules.aspx)). Each school publishes its own fees.
- **International school fees** are approved under MOE's international-schools regulations and set by each school.
- Registration windows for non-Saudi students in Noor are announced by MOE each year; check MOE and Noor for dates.

## Common questions

**Is a public school seat guaranteed?**
No. Non-Saudi admission depends on vacant seats within the 15% cap and the priority order MOE sets.

**What documents need attestation?**
It depends on the country where the documents were issued. MOE's residents page lists requirements for some countries; ask the school for your case.

**Can my child join mid-year?**
Ask the school. Placement depends on seats and the equivalency of previous studies.

**How do I check that an international school is licensed?**
International schools must be licensed by MOE. Ask the school for its licence and confirm through MOE.
