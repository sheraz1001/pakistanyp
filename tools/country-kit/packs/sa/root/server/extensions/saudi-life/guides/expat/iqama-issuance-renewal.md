# Iqama (Resident ID) Issuance and Renewal

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

The Iqama is the resident identity card that every foreign resident in Saudi Arabia must hold. It is issued and renewed by the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) under the Ministry of Interior, almost entirely online.

## Who this applies to

- **Private-sector employees:** your employer issues and renews your Iqama through Absher Business or the Muqeem portal. You do not apply yourself.
- **Family members and domestic workers:** the sponsoring resident (head of family or household employer) issues and renews their Iqama through Absher Individuals.
- **Investors and Premium Residency holders** have their own dedicated services on the unified national platform (my.gov.sa).

## What you need

According to the official service descriptions on my.gov.sa and Absher:

- A valid passport, and the person must be inside Saudi Arabia.
- For a first issuance: a medical examination at an approved medical centre, plus fingerprints and a recent photo.
- The person must already be registered electronically under the sponsor in Absher (for family members and domestic workers).
- Any outstanding delay fines and traffic violations must be paid before renewal.
- For employees: the worker must not be reported as absent from work.

## Steps

1. **Check the expiry date** in your own Absher Individuals account or the Tawakkalna app.
2. **Employees:** ask your employer's HR or government-relations team to renew; they use Absher Business or Muqeem.
3. **Sponsors of family members or domestic workers:** sign in to Absher Individuals and choose the family-member or labour services section, then the issuance or renewal service.
4. **Choose the renewal period** offered by the platform (see below) and pay the fees shown through SADAD or the payment options listed.
5. **Delivery:** where a physical card is printed, it is delivered by Saudi Post to the registered national address. The digital Iqama in Absher/Tawakkalna is also accepted for many purposes.

## Fees and timing

- **Who pays:** under the Saudi Labor Law, the employer bears the fees for the residence permit and work permit and their renewal, and fines for delay ([HRSD Labor Law](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2023-02/Labor.pdf)).
- **Renewal periods:** Absher lets users choose a period starting from three months or multiples of three months ([Absher guide](https://www.absher.sa)). Domestic workers may follow different rules.
- **Amounts:** the Iqama fee, the work-permit fee and any dependant fees are calculated and shown on Absher or Muqeem at the time of payment. Check the official service rather than third-party fee tables.
- **Late renewal:** the Ministry of Interior's published penalties state that a first late renewal is fined an amount equal to the renewal fee, a second is doubled, and repeated violations can lead to deportation ([MOI – Iqama violations](https://www.moi.gov.sa)). Confirm current penalties on Absher.

## Common questions

**Can I renew my own work Iqama?**
No. For employees, renewal is done by the employer through Absher Business or Muqeem. You can check the status in your own Absher account.

**Can a family member's Iqama be renewed while they are outside the Kingdom?**
Jawazat has announced that dependants and domestic workers who are abroad can have their Iqama renewed through Absher and Muqeem after the fees are paid. The platform will tell you if a specific case is eligible.

**Is the digital Iqama valid?**
The digital ID in Absher and Tawakkalna is widely accepted. Some entities may still ask for the physical card.

**Does my child need an Iqama?**
Yes. Children must be added under the sponsor. The Ministry of Interior lists fines for delaying the addition of a child born in the Kingdom beyond three months.
