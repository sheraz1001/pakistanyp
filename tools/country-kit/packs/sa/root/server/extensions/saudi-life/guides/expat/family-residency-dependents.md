# Family Residency and Dependants

SaudiYP is not a government service; complete official procedures only on the official platforms linked below.

Expat residents can sponsor close family members to visit or live with them in Saudi Arabia. Visas are handled through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) visa platform, and once family members arrive, their residence (Iqama) is issued by the sponsor through Absher.

## Who this applies to

- Residents holding a **work Iqama** who want to bring a spouse and children. MOFA's family visit service requires the sponsor to hold a work Iqama, not a dependant's Iqama.
- First-degree relatives (parents, spouse, children) for **family visit visas**.
- Health-insurance rules define dependants as a spouse, sons up to age 25 and unmarried daughters ([CHI](https://www.chi.gov.sa)).
- **New in October 2026:** the Ministry of Interior announced that skilled expatriate heads of household can start bringing eligible family members (spouse, sons under 25 and unmarried daughters) while their own work-visa procedures are still being completed ([SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2694399)). Eligibility depends on the worker's skill classification.

## What you need

- A valid work Iqama (for the sponsor) and valid passports for each family member; each family member needs a separate passport.
- Proof of relationship (for example marriage and birth certificates), attested as required by the MOFA platform.
- For Iqama issuance after arrival: medical examination at an approved centre, fingerprints and photo (for family members above the age set by Absher), and payment of fees.
- Employer-provided or family health insurance for dependants, as required by CHI rules.

## Steps

1. **Choose the visa type** on the MOFA visa platform: family visit visa for a temporary stay, or a family residence visa for dependants who will live with you.
2. **Submit the application** online with the requested documents and pay any fee displayed.
3. **Complete visa stamping** abroad through the channel the platform specifies, then travel.
4. **Register and issue the Iqama** for each family member through Absher Individuals (family member services) after arrival. The card is delivered by Saudi Post to your national address.
5. **Add newborns promptly.** The Ministry of Interior fines delays in adding a child born in the Kingdom beyond three months.

## Fees and timing

- **Family visit visa processing:** MOFA states a processing time of 3 business days for the family visit request service ([MOFA](https://www.mofa.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/svc13.aspx)).
- **Dependants fee:** the Ministry of Interior's published schedule set the expatriate dependants fee at SAR 400 per dependant per month from July 2020, collected in advance at Iqama issuance or renewal ([MOI](https://www.moi.gov.sa)). Confirm the current amount in Absher before paying.
- **Visa and Iqama fees:** shown on the MOFA platform and Absher at the time of application.

## Common questions

**Can my wife sponsor me or our children?**
MOFA's family visit service requires the sponsor to hold a work Iqama. Whether a working wife can sponsor her husband or children depends on the rules shown on the platform for her profession and status.

**What happens when my son turns 18?**
The Ministry of Interior notes that a resident's son or daughter must obtain a separate residence permit once they turn 18, with the applicable charges.

**Is my employer required to insure my family?**
CHI rules require the employer's health policy to cover workers and their eligible dependants. Check your policy schedule with HR.

**Can family members renew their Iqama while abroad?**
Jawazat has announced that dependants' Iqamas can be renewed from outside the Kingdom via Absher after paying the fees.
