Customers are more likely to call a business they trust. The SaudiYP verified badge is a simple signal that a business is genuine and that its listing is managed by the right people. This article explains which documents you can use and how to get approved quickly.

## Documents you can use

You only need one document that matches your business name. The more official it is, the faster the check.

- **Commercial registration (CR).** Issued by the Ministry of Commerce to establishments and companies. Most businesses can print a copy from the ministry's online services.
- **VAT registration certificate.** Issued by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) to businesses registered for VAT.
- **MISA investment licence.** For foreign-owned businesses licensed by the Ministry of Investment.
- **Balady municipal licence.** The shop or activity licence issued through the municipal Balady platform.
- **Chamber of Commerce membership certificate.** From your city's chamber of commerce.
- **SFDA licence.** For businesses that handle food, medicines, medical devices or cosmetics, from the Saudi Food and Drug Authority.
- **Professional licence.** For example the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties for doctors and dentists, the Saudi Council of Engineers for engineers, or a Ministry of Justice licence for lawyers.

## Tips for a quick approval

- **Match the names.** The name on the document should match the name on your listing. If they differ (for example an English brand name versus the Arabic trade name on your CR), mention both in your listing description.
- **Upload a clear copy.** A sharp photo or PDF of the whole document, with no parts cut off.
- **Confirm your phone number** from the dashboard. It is part of verification.
- **Keep it current.** Expired licences can't be used.

## What happens to your documents

Documents are used only to verify your business and are never shown on your public listing. You can see the status of each document in **My verification** in your dashboard.

## Is verification required?

No. Listing on SaudiYP is free and does not require verification. But the badge helps customers choose you over unverified competitors, and on region pages customers can filter to show verified businesses only.

## Already registered with the authorities?

Even if your CR and licences are all in order, customers can't see that unless you show it. The badge does that for you in one glance.
