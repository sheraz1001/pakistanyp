Getting your business onto SaudiYP takes about ten minutes and costs nothing. This guide walks through each step and explains the choices that make the biggest difference to how customers find you.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your business name exactly as customers know it (and in Arabic, if your signboard or CR uses an Arabic name).
- A short description of what you do, who you serve and what makes you different.
- Your phone and WhatsApp numbers, email and website (if you have one).
- Your full address and working hours.
- Your logo or a clear photo of your shopfront.

## Step 1: Create a free account

Choose **List your business** at the top of any page and sign up with your email. You will use this account to manage your listing, reply to enquiries and request verification later.

## Step 2: Choose your main category and sub-category

Categories decide where your business appears, so take a moment here.

- First choose the **main category**, the broad sector such as Construction & Contractors, Health & Medical or Education & Training.
- Then choose the **sub-category** that describes what you actually do, for example MEP & HVAC Contractors, Dental Clinics or Tutoring & Test Prep.
- Where available, add the **specialised category** too, such as AC Maintenance or Qudurat & Tahsili Prep.

Both a main category and a sub-category are required. Main categories group related businesses; customers browse them to reach the sub-category lists where businesses actually appear.

## Step 3: Choose your region, city and district

SaudiYP is organised the way people search: by region, city and neighbourhood.

- Pick your **region**.
- Pick your **city**.
- Pick your **district**, such as Olaya, Al Hamra, Al Aziziyah or Al Shati. If your town has no separate districts listed, your business is filed under the town itself.

Choosing the right district matters: your business will appear on that district's page and on its city page, which is where nearby customers look.

## Step 4: Add contact details customers can use

Add your phone number in the usual Saudi format, for example 050 123 4567. SaudiYP shows it as +966 50 123 4567 and turns it into a click-to-call button. Add a WhatsApp number if you take orders or questions there; many customers prefer it.

## Step 5: Write a description people will read

Write two or three short paragraphs:

1. What you do, in plain words.
2. Who you serve and where (for example "families in Al Malqa and Al Nakheel").
3. Why customers choose you: experience, guarantees, delivery, timings.

Avoid writing in all capital letters or repeating keywords. A clear description helps both customers and search engines.

## Step 6: Hours, logo and photos

Add your working hours, including weekend timings and any break during prayer times, and upload your logo. Listings with a logo and accurate hours get noticeably more attention.

## Step 7: Submit and get verified

Submit the listing. Our team reviews new listings before they go live to keep the directory accurate. While you wait, you can start verification from your dashboard by confirming your phone number and uploading a document such as your commercial registration (CR) or VAT certificate. Verified businesses get a badge that customers trust.

## After you're listed

- Reply quickly to enquiries and quote requests in your dashboard.
- Ask happy customers to leave a review.
- Keep your hours and phone number up to date, especially around Ramadan and the Eid holidays.

That's it. Your business now has a page that customers in your city and district can find, call and message.
