Choosing between wedding halls in Riyadh is usually the first and biggest decision in planning a Saudi wedding. The venue sets your date, guest numbers and much of your budget, and many popular halls are booked months in advance for peak seasons. Most couples choose between a dedicated wedding hall (*qasr afrah*), a hotel ballroom and a private rest house (*istiraha*), each with different costs and flexibility.

After the venue, the key bookings are catering, décor and the bridal stage (*kosha*), bridal beauty, photography and entertainment. This guide walks you through venue types, a planning timeline, the vendors to book and an indicative budget, whether you are marrying in Riyadh, Jeddah or elsewhere in the Kingdom.

:::takeaways
- Book your venue first: popular wedding halls and hotel ballrooms fill up months ahead of peak seasons.
- Hotel ballrooms usually price per guest, while many halls price per night with catering separate.
- Many Saudi weddings have separate celebrations or sections for men and women, which affects layout and vendors.
- Book female photographers and bridal beauty artists early for the women's celebration.
- Get every quote in writing, including VAT at 15%, service hours and cancellation terms.
:::

## Types of wedding venues in Saudi Arabia

### Dedicated wedding halls

Wedding halls are built for weddings, with large halls, separate sections or entrances for men and women, a stage for the *kosha*, kitchens and parking. They are often booked per night, sometimes with catering packages offered by the hall or by approved caterers.

### Hotel ballrooms

Hotel ballrooms offer convenience: catering, staff, décor partners and rooms for out-of-town guests in one place. They typically quote a package per guest, plus VAT. Prices vary widely between mid-range and five-star hotels.

### Rest houses and private venues

An *istiraha* (rest house) or private villa venue can suit smaller weddings, family gatherings and pre-wedding events such as the *milka* (marriage contract celebration) or henna night. Costs can be lower, but you organise more yourself, from catering to furniture. Browse [rest houses and chalets](/categories/rest-houses-chalets) to compare options.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Hotel ballroom | Per guest package plus VAT | All-in-one convenience | Minimum guest numbers |
| Rest house | Per night or per day | Smaller events, henna nights | You arrange most services |
| Outdoor venue | Per event, varies | Seasonal events, winter months | Weather and permits |

## How to choose wedding halls in Riyadh

Riyadh has a wide range of wedding halls, from large halls along main roads to hotel ballrooms in Olaya and north Riyadh. Start with four filters.

1. **Guest numbers:** confirm the capacity of each section, not just the total.
2. **Location:** a central or north Riyadh hall makes it easier for guests to arrive, and parking is critical.
3. **Date:** check whether your preferred date falls in a peak period. Use the [Hijri date converter](/holidays/hijri-converter) to see how Islamic calendar dates fall in the Gregorian year.
4. **What is included:** ask about catering, décor, sound, lighting, staff and service hours.

> [!TIP]
> Visit shortlisted wedding halls in Riyadh on a night when another wedding is being held, if the hall allows it. You will see how the staff manage entry, service and timing, which a brochure cannot show.

## Your wedding planning timeline

:::steps Saudi wedding planning timeline
- 9–12 months before | Agree the budget and guest list, and book the venue for your date.
- 6–8 months before | Book catering, décor and kosha, photography and entertainment.
- 4–5 months before | Choose the dress, abaya and thobe, and book bridal beauty.
- 2–3 months before | Send invitations and confirm the menu and seating.
- 1 month before | Confirm timings with every vendor and pay agreed deposits.
- Wedding week | Final fittings, décor setup and a timeline shared with family helpers.
:::

Before the celebration, the marriage contract must be completed. Saudi nationals must complete the premarital medical screening required by the Ministry of Health, and marriage contracts are documented through licensed marriage officials and the Ministry of Justice. Check the current requirements well before your date, especially if one partner is not Saudi.

## The vendors you need for a Saudi wedding

### Catering and hospitality

Food is central to Saudi hospitality. Traditional dishes, Arabic coffee and dates, sweets and a dinner buffet are typical. Compare [event catering and hospitality](/categories/event-catering-hospitality) providers, and ask for a tasting.

### Décor and the kosha

The *kosha*, the bridal stage, is the centrepiece of the women's celebration. Décor companies handle flowers, lighting, entrance design and table setups. See [décor and setup companies](/categories/decor-setup).

### Bridal beauty and fashion

Book hair and make-up artists early, especially for busy seasons. Many brides also plan separate looks for the henna night and the wedding. Find [bridal beauty specialists](/categories/bridal-beauty).

### Photography and video

Families often prefer female photographers and videographers for the women's section, and strict rules about photos are common. Agree in writing what can be filmed and shared. Browse [photography and media](/categories/photography-media).

### Entertainment, gifts and extras

Traditional performers, such as *ardah* troupes, singers and drummers, are popular. Gifts of oud, perfume and gold for the bride and families are common traditions. See [traditional arts and entertainment](/categories/traditional-arts-entertainment) and [oud and perfume shops](/categories/oud-perfumes).

## Wedding budget: what a Saudi wedding costs

Wedding costs vary enormously by city, guest numbers and style. Hotel ballrooms commonly quote per guest. Indicative packages range from a few hundred riyals per guest at mid-range hotels to well over SAR 1,000 per guest at some five-star hotels, plus 15% VAT. Halls are often booked per night with catering priced separately.

:::bars Indicative wedding budget split (% of total, indicative)
- Venue | 30 | about 30%

- Décor and kosha | 15 | about 15%
- Dress, beauty and fashion | 10 | about 10%

> [!NOTE]
> These shares are indicative. Your own split depends on your priorities. Many families spend more on catering and décor, and less on entertainment. Set your total first, then allocate.

## Questions to ask every wedding vendor

:::checklist
- Is the date available, and how long is the hold before a deposit?
- What exactly is included, and what costs extra?
- Is VAT at 15% included in the quote?
- What are the service hours and overtime charges?
- What is the cancellation and postponement policy?
- Can you show photos or videos of recent events?
- Who will be in charge on the night, and how do we reach them?
- Is the final agreement in writing with payment dates?
:::

> [!WARNING]
> Never pay a full amount upfront to a vendor you found only on social media. Check that the business is registered, pay deposits by traceable methods and keep the written agreement.

:::faq
### How much does a wedding hall cost in Riyadh?
Prices vary widely by hall, location, season and day of the week. Many halls are booked per night, with catering priced separately, while hotel ballrooms usually quote a package per guest, from a few hundred riyals at mid-range hotels to well over SAR 1,000 at some five-star hotels, plus VAT (indicative). Always request written quotes from several venues.

### How far in advance should I book a wedding hall in Saudi Arabia?
Book as early as you can, ideally nine to twelve months ahead for a popular venue in peak season. Demand often rises after Ramadan and Eid and during school holidays. If your date is flexible, ask venues about quieter weeks and weekday options, which can be easier to secure and sometimes cheaper.

### What is a kosha at a Saudi wedding?
The kosha is the decorated bridal stage, usually in the women's section, where the bride and sometimes the groom sit and are greeted by guests. It is often the centrepiece of the décor, with flowers, lighting and fabric. Décor companies design and set up the kosha, and prices depend on size and materials.

### Do Saudi weddings have separate sections for men and women?
Many Saudi weddings have separate celebrations or sections for men and women, which is why wedding halls are often designed with separate halls and entrances. Practices vary between families and regions, and some celebrations are mixed. Confirm the layout you need before booking a venue and choosing vendors such as photographers.

### What documents are needed to get married in Saudi Arabia?
Requirements depend on nationality. Saudi couples complete the Ministry of Health premarital medical screening, and the marriage contract is documented by a licensed marriage official through the Ministry of Justice systems. Marriages involving a non-Saudi partner can need additional approvals. Check current requirements with the Ministry of Justice well before your planned date.

### Is a hotel ballroom better than a wedding hall?
A hotel ballroom offers convenience, with catering, staff and guest rooms in one place, and typically charges per guest. A dedicated wedding hall often offers more space, separate sections and more flexibility with vendors, usually priced per night. Compare total costs for your guest numbers, including catering, VAT and décor, before deciding.

### Can I hold a wedding at a rest house (istiraha)?
Yes. Rest houses are popular for smaller weddings, henna nights and family celebrations. They can cost less than a hall or hotel, but you usually arrange catering, furniture, décor and staff yourself. Check the capacity, parking, facilities and the owner's rules on timing and noise before you book.

### What should I ask a wedding photographer?
Ask to see full galleries from recent weddings, confirm whether female photographers are available for the women's section, agree what can be filmed and shared, and get the delivery timeline in writing. Ask about the number of photographers, the hours covered, editing, albums and the cancellation policy.

### How much should I budget for wedding catering?
Catering is often one of the largest costs, typically around a quarter of the total budget (indicative). Hotel ballroom packages usually include catering in the per-guest price, while halls may use their own or approved caterers. Ask for a tasting, a clear menu, staff numbers and whether coffee, dates and sweets are included.

### How do I find reliable wedding vendors in Saudi Arabia?
Use verified business listings, ask friends and family for recommendations, look at full examples of recent work and check reviews. Make sure each vendor is a registered business, put agreements in writing and pay deposits by traceable methods.
