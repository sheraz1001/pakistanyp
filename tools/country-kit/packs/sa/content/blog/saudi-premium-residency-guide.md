Saudi Premium Residency is a residence permit that lets eligible foreigners live, work and do business in the Kingdom without an employer or other sponsor. It is managed by the Premium Residency Center, and there are now seven categories: two paid options (unlimited duration and limited duration) and five categories introduced in January 2024 for special talent, gifted individuals, investors, entrepreneurs and real estate owners.

The right category depends on what you bring. Senior professionals with high salaries usually look at Special Talent, business owners at Investor or Entrepreneur, and property buyers at Real Estate Owner. The paid options suit people who can meet the general conditions and pay a fee. This guide explains each category, the fees and thresholds reported at the time of writing, the benefits and how to apply.

:::takeaways
- There are seven Premium Residency categories, after five new ones were added in January 2024.
- The original options are widely reported at SAR 800,000 for unlimited duration and SAR 100,000 a year for limited duration.
- The five newer categories are widely reported to carry a SAR 4,000 fee, with eligibility based on salary, investment, talent or property.
- Holders can live with family, own property, run a business and travel freely without a sponsor, subject to the rules.
- Thresholds and fees change, so confirm them with the Premium Residency Center before you apply.
:::

## What is Saudi Premium Residency?

Premium Residency, sometimes called the Saudi "green card", was introduced in 2019. Unlike a standard iqama, which is tied to an employer, a Premium Residency holder is sponsored by no one. That gives more freedom to change jobs, start a company or invest.

Uptake is growing. Saudi Arabia issued 8,074 Premium Residency permits in 2024, according to figures from the National Transformation Program reported in the media.

:::stats Premium Residency at a glance
- 7 | Premium Residency categories since January 2024
- SAR 800,000 | unlimited-duration option (widely reported)
- SAR 100,000 | limited-duration option, per year (widely reported)
- 8,074 | permits issued in 2024 (as reported)
:::

## The seven Saudi Premium Residency categories

| Category | Who it is for | Main reported requirement | Reported fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unlimited duration | General applicants | Meet general conditions | SAR 800,000 one-time |
| Limited duration | General applicants | Meet general conditions | SAR 100,000 per year |
| Special Talent | Executives, health and science professionals, researchers | Minimum monthly salary by profession | SAR 4,000 |
| Gifted | Talents in culture, arts and sport | Recognised achievements and endorsement | SAR 4,000 |
| Investor | Investors | Investment of at least SAR 7 million and job creation | SAR 4,000 |
| Entrepreneur | Startup founders | Funding from an accredited entity (reported from SAR 400,000) | SAR 4,000 |
| Real Estate Owner | Property owners | Residential property worth at least SAR 4 million | SAR 4,000 |

These figures are as reported by reputable law firms and news outlets at the time of writing. The Premium Residency Center sets the official criteria and can change them.

### Special Talent

This category targets highly skilled professionals. Reported salary thresholds are at least SAR 80,000 a month for executives, at least SAR 35,000 for healthcare and science professionals, and at least SAR 14,000 for researchers, along with qualifications, experience and an employer endorsement.

### Gifted

The Gifted category covers people with exceptional talent in culture, the arts and sport, usually with recognised awards or achievements and an endorsement from the relevant Saudi authority.

### Investor

The Investor category is reported to require an investment of at least SAR 7 million in an economic activity in Saudi Arabia, a commercial registration and the creation of jobs, reported as at least ten within two years. Compare [investment firms and advisers](/categories/investments) if you are structuring a large investment.

### Entrepreneur

The Entrepreneur category is for startup founders backed by an accredited investor, incubator or accelerator. Widely reported conditions include funding of at least SAR 400,000 and a meaningful ownership stake. Read our [guide to small business ideas](/blogs/small-business-ideas-in-saudi-arabia) and the [steps to start a business in Saudi Arabia](/blogs/how-to-start-a-business-in-saudi-arabia).

### Real Estate Owner

This category is reported to require ownership of developed residential property worth at least SAR 4 million, free of mortgage and valued by an accredited valuer. [Property developers and sales agents](/categories/property-sales-development) can tell you which listings meet the threshold. Our news analysis of the [foreign real estate ownership law](/news/foreign-ownership-real-estate-law-saudi) explains the wider rules for foreign buyers.

## Benefits of Premium Residency

According to the Premium Residency Center, benefits generally include:

- **No sponsor:** you are not tied to an employer and can change jobs more freely.
- **Family:** you can live with your family and sponsor relatives, subject to the rules.
- **Business:** you can do business and own companies, subject to the investment rules.
- **Property:** you can own residential, commercial and industrial property, with restrictions in some areas, such as Makkah and Madinah.
- **Travel:** you can leave and re-enter the Kingdom without applying for [exit and re-entry visas](/expat-guide/exit-re-entry).
- **Fees:** holders are commonly reported to be exempt from the expatriate levy and the dependent fee, which is SAR 400 a month per dependent for standard iqama holders.

> [!NOTE]
> Benefits and exemptions can differ by category and change over time. Confirm the exact benefits for your category before you compare costs with a standard iqama.

To compare with standard residency, read our guides to [iqama issuance and renewal](/expat-guide/iqama-issuance-renewal) and [family residency and dependents](/expat-guide/family-residency-dependents).

## How to apply for Saudi Premium Residency

Applications are submitted online through the Premium Residency Center's official portal. The process is broadly similar for every category.

:::steps Premium Residency application steps
- Check eligibility | Choose the category that matches your salary, investment, talent or property.
- Prepare documents | Gather your passport, photos, financial and category-specific documents.
- Apply online | Create an account on the official Premium Residency portal and submit the application.
- Pay the fee | Pay the application or residency fee for your category.
- Complete checks | Pass medical, security and credit checks as required.
- Receive approval | Receive your Premium Residency and register your family members.
:::

### General conditions

Reported general conditions include a valid passport, being over 21 years old, proof of financial solvency, a clean criminal record and a recent medical report. If you are already in the Kingdom, you need a valid residence permit.

### Documents to prepare

:::checklist
- Valid passport and recent photographs
- Current iqama, if you already live in Saudi Arabia
- Bank statements or other proof of financial solvency
- Criminal record certificate, attested if required
- Medical report
- Employment contract and salary certificate (Special Talent)
- Commercial registration and investment proof (Investor)
- Funding agreement and investor letter (Entrepreneur)
- Title deed and accredited valuation (Real Estate Owner)
- Certified Arabic translations where required
:::

[Translation and attestation offices](/categories/translation-attestation) can prepare certified translations, and [lawyers and law firms](/categories/lawyers-law-firms) can review your eligibility.

> [!WARNING]
> Apply only through the official Premium Residency Center portal or a registered adviser. Be wary of anyone who promises guaranteed approval or asks for large fees in cash.

### How much does Saudi Premium Residency cost?
The original options are widely reported at SAR 800,000 for unlimited duration and SAR 100,000 a year for limited duration. The five categories introduced in January 2024 (Special Talent, Gifted, Investor, Entrepreneur and Real Estate Owner) are widely reported to carry a fee of SAR 4,000, with eligibility based on salary, talent, investment or property. Confirm current fees with the Premium Residency Center.

### What are the Premium Residency categories?
There are seven: unlimited duration, limited duration, Special Talent, Gifted, Investor, Entrepreneur and Real Estate Owner. The first two are paid options available to applicants who meet the general conditions. The five newer categories, added in January 2024, are aimed at skilled professionals, talented individuals, investors, startup founders and owners of high-value residential property.

### Who is eligible for Premium Residency?
General reported conditions include a valid passport, being over 21, proof of financial solvency, a clean criminal record and a recent medical report, plus a valid iqama if you already live in the Kingdom. Each category adds its own requirements, such as a minimum salary for Special Talent or a minimum investment for the Investor category.

### What salary do I need for Special Talent residency?
Reported thresholds are a minimum monthly salary of SAR 80,000 for executives, SAR 35,000 for healthcare and science professionals and SAR 14,000 for researchers. Applicants also need relevant qualifications, experience and an endorsement from their employer. Thresholds can change, so check the current criteria with the Premium Residency Center before applying.

### Can I buy property with Premium Residency?
Yes. Premium Residency holders can generally own residential, commercial and industrial property, with restrictions in some areas, such as Makkah and Madinah. A separate Real Estate Owner category grants residency to owners of developed residential property reported at a value of at least SAR 4 million. Check the latest real estate ownership rules before buying.

### Can Premium Residency holders sponsor their family?
Yes. Holders can live with their family in Saudi Arabia and sponsor relatives, subject to the rules for their category. Holders are commonly reported to be exempt from the dependent fee of SAR 400 per month that standard iqama holders pay for each dependent. Confirm the family rules for your category when you apply.

### Do Premium Residency holders need an exit and re-entry visa?
Premium Residency holders can generally leave and re-enter Saudi Arabia freely, without applying for the exit and re-entry visas that standard iqama holders need for each trip. This is one of the main practical benefits for people who travel often. Check the conditions attached to your category, as rules can be updated.

### How long does Premium Residency last?
It depends on the category. The unlimited-duration option is permanent while conditions are met, and the limited-duration option is renewed annually. Several of the newer categories are reported to offer five-year renewable residency, while others are linked to your continued investment or property ownership. Confirm the duration for your category with the Premium Residency Center.

### How do I apply for Premium Residency?
Apply online through the Premium Residency Center's official portal. Choose your category, create an account, upload your passport, photos and supporting documents, and pay the fee. The centre carries out checks and may request more information. Once approved, you can register family members. A registered adviser can help prepare documents.

### Is Premium Residency better than a normal iqama?
It offers more freedom: no sponsor, freer travel, property ownership, business activity and commonly reported fee exemptions. A normal iqama is tied to your employer but costs little to you directly if the employer pays. For high earners, investors and founders, the newer categories can make Premium Residency good value. Compare total costs for your family.
