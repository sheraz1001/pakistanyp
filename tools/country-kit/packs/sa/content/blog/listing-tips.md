Two businesses can offer the same service on the same street, yet one gets far more calls. Usually the difference is the listing. Here are ten things that help customers notice you and choose you.

## 1. Choose the most specific category

Choose a sub-category, and the specialised category where one fits. "AC Maintenance" or "Satha Tow Trucks" brings better enquiries than a general services category.

## 2. Pick the right district

Customers search close to home. Choose the district where your shop or office actually is, not the best-known area nearby. Your listing appears on that district's page and on your city page.

## 3. Use your real name

Use the name on your signboard and your commercial registration. Add your Arabic name too if customers know you by it.

## 4. Write for customers, not for search engines

Describe what you do, for whom and where, in plain words. Mention the brands you stock, the districts you deliver to, or the qualifications you hold.

## 5. Add a WhatsApp number

Many customers in Saudi Arabia prefer to send a message first. A WhatsApp button lowers the barrier to that first contact.

## 6. Keep your hours accurate

Show your evening hours and any closure during prayer times, and change your hours for Ramadan and the Eid holidays. Nothing loses a customer faster than a closed door during "open" hours.

## 7. Upload a logo and real photos

A clear logo and photos of your shop, team or work make a listing look established. Use your own photos, not stock images.

## 8. Get verified

Upload a commercial registration, VAT certificate, Balady licence or other registration document to earn the verified badge. Customers can filter for verified businesses on region pages, and the badge helps you stand out among unverified competitors.

## 9. Answer enquiries quickly

Quote requests are sent to several businesses at once. The first clear, polite reply often wins the job. Check your dashboard daily.

## 10. Ask for reviews

After a job well done, ask the customer to leave a review on SaudiYP. Read every review and act on fair criticism; how you handle a complaint says a lot about your business.

Small, steady improvements add up. Revisit your listing every few months and keep it as current as your shopfront.
