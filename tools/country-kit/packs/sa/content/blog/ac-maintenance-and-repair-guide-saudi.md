## Why AC maintenance matters so much in Saudi Arabia

Saudi conditions are hard on air conditioners. Long, hot summers keep compressors running for many hours a day, and dust and sandstorms clog filters and outdoor coils. In coastal cities such as Jeddah and Dammam, humidity and salty air add corrosion and drainage problems.

A neglected unit works harder to cool the same room. That means higher bills, more breakdowns and a shorter life. Residential electricity is charged at 18 halalas per kWh for the first 6,000 kWh a month and 30 halalas above that at the time of writing, so a heavy summer bill can quickly cross into the higher tier.

## A yearly AC maintenance schedule for Saudi homes

You can do some tasks yourself and leave the technical work to a professional.

:::steps Your yearly AC maintenance plan
- February to April | Book a full professional service for every unit before the heat arrives.
- Every 2–4 weeks in summer | Rinse washable filters and check the drain is not dripping indoors.
- After sandstorms | Check filters and clear dust and debris around outdoor units.
- July or August | For heavy use or dusty areas, book a second check of gas and coils.
- October to November | Note any noises, leaks or weak cooling and fix them before next summer.
:::

### What a proper service should include

- Cleaning or replacing filters
- Cleaning the indoor coil and blower
- Cleaning the outdoor condenser coil
- Flushing the drain line and checking the drip tray
- Checking refrigerant pressure and electrical connections
- Testing the capacitor and thermostat

If a technician only sprays water on the filters and leaves within ten minutes, you have not had a proper service.

## Common AC problems and what they usually mean

### The AC runs but does not cool

This is usually a dirty filter or coil, low refrigerant from a leak, or a failing compressor or capacitor. Start by cleaning the filter. If cooling does not improve, call a technician.

### Water dripping indoors

The drain line is blocked or the unit is not level. Algae and dust build up in drain pipes, especially in humid cities. A technician can flush the line quickly.

### Ice on the pipes or indoor coil

Ice usually points to low refrigerant or restricted airflow from a dirty filter. Switch the unit off, let it thaw and book an inspection.

### Strange noises or smells

Rattling can be a loose panel or fan. A burning smell needs immediate attention. Switch off at the breaker and call a professional.

> [!WARNING]
> Repeated gas top-ups are a sign of a leak, and every top-up is money lost. Ask the technician to find and repair the leak, and to tell you which refrigerant your unit uses. Older units may use R-22, which is being phased out under the international Montreal Protocol and is becoming harder to find.

## Repair or replace? Making the call

As a rule of thumb, consider replacing a unit if it is more than 10–12 years old, needs a compressor, or uses a refrigerant that is being phased out. A newer unit with a good energy efficiency rating can cut your bill noticeably, especially if you run it many hours a day.

When buying, compare energy efficiency labels, choose the right capacity for the room, and ask about warranty and installation. :::faq
### How much does AC repair cost in Riyadh?
It depends on the fault. Indicative 2025–26 prices are about SAR 50–150 for a standard split-unit cleaning, SAR 150–350 for a gas refill and SAR 100–200 extra for leak repair. Parts such as capacitors, fan motors or compressors are priced separately and vary widely. Always ask for a written quote that lists parts and labour before work begins.

### How often should I service my AC in Saudi Arabia?
Have every unit professionally serviced at least once a year, ideally between February and April before the summer heat. In dusty areas, or if you run the AC most of the day, a second service in mid-summer is worth it. Clean or rinse washable filters every two to four weeks during summer.

### How much does an AC gas refill cost in Saudi Arabia?
Indicative prices are about SAR 150–350 for a split unit, depending on the refrigerant type, the quantity needed and the unit size. Larger units can cost more. If your unit needs gas every season, it has a leak, and repairing the leak is cheaper in the long run than repeated refills.

### Why is my AC running but not cooling?
The most common causes are a dirty filter, a clogged indoor or outdoor coil, low refrigerant due to a leak, or a failing capacitor or compressor. Start by cleaning the filter and clearing debris around the outdoor unit. If cooling does not improve within an hour, book a technician to check gas pressure and electrical parts.

### Why is my AC leaking water inside?
Indoor leaks usually come from a blocked drain line, a full or cracked drip tray, or a unit that is not level. Dust and algae build up in drain pipes, especially in humid cities such as Jeddah and Dammam. A technician can flush the drain line quickly, and regular servicing prevents it.

### When is the best time to book AC maintenance?
February to April is ideal. Demand rises sharply from May, when temperatures climb and many households book at once, so waiting times and prices often increase. Booking early also gives the technician time to spot problems such as leaks or weak capacitors before they fail in the peak of summer.

### How long does a split AC last in Saudi Arabia?
With regular servicing, many split units last around 10 to 15 years, though heavy use, dust and coastal air can shorten that. If a unit is over 10 to 12 years old and needs a major repair such as a compressor, replacing it with an efficient new model is often better value.

### Does AC maintenance reduce my electricity bill?
Yes. Clean filters and coils let the unit cool the room with less effort, so the compressor runs for shorter periods. Since residential electricity is charged in tiers, keeping summer consumption down can also keep you out of the higher tariff band. Setting the thermostat a few degrees higher helps too.

### How do I find a trustworthy AC technician?
Choose a registered company with a commercial registration, clear written quotes and a warranty on parts and labour. Read reviews from customers in your district and ask the technician to explain the fault. On SaudiYP you can filter for verified businesses and send a quote request to several AC companies at once.

### Can I clean my split AC myself?
You can safely remove and rinse washable filters, wipe the front panel and keep the outdoor unit clear of dust and debris. Deep cleaning of coils, drain lines and blowers, and anything involving refrigerant or electrical parts, should be left to a trained technician. Always switch off the power before touching the unit.
