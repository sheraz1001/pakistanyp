Madinah is built around the Prophet's Mosque, and much of the city's commercial life takes place within walking distance of it. Hotels, restaurants, retailers and transport services serve a constant stream of visitors, many of them combining a visit to Madinah with Umrah in Makkah.

The city had 1,411,599 residents in the 2022 census, making it the fourth largest in the Kingdom. Beyond the central area it has extensive residential districts, farms and a growing knowledge and services sector, with Knowledge Economic City developed close to the centre.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Capital of Madinah Region
- **Population (2022 census, city):** 1,411,599 (GASTAT)
- **Telephone area code:** 014
- **Airport:** Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport
- **Rail:** Haramain High Speed Railway to King Abdullah Economic City, Jeddah and Makkah
- **Economic city:** Knowledge Economic City, established in 2010
- **Chamber of commerce:** Madinah Chamber

## Economy and main industries
Hospitality and retail dominate the central economy. Hotels and serviced apartments around the Prophet's Mosque need a steady supply of food, linen, cleaning, maintenance and staffing services, and the restaurants and shops that serve visitors operate long hours throughout the year.

Dates are the city's signature product. Madinah is the home of the Ajwa variety, and the Central Dates Market is one of the city's most important trading places, especially during the harvest from June to August. Recent market reports show sales approaching SAR 12 million over a single busy period, driven by demand from pilgrims and visitors, with Ajwa, Safawi and Sagai among the best sellers.

Knowledge Economic City, about five kilometres from the Prophet's Mosque and eight kilometres from the airport, was established in 2010 as one of the Kingdom's economic cities and is planned for offices, services and knowledge-based business.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Central area (Al-Haram):** the core of the city around the Prophet's Mosque, with the densest concentration of hotels, restaurants and shops
- **Quba:** the district of the Quba Mosque, with the Quba market along Quba Street selling clothing, spices and local goods
- **Al-Uyun:** a residential area with community markets and cafés
- **Central Dates Market:** near the Prophet's Mosque on King Faisal Road, with a new building inaugurated in September 2025; auctions and retail sales of dozens of date varieties
- **Shopping near the Haram:** supermarkets and shopping centres serving both visitors and residents close to the central area
- **Knowledge Economic City:** a planned economic city close to the centre

## Dates and the visitor trade
The date trade links Madinah's farms with its visitor economy. Harvesting begins in the first part of June across thousands of farms in the region, and the Central Dates Market handles auctions as well as retail sales to visitors. The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture inspects the market for health and quality standards. Packers, gift-box suppliers, cold stores and courier companies all depend on this trade.

New businesses register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain a municipal licence through the Balady platform. The Madinah Chamber provides certificates of origin, training and business networking.

## Finding businesses in Madinah
People search Madinah for hotels close to the Prophet's Mosque, date sellers and gift shops, transport and car rental, restaurants and caterers, and clinics. Businesses look for hotel suppliers, maintenance contractors and staffing firms. SaudiYP lists Madinah businesses by category and district.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Madinah?
Madinah's landline code is 014.

### Where is the dates market in Madinah?
The Central Dates Market is near the Prophet's Mosque on King Faisal Road. A new market building was opened in September 2025.

### How far is Knowledge Economic City from the Prophet's Mosque?
Knowledge Economic City is about five kilometres from the Prophet's Mosque and about eight kilometres from Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport.

### Which chamber of commerce covers Madinah?
The Madinah Chamber serves businesses in the city. Yanbu, elsewhere in the region, has its own separate chamber.
