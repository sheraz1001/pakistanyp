Dhahran is the smallest of the three cities of the Dammam metropolitan area, but its importance to the Saudi economy is out of proportion to its size. It is the headquarters of Saudi Aramco and has long been the centre of the Kingdom's petroleum industry, and it is home to King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), the King Abdulaziz Center for World Culture (Ithra) and the Dhahran Techno Valley research park.

For businesses, Dhahran is above all a place to work with the energy sector: suppliers, contractors, technology firms and research partners locate here or nearby in Khobar and Dammam to be close to Aramco and the university.

## Key facts
- **Status:** City in the Eastern Province, part of the Dammam metropolitan area
- **Population (2022 census, city):** 143,936, of whom 83,745 Saudi and 60,191 non-Saudi (GASTAT)
- **Telephone area code:** 013
- **Main employer:** Saudi Aramco, headquartered in Dhahran
- **University:** King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals
- **Research park:** Dhahran Techno Valley, owned by KFUPM and supported by Saudi Aramco
- **Chamber of commerce:** Asharqia Chamber

## Economy and main industries
The oil and gas industry defines Dhahran's economy. Aramco's headquarters brings with it a large network of contractors and service providers in engineering, project management, IT, facilities management, security, training and catering. Many of these firms are based in Dhahran itself, while others have offices in Khobar or yards in the Dammam industrial cities.

Research and technology form the city's second economic strand. Dhahran Techno Valley began as a park supporting the upstream oil and gas industry and is described as the Kingdom's largest concentrated park for oil and gas research and development. It operates as a company wholly owned by KFUPM, with support from Aramco, and international companies including Emerson, Halliburton and Baker Hughes have research or technology facilities there.

## Main business areas and institutions
- **Saudi Aramco headquarters:** the company's main offices and the centre of the city's energy economy
- **King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals:** the university that owns Dhahran Techno Valley and works closely with the energy industry
- **Dhahran Techno Valley:** the research park hosting corporate research centres and technology firms
- **King Abdulaziz Center for World Culture (Ithra):** an architecturally striking cultural centre and one of the city's landmarks
- **Links to Khobar and Dammam:** most retail, hotels and commercial premises serving Dhahran are found in neighbouring Khobar and along the roads to Dammam

## Research, education and business
The combination of Aramco, KFUPM and the Techno Valley brings industry and academia together on energy technology in one small city. Companies looking to work with the energy sector often seek a presence close to the headquarters and the university, and the Techno Valley offers technology firms a way to set up research facilities alongside both.

Businesses register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain a municipal licence through the Balady platform. Firms in Dhahran join the Asharqia Chamber, which serves the Dammam metropolitan area.

People search Dhahran and its neighbours for engineering and oilfield service firms, inspection and testing companies, IT and digital services, recruitment agencies, training providers and facilities management.
