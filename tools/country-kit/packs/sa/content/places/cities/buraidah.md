Buraidah is the capital of Qassim Region and the commercial centre of one of Saudi Arabia's main farming areas. The city's name is closely tied to dates: its central date market is widely described as the largest of its kind in the world, and each year's harvest turns the city into a meeting point for growers, wholesalers and buyers from across the Kingdom and beyond.

The city had 571,169 residents in the 2022 census, up from 467,410 in 2010, and Buraidah Governorate had 677,647. Besides the date trade, Buraidah is the region's administrative and retail centre, with government offices, hospitals, schools and shops serving the wider Qassim area.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Capital of Qassim Region
- **Population (2022 census):** 571,169 in the city; 677,647 in Buraidah Governorate (GASTAT)
- **Telephone area code:** 016
- **Main market:** the Buraidah date market, in the centre of the city
- **Livestock market:** the Buraidah camel market, on the outskirts of the city
- **Airport:** Qassim's regional airport at Al-Mulaida, about 30 kilometres west of the city
- **Chamber of commerce:** Qassim Chamber

## Economy and main industries
Date trading is the city's best-known business. During the season, more than a thousand trucks a day have been reported bringing dates to market, and organisers estimate annual sales in the hundreds of thousands of tonnes. More than 35 specialised date trading companies operate in the market, and dates sold at the Buraidah festival have been imported by more than 70 countries.

Around this trade sits a network of supporting businesses: packing and grading houses, cold stores, packaging suppliers, transport firms and exporters. As the regional capital, Buraidah is also a natural base for farm suppliers, from irrigation equipment and fertilisers to machinery and spare parts. Retail, construction, healthcare and education serve the growing urban population.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Buraidah date market:** in the city centre, the focus of the date trade and the annual festival, with auctions, wholesale and retail sales
- **Buraidah camel market:** also known as the Al-Qassim camel market, on the outskirts of the city
- **City centre:** government offices, banks and the main retail streets
- **Modon Oasis in Qassim:** MODON's industrial zone for the region, offering serviced plots for manufacturers

## The date season and festival
The date season usually starts in August and can last two to three months. The Buraidah date festival runs for around five weeks and works as an economic carnival, with farmers and traders selling more than 45 varieties of Qassimi dates. Sales during the festival have reached hundreds of millions of riyals; one report recorded SR230 million in just two weeks. For packers, logistics companies and exporters, these weeks are the busiest of the year.

Businesses in Buraidah obtain a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and a municipal licence through the Balady platform. Date traders and exporters usually join the Qassim Chamber for certificates of origin and trade documents.
