Jeddah is the Kingdom's second most populous city and its principal commercial centre on the Red Sea. Generations of merchants have traded here, and today the city remains the base for a large share of the importers, distributors, shipping agents and family trading houses that supply western Saudi Arabia.

The city is also the gateway for many pilgrims travelling to Makkah and Madinah, which adds hospitality, travel and transport to an already diverse economy. For anyone sourcing goods, services or suppliers in the west of the Kingdom, Jeddah is usually the first place to look.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Governorate of Makkah Region
- **Population (2022 census, city):** 3,712,917; Jeddah Governorate 3,751,722 (GASTAT)
- **Telephone area code:** 012
- **Port:** Jeddah Islamic Port on the Red Sea
- **Airport:** King Abdulaziz International Airport, with its own Haramain railway station
- **Rail:** Haramain High Speed Railway stations at Al Sulaymaniyah and the airport
- **Industrial land:** Jeddah first, second and third industrial cities, managed by MODON
- **Chamber of commerce:** Jeddah Chamber

## Economy and main industries
Trade is Jeddah's core business. Jeddah Islamic Port is the main port on the Saudi Red Sea coast, and a large share of the Kingdom's imports arrive through it, which supports clearing agents, freight forwarders, warehousing and wholesale distribution throughout the city. Makkah Region's oil export facilities are also located in Jeddah.

Manufacturing is concentrated in MODON's three Jeddah industrial cities; the first was among the original three Saudi industrial cities opened in 1973. Retail, real estate, healthcare, education and tourism complete the picture, and the steady flow of pilgrims supports hotels, travel agents and transport companies.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Al Balad:** the historic old town, founded in the 7th century and inscribed by UNESCO as Historic Jeddah, the Gate to Makkah, in 2014; traditional souqs, merchants' houses and small traders
- **Tahlia Street:** the city's best-known shopping and dining strip, with international shops, malls, restaurants and commercial offices
- **Al Hamra:** a central district along the Corniche with hotels and offices, often counted with Tahlia Street as part of the central business area
- **Al Rawdah:** an upscale residential and shopping district with schools, restaurants and shopping centres
- **Jeddah Islamic Port:** the port area, surrounded by logistics, clearing and shipping businesses
- **Jeddah industrial cities:** MODON's first, second and third industrial cities, home to factories and distribution centres

## Trade and logistics
Because so many goods enter the Kingdom through Jeddah, the city has a dense network of logistics services: customs brokers, shipping lines and agents, container hauliers, cold stores and bonded warehouses. Importers often keep showrooms or head offices in central districts and warehouses near the port or in the industrial cities. The Haramain High Speed Railway links Jeddah with Makkah, King Abdullah Economic City and Madinah, which makes the city a practical base for businesses serving both holy cities.

New companies register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain a municipal licence through the Balady platform. The Jeddah Chamber provides certificates of origin and other trade documents that importers and exporters need.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Jeddah?
Jeddah's landline code is 012, the same as Makkah and Taif.

### Where is the old town of Jeddah?
Al Balad is Jeddah's historic district, founded in the 7th century. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site, as Historic Jeddah, since 2014.

### Which chamber of commerce covers Jeddah?
The Jeddah Chamber serves businesses in Jeddah Governorate and issues certificates of origin and other trade documents.

### What are Jeddah's main shopping streets?
Tahlia Street is the best-known shopping and dining street, with Al Hamra, Al Rawdah and the historic souqs of Al Balad also popular for retail.
