Dammam is the administrative capital of the Eastern Province and the largest of the three cities, with Khobar and Dhahran, that make up the Dammam metropolitan area. While Dhahran is known for oil company offices and Khobar for retail and the waterfront, Dammam is where much of the area's port, industrial and wholesale activity takes place.

The city had 1,386,166 residents in the 2022 census. Its businesses serve both the local population and the wider industrial economy of the Gulf coast, supplying equipment, materials, labour and logistics to projects across the province.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Capital of the Eastern Province
- **Population (2022 census, city):** 1,386,166 (GASTAT)
- **Telephone area code:** 013
- **Port:** King Abdulaziz Port
- **Airport:** King Fahd International Airport
- **Industrial land:** Dammam first, second and third industrial cities, managed by MODON
- **Chamber of commerce:** Asharqia Chamber, headquartered on the Khobar–Dammam Highway in the Khaldiah district

## Economy and main industries
Industry and trade are the city's main economic activities. Dammam's first industrial city was one of the three original Saudi industrial cities opened in 1973, and today MODON runs three industrial cities here, home to factories, fabrication yards, workshops and warehouses that serve the oil, gas, petrochemical and construction sectors.

King Abdulaziz Port is the province's main commercial seaport and supports a cluster of shipping agents, customs brokers, hauliers and warehousing companies. Retail, healthcare, education and real estate serve the city's large resident population, and Dammam is also home to many of the government offices that administer the province.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Al Faisaliyah:** a central mixed residential and commercial district, close to business hubs, schools and hospitals
- **King Fahd Road and the Dammam–Khobar highway:** prime locations for offices, showrooms and commercial premises
- **Al Shatea:** the coastal district with the seaside corniche, cafés and upscale villas
- **Al Adamah:** the older core of the city, with its traditional market, shops, offices and business centres
- **Khaldiah:** the district on the Khobar–Dammam Highway where the Asharqia Chamber has its main office
- **Dammam industrial cities:** MODON's first, second and third industrial cities, the base for manufacturing and heavy services
- **King Abdulaziz Port:** the port zone and its surrounding logistics businesses

## Ports, logistics and the metropolitan area
Dammam's position on the Gulf, with the port, the industrial cities and King Fahd International Airport, makes it the main logistics base of the Eastern Province. Many companies keep warehouses and yards in the industrial cities while placing offices along King Fahd Road or the Dammam–Khobar highway, which links Dammam with Khobar and Dhahran. Road connections through Khobar lead to the King Fahd Causeway and Bahrain.

New businesses register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain a municipal licence through the Balady platform. Industrial firms apply to MODON for land in the Dammam industrial cities. The Asharqia Chamber, headquartered in Dammam, issues certificates of origin and runs the province's main business events.

## Finding businesses in Dammam
Common searches include industrial and safety equipment suppliers, steel and fabrication workshops, contractors, freight forwarders and clearing agents, spare parts dealers and staffing companies. Residents look for schools, clinics, car services and home maintenance. SaudiYP lists Dammam businesses by category and district.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Dammam?
Dammam uses the 013 landline code, the same as Khobar, Dhahran and the rest of the Eastern Province.

### Where is the Asharqia Chamber in Dammam?
The Asharqia Chamber's main office is on the Khobar–Dammam Highway in the Khaldiah district, with branches elsewhere in the province.

### Where are the industrial areas in Dammam?
MODON operates the Dammam first, second and third industrial cities, which host factories, workshops and warehouses.

### Which airport serves Dammam?
King Fahd International Airport serves Dammam and the wider metropolitan area, including Khobar and Dhahran.
