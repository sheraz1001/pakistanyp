Madinah Region covers about 150,000 square kilometres of north-western Saudi Arabia, from the Red Sea coast at Yanbu inland across the Hejaz to the oases of Khaybar and Al-Ula. Its capital, Madinah, is the second holy city of Islam and receives pilgrims and visitors throughout the year.

The region's economy rests on three quite different bases: religious tourism in Madinah, industry and shipping on the coast at Yanbu, and agriculture, especially dates, in the oases. Al-Ula has added a fourth through heritage tourism. For businesses this means a spread of opportunities, from hospitality and retail in the capital to industrial services on the coast and farm supplies in the interior.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia, made up of Madinah city and eight governorates
- **Regional capital:** Madinah
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 2,137,983, of whom 1,352,146 Saudi and 785,837 non-Saudi (GASTAT)
- **Governorates:** Yanbu, Al-Ula, Mahd al-Dhahab, Badr, Khaybar, Al-Hinakiyah, Al-Ais and Wadi Al-Far'
- **Largest governorate:** Yanbu, with 359,631 residents in the 2022 census
- **Telephone area code:** 014
- **Airports:** Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport (Madinah), Prince Abdulmohsen bin Abdulaziz International Airport (Yanbu) and Al-Ula International Airport
- **Chambers of commerce:** Madinah Chamber and Yanbu Chamber

## Economy and main industries
Madinah city dominates the services economy. Hotels, restaurants, transport operators, retailers and tour companies serve visitors to the Prophet's Mosque, and that demand supports a wide supply chain of food, cleaning, maintenance and textile businesses.

Agriculture is the second pillar. Dates are the region's best-known crop: the Ajwa variety is grown in Madinah Region, with roughly 800,000 Ajwa palms, and the harvest across around 29,000 farms in the region begins in early June and runs to late August. Other varieties traded locally include Safawi, Sagai, Amber, Medjool and Barhi.

On the coast, Yanbu is the region's most populous governorate after the capital, with its own chamber of commerce and international airport, and it is the base for industrial and marine businesses. Inland, Al-Ula has become a focus for heritage and tourism investment, while Khaybar, Al-Hinakiyah and the other governorates remain largely agricultural.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Madinah city:** the central area around the Prophet's Mosque, the Quba district and the city's central dates market; the city has its own guide
- **Knowledge Economic City:** an economic city established in 2010, about five kilometres from the Prophet's Mosque and eight kilometres from the airport
- **Yanbu:** the coastal governorate with industrial, marine and commercial activity, covered in its own guide
- **Al-Ula:** a governorate with heritage tourism, hospitality and agriculture, served by its own international airport
- **Khaybar, Badr and Al-Hinakiyah:** agricultural and trading towns on the region's inland roads

## Getting around and logistics
The region is connected to Jeddah, King Abdulaziz International Airport, King Abdullah Economic City and Makkah by the Haramain High Speed Railway, which makes day trips between the two holy cities practical for businesses and visitors. Three international airports, in Madinah, Yanbu and Al-Ula, give the region more air connections than its population alone would suggest.

New businesses register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain a municipal licence through the Balady platform for their premises. Firms in Madinah city and the inland governorates usually join the Madinah Chamber, while those based in Yanbu join the Yanbu Chamber.

## Finding businesses in Madinah Region
People searching in the region most often look for hotels and serviced apartments, date traders and packers, transport and car hire, restaurants and caterers, and building contractors. In Yanbu searches lean towards industrial and marine services, and in Al-Ula towards tourism operators. SaudiYP lists businesses by city, so results can be narrowed to Madinah, Yanbu or a smaller governorate.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Madinah Region?
Madinah Region uses the 014 landline code, which it shares with Tabuk, Al-Jouf and the Northern Borders.

### Which airports serve Madinah Region?
Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport serves Madinah, Prince Abdulmohsen bin Abdulaziz International Airport serves Yanbu, and Al-Ula has its own international airport.

### Is Yanbu part of Madinah Region?
Yes. Yanbu is a governorate of Madinah Region and the region's largest after the capital, with 359,631 residents in 2022. It has its own chamber of commerce.

### When is the date harvest in Madinah Region?
The harvest generally starts in early June and continues to the end of August, when the central dates market in Madinah is at its busiest.
