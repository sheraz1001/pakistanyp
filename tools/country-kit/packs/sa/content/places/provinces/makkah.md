Makkah Region stretches from the Red Sea coast up into the Sarawat mountains, taking in the holy city of Makkah, the port city of Jeddah and the highland governorate of Taif. It is the Kingdom's main western gateway: most sea imports and a large share of international air travellers arrive here, and millions of pilgrims pass through every year for Hajj and Umrah.

The 2022 census recorded just over eight million residents, second only to Riyadh Region. That population, combined with the pilgrimage economy and the port, supports a deep market for trading, hospitality, transport, construction and food services. Businesses in the region often serve three audiences at once: local households, importers and distributors, and visitors to the holy sites.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia, with sixteen governorates
- **Regional capital:** Makkah
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 8,021,463, of whom 4,153,723 Saudi and 3,867,740 non-Saudi (GASTAT)
- **Largest governorates by population:** Jeddah (3,751,722) and Taif (913,374), both 2022 census figures
- **Category A governorates:** Jeddah, Taif, Al-Qunfudhah, Al-Lith and Rabigh
- **Telephone area code:** 012
- **Chambers of commerce:** Jeddah Chamber, Makkah Chamber and Taif Chamber
- **Rail:** Haramain High Speed Railway linking Makkah, Jeddah, King Abdulaziz International Airport, King Abdullah Economic City and Madinah

## Economy and main industries
Three engines drive the regional economy. The first is trade through Jeddah, which hosts the region's port and oil export facilities and acts as the main distribution point for imported goods destined for western Saudi Arabia. The second is religious tourism centred on Makkah, which supports hotels, catering, transport, retail and a large range of seasonal services. The third is industry: Jeddah has first, second and third industrial cities managed by MODON, and Rabigh, further up the coast, is home to oil refining.

Rabigh Governorate is also the site of King Abdullah Economic City, established in 2005 as one of the Kingdom's economic cities, with its own station on the Haramain railway. Taif, the largest governorate in the region by area, adds agriculture and highland tourism, and is known for its rose farms in the Sarawat mountains.

## Main business areas and markets
The region's commercial activity is spread over several distinct centres, each with its own guide where available:
- **Jeddah:** the largest commercial city, with the historic Al Balad district, office and retail corridors, the Islamic port and three MODON industrial cities
- **Makkah:** hotels, retail and services clustered around the Grand Mosque and the districts that serve pilgrims
- **Taif:** a highland governorate with farming, rose cultivation and a growing visitor economy
- **Rabigh:** refining and King Abdullah Economic City on the coast north of Jeddah
- **Al-Qunfudhah and Al-Lith:** southern coastal governorates with fishing, farming and local trade

## Pilgrimage and the service economy
The Hajj and Umrah seasons shape demand across the region far more than in any other part of the Kingdom. Hotel operators, transport companies, caterers, laundries, telecom shops and money services all scale up for peak periods, and suppliers in Jeddah and Taif support them. The Haramain High Speed Railway, which links Makkah with Jeddah, the airport, King Abdullah Economic City and Madinah, has made it easier for businesses to serve both holy cities from a Jeddah base.

Businesses setting up in the region need a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and, for premises, a municipal licence through the Balady platform from the relevant municipality. Factories in the Jeddah industrial cities deal with MODON for land and permits. Membership of the local chamber, Jeddah, Makkah or Taif depending on location, is normally required for trade documents.

## Finding businesses in Makkah Region
Common searches include freight forwarders and clearing agents, wholesalers, hotel suppliers, construction and maintenance contractors, transport and car rental, and food producers. Visitors look for hotels, travel agents and services near the holy sites. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Jeddah, Makkah and Taif?
All three, and the rest of Makkah Region, use the 012 landline code.

### Which chamber of commerce should a business in Makkah Region join?
The region has separate chambers in Jeddah, Makkah and Taif. A business usually joins the chamber for the city or governorate where its Commercial Registration is issued.

### Where is King Abdullah Economic City?
It lies in Rabigh Governorate on the Red Sea coast north of Jeddah, and it has its own station on the Haramain High Speed Railway.

### Is Jeddah in Makkah Region?
Yes. Jeddah is a governorate of Makkah Region, and with 3,751,722 residents in 2022 it is the region's most populous governorate.
