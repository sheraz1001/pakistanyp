Hail Region sits in northern Saudi Arabia, north-west of Qassim. Its capital, Hail city, holds well over half of the region's people, and the land around it combines irrigated farms with open desert and mountain ranges such as Aja and Salma.

The economy is built on agriculture, government services and trade, with heritage tourism a smaller but growing addition. Government and investment bodies have highlighted the region's potential in food production and processing, and new investment is aimed at linking its farms more closely with packaging, processing and distribution businesses.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia
- **Regional capital:** Hail
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 746,406 (GASTAT)
- **Population (2022 census, Hail city):** 448,623, well over half of the regional total
- **Telephone area code:** 016
- **Agricultural holdings:** nearly 15,000, including modern farms and hydroponic facilities
- **World Heritage:** Rock Art in the Hail Region, inscribed by UNESCO in 2015
- **Chamber of commerce:** Hail Chamber

## Economy and main industries
Farming has long been Hail's economic base. Surveys of the region's farms have found date palms and alfalfa as the main crops, alongside citrus, grapes, vegetables, wheat and rye. Wheat farming expanded in earlier decades, when national self-sufficiency policy guaranteed a market for domestic wheat at a supported price.

Today the region counts nearly 15,000 agricultural holdings, ranging from traditional farms to modern hydroponic units. Initiatives are under way to expand food processing capacity and integrate the food value chain, which creates opportunities for packaging suppliers, cold storage, transport and equipment firms. The Ministry of Investment has also said it will help the region attract investment suited to its strengths.

Industrial activity is organised through Hail Industrial City, managed by MODON, while government services, retail and construction make up much of the urban economy in Hail city.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Hail city:** the regional capital, with government offices, the historic Barzan market, retail and wholesale trade and most professional services; it has its own guide
- **Hail Industrial City:** MODON's industrial zone for the region, offering serviced plots for factories and workshops
- **Jubbah:** a town about 90 kilometres north-west of Hail city, home to the Jabal Umm Sinman rock art site
- **Shuwaymis:** a remote area roughly 250 kilometres south of Hail city, home to the Jabal al-Manjor and Raat rock art site

## Heritage and tourism
The UNESCO listing covers two parts: Jabal Umm Sinman at Jubbah, set on the shore of an ancient lake, and Jabal al-Manjor and Raat at Shuwaymis. The panels record thousands of years of human activity, and the sites are protected and managed by the antiquities office in Hail. Visitors travelling to Jubbah or Shuwaymis usually base themselves in Hail city, which supports hotels, guides, car hire and four-wheel-drive tour operators.

Businesses opening in the region need a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and a municipal licence through the Balady platform for premises. Factories apply to MODON for land in Hail Industrial City. The Hail Chamber issues certificates of origin and other trade documents and runs business events in the region.

## Finding businesses in Hail Region
Common searches include farm and irrigation suppliers, agricultural machinery, fodder and feed traders, date sellers, food processors, contractors and building materials. Visitors look for hotels, tour guides and car rental for trips to the rock art sites. SaudiYP lets you search Hail city and the wider region by category.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Hail?
Hail Region uses the 016 landline code, which it shares with Qassim.

### Where are the Hail rock art sites?
There are two: Jabal Umm Sinman at Jubbah, about 90 kilometres north-west of Hail city, and Jabal al-Manjor and Raat at Shuwaymis, about 250 kilometres to the south.

### What does Hail Region produce?
Farms in the region grow dates, alfalfa, citrus, grapes, vegetables and grains including wheat. Food processing is a focus for new investment.

### Is there a chamber of commerce in Hail?
Yes. The Hail Chamber is the regional chamber of commerce and a member of the Federation of Saudi Chambers.
