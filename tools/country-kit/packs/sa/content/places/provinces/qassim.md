Qassim Region lies in the centre of the Arabian Peninsula, north-west of Riyadh, and is one of the Kingdom's main farming areas. Its towns sit among irrigated fields and palm groves, and the region is widely associated with dates: millions of palms grow here, and the date season brings traders and buyers from across the country and abroad.

Agriculture shapes almost every part of the regional economy, from farm suppliers and packing houses to cold storage, transport and food processing. Around that core, the region's towns support the usual mix of retail, construction, healthcare and education for a population of more than 1.3 million.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia
- **Regional capital:** Buraidah
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 1,336,179 (GASTAT)
- **Category A governorates:** Unaizah, Ar-Rass, Al-Midhnab, Al-Bukayriyah and Al-Bada'i
- **Telephone area code:** 016
- **Main crop:** dates, from more than eight million palm trees, with annual harvests above 200,000 tonnes
- **Chambers of commerce:** Qassim Chamber, with a separate chamber in Ar-Rass
- **Industrial land:** Modon Oasis in Qassim, managed by MODON

## Economy and main industries
Dates are the region's best-known product and the main driver of its agricultural economy. The Buraidah date season, which runs for several weeks from late summer, works as an economic event in its own right, with farmers and traders selling dozens of Qassimi date varieties. Al-Bukayriyah's date season is also described as a pillar of the regional economy.

Qassim produces much more than dates. Farms grow grapes, lemons, grapefruit, mandarins, oranges and pomegranates, together with a wide range of vegetables. This creates steady demand for irrigation equipment, fertilisers, greenhouses, farm machinery, packaging and refrigerated transport, and many of the region's small and medium-sized businesses work in these supply chains.

Food processing and light manufacturing are organised partly through Modon Oasis in Qassim, which provides serviced industrial land. Retail and wholesale trade are concentrated in Buraidah and Unaizah.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Buraidah:** the regional capital, with the main date market, wholesale trade and government offices; it has its own guide
- **Unaizah:** a Category A governorate close to Buraidah, with its own commercial centre and retail trade
- **Ar-Rass:** a Category A governorate with its own chamber of commerce
- **Al-Bukayriyah:** a farming governorate south-west of Buraidah and west of Unaizah, known for its date season
- **Al-Midhnab and Al-Bada'i:** Category A governorates with farming and local trade

## Trade and the date season
For traders, the late summer and autumn date season is the busiest time of year. Buraidah's date market and festival draw buyers from across the Kingdom, and dates sold there are imported by more than 70 countries, and packing, grading and logistics companies work at capacity. Outside the season, cold storage and processing keep part of the trade running year-round.

The regional airport, located at Al-Mulaida about 30 kilometres west of Buraidah and 40 kilometres north-west of Unaizah, serves both main towns. Businesses register with the Ministry of Commerce for a Commercial Registration and obtain municipal licences for premises through the Balady platform. Most firms join the Qassim Chamber, while those in Ar-Rass can join the Ar-Rass chamber.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Qassim?
Qassim Region uses the 016 landline code, which it shares with Hail.

### Where is the airport for Buraidah and Unaizah?
The regional airport is at Al-Mulaida, about 30 kilometres west of Buraidah and 40 kilometres north-west of Unaizah.

### When is the date season in Qassim?
The main date season and the Buraidah date market run from late summer, usually starting in August and lasting for several weeks.

### Which chamber of commerce covers Qassim?
The Qassim Chamber covers most of the region, and Ar-Rass has its own chamber of commerce. Both are members of the Federation of Saudi Chambers.
