Jazan Region occupies the far south-west of Saudi Arabia, between the Red Sea and the mountains along the border with Yemen. It had more than 1.4 million residents in 2022, spread across a string of towns rather than concentrated in one large city. The region also includes more than 200 islands in the Red Sea, the largest of which form the Farasan Islands governorate.

The economy combines agriculture and fishing with government services and trade, and in recent years a large industrial project on the coast. For businesses, the region offers a dense local market spread across several mid-sized towns rather than one dominant city.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia, with 16 governorates
- **Regional capital:** Jazan
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 1,404,997 (GASTAT)
- **Category A governorates:** Sabya, Abu Arish and Samtah, the region's main population centres
- **Islands:** more than 200 in the Red Sea, including the Farasan Islands governorate
- **Telephone area code:** 017
- **Chamber of commerce:** Jazan Chamber, founded in 1984
- **Industrial development:** Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries

## Economy and main industries
Agriculture is central to the region's economy. Jazan is known for honey and for coffee; about 1.6 per cent of the region's land is reported to be set aside for coffee growing, with some 400,000 trees. Farms also supply fruit and vegetables to local markets, and the long coastline and islands support fishing and marine trades.

The largest economic development of recent years is Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries, a large-scale industrial project on the coast developed under the Royal Commission for Jubail and Yanbu. It is intended to bring heavy and downstream industry to the region and to diversify an economy that has traditionally relied on farming, government employment and trade. Construction and operation of the project have increased demand for contractors, engineering services, transport and accommodation.

Business leaders have described the region's geostrategic position on the southern Red Sea as a key attraction for investment.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Jazan city:** the regional capital and administrative centre, with government offices and the main commercial streets; it has its own guide
- **Sabya:** a Category A governorate and one of the region's main population centres
- **Abu Arish:** a Category A governorate with its own commercial centre and markets
- **Samtah:** a Category A governorate and the third of the region's main population centres
- **Farasan Islands:** an island governorate and a major draw for visitors
- **Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries:** the coastal industrial zone

## Agriculture and local produce
Coffee and honey give the region a distinctive set of small businesses: farms, roasters, honey producers and shops selling local products. Coffee farms have become a focus of agricultural development and of visitor interest, and fruit and vegetable growers supply markets across the region. Businesses in these supply chains rely on agricultural suppliers, packaging firms and refrigerated transport.

New businesses obtain a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and a municipal licence through the Balady platform. The Jazan Chamber serves around 40,000 businesses in the region and provides certificates, training and investment events.

## Finding businesses in Jazan Region
People commonly search for contractors and building materials, transport and logistics firms, fish traders, honey and coffee producers, car services and clinics. Visitors look for boat trips, tour operators and accommodation for the Farasan Islands. SaudiYP lists businesses by city, so you can search Jazan city, Sabya, Abu Arish or Samtah separately.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Jazan?
Jazan Region uses the 017 landline code, shared with Asir, Najran and Al-Baha.

### What are the main towns in Jazan Region?
Besides the capital, Jazan city, the main population centres are the Category A governorates of Sabya, Abu Arish and Samtah.

### What is Jazan known for producing?
The region is known for honey and coffee, and its farms and fishermen also supply fruit, vegetables and fish.

### When was the Jazan Chamber founded?
The Jazan Chamber was established in 1984 and is one of the Kingdom's regional chambers of commerce, serving around 40,000 businesses.
