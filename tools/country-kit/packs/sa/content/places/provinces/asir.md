Asir Region occupies the high mountains of south-western Saudi Arabia, where the Sarawat range rises above the Red Sea coastal plain. Its cooler climate and green terraces set it apart from most of the Kingdom, and for decades it has been a summer destination for Saudi families escaping the heat of the interior.

The region's two largest centres, Abha and Khamis Mushait, sit close together on the plateau and function as one urban area for many purposes. Further afield, Bisha in the north-east and the coastal and mountain governorates to the west each have their own local economies. Government investment material describes Asir's economy as historically built on agriculture, military services and seasonal domestic tourism, with tourism and agriculture now identified as its strategic sectors.

## Key facts
- **Administrative status:** One of the 13 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia, divided into governorates including Abha, Khamis Mushait, Bisha, Muhayil and An-Namas
- **Regional capital:** Abha
- **Population (2022 census, region):** 2,024,285 (GASTAT)
- **Largest governorates (2022 census):** Khamis Mushait 601,305, Abha 422,243 and Bisha 202,096
- **Telephone area code:** 017
- **Highest terrain:** Jabal Soudah, close to 3,000 metres, near Abha
- **Chambers of commerce:** Abha Chamber and Bisha Chamber
- **Industrial land:** Modon Oasis in Asir, managed by MODON

## Economy and main industries
Agriculture remains important in the highlands, where farms grow a wide range of crops including corn, peaches, figs, apricots, pomegranates, grapes and apples, and strawberries have become a notable product around Abha. Honey and other local produce are sold in traditional markets throughout the region.

Tourism is the area receiving most new investment. Under Vision 2030, Asir is being repositioned from a summer-only destination to a year-round one, and the cable car linking Abha with the Soudah highlands is one of its established attractions. Hotels, furnished apartments, restaurants, tour operators and event companies all benefit from this shift.

Military services, linked to the region's position near the Yemeni border, have also shaped the economy and support demand for housing, retail and services. Industrial activity is organised through Modon Oasis in Asir, where MODON provides serviced land for manufacturers and workshops.

## Main business areas and markets
- **Abha:** the regional capital, with government offices, the Al Muftaha cultural district and traditional markets such as Souq Al Thulatha; covered in its own guide
- **Khamis Mushait:** the region's most populous governorate and a large commercial and residential centre next to Abha, with its own guide
- **Bisha:** the main centre of the north-eastern part of the region, with its own chamber of commerce
- **Muhayil and An-Namas:** smaller governorates with their own municipalities, shops and service providers

Weekly markets remain a feature of Asir's commercial life. Traditionally each souq is named after the day it was held, a pattern still visible in market names across the region.

## Tourism and the visitor economy
Because Asir's appeal rests on climate and landscape, demand for accommodation, transport and food services peaks in the summer months and on public holidays. Businesses serving visitors cluster in Abha and along the routes to Soudah and other mountain areas. Many seasonal operators are small family firms, and local sourcing of honey, fruit and handicrafts is common.

Anyone opening a shop, restaurant or guesthouse needs a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and a municipal licence through the Balady platform. Tourism businesses may also need licensing from the tourism authorities. Membership of the Abha Chamber, or the Bisha Chamber in the north-east, gives access to certificates and local business events.

## Finding businesses in Asir Region
Visitors typically look for hotels, furnished apartments, car rental, restaurants and tour guides, while residents and companies search for contractors, building materials, car repairs, clinics and agricultural suppliers. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Abha and Khamis Mushait?
Both cities, and the rest of Asir Region, use the 017 landline code, which is shared with Jazan, Najran and Al-Baha.

### What is the largest city in Asir Region?
Khamis Mushait is the most populous governorate, with 601,305 residents in the 2022 census, ahead of Abha with 422,243. Abha is the regional capital.

### Is there a chamber of commerce in Asir?
Yes. The Abha Chamber serves the regional capital and surrounding areas, and Bisha has its own chamber for the north-east of the region.

### What are Asir's main economic sectors?
Tourism and agriculture are the region's strategic sectors, supported by government services, retail and a growing hospitality industry around Abha and Soudah.
