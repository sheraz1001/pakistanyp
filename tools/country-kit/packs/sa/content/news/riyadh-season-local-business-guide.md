**Riyadh Season** 2026, the seventh edition of the capital's winter entertainment festival, opened on 21 October 2026 with a headline programme of about ten weeks. Turki Alalshikh, chairman of the General Entertainment Authority (GEA), announced the dates in late September under the slogan "Big Time".

For local businesses, the season means months of extra footfall: concerts, boxing and wrestling nights, tennis, family zones and visitors from across the Kingdom and abroad. Here is what is on this year, what past seasons achieved, and how cafés, restaurants, caterers, drivers, event suppliers and shops can make the most of it.

:::takeaways
- The 2026 edition opened on 21 October 2026, with a headline programme of around ten weeks; some zones stay open into 2027.
- The GEA announced the season in late September 2026 under the slogan "Big Time".
- Reported highlights include the Six Kings Slam tennis event, boxing, WWE and UFC events, concerts and family zones such as Boulevard World and Wonder Garden.
- Previous seasons drew millions of visitors; the 2025 edition passed 11 million visitors by 31 December 2025.
- Most of the business benefit comes indirectly, through spending around venues, events and visitor stays.
:::

## Riyadh Season 2026: dates, slogan and what is on

The GEA confirmed 21 October 2026 as the opening date, and the Saudi Press Agency carried the announcement with the theme and programme. Some sources describe a wider season running until early March 2027 for certain zones, while the headline programme covers about ten weeks.

:::stats The 2026 season at a glance
- 7th | edition of the festival
- 21 Oct 2026 | opening date
- ~10 weeks | headline programme
- 11 million+ | visitors to the 2025 edition by 31 December 2025
:::

### Headline events

Reported highlights for 2026 include:

- **Six Kings Slam** tennis, listed for 21, 22 and 24 October at ANB Arena
- **Boxing, wrestling and UFC** events, including WWE Crown Jewel, listed for 7 November
- **Concerts, theatre and live shows** across the season
- **Family zones**, with Boulevard World, Boulevard City and Wonder Garden among the principal zones named in reports

> [!NOTE]
> Event dates and zone opening hours change during the season. Check the official Riyadh Season channels and the GEA website, https://www.gea.gov.sa, for the current programme rather than relying on listings sites.

Visitor numbers are the clearest sign of the season's pull, though figures from different sources are not always comparable.

## Where season spending reaches local businesses

Ticket revenue goes to organisers and venues, but visitors spend much more around their night out.

### Food, coffee and catering

Visitors eat before and after events, and many zones host food outlets. [Cafés and coffee shops](/categories/cafes-coffee), [fast-food and casual restaurants](/categories/fast-food-casual) and [catering and home food businesses](/categories/catering-home-food) near venues and on the main routes see the effect first.

### Transport

Big events create demand for [taxis and transport services](/categories/transport-taxis), [car rental](/categories/car-rental-leasing) and parking. The [Riyadh Metro](/news/riyadh-metro-lines-fares-guide) carries many visitors too, so businesses near stations on event routes can benefit.

### Event suppliers and staffing

Organisers and sponsors need [event management companies](/categories/event-management), [event supplies and staffing](/categories/event-supplies-staffing), [decor and setup firms](/categories/decor-setup) and [photography and media](/categories/photography-media) teams, plus security, cleaning and logistics.

### Accommodation and visitors from outside Riyadh

Visitors from other cities and abroad need hotels and furnished apartments. National tourism figures show how much domestic travel already drives demand; see our report on [Saudi tourism growth](/news/saudi-tourism-growth-local-businesses).

## Ways to take part in the season directly

Some businesses go further and take part inside the season itself.

- **Outlets in zones.** In 2024 the GEA signed an agreement with the Social Development Bank to allocate sales outlets for productive families and entrepreneurs in key locations such as Boulevard World. Watch for similar schemes each year.
- **Supplying organisers.** Event companies and contractors buy food, equipment, printing, staffing and services. Being easy to find and vet gives you a better chance.
- **Sponsorship.** Larger businesses sign sponsorship contracts each season. These are usually negotiated well before launch.
- **Your own licensed event.** If you want to run an entertainment activity, you need a GEA licence. Apply through the GEA's official website, and allow time for approval.

> [!WARNING]
> Running an entertainment event, show or activity for the public without the right licence can lead to it being shut down and fines. Check the GEA's licensing requirements before you sell a single ticket.

## A practical Riyadh Season plan for small businesses

The season is already under way, but there is time to act on the busiest weeks still ahead, and to prepare properly for next year.

:::steps Your season plan
- Check the calendar | Note big event nights near you and the dates of public holidays.
- Adjust hours | Open later on event nights and around weekends.
- Staff up | Add shifts or temporary staff for the busiest evenings.
- Update your listing | Show current hours, location and menu or services.
- Prepare offers | Create pre-event and post-event deals that are quick to serve.
- Review and repeat | Record sales by night and use them to plan next season.
:::

:::checklist
- Opening hours updated online for event nights
- Card and mobile payments working at every till
- Stock levels raised for weekends and big events
- Delivery partners briefed for traffic and road closures
- Staff briefed on peak-hour service and visitor questions in English
- Photos and menu up to date on your listing
:::

> [!TIP]
> Roads near major venues get congested on event nights. If you deliver, plan routes and times around the event calendar, and tell customers about delays before they order.

The [public holidays calendar](/holidays) helps you line up the season's peaks with long weekends and school breaks.

## What the season means for Riyadh's year-round economy

The festival is no longer a one-off. Together with other events such as the AFC Asian Cup in January and February 2027 and, later, Expo 2030, it gives Riyadh businesses several predictable peaks each year. Firms that build systems for staffing, stock and online visibility around these peaks tend to benefit every year, not just once.

If you are new to the city, browse [businesses in Riyadh](/cities/riyadh) on SaudiYP to see who is already serving event-goers in your area.

## Final word: be easy to find during Riyadh Season

The season brings millions of people out into the city, but most of them decide where to eat, park or shop on their phones, often minutes before. Make sure your hours, location and contact details are correct, plan your staffing around the big nights, and give visitors a reason to come back after the season ends.

### When does Riyadh Season 2026 start and end?
The 2026 edition opened on Wednesday 21 October 2026, with a headline programme of about ten weeks, which takes it to around the end of the year. Some zones are reported to stay open longer, into early 2027. Check the official Riyadh Season channels and the GEA for exact closing dates of each zone, as these can change.

### What is the theme this year?
The seventh edition runs under the slogan "Big Time". GEA chairman Turki Alalshikh announced the opening date and slogan in late September 2026, and the Saudi Press Agency carried the announcement. The programme includes major sporting events, concerts, theatre and family zones across the capital.

### What events are on this season?
Reported highlights include the Six Kings Slam tennis event at ANB Arena in October, boxing and wrestling events including WWE Crown Jewel, UFC bouts, concerts and live shows. Family zones named in reports include Boulevard World, Boulevard City and Wonder Garden. Dates can change, so check the official programme before booking.

### How many people visit Riyadh Season?
Press reports attributed a record 20 million visitors to the fifth edition in 2024, citing the GEA chairman. The 2025 edition passed 11 million visitors by 31 December 2025, according to Saudi Gazette, and a later figure of 14 million was also reported. Official totals for 2026 will come from the GEA during and after the season.

### How can my business take part?
You can supply organisers and contractors, apply for outlets when schemes for small businesses are offered, sponsor events if you are a larger company, or run your own licensed activity with a GEA licence. Most small businesses benefit indirectly by serving visitors near venues, so up-to-date listings and opening hours matter.

### Do I need a licence to run an event?
Yes. Entertainment activities and events open to the public in Saudi Arabia need a licence from the General Entertainment Authority. Apply through the GEA's official website and allow time for approval. Running an unlicensed event can lead to it being closed and to penalties, so check the requirements before you advertise or sell tickets.

### Is the season good for restaurants and cafés?
Usually, yes, particularly for businesses near venues, on main routes or inside event zones. Visitors eat before and after events and often decide at the last minute. Extending hours on event nights, offering quick menus, accepting card and mobile payments and keeping your online listing current all help you win that spending.

### How do I get to the venues?
Many visitors drive or use taxis and ride-hailing apps, and the Riyadh Metro serves several venue areas. Expect heavy traffic and road closures near major events, especially at weekends. Check venue information on the official season channels for parking, metro access and recommended routes before you set off.

### What jobs does the season create?
Reports have cited more than 25,000 direct jobs and 100,000 indirect jobs linked to the season, covering events, hospitality, security, transport and retail, though the year these figures refer to is not always clear. Many roles are seasonal. Event companies and staffing agencies usually recruit in the weeks before and during the season.

### Who organises Riyadh Season?
Riyadh Season is overseen by the General Entertainment Authority (GEA), chaired by Turki Alalshikh, with events delivered by a wide range of organisers, venues, promoters and contractors. Reports say around 2,100 Saudi companies took part in delivering recent editions, most of them small and medium-sized businesses.
