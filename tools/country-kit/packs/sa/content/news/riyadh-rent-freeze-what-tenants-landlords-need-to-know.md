The **Riyadh rent increase freeze** means that, for five years from 25 September 2025, landlords cannot raise the rent on residential or commercial property inside Riyadh's urban boundary. It applies to existing leases and new ones, and a landlord who breaks the rules can be fined up to the equivalent of 12 months' rent.

The measure was directed by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and issued by royal decree after soaring rents in the capital. The Real Estate General Authority (REGA) oversees it. More than a year in, it remains in force, and no extension to other cities had been announced at the time of writing. Here is what it covers, what it does not, and what to do if your landlord asks for more.

:::takeaways
- Annual increases in total rent are suspended for five years from 25 September 2025 in Riyadh's urban area.
- The freeze covers residential and commercial leases, both existing and new.
- Vacant units that were rented before are capped at the last registered rent; never-rented units can be priced freely.
- Leases renew automatically unless notice is given at least 60 days before expiry, and Riyadh landlords can refuse renewal only in limited cases.
- Breaches can lead to fines of up to 12 months' rent, plus compensation for the affected party.
:::

## What the Riyadh rent increase freeze says

The decision, announced on 25 September 2025, suspends "the annual increase in the total rent value" for residential and commercial leases within Riyadh's urban boundary for five years. It took effect the same day.

:::stats The freeze at a glance
- 5 years | length of the freeze from 25 September 2025
- 60 days | notice needed before expiry to stop automatic renewal
- 12 months' rent | maximum fine for a breach
- 30 days | to appeal a fine
:::

### Which properties are covered

The rules apply to properties inside Riyadh's urban boundary, both homes and commercial units such as shops and offices. Properties elsewhere in Riyadh Province, and in other cities such as Jeddah, Dammam and Madinah, are not covered unless the freeze is formally extended.

### Vacant and new units

Reports of the rules describe two cases:

- **A unit that was rented before** and is now empty: the rent for a new tenant is fixed at the value of the most recent registered contract.
- **A unit that has never been rented**, such as a new build: the landlord and tenant can agree the first rent freely. After that, the freeze applies.

> [!NOTE]
> Press and law-firm summaries differ slightly in wording. For the exact legal text, check REGA at https://rega.gov.sa or the official decision published by the Saudi Press Agency.

## Automatic renewal and when a landlord can refuse

The rules package also changed how leases end. A lease renews automatically unless one party gives notice at least **60 days** before it expires.

In Riyadh, a landlord cannot refuse renewal if you want to stay, except in three situations:

1. **Non-payment** of rent by the tenant.
2. **Structural defects** that make the property unsafe.
3. **Personal use**, where the landlord or a first-degree relative will live in or use the property.

This matters as much as the rent cap itself. Without it, a landlord could sidestep the freeze by ending a lease and finding a new tenant.

| Situation | What the rules say |
| --- | --- |
| You renew your current lease | Rent stays the same; no annual increase |
| You move into a unit that was rented before | Rent fixed at the last registered contract value |
| You rent a brand-new unit | First rent agreed freely, then frozen |
| Landlord wants you out to re-let at a higher rent | Not a valid reason to refuse renewal in Riyadh |
| Landlord needs the unit for a first-degree relative | A permitted reason, with proper notice |
| Property is outside Riyadh's urban boundary | Not covered unless REGA extends the freeze |

## Ejar registration and the 60-day objection window

Leases must be registered on **Ejar**, the government's rental contract platform. Under the new rules, once a contract is registered, the other party has 60 days to object to its details. If nobody objects, the registered data is treated as valid.

That puts the onus on you to check what your landlord or broker registers. Look carefully at:

- the annual rent and payment schedule
- the start and end dates
- the unit description and number
- any extra charges such as service fees

> [!TIP]
> Download your Ejar contract as soon as it is registered and compare it with what you agreed. A wrong rent figure that you do not challenge within 60 days can become the official record, and the frozen rent is based on that record.

## Fines and enforcement under the Riyadh rent increase freeze

The penalties are designed to bite. Reported rules allow:

- a fine of up to the equivalent of **12 months' rent** for the unit concerned
- an order to correct the breach and compensate the affected party
- an appeal against a fine within **30 days**
- a reward of up to **20% of the fine** for people who report violations

These amounts are maximums. How REGA applies them case by case is not fully public, so if you are a landlord, take advice before you change any lease terms.

## What to do if your landlord tries to raise the rent

If your landlord asks for more money at renewal, do not simply pay it. Follow these steps.

:::steps If your rent goes up in Riyadh
- Check your location | Confirm the property is inside Riyadh's urban boundary.
- Check Ejar | Compare the new rent with your last registered contract.
- Reply in writing | Point out the freeze politely and keep a record.
- Keep paying the old rent | Pay on time so non-payment cannot be used against you.
- Report it | Raise the case with REGA through its official channels.
- Get advice | Speak to a lawyer if the landlord refuses renewal.
:::

- Dates of any renewal or non-renewal notices

## What it means for landlords, agents and the market

For landlords, the five-year horizon changes the maths. Income from existing tenants is now fixed, so maintenance costs, service fees and vacancy periods matter more. Good tenants who pay on time have become more valuable.

For agents and [property managers](/categories/property-management), accurate Ejar registration is now essential, because the registered rent sets the cap for the next tenant. [Real estate brokers](/categories/brokers-agents) handling new developments can still agree the first rent freely with the first tenant.

Early market data points to easing rents. Industry reports citing CBRE put Riyadh residential rents about 2.1% lower year on year in March 2026. Treat single figures with caution, and check REGA's own rental indicators for the official picture.

### Will the freeze spread to other cities?

REGA can extend the framework to other cities and governorates with the approval of the Council of Economic and Development Affairs. In October 2025 a REGA spokesman said studies were under way. We found no official extension at the time of writing, so tenants in [Jeddah](/cities/jeddah) and other cities should not assume they are covered.

The Riyadh rent increase freeze gives tenants something rare in a hot market: certainty for five years. It works only if your Ejar contract is accurate and you know your rights at renewal. ### Is rent frozen in Riyadh?

### When does the Riyadh rent freeze end?
The freeze runs for five years from 25 September 2025, so on current rules it ends in September 2030. The authorities could change, extend or end the measure by a new decision, so check announcements from the Real Estate General Authority (REGA) as the end date approaches rather than relying on the original timeline.

### Does the rent freeze apply to commercial property?
Yes. The decision covers both residential and commercial leases within Riyadh's urban area, so shops, offices and other commercial units are included. If you rent business premises, your rent should not rise at renewal. Check that your lease is registered correctly on Ejar, because the registered contract is the reference point for the frozen rent.

### Does the rent freeze apply in Jeddah or Dammam?
No, not at the time of writing. The freeze covers Riyadh's urban boundary only. REGA can extend it to other cities with approval from the Council of Economic and Development Affairs, and a REGA spokesman said in October 2025 that studies were under way. No official extension had been announced when this article was published.

### Can my landlord refuse to renew my lease in Riyadh?
Only in limited cases. Under the new rules, a Riyadh landlord can refuse renewal if the tenant has not paid rent, if structural defects make the property unsafe, or if the landlord or a first-degree relative will use the property. Otherwise, the lease renews automatically unless notice is given at least 60 days before it expires.

### What is the fine for raising rent in Riyadh?
Reported rules allow a fine of up to the equivalent of 12 months' rent for the unit concerned. The violator may also have to correct the breach and compensate the affected party. Fines can be appealed within 30 days, and people who report violations can receive up to 20% of the fine collected.

### What rent applies to an empty flat in Riyadh?
If the flat was rented before, the rent for a new tenant is fixed at the value of the most recent registered contract on Ejar. If the unit has never been rented, for example a newly built flat, the landlord and tenant can agree the first rent freely, after which the freeze applies for the rest of the five-year period.

### How do I report a rent increase in Riyadh?
Start by checking your registered Ejar contract and replying to the landlord in writing. Keep paying your current rent on time. If the landlord insists, raise the case with the Real Estate General Authority through its official channels, listed on rega.gov.sa. A lawyer who handles tenancy disputes can also advise you on your options.

### Why is Ejar registration important under the freeze?
The rent registered on Ejar is the reference for the frozen rent and for the cap on the next tenancy. Under the new rules, the other party has 60 days to object to a registered contract's details, after which the data is treated as valid. Always check the rent, dates and unit details as soon as the contract is registered.

### Can rent go down during the freeze?
Yes. The freeze stops increases, not reductions. A landlord and tenant can agree a lower rent at renewal, and market reports in 2026 suggested average residential rents in Riyadh had eased slightly. If similar units nearby are advertised for less, it is reasonable to ask your landlord to review your rent.
