**Riyadh, 2 November 2026.** SaudiYP (saudiyp.com), a new business directory built for Saudi Arabia, is now live. It helps people find, compare and contact businesses near them, and gives shops, professionals and companies a free, trustworthy place to be found online.

## Built around how Saudi Arabia is organised

Most people don't search for "an AC technician in Saudi Arabia". They search for an AC technician in Al Malqa, a dentist in Al Rawdah or a furnished apartment in Al Aqrabiyah. SaudiYP is organised the same way:

- **{{REGIONS}} regions:** Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Eastern Province, Asir, Tabuk, Qassim, Hail, Northern Borders, Jazan, Najran, Al Bahah and Al Jawf.
- **{{CITIES}} cities and towns**, each with its own page.
- **{{LOCALITIES}} districts** in the big cities, from Olaya and Al Nakheel to Al Hamra, Al Aziziyah and Al Shati.

Every business is listed under a region, a city and a district, so a search for a neighbourhood shows the businesses that are actually there. Place names are also shown in Arabic.

## Categories written for Saudi Arabia

The directory has more than {{CATEGORIES}} categories in {{SECTORS}} sectors, written in the words people in the Kingdom use: business setup and PRO services, Qiwa, GOSI and Muqeem services, MEP and HVAC contractors, AC maintenance, Tashleeh used parts and Satha tow trucks, Kabsa and Mandi restaurants, dates and Arabic coffee, oud and bakhoor, abaya tailors, Qasr Afrah wedding halls, Hajj and Umrah operators, Tahfeez Quran centres, Qudurat and Tahsili prep, and many more. Every main category opens into sub-categories, so customers land on exactly the right list.

## What customers get

- **Search by category and area**, then filter by region, city and district.
- **Call, WhatsApp or get directions** straight from a listing. Saudi numbers are shown in a clear +966 format.
- **Request a quote once** and have it sent to up to five matching businesses.
- **Verified badges** that show which businesses have proved who they are.
- **Reviews and ratings** from other customers.

## What businesses get

- **A free listing.** Any business can add its profile at no cost: name, description, categories, location, phone, WhatsApp, website, working hours and logo.
- **Verification.** Businesses can upload documents such as a commercial registration (CR), VAT certificate, Balady municipal licence, Chamber of Commerce membership or professional licence (Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, Saudi Council of Engineers) to earn a verified badge.
- **Enquiries and leads** in the owner dashboard, including quote requests from customers.
- **Claim an existing listing** if your business is already in the directory.
- **Pages that work on any phone**, which is how most customers in Saudi Arabia search.

## Built for search engines

Each region, city and district has its own page, and every listing has a clean, shareable address. The site's map for search engines updates automatically whenever a business, city or article is added, so new listings can be found on Google and Bing quickly.

## How to get listed

Adding a business takes a few minutes: create a free account, choose your main category and sub-category, pick your region, city and district, and add your contact details. Listings are reviewed before they go live, to keep the directory accurate.

Visit **saudiyp.com** to search, or choose **List your business** to add yours for free.

## About SaudiYP

SaudiYP is a Saudi business directory. Its aim is simple: make it easy to find a trustworthy local business anywhere in the Kingdom, and easy for every business to be found. Contact: info@saudiyp.com.
