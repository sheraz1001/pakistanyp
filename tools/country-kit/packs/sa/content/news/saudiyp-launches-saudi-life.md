### End of service calculator

The [end of service calculator](/calculators/end-of-service) works out the end-of-service award under Articles 84 and 85 of the Labour Law. In outline, the law gives half a month's wage for each of the first five years of service and a full month's wage for each year after that. If you resign, Article 85 reduces the award depending on length of service: one third after two to five years, two thirds after five to ten years, and the full award after ten years. The calculator handles the five- and ten-year boundaries for you and shows each step.

### Saudi salary calculator and other pay tools

The [salary breakdown calculator](/calculators/salary-breakdown) splits your pay into its parts. Alongside it you will find:

- an [overtime calculator](/calculators/overtime)
- annual leave and unused leave calculators
- a [GOSI calculator](/calculators/gosi) for social insurance contributions
- notice period, contract duration and salary increase calculators

These tools follow the current rules, including the Labour Law amendments that took effect in February 2025. Our explainer on the [Saudi labour law amendments](/news/saudi-labour-law-amendments-explained) covers what changed.

> [!NOTE]
> The calculators give estimates based on the rules and inputs you enter. Your contract, your employer's records and official decisions take precedence. For a dispute or an unusual case, check with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development or a qualified adviser.

## How we keep the figures accurate

Fees, tariffs and rules change. Saudi Life is built so they can be updated without rebuilding the tools.

:::steps How a figure gets into a calculator
- Research | Each rate or rule is taken from an official source where possible.
- Label | Every source is marked as confirmed, secondary or unconfirmed.
- Date | The tool shows when the source was checked and when the rule took effect.
- Review | Our editors update rates and dates as decisions change.
- Flag | Any rule not re-verified for 180 days is flagged for review.
:::

Each calculator page also shows a last-reviewed date. If a figure looks out of date to you, please tell us through the contact page.

### How is end of service calculated in Saudi Arabia?
Under Article 84 of the Labour Law, the award is half a month's wage for each of the first five years of service and a full month's wage for each year after that, based on the last wage. If you resign, Article 85 gives one third of the award after two to five years, two thirds after five to ten years, and the full award after ten years.
