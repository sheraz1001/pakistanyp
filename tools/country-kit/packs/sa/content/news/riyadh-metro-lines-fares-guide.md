The **Riyadh Metro** is now a normal part of daily life in the capital. All six lines have been running since 5 January 2025, the network covers 176 km with 85 stations, and a standard two-hour ticket costs SR4, valid on both the metro and Riyadh's buses.

In 2026 the Royal Commission for Riyadh City (RCRC), which built and oversees the network, awarded a contract to extend the Red Line to Diriyah, reported passenger numbers above 300 million, and set out a SR5.5 billion plan to add 94 trains. This explainer covers the lines, fares and tickets, the expansion timeline, and what the metro means if you run a shop, clinic, café or office near a station.

:::takeaways
- All six lines (Blue, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green and Purple) are open, with 85 stations over 176 km.
- Standard fares, as announced at launch: SR4 for two hours, SR20 for three days, SR40 for seven days and SR140 for 30 days. First class starts at SR10.
- Tickets cover unlimited metro and bus trips during their validity and can be bought in the Darb app, at machines or at ticket offices.
- RCRC awarded an 8.4 km Red Line extension to Diriyah, with five new stations, in January 2026.
- A SR5.5 billion order for 94 extra trains, reported in October 2026, should lift capacity by about 60%, with new trains from 2028.
:::

## How the network opened: a short timeline

The network opened in three stages over five weeks. The RCRC opened the Blue, Yellow and Purple lines on 1 December 2024, the Red and Green lines on 15 December 2024, and the Orange Line on 5 January 2025, completing the network.

:::steps Rollout and expansion timeline
- 1 December 2024 | Blue, Yellow and Purple lines open to passengers.
- 15 December 2024 | Red and Green lines join the network.
- 5 January 2025 | The Orange Line opens and all six lines are in service.
- January 2026 | RCRC awards the 8.4 km Red Line extension to Diriyah.
- March 2026 | Reported journeys pass 200 million.
- October 2026 | SR5.5 billion plan for 94 new trains is announced.
:::

Press reports in March 2026 put total journeys above 200 million. In October 2026 the RCRC's chief executive was quoted as saying the metro had carried more than 300 million passengers since opening, and that average ridership from January to September 2026 was about 25% higher than in the same period of 2025.

## Riyadh Metro fares and ticket types

The fare structure is simple: you buy time, not distance. The figures below were announced before the launch in late 2024 and widely reported at the time. We found no official fare change since, but check the Darb app before you buy a long pass.

:::bars Standard-class passes (SR, launch fares)
- 2 hours | 4 | SR4
- 3 days | 20 | SR20
- 7 days | 40 | SR40
- 30 days | 140 | SR140
:::

### First class and discounts

First class costs more (a two-hour first-class ticket was announced at SR10) and gives you a quieter carriage. Discounts of 50% have been reported for groups such as eligible students, older passengers and people with disabilities, usually after you validate your status at a ticket office. Rules vary by group, so confirm eligibility with Darb before you rely on it.

### Where to buy tickets

You can buy tickets in the **Darb** app, at ticket vending machines in stations, or at ticket offices. Guides also report contactless bank card payment at the gates and a reloadable Darb card. Every ticket gives unlimited trips on both the metro and the Riyadh Bus network during its validity, which makes the two-hour ticket good value for a bus-plus-metro trip.

> [!TIP]
> If you commute five days a week, the 30-day pass at SR140 usually beats buying two-hour tickets. Compare it with your fuel spend using our [fuel cost calculator](/calculators/fuel-cost) and the full [car ownership cost calculator](/calculators/car-ownership).

## Expansion plans: the Red Line extension and 94 new trains

Two announcements in 2026 shape the next phase.

### Red Line extension to Diriyah

In January 2026 the RCRC announced that it had awarded the contract to extend the Red Line by 8.4 km, from King Saud University towards Diriyah, with five new stations. Reported completion dates differ between outlets, so treat any opening date you see as provisional until the RCRC confirms it.

### SR5.5 billion fleet expansion

In October 2026 a SR5.5 billion plan to add 94 trains was reported, taking the fleet from 183 to 277 trains and lifting passenger capacity by about 60%. The Red Line is due for the largest increase, roughly 142%, from about 154,000 to around 370,000 passengers a day. The new trains are expected to enter service from 2028, ahead of Expo 2030 and the 2034 FIFA World Cup.

> [!WARNING]
> Some websites quote much higher daily or total ridership figures than the RCRC has published. Where numbers conflict, rely on RCRC statements and treat other figures with caution.

## What the Riyadh Metro means for local businesses

A station changes footfall around it. Riyadh is still a car-first city, but walk-in commuters now shape where many people shop, eat and book appointments.

### Retail, cafés and quick-service food

Businesses within walking distance of stations, especially interchanges and stops near offices and universities, gain commuters who arrive on foot and want something quick. Coffee, breakfast, grab-and-go meals, pharmacies and phone accessories tend to benefit first. If you run a [café or coffee shop](/categories/cafes-coffee) or a [fast-food or casual restaurant](/categories/fast-food-casual), opening hours that match the morning and evening peaks matter more than they used to.

### Clinics, training centres and offices

Services that people visit by appointment, such as [polyclinics and medical centres](/categories/polyclinics-medical-centres), [professional training providers](/categories/professional-training) and language schools, can now reach staff and students who do not drive. Saying "a five-minute walk from the station" in your listing is useful, specific information for them.

### Property and office space

Landlords already sell metro access. If you are looking for [office space or business centres](/categories/office-space-business-centres) near a line, ask for the walking time to the nearest station rather than just the straight-line distance, because large junctions can make short distances slow on foot.

## Practical tips for residents and new arrivals

If you have just moved to the city, the metro can cut your transport costs, but plan around a few practical points.

1. **Check your first and last mile.** Many districts are a long walk from a station, so you may still need a bus, taxi or ride-hailing trip for part of the journey.
2. **Use the two-hour ticket for transfers.** It covers buses too, so a bus-to-metro trip costs SR4 within the window.
3. **Budget the savings.** If the metro lets your household drop a second car, the saving can be large. The [household budget calculator](/calculators/household-budget) helps you see the difference.

Two years after it opened, the Riyadh Metro is moving from novelty to habit. Fares are low, the network covers the main corridors, and the 2026 decisions on the Red Line extension and the new trains show where capacity is heading before Expo 2030.

### How much is a metro ticket in Riyadh?
Standard-class tickets, as announced at launch, cost SR4 for two hours, SR20 for three days, SR40 for seven days and SR140 for 30 days. First class costs more, starting at SR10 for two hours. Each ticket gives unlimited trips on the metro and Riyadh Bus network during its validity. Check the Darb app for current prices before buying a long pass, as fares can be reviewed.

### How many metro lines are there in Riyadh?
The network has six lines: Blue (Line 1), Red (Line 2), Orange (Line 3), Yellow (Line 4), Green (Line 5) and Purple (Line 6). Together they cover about 176 km and 85 stations. All six have been running since the Orange Line opened on 5 January 2025, according to the Royal Commission for Riyadh City.

### Does the metro go to Riyadh airport?
Yes. The Yellow Line (Line 4) runs along the King Khalid International Airport Road and serves the airport. It opened on 1 December 2024 with the Blue and Purple lines. Check the Darb app for the current timetable before you travel.

### Where can I buy metro tickets?
You can buy tickets in the Darb app, at ticket vending machines in stations or at station ticket offices. Guides also report contactless bank card payments at the gates and a reloadable Darb card. Discounted fares usually need validating at a ticket office first.

### What time does the metro open and close?
At launch, the network was reported to run from about 6am to midnight daily. Hours can be adjusted by line, season or during major events, so check the Darb app or the Royal Commission for Riyadh City channels for the current timetable rather than relying on older articles.

### Is there a student discount?
Discounts of 50% have been reported for eligible students, as well as for older passengers, people with disabilities and some other groups. University students are usually asked for an ID or iqama and a valid student card. Eligibility rules differ by group, so confirm the current conditions with Darb or at a station ticket office before you buy.

### Is the metro being extended?
Yes. In January 2026 the Royal Commission for Riyadh City awarded a contract to extend the Red Line by 8.4 km towards Diriyah, with five new stations. In October 2026 a SR5.5 billion plan to add 94 trains was reported, raising capacity by about 60%. Opening dates for the extension vary between reports, so wait for official confirmation.

### Can I use one ticket for the metro and buses?
Yes. Metro tickets are integrated with the Riyadh Bus network, so a single ticket gives unlimited trips on both during its validity. The SR4 two-hour ticket is useful for a bus-to-metro journey, while the 7-day and 30-day passes suit regular commuters who use both modes every day.

### How many people use the metro?
In October 2026 the RCRC's chief executive was quoted as saying the metro had carried more than 300 million passengers since it opened, with average ridership from January to September 2026 about 25% higher than a year earlier. Earlier reports in March 2026 put total journeys above 200 million.

### How can my business benefit from being near a metro station?
Businesses near busy stations gain walk-in customers who do not drive, especially at morning and evening peaks. Cafés, quick-service food, pharmacies, clinics and training centres tend to benefit first. Add your nearest station and walking time to your listing, check your map pin and match your hours to commuter peaks so riders can find you easily.
