Trust is the first question customers ask before calling a business they found online. From today, businesses listed on SaudiYP can earn a **verified badge** that answers it.

## What the badge means

A verified badge shows that the SaudiYP team has checked that the business is real and that the person managing the listing is entitled to do so. It appears next to the business name in search results and on the business profile.

## How a business gets verified

1. **Add or claim the listing.** Listing is free.
2. **Confirm the phone number** shown on the listing.
3. **Upload one or more documents** from the owner dashboard. Accepted documents include:
   - Commercial registration (CR) certificate
   - VAT registration certificate (ZATCA)
   - MISA investment licence
   - Balady municipal licence
   - Chamber of Commerce membership
   - SFDA licence
   - Professional licence such as the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, Saudi Council of Engineers or a Ministry of Justice lawyer licence
4. **Our team reviews the documents** and the badge is added once they match the listing.

Documents are used only for verification and are never shown publicly.

## Why it matters for customers

Customers can filter for verified businesses on region pages, and the badge helps them choose with more confidence, especially for services where trust matters most: contractors, real estate offices, clinics, Hajj and Umrah operators and education providers.

## Why it matters for businesses

A verified listing stands out in a list of similar businesses and tells customers that the details are current. It is one of the simplest ways for a small business to look as credible online as it is in person.

To get started, open **List your business** on saudiyp.com, or go to **My verification** in your dashboard if your business is already listed.
