Chandigarh is a union territory and planned city in northern India, bordered by Punjab and Haryana. It serves as the capital of both states while being administered separately by the central government. Together with Mohali in Punjab and Panchkula in Haryana, it forms a continuous urban area often called the Tricity.

The city was planned by Le Corbusier and his team, including Pierre Jeanneret, and its grid of numbered sectors still shapes where businesses are located. Its economy is led by government, services, education, healthcare and retail rather than heavy industry.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Union territory; shared capital of Punjab and Haryana
- **Population (2011 census, union territory):** 1,055,450
- **Area:** 114 km², almost all of it urban
- **Elevation:** about 321 m
- **Telephone area code:** 0172
- **Airport:** Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport, located in Mohali
- **Notable sectors:** government and administration, IT and IT-enabled services, education, healthcare, retail

## Economy and main industries
As the seat of two state governments and the union territory administration, Chandigarh has a large public-sector workforce, which supports demand for housing, retail, education and professional services.

Le Corbusier's plan set aside a separate Industrial Area for light, non-polluting industry, rather than allowing factories among the residential sectors. The Rajiv Gandhi Chandigarh Technology Park, usually called the IT Park, is on the city's eastern side near Sukhna Lake and hosts IT and IT-enabled services companies. It is described as one of northern India's notable IT locations.

## Main business areas and markets
In Le Corbusier's plan the City Centre was the "heart" of the city, and Sector 17 fulfils that role, with its plaza, banks, offices and showrooms. Sector 22 and Sector 35 are other established commercial sectors with shops, restaurants, hotels and offices.

The Industrial Area, divided into Phase I and Phase II, holds light manufacturing units, workshops, warehouses and commercial premises; Phase II addresses are often given by reference to Tribune Chowk. Each residential sector also has its own small market for daily needs, so local services are spread throughout the city.

## Planning and the Tricity
Chandigarh's sector system makes addresses easy to read: most are given as a house or shop number followed by a sector number. Business activity spills over the city's borders into Mohali, which hosts the airport and IT offices, and into Panchkula on the Haryana side. Many firms serving Chandigarh are located in these neighbouring cities, so buyers often compare suppliers across the whole Tricity.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Chandigarh?
The STD code for Chandigarh is 0172.

### Is Chandigarh part of Punjab or Haryana?
Neither. Chandigarh is a union territory administered by the central government, and it serves as the capital of both Punjab and Haryana.

### Where is Chandigarh's airport?
Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport is located in Mohali, just outside the city.

### What is the Industrial Area in Chandigarh?
It is the zone set aside in the city plan for light, non-polluting industry, divided into Phase I and Phase II, and now also holds warehouses and commercial premises.
