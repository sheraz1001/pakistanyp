Telangana is a state in south-central India on the Deccan plateau, formed on 2 June 2014 from the north-western part of Andhra Pradesh. Its capital, Hyderabad, is by far its largest city and the centre of most of the state's commercial activity. Warangal, Nizamabad, Karimnagar and Khammam are the main regional towns.

Commercially, Telangana is known above all for Hyderabad's information technology and life sciences industries. The state promotes itself to investors through a single-window approvals system, and it has added new manufacturing projects outside the capital, including a large textile park at Warangal.

## Economy and main industries
Hyderabad's economy rests on two main pillars. The first is information technology, with offices of Indian and international firms spread across the western side of the city in areas such as HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli and Kondapur.

The second is life sciences. Genome Valley, on the outskirts of Hyderabad, is a cluster of more than 200 companies covering about 2,000 acres and employing around 25,000 people. Four of India's five leading vaccine makers are reported to be based there. The state announced a second phase of Genome Valley in 2024, with new pharmaceutical clusters planned in Vikarabad, Medak and Nalgonda districts.

Outside the capital, the PM MITRA Kakatiya Mega Textile Park at Warangal, covering about 1,327 acres, was inaugurated in May 2026 and is expected to draw more than Rs 6,000 crore of investment. Coal is mined in the state by the Singareni Collieries Company (SCCL).

## Main business areas and markets
Hyderabad's old city, around the Charminar, is the traditional trading area. Laad Bazaar, beside the Charminar, is known for lacquer bangles, pearls and jewellery, while Begum Bazaar is a large wholesale market for textiles, spices, household goods and electrical items. The Charminar area is also the main place to buy Hyderabadi pearls.

In the newer city, Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills hold many offices, showrooms, restaurants and hospitals. HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli and Kondapur form the IT corridor. Secunderabad, Begumpet and Ameerpet are established commercial areas, Kukatpally is a large residential and retail district in the north-west, and Dilsukhnagar and LB Nagar serve the eastern side of the city.

Warangal is the main city of northern Telangana and the centre of the new textile park. Nizamabad, Karimnagar and Khammam are district trading towns for their agricultural hinterlands.

## Business approvals
Telangana's industrial approvals system, TS-iPASS, is a single-window process for new units. It sets time limits for approvals, with reported targets of 15 days for smaller projects and 30 days for larger ones. The Federation of Telangana Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FTCCI), based in Hyderabad, traces its origins to the Hyderabad Chamber of Commerce founded in 1917 and has more than 3,000 direct members.
