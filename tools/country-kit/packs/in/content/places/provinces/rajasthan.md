Rajasthan is India's largest state by area, covering much of the north-west of the country and the Thar Desert along the border with Pakistan. Its capital, Jaipur, is also its largest commercial city, and other major centres include Jodhpur, Udaipur, Kota, Ajmer, Bikaner, Alwar and Bhilwara.

For business, Rajasthan stands out for minerals and stone, textiles, gems and jewellery, and tourism. Its north-eastern districts lie close to Delhi, which has drawn manufacturers to industrial areas along the Delhi–Jaipur road, while its western and southern districts are known for mining and stone processing.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India, the largest by area
- **Capital:** Jaipur
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 68,548,437
- **Area:** 342,239 km²
- **Telephone area code (Jaipur):** 0141
- **Industrial development agency:** RIICO (Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation)
- **Notable industries:** zinc and lead mining, marble and stone, cement, textiles, gems and jewellery, tourism

## Economy and main industries
Mining is central to the state's economy. Rajasthan is India's only producer of zinc and lead ore, and Rampura Agucha in Bhilwara district is described as the world's largest open-cast zinc-lead mine. Hindustan Zinc, headquartered in Udaipur, runs smelters at Debari near Udaipur and Chanderiya in Chittorgarh district.

Stone is the other well-known resource. Makrana in Nagaur district is famous for white marble, and Kishangarh in Ajmer district is a major marble and granite processing centre. Kota and nearby districts have abundant limestone, which supports a large cement industry.

Textiles are concentrated in Bhilwara, known as the "Textile City" for its synthetic suitings and blended yarn spinning; the Ministry of Textiles declared it a Mega Powerloom Cluster in 2009. Pali, Balotra and Sanganer near Jaipur are important dyeing and processing centres. Jaipur is also a centre for gems and jewellery, with a dedicated gems zone at Sitapura.

## Main business areas and markets
Jaipur is the state's main market for wholesale trade, retail and professional services. Commercial and residential localities such as C-Scheme, Malviya Nagar, Vaishali Nagar, Mansarovar, Raja Park, Tonk Road and Jagatpura hold many of the city's offices, showrooms, clinics and service providers. Sitapura Industrial Area, on the city's southern side, includes a gems park and was the site of Rajasthan's first special economic zone, for gems and jewellery.

RIICO manages a large network of industrial areas across the state, stating that it has 472 industrial areas covering about 102,723 acres. Neemrana, in the north-east near the Delhi border, hosts India's first country-specific industrial zone, a Japanese zone of about 1,167 acres developed with JETRO, and a second Japanese zone at Ghiloth covers about 533 acres. RIICO has also developed export promotion industrial parks at Sitapura, Neemrana and Boranada in Jodhpur.

Outside Jaipur, Jodhpur is a centre for trade and handicrafts in western Rajasthan, Udaipur combines tourism with mining-related business, Kota is known for its cement and stone industries, and Bhilwara is the textile town.

## Tourism and hospitality
Tourism is one of Rajasthan's most visible industries, built around its forts, palaces and desert landscapes. Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer draw large numbers of domestic and foreign visitors, supporting hotels, heritage properties, travel agents, drivers, guides and handicraft sellers. Many businesses in these cities combine retail with tourism, selling textiles, jewellery, stone articles and crafts to visitors.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Jaipur?
The STD code for Jaipur is 0141.

### Where does Makrana marble come from?
Makrana is a town in Nagaur district known for white marble. Kishangarh in Ajmer district is a major processing centre for marble and granite.

### What is the Neemrana Japanese zone?
It is an industrial zone of about 1,167 acres at Neemrana, developed by RIICO with JETRO for Japanese companies, and the first country-specific zone of its kind in India.

### Which agency allots industrial land in Rajasthan?
RIICO develops and manages the state's industrial areas and allots plots to manufacturers.
