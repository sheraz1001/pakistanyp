Punjab is a state in north-west India bordering Pakistan, Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana and Rajasthan. Its capital is Chandigarh, a union territory that it shares as a capital with Haryana. The state is best known for agriculture, but its towns also hold some of the country's most established clusters of small and medium manufacturers.

Commercially, Punjab combines a large farm economy with specialised industry. Ludhiana is the main industrial city, Jalandhar is known for sports goods and hand tools, Amritsar is a religious, tourism and trading centre, and Mohali has grown as a technology and services suburb next to Chandigarh.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India
- **Capital:** Chandigarh (shared with Haryana; Chandigarh is a union territory)
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 27,743,338
- **Area:** 50,362 km²
- **Districts:** 23 (Malerkotla, the newest, was formed in 2021)
- **Telephone area code (Ludhiana):** 0161
- **International airports:** Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport, Amritsar, and Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport, Mohali (serving Chandigarh)
- **Notable industries:** agriculture and food processing, hosiery and knitwear, bicycles and parts, sports goods, hand tools, auto components

## Economy and main industries
Farming underpins the economy, and the processing, storage and trading of farm produce support many businesses in market towns across the state. Alongside agriculture, Punjab's industry is dominated by small and medium enterprises grouped into product clusters.

Ludhiana is the largest of these. It is known for yarn, textiles, readymade garments and hosiery, and for bicycles and bicycle parts, with many related engineering and component suppliers. Jalandhar specialises in sports goods, leather products and hand tools, along with pipe fittings, valves and rubber goods. The MSME Ministry has approved a sports technology extension centre for Jalandhar to support the sports goods sector.

Mohali has been developed by the state as its IT and IT-enabled services hub, drawing on its location beside Chandigarh and its international airport.

## Main business areas and markets
Ludhiana is the state's busiest commercial city, with industrial areas and focal points developed by the Punjab Small Industries and Export Corporation (PSIEC) housing knitwear, hosiery and bicycle component units.

Jalandhar's manufacturers of sports goods and hand tools are spread across its industrial areas and supply both domestic retailers and export buyers. Amritsar, home to the Golden Temple, has a large pilgrimage and tourism economy, with hotels, restaurants, transport operators and retailers serving visitors. Patiala is a historic city and administrative centre, Bathinda is a commercial town in the south-west of the state with its own domestic airport, and Mohali hosts IT offices, hospitals and new residential and commercial development.

## Getting around and connectivity
Punjab has two international airports: Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport at Amritsar and Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport at Mohali, which serves the Chandigarh area. Domestic airports at Ludhiana, Pathankot and Bathinda handle smaller volumes. The state is flat and well served by national highways and rail lines that connect its industrial towns with Delhi and the rest of northern India, which matters for manufacturers moving goods to wholesale markets and ports outside the state.

## Finding businesses in Punjab
People looking for businesses in Punjab often want hosiery and garment manufacturers, bicycle and component makers, sports goods suppliers, agricultural equipment dealers, and food and grain traders. In Mohali and around Chandigarh, IT companies, hospitals and education providers are also in demand. IndiaYP lists businesses across Ludhiana, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Patiala, Mohali and Bathinda.

### What is the area code for Ludhiana?
The STD code for Ludhiana is 0161.

### What is Ludhiana known for commercially?
Ludhiana is known for hosiery, knitwear, readymade garments, yarn and textiles, and for bicycles and bicycle parts.

Jalandhar is the main centre for sports goods, and also produces hand tools, leather goods, valves and pipe fittings.
