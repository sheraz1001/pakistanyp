Tamil Nadu occupies the south-eastern tip of the Indian peninsula, with a long coastline on the Bay of Bengal. Its capital, Chennai, is the state's largest city and one of India's main industrial and port cities. Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Tiruppur, Vellore, Erode and Tirunelveli are other important commercial centres.

The state has one of India's most diversified industrial economies. Vehicle and electronics manufacturing is concentrated west of Chennai, textiles and knitwear around Tiruppur and Coimbatore, and engineering industries in the Coimbatore region, with three major ports handling the state's trade.

## Economy and main industries
The belt west of Chennai around Sriperumbudur, Oragadam and Irungattukottai is one of India's main vehicle and electronics manufacturing clusters, linked by NH-48. Vehicle assembly is concentrated at Oragadam and Irungattukottai, with components and electronics around Sriperumbudur. Hyundai opened its first fully integrated car plant outside South Korea at Sriperumbudur in 1998. Much of this land was developed by SIPCOT, the State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu, which was founded in 1971.

Tiruppur is India's leading cotton knitwear centre. Industry estimates citing the Tiruppur Exporters Association put its knitwear exports at around Rs 34,000 crore in 2023–24, produced by thousands of small and medium units. Coimbatore is known as a manufacturing centre for pumps and motors, has a deep textile base and supplies components to the vehicle plants near Chennai.

## Main business areas and markets
In Chennai, T. Nagar is the main retail district, centred on Ranganathan Street, while Parry's Corner and George Town form the city's traditional wholesale area, with narrow lanes selling textiles, toys, stationery, plastic and steel ware. Commercial and residential localities such as Anna Nagar, Adyar, Velachery, Mylapore, Nungambakkam, Egmore, Guindy, Porur, Tambaram and Besant Nagar hold many of the city's shops, clinics and service businesses. The OMR corridor, running south from the city, is where many IT and technology offices are located.

Beyond Chennai, Coimbatore is the main commercial centre of the west, Tiruppur is the knitwear town, Salem and Erode are trading centres in the west, and Madurai is the main city of the south. Vellore, Tiruchirappalli and Tirunelveli serve their surrounding districts as trading, education and healthcare centres.

## Ports and trade
Tamil Nadu has three of India's major ports. Chennai Port and Kamarajar Port at Ennore serve the Chennai region, and V. O. Chidambaranar Port at Thoothukudi serves the south. All three appear among the country's busier ports, and expansion plans have been announced for them. Together they handle the exports and imports of the state's vehicle, textile and engineering industries, which supports a large network of freight forwarders, customs brokers, shipping agents and warehousing firms around Chennai and Thoothukudi.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Chennai?
The STD code for Chennai is 044.

### Where are Tamil Nadu's car plants?
Most are in the Sriperumbudur, Oragadam and Irungattukottai belt west of Chennai, along NH-48.

### Which agency manages industrial parks in Tamil Nadu?
SIPCOT, the State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu, develops and manages the state's industrial parks and complexes.

### What is the oldest chamber of commerce in Tamil Nadu?
The Madras Chamber of Commerce, formed in Chennai in September 1836, describes itself as the oldest chamber of commerce in South India.
