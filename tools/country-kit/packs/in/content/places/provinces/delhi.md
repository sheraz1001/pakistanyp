Delhi, officially the National Capital Territory of Delhi, is a union territory with its own elected legislature and the seat of India's central government. New Delhi, within it, is the national capital. The territory is almost entirely urban and sits at the centre of the National Capital Region, with Noida and Ghaziabad in Uttar Pradesh and Gurugram and Faridabad in Haryana on its borders.

For businesses, Delhi is one of India's largest consumer markets and a major wholesale distribution centre for northern India. Government, professional services, retail and trading dominate, while manufacturing is concentrated in planned industrial areas on the city's edges.

## Key facts
- **Status:** National Capital Territory (union territory with a legislature)
- **Capital:** New Delhi
- **Population (2011 census, territory):** 16,787,941
- **Area:** about 1,483 km²
- **Districts:** 11 revenue districts since September 2012 (nine at the 2011 census)
- **Telephone area code:** 011
- **Airport:** Indira Gandhi International Airport
- **Industrial development agency:** DSIIDC (Delhi State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation)

## Economy and main industries
Delhi's economy is led by services: central and territory government, finance, professional firms, education, healthcare, hospitality and retail. As the capital, it hosts head offices, embassies, national associations and many companies that need to be close to government.

Trade is the other main pillar. Old Delhi's wholesale markets supply retailers across northern India, and the city's large population supports a dense network of shops and service businesses in every neighbourhood. Manufacturing is mainly small and medium-scale and is concentrated in planned industrial areas.

## Main business areas and markets
Old Delhi holds the city's best-known wholesale markets. Chandni Chowk is the historic trading street. Khari Baoli is lined with dealers in spices, dried fruits, nuts and herbs, and the adjoining Gadodia Market, built in the 1920s, is described as Asia's largest wholesale spice market. Sadar Bazaar is a major wholesale market for household goods and general merchandise. Azadpur Mandi, in north Delhi, is the city's main wholesale fruit and vegetable market.

Connaught Place is the central business and retail district of New Delhi. Nehru Place is known for computer and electronics trade and offices, and Karol Bagh, Lajpat Nagar, Rajouri Garden and Greater Kailash are large retail and commercial areas. Saket, Hauz Khas and Vasant Kunj in the south, Dwarka and Janakpuri in the west, Rohini and Pitampura in the north-west and Mayur Vihar in the east are major residential districts with their own shopping centres and local services.

Industry is concentrated in planned estates. Okhla Industrial Area, with its Phase I and Phase II, is the main industrial zone in the south. Bawana, developed on about 1,900 acres in the north-west to relocate industrial units from residential areas, is one of the largest estates built by DSIIDC. Narela, where DSIIDC developed about 612 acres, is another northern industrial area.

## Working in the capital region
Many businesses in Delhi operate across the wider National Capital Region. Offices, warehouses and factories are often split between Delhi and the neighbouring cities of Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and Faridabad, and buyers frequently compare suppliers across state lines. For trade and logistics firms, Delhi's wholesale markets and its position at the centre of the region's road and rail network remain the main draw, while Indira Gandhi International Airport handles both passenger and cargo traffic for the region.

## Finding businesses in Delhi
People searching for businesses in Delhi most often look for wholesale suppliers in Old Delhi, retailers and showrooms in markets such as Karol Bagh and Lajpat Nagar, computer and electronics dealers in Nehru Place, manufacturers in Okhla and Bawana, and professional services such as lawyers, chartered accountants and consultants. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the telephone area code for Delhi?
The STD code for Delhi is 011.

### Where are Delhi's main wholesale markets?
Most are in Old Delhi: Chandni Chowk, Khari Baoli for spices and dried fruit, and Sadar Bazaar for household goods. Azadpur Mandi in north Delhi is the main fruit and vegetable market.

### Which are Delhi's main industrial areas?
Okhla Industrial Area in the south and Bawana and Narela in the north are the largest, along with other DSIIDC-developed estates.

### Who allots industrial plots in Delhi?
DSIIDC, the Delhi State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation, develops industrial estates and handles allotment and relocation schemes.
