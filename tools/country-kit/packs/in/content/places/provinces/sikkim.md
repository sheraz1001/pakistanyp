Sikkim is a small mountain state in the eastern Himalaya, bordered by Nepal, Bhutan, China's Tibet region and West Bengal. Its capital, Gangtok, is the main town and commercial centre, and Namchi is the other principal town. With just over six lakh residents at the last census, it is one of India's least populous states.

Despite its size, Sikkim has a distinctive business profile. A cluster of pharmaceutical plants has grown up in the state, its farm sector is entirely organic, and tourism supports a large share of local enterprise. Trade with Tibet also passes through the Nathu La pass, on a limited scale.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India
- **Capital:** Gangtok
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 610,577
- **Area:** about 7,096 km²
- **Districts:** 6 (Soreng and Pakyong were created in December 2021; the 2011 census counted four)
- **Telephone area code (Gangtok):** 03592
- **Airport:** Pakyong Airport, about 30–35 km from Gangtok, inaugurated in 2018
- **Notable sectors:** pharmaceuticals, organic agriculture, tourism and hospitality

## Economy and main industries
Pharmaceutical manufacturing is the state's most significant modern industry. Many of India's best-known drug makers have plants in Sikkim, including Cipla, Sun Pharma, Mankind, Zydus, Torrent, Alkem, Alembic, Glenmark and Macleods. The cluster grew on the back of tax incentives for new units, and a local supplier quoted in 2019 put the number of pharmaceutical units at about 36. Many of the plants are in East Sikkim, along the road towards Rangpo and around Ranipool.

Agriculture is the other important sector. Sikkim was formally named India's first fully organic state in early 2016, after more than a decade of policy work, and organic produce is part of how the state markets itself. This creates openings for certified organic produce buyers, food processors and agricultural input suppliers who meet organic standards.

## Main business areas and markets
Gangtok is the state's commercial centre, where most retail, hotels, travel agencies, banks and professional services are found. The town's markets and main roads serve both residents and the large number of visitors. Namchi, in the south, is the second town and an administrative centre with its own retail and tourism trade.

Industrial activity is concentrated in East Sikkim, particularly along the corridor from Gangtok down to Rangpo near the West Bengal border, where most of the pharmaceutical units are located. Supplies for the state largely come by road from Siliguri in West Bengal.

## Trade, permits and getting around
Pakyong Airport, Sikkim's first airport, was inaugurated in September 2018 and lies roughly 30–35 km from Gangtok. Road links to Siliguri and Bagdogra airport in West Bengal remain the main supply route.

Nathu La, on the border with Tibet, reopened for trade in 2006 after being closed since 1962. Trade through the pass is limited to an agreed list of mostly low-value goods, such as blankets, agricultural products and wool, and has been disrupted at times by India–China tensions. Businesses planning to bring in foreign staff or visitors should check permit requirements with the state government before travel.

### What is the area code for Gangtok?
The STD code for Gangtok is 03592.

### Does Sikkim have an airport?
Yes. Pakyong Airport, inaugurated in 2018, is about 30–35 km from Gangtok. Many travellers still use Bagdogra airport in West Bengal and continue by road.

Companies with plants in Sikkim include Cipla, Sun Pharma, Mankind, Zydus, Torrent, Alkem, Alembic, Glenmark and Macleods, mostly in East Sikkim.
