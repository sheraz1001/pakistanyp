Puducherry is a union territory made up of four separate regions on the coast of southern India: Puducherry and Karaikal, both enclosed by Tamil Nadu; Mahe, an enclave within Kerala; and Yanam, an enclave within Andhra Pradesh. All four were part of French India until 1954, and the town of Puducherry, the territory's capital, still shows that history in its street plan and architecture.

For business, the territory combines tourism and hospitality, small and medium industry in planned estates, education and healthcare, and a large retail and services sector. Because its regions are far apart, each functions largely as part of the economy of the surrounding state.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Union territory with a legislature
- **Capital:** Puducherry
- **Population (2011 census, union territory):** 1,247,953
- **Area:** about 479 km² (Puducherry 293, Karaikal 160, Yanam 17, Mahe 9)
- **Regions:** Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam
- **Telephone area code (Puducherry):** 0413
- **Airport:** Puducherry Airport at Lawspet, operated by the Airports Authority of India
- **Industrial finance and estates:** PIPDIC (Pondicherry Industrial Promotion Development and Investment Corporation)

## Economy and main industries
Industry in the territory is mostly small and medium-scale and concentrated in planned estates around Puducherry town. PIPDIC, a government-owned finance corporation, has supported industrial development by financing units and developing estates, including the one at Mettupalayam.

Tourism is the other main draw. Visitors come for the French-era quarter, the seafront and the nearby international community of Auroville, which supports hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, cafés, tour operators and craft shops. Education and healthcare institutions also draw students and patients from neighbouring districts of Tamil Nadu.

## Main business areas and markets
Puducherry town is the commercial centre of the territory, with shops, hotels, banks and professional services concentrated in and around the old town and along its main roads. Lawspet, on the edge of the town, is where the airport is located. Industrial units are found in the estates managed by PIPDIC and the territory's industries department.

Karaikal, on the Tamil Nadu coast to the south, is the second-largest region and serves its own agricultural hinterland with trade and services. Mahe, within Kerala, and Yanam, within Andhra Pradesh, are small regions whose shops and services serve mainly local residents.

## History and administration
The French settlements of Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam were transferred to India in practice on 1 November 1954, and the transfer became legally complete on 16 August 1962. The four regions were then grouped into a single union territory. For businesses, the practical result is that registrations, licences and tax matters are handled by the territory's own administration, even in Mahe and Yanam, which are hundreds of kilometres from the capital.

Air links are limited. Scheduled flights at Puducherry Airport have been intermittent; services restarted under the UDAN regional connectivity scheme in 2017, so businesses should check current schedules before relying on them.

## Finding businesses in Puducherry
People looking for businesses in Puducherry often search for hotels, guesthouses, restaurants and tour operators, along with manufacturers and suppliers in the industrial estates. Schools, colleges, hospitals and retailers are also common searches. IndiaYP lists businesses in Puducherry and Karaikal.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Puducherry?
The STD code for Puducherry is 0413.

### What are the four regions of Puducherry?
Puducherry and Karaikal, which are surrounded by Tamil Nadu; Mahe, within Kerala; and Yanam, within Andhra Pradesh.

### Does Puducherry have an airport?
Yes. Puducherry Airport is at Lawspet and is run by the Airports Authority of India, though scheduled flights have been intermittent.

### Which agency supports industry in Puducherry?
PIPDIC, the Pondicherry Industrial Promotion Development and Investment Corporation, finances industrial units and has developed estates such as Mettupalayam.
