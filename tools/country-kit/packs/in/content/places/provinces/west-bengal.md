West Bengal stretches from the Himalayan foothills around Darjeeling in the north to the Bay of Bengal in the south, bordering Bangladesh, Nepal and Bhutan. Its capital, Kolkata, has long been eastern India's main centre for trade, finance and port activity. Howrah, across the Hooghly river, and the industrial towns of Durgapur, Asansol and Kharagpur are other important centres, while Siliguri is the main city of the north.

The state's business profile mixes long-established industries such as jute and tea with port trade, petrochemicals at Haldia and an IT sector built up in Salt Lake and New Town. Its position also makes it a gateway to the North East and to neighbouring countries.

## Economy and main industries
Jute is the industry most closely tied to West Bengal. The state accounts for most of India's raw jute production, with estimates ranging from about half to over 80 per cent depending on year and source, and most of the country's jute mills stand along the Hooghly. Reports suggest the state has around 70 of India's roughly 93 jute mills, though fewer are operating at any one time.

Tea is the other well-known product. Darjeeling tea was India's first product to receive a Geographical Indication, in 2004, which means only tea grown in the defined region can be sold under that name. Tea gardens in the north of the state support estate suppliers, packers, auctions and transport businesses.

In the south, Haldia is an industrial and port town about 125 km from Kolkata. Haldia Petrochemicals operates a 1,025-acre naphtha-based complex there, jointly promoted by the West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation, the Chatterjee group and the Tata group.

## Main business areas and markets
Kolkata's traditional trading districts are in the north and centre of the city. Burrabazar, a dense area of narrow lanes in north Kolkata, is one of the city's oldest trading areas, while Esplanade and Park Street are central commercial and retail areas. Gariahat and Ballygunge in the south, Behala and Tollygunge in the south-west, Alipore and Dum Dum are established residential and retail localities.

Salt Lake's Sector V is the city's main IT cluster, and New Town (Rajarhat) has become its second IT hub. Bengal Silicon Valley, a technology hub with a notified area of 250 acres, was set up in New Town in 2018.

Howrah is closely linked with Kolkata across the river. Durgapur and Asansol form the state's western industrial belt, Kharagpur is an education and railway centre, and Siliguri, described as the gateway to the North East, is a trading and logistics centre that is also developing as an IT location.

## Ports and logistics
The Kolkata port system has two parts. The city docks on the Hooghly are complemented by the Haldia Dock Complex downstream, which began operating in 1968 as an adjunct to Kolkata Port. Freight forwarders, customs brokers, shipping agents and warehousing firms cluster around both. In the north, Siliguri is described as the gateway to the North East, which makes it a base for transport and distribution companies serving the north-eastern states and Sikkim.

### What is the area code for Kolkata?
