Andhra Pradesh runs along the Bay of Bengal on India's south-eastern coast. In its present form it dates from 2 June 2014, when Telangana was separated from it, and Hyderabad went with Telangana. Since then the state has built up its own administrative and commercial centres. Amaravati, in Guntur district, is the designated capital, and Visakhapatnam is the largest city.

Commercially, the state has two main strengths. The long coastline carries a chain of deep-water and private ports that serve bulk cargo, and the fertile Krishna and Godavari deltas support a large farm, food processing and aquaculture economy. Most new manufacturing investment goes into industrial corridors near the ports and along the highways to Chennai and Bengaluru.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India, reorganised on 2 June 2014
- **Capital:** Amaravati (Guntur district)
- **Population (2011 census, present state area):** 49,386,799
- **Districts:** 28, after Polavaram and Markapuram districts were notified in December 2025
- **Largest city:** Visakhapatnam
- **Main ports:** Visakhapatnam, Gangavaram, Krishnapatnam and Kakinada
- **Airports with scheduled flights:** Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada (Gannavaram), Tirupati (Renigunta), Rajamahendravaram, Kadapa and Kurnool
- **Notable sectors:** port logistics, pharmaceuticals, automobiles, rice, chilli, aquaculture and food processing

## Economy and main industries
Agriculture remains the largest employer. Rice is the staple crop in the canal-irrigated deltas, and Guntur district is known nationally for chilli. Aquaculture changed the coastal districts from the 1990s onwards. Farmers in Godavari, Krishna, Guntur and Nellore converted large areas of paddy land to shrimp ponds, and shrimp is now the state's main aquaculture export. The sector depends on export prices and feed costs, so processors, hatcheries, feed suppliers and cold-chain operators are a sizeable part of the business community.

Industry is grouped around ports and corridors. Visakhapatnam has heavy industry, pharmaceuticals and shipbuilding-related activity, and its port is one of India's larger cargo ports. Adani Ports runs Krishnapatnam in Nellore district and Gangavaram near Visakhapatnam. Kakinada handles bulk cargo and supports the Godavari region's industries.

The automobile sector grew after Kia built its Indian car plant at Erramanchi, near Penukonda, in what is now Sri Sathya Sai district. Trial production began there in January 2019, and supplier firms have since set up around the plant. Electronics, auto components and food processing are other priority sectors for the state's industrial development agency, APIIC, which has its headquarters at Mangalagiri.

## Main business areas and markets
Visakhapatnam is the main industrial and port city. Its pharmaceutical SEZ at Parawada and APIIC's multi-product SEZ at Atchutapuram and Rambilli lie to the south-west of the city, with port-related logistics and engineering firms nearby. Vijayawada, on the Krishna river, is the state's main wholesale and transport centre for trade between the coast and the interior. Nearby Guntur is a major agricultural trading town, especially for chilli.

In the south, Sri City is a private industrial park of about 7,000 acres near the Tamil Nadu border. It contains an SEZ, and its tenants include automotive, electronics, pharmaceutical and food-processing companies. Tirupati is a temple town that is also growing as an industrial and services centre. Nellore is the main city near Krishnapatnam port, which is an industrial node on the Chennai–Bengaluru Industrial Corridor. Kakinada and Rajamahendravaram serve the Godavari districts, and Kurnool is the main commercial town in western Rayalaseema.

## Industrial corridors and ports
Andhra Pradesh is crossed by three national industrial corridors: the Visakhapatnam–Chennai Industrial Corridor, part of the East Coast Economic Corridor; the Chennai–Bengaluru Industrial Corridor; and the Hyderabad–Bengaluru Industrial Corridor. Under the state's port-led development plan, industrial clusters are being built around existing and new ports, including Machilipatnam and Ramayapatnam. The state maritime board treats the area within 100 km of each port as a port-proximal zone for industrial planning. Sites in or near these corridors usually offer better land and infrastructure for manufacturers, warehousing firms and exporters than locations further inland.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the capital of Andhra Pradesh?
Amaravati, in Guntur district, is the designated capital. The revised 2026 state map marks it as the sole capital.

### Which are the main industrial areas in Andhra Pradesh?
The main areas are the Visakhapatnam belt, including Parawada and Atchutapuram, Sri City near the Tamil Nadu border, the Krishnapatnam area near Nellore, and the Penukonda automobile cluster in Sri Sathya Sai district.

### Which ports serve Andhra Pradesh businesses?
Visakhapatnam is the largest port. Gangavaram, Krishnapatnam and Kakinada handle much of the remaining cargo, and new ports are being developed at Machilipatnam and Ramayapatnam.

### Is there a state chamber of commerce?
Yes. The Andhra Pradesh Chambers of Commerce and Industry Federation, known as AP Chambers, represents trade and industry and is based in Vijayawada.
