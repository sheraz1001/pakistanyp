Uttar Pradesh is India's most populous state, stretching across the Ganga plain in the north of the country between Delhi and Bihar. Its capital is Lucknow, and its other large cities include Kanpur, Ghaziabad, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Meerut, Bareilly, Aligarh and Gorakhpur. Noida and Greater Noida, on the border with Delhi, form part of the National Capital Region.

With close to 20 crore residents at the last census, the state is a very large consumer market as well as a production base. Its economy combines agriculture, a long tradition of district-level crafts and manufacturing, and newer corporate and technology growth in the western districts near Delhi.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India, the most populous
- **Capital:** Lucknow
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 199,812,341
- **Area:** 240,928 km²
- **Districts:** 75 (71 at the 2011 census)
- **Telephone area code (Lucknow):** 0522
- **Airports:** Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow; Noida International Airport at Jewar, which began commercial flights on 15 June 2026
- **Notable sectors:** agriculture and food processing, handicrafts and textiles, metalware, carpets, leather products, IT and services in Noida

## Economy and main industries
Much of the state's industry is organised around district specialities. The state government's One District One Product (ODOP) programme, launched in January 2018, aims to identify a distinctive product in each of the 75 districts and build a product-specific industrial hub around it. After 12 additions in May 2025, the list covers 74 products. Support under the programme includes common facility centres, marketing assistance, finance and skill training.

Well-known examples include brassware from Moradabad, carpets from Bhadohi, Banarasi silk from Varanasi and perfumes from Kannauj. Agra's ODOP listing covers leather products and items made from stone and marble.

In the west of the state, Noida and Greater Noida host corporate offices, IT companies and manufacturers, attracted by their location next to Delhi. Ghaziabad and Meerut are also part of this belt.

## Main business areas and markets
In Lucknow, Hazratganj is the traditional central shopping and business district. Gomti Nagar is a newer district with offices, malls and hospitals, and Aliganj, Indira Nagar, Alambagh and Mahanagar are established residential and commercial localities.

In Noida, Sector 18 is the main retail and commercial centre, Sector 62 holds many IT and corporate offices, and Sector 50, Sector 137 and Noida Extension are large residential areas with growing local business. Greater Noida, further south-east, has industrial areas and large institutional campuses.

Kanpur is a long-established industrial and trading city. Agra and Varanasi have large tourism economies alongside their craft industries, Prayagraj is an administrative and education centre, and Bareilly, Aligarh and Gorakhpur serve as commercial centres for their regions.

## Getting around and connectivity
Noida International Airport at Jewar, in Gautam Buddh Nagar district, opened for commercial flights on 15 June 2026, with the first flight arriving from Lucknow. It gives the National Capital Region a second major airport, close to Greater Noida. Lucknow's Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport serves the centre of the state. For businesses in the west of Uttar Pradesh, Delhi's airport and markets are also close at hand.

## Finding businesses in Uttar Pradesh
People looking for businesses in Uttar Pradesh often search for craft and textile manufacturers in the ODOP districts, wholesale traders, IT companies and offices in Noida, builders and property dealers, and hospitals and schools in Lucknow and other large cities. Exporters often look for suppliers of metalware, carpets and silk.
