Arunachal Pradesh is India's north-easternmost state. It is mountainous and sparsely populated, and borders Bhutan, Myanmar and China. It became a Union Territory in 1972 and a full state in 1987. Most of its population lives in small towns and villages in river valleys, and much of the commercial activity is in the Itanagar capital complex, which includes Itanagar and Naharlagun.

For businesses, Arunachal is a market shaped by public spending and long supply lines. Road building, power projects and government offices create demand for contractors, transporters, building materials and professional services. At the same time, much of the state's consumer goods come in by road from neighbouring Assam. Kiwi, oranges and other horticultural produce, together with tourism to places such as Tawang, give the state a growing private sector.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India since 1987, previously a Union Territory from 1972
- **Capital:** Itanagar
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 1,383,727
- **Districts:** 27, plus the Itanagar Capital Complex, after Keyi Panyor and Bichom were notified in February 2024
- **Telephone code (Itanagar and Naharlagun):** 0360
- **Airport:** Donyi Polo Airport at Hollongi, about 15 km from Itanagar, opened in November 2022
- **Railhead:** Naharlagun, with a direct train to Delhi
- **Entry rule:** Indian citizens who are not permanent residents of the state need an Inner Line Permit

## Economy and main industries
The economy is still mainly agricultural. Shifting (jhum) cultivation and terrace farming continue in many districts. Horticulture is the fastest-growing part of the farm sector. Arunachal is reported to be India's largest kiwi producer and the second-largest producer of mandarin oranges. In March 2025 the state cabinet approved a dedicated horticulture policy, which includes a kiwi mission, and this is likely to bring more investment in cold storage, packing and food processing.

Hydropower is the main long-term industrial theme. The state's rivers have a large hydroelectric potential, and dozens of projects have been planned. Installed generating capacity was about 784 MW in March 2025. Project construction supports civil contractors, equipment hire, quarrying, transport and accommodation businesses near the sites.

Manufacturing is limited. Commentators in the state press point to unreliable power and poor facilities at government industrial estates as reasons why few factories have set up. Most private enterprise is in trade, construction, transport, hospitality and services for the large public sector workforce.

## Main business areas and markets
The Itanagar Capital Complex is the main commercial centre. Itanagar has the state secretariat, government offices, banks and most professional firms, and its markets serve the whole capital region. Naharlagun, about 10 km away, is the railhead and a residential and trading town with its own shops and offices. Together they hold the largest concentration of wholesalers, distributors, vehicle dealers and building-material suppliers in the state. Weekly markets in the capital region and Papum Pare district have been limited to Saturdays for security reasons, so check local arrangements before planning to sell at them.

Pasighat in East Siang district is an important town in the eastern part of the state, serving the Siang valley's farming communities. Tawang in the far west is mainly a tourism and pilgrimage town, with hotels, guesthouses, taxi operators and handicraft shops. Most other district headquarters have a main bazaar where general traders, pharmacies and hardware shops serve the surrounding villages. The state has no SEZ or large industrial corridor, so manufacturers and wholesalers who supply Arunachal often work from warehouses in neighbouring Assam.

## Getting around and access
Access has improved in recent years. Donyi Polo Airport at Hollongi handles scheduled flights to cities including Delhi, Kolkata and Guwahati, and a new terminal opened in September 2025. Naharlagun station links the capital to the national rail network, and a rail line towards Pasighat is under construction. On the roads, the Trans-Arunachal Highway and the east-west corridor along the foothills are being upgraded. The Arunachal Frontier Highway, a largely new route close to the international border, is planned. Travel times between districts can still be long, especially during the monsoon, so suppliers often keep local stock in each district town.

Visitors from elsewhere in India, including business travellers, need an Inner Line Permit. It is issued online through the state's ILP system and by district authorities. In 2025 and 2026 the police carried out checks on permit violations, so people visiting or hiring workers should keep their documents up to date.

## Finding businesses in Arunachal Pradesh
People commonly look for civil contractors, transporters, building-material and hardware suppliers, travel agents and hotels, and suppliers of farm inputs and fruit packaging. Government departments and project companies also search for consultants, IT vendors and equipment hire firms. IndiaYP lists businesses in Itanagar, Naharlagun, Pasighat and Tawang by category.

## Frequently asked questions
### Do I need a permit to visit Arunachal Pradesh for business?
Yes. Indian citizens who are not permanent residents of the state need an Inner Line Permit, which can be applied for online or obtained from the state's designated officials.

### What is the telephone code for Itanagar?
The STD code for Itanagar and Naharlagun is 0360.

### Which airport serves Itanagar?
Donyi Polo Airport at Hollongi, about 15 km from the city, opened in November 2022 and has scheduled domestic flights.

### What are the main business opportunities in the state?
The main areas are construction and services linked to roads and hydropower projects, horticulture such as kiwi and oranges, tourism, and supplies for government offices.
