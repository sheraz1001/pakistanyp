Ladakh is a union territory in the far north of India, created on 31 October 2019 when the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 divided the former state of Jammu and Kashmir into two union territories. It is a high-altitude, sparsely populated region, and its two main towns, Leh and Kargil, are the headquarters of its two census districts.

For businesses, Ladakh is defined by distance, altitude and season. A large share of commercial activity revolves around visitors, government and defence establishments, and the supply of goods that must be brought in from the rest of the country by road or air. Many firms operate intensively during the warmer months and scale down in winter.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Union territory (since 31 October 2019)
- **Main towns:** Leh and Kargil
- **Population (2011 census, area now forming the union territory):** 274,289
- **Population by district (2011 census):** Kargil 140,802; Leh 133,487
- **Telephone area code (Leh):** 01982
- **Airport:** Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport, Leh (IATA code IXL)
- **Main sectors:** tourism and hospitality, transport, government services, construction, local agriculture and crafts

## Economy and main industries
Tourism is the most visible part of Ladakh's private economy. Visitors come for its monasteries, high passes, lakes and trekking routes, which supports hotels, guesthouses, homestays, tour operators, taxi unions, motorbike rental firms, outdoor equipment shops and restaurants, particularly in and around Leh.

Because most manufactured goods, fuel and building materials come from outside the territory, transport and wholesale supply are important businesses in their own right. Construction contractors and suppliers are active on roads, buildings and public works. Local agriculture and traditional crafts continue in the villages, and their products are sold to residents and visitors in the main towns.

## Main business areas and markets
Leh is the commercial centre of the territory and holds most of its hotels, travel agencies, banks, shops and service providers. Its central market area serves both residents and tourists, and many tour operators and equipment rental businesses are based there. Kargil is the main town of the western district, a trading and service centre on the road between Srinagar and Leh, with its own markets, hotels and transport operators.

Outside the two towns, businesses are small and scattered, mainly guesthouses, homestays, eateries and shops serving travellers along the main routes and in popular valleys.

## Getting there, permits and seasons
Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport at Leh is among the highest commercial airports in the world and has direct flights from cities including Delhi, Chandigarh, Jammu, Srinagar and Mumbai. It is the most reliable year-round link, since road access depends on mountain passes and weather. Permits are not needed for travel on the Srinagar–Leh and Manali–Leh highways.

Permit rules have changed in recent years. In January 2022 the administration announced that Indian citizens no longer needed an Inner Line Permit anywhere in Ladakh, although some travel guides still describe permit procedures for areas such as Nubra, Pangong and Tso Moriri. Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit to visit protected areas. Tour operators and travellers should check the current rules with the Leh district administration before planning trips.

## Finding businesses in Ladakh
People looking for businesses in Ladakh most often search for hotels, homestays, tour operators, taxi and bike rental services, and outdoor equipment shops in Leh. Suppliers, transporters and building contractors are also in demand, especially before and during the main season. IndiaYP lists businesses in Leh and Kargil.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Leh?
The STD code for Leh is 01982.

### Which airport serves Ladakh?
Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport at Leh, with direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Chandigarh, Jammu and Srinagar.

### Do Indian visitors need a permit for Ladakh?
In 2022 the administration removed the Inner Line Permit requirement for Indian citizens, but guidance for some protected areas varies, so check with the Leh district administration before travel. Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for protected areas.

### What are Ladakh's main towns?
Leh and Kargil, the headquarters of the territory's two census districts.
