The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are a union territory made up of island groups in the Bay of Bengal, well to the east of the Indian mainland. The territory has three districts: South Andaman, North and Middle Andaman, and Nicobar. Its capital, formerly Port Blair, was renamed Sri Vijaya Puram in September 2024 and remains the islands' main town, port and airport.

For businesses, the islands are defined by distance from the mainland and by tourism. Most goods arrive by sea or air, and a large share of private enterprise serves visitors who come for beaches, diving and the islands' history. Government and public services are the largest employers.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Union territory
- **Capital:** Sri Vijaya Puram (formerly Port Blair)
- **Population (2011 census, union territory):** 380,581
- **Area:** about 8,249 km²
- **Districts:** 3 – South Andaman (headquarters Sri Vijaya Puram), North and Middle Andaman (Mayabunder) and Nicobar (Car Nicobar)
- **Telephone area code (Sri Vijaya Puram):** 03192
- **Airport:** Veer Savarkar International Airport, Sri Vijaya Puram
- **Main sectors:** tourism and hospitality, shipping and transport, government services, construction, fisheries and local agriculture

## Economy and main industries
Tourism is the main private-sector industry. Visitors arrive mostly through Sri Vijaya Puram and travel on to islands such as Swaraj Dweep (formerly Havelock Island) and Shaheed Dweep (formerly Neil Island), both renamed in 2018 along with Ross Island, now Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island. This supports hotels and resorts, dive centres, boat and ferry operators, tour agencies, taxis and restaurants.

Because almost everything consumed on the islands is brought in from the mainland, shipping agents, freight handlers, wholesalers and building material suppliers form an important part of the economy. Government departments and public works generate steady demand for contractors and service firms.

## Main business areas and markets
Sri Vijaya Puram, in South Andaman district, is the islands' commercial centre, with most of the territory's shops, wholesale trade, banks, hotels and professional services. South Andaman had about 238,000 residents at the 2011 census, well over half the territory's total.

North and Middle Andaman, with its headquarters at Mayabunder, had about 106,000 residents and a mainly rural economy, with small markets serving local villages. The Nicobar district, headquartered at Car Nicobar, had about 37,000 residents.

Tourist businesses outside the capital are concentrated on Swaraj Dweep and Shaheed Dweep, which are linked to Sri Vijaya Puram by ferry.

## Getting there and access
Veer Savarkar International Airport is the main link with the mainland, with flights from several Indian cities. In early 2026 it was reported that an international service to Kuala Lumpur was planned, and the airport has been designated as an immigration checkpoint so that foreign tourists can fly in directly. Passenger and cargo ships also connect the islands with mainland ports. Anyone planning business travel beyond the main tourist islands should check current entry rules, including any permit requirements, with the Andaman and Nicobar administration.

## Finding businesses in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands
People looking for businesses in the islands most often search for hotels and resorts, dive and water-sports operators, ferry and tour bookings, taxis, and suppliers of food and building materials in Sri Vijaya Puram. Shipping and freight services are also common searches. IndiaYP lists businesses in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair).

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the new name of Port Blair?
Port Blair was renamed Sri Vijaya Puram, as announced by the central government on 13 September 2024.

### What is the area code for Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair)?
The STD code is 03192.

### Which airport serves the Andaman Islands?
Veer Savarkar International Airport in Sri Vijaya Puram.

### What is Havelock Island called now?
Havelock Island was renamed Swaraj Dweep in 2018, and Neil Island became Shaheed Dweep.
