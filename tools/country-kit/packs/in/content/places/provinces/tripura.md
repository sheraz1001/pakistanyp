Tripura is a small state in north-east India, surrounded on three sides by Bangladesh and connected to the rest of India through Assam and Mizoram. Its capital, Agartala, lies close to the Bangladesh border and is the state's main city and commercial centre. Udaipur, in Gomati district, is another important town.

For businesses, Tripura is notable for its natural rubber plantations, its natural gas and gas-based power generation, and its growing transport links with Bangladesh. These links, including a cross-border railway, matter because the state's road and rail routes to the rest of India are long.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India
- **Capital:** Agartala
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 3,673,917
- **Area:** about 10,486 km²
- **Districts:** 8 (four new districts were formed in 2012; the 2011 census counted four)
- **Telephone area code (Agartala):** 0381
- **Airport:** Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport, Agartala
- **Notable sectors:** natural rubber, rubber-based products, gas-based power, agriculture, bamboo

## Economy and main industries
Rubber is the defining commercial crop. Tripura is the second-largest producer of natural rubber in India after Kerala, with plantations covering roughly 97,000 hectares. Recent estimates of annual output vary between about one lakh and 1.16 lakh tonnes. The state has tried to move from selling raw rubber to processing it locally, though officials say logistics costs and a lack of other locally available materials have held back rubber-based manufacturing.

Natural gas supports power generation. The 726 MW gas-based thermal power project at Palatana in Gomati district is the best known; when its first unit began operating, its output alone exceeded the state's demand of about 280 MW. Bamboo is another important local resource, and NIT Agartala has set up research centres on rubber and bamboo.

## Main business areas and markets
Agartala holds most of the state's shops, wholesale trade, hotels, banks and professional services.

The Bodhjungnagar Industrial Growth Centre, about 12 km from Agartala and 22 km from the airport, is the state's main industrial area. It contains the Tripura Industrial Rubber Park, a 58.81-acre park developed by the Tripura Industrial Development Corporation with the Rubber Board, which was the second such rubber park in India after one in Kerala. A tyre unit operates in the Bodhjungnagar area, and the state government has announced a second rubber park.

Udaipur, south of Agartala, is the main town of Gomati district, where the Palatana power project is located.

## Trade and connectivity with Bangladesh
The Agartala–Akhaura railway, a cross-border line of about 12 km linking Agartala with Akhaura in Bangladesh, was inaugurated in November 2023. It was built with Indian grant assistance of Rs 392.52 crore, and the Tripura government described it as historic for the North East. The land customs station at Agartala was also taken up for upgrading to an integrated check post under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport in Agartala is the state's air gateway, and the state government has sought international flights to destinations such as Dhaka and Chittagong. For importers, exporters and transport firms, these cross-border links are a central part of Tripura's commercial outlook.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Agartala?
The STD code for Agartala is 0381.

### Where is Tripura's main industrial area?
The Bodhjungnagar Industrial Growth Centre, about 12 km from Agartala, which includes the Tripura Industrial Rubber Park.

### Is there a rail link from Agartala to Bangladesh?
Yes. The Agartala–Akhaura cross-border railway, about 12 km long, was inaugurated in November 2023.

Tripura is India's second-largest natural rubber producer after Kerala, with output estimated at about one lakh tonnes or more a year.
