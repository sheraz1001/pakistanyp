Uttarakhand is a Himalayan state in northern India, bordering Tibet (China) and Nepal, with Himachal Pradesh to the west and Uttar Pradesh to the south. It is divided into two regions, Garhwal and Kumaon. Dehradun is its winter capital and largest city, and Gairsain in Chamoli district has been declared the summer capital.

The state has two quite different economies. In the southern plains and foothills, industrial estates host pharmaceutical, automotive and other manufacturers. In the mountains, tourism and pilgrimage support a large number of hotels, transport operators and small businesses.

## Key facts
- **Status:** State of India
- **Capital:** Dehradun (winter capital); Gairsain (summer capital)
- **Population (2011 census, state):** 10,086,292
- **Area:** 53,483 km²
- **Districts:** 13, in the Garhwal and Kumaon divisions

- **Notable sectors:** pharmaceuticals, automobiles and components, tourism and pilgrimage

## Economy and main industries
Most of the state's manufacturing sits in integrated industrial estates developed by SIIDCUL, the State Infrastructure and Industrial Development Corporation of Uttarakhand (formerly SIDCUL). The corporation has developed seven integrated industrial estates, at Pantnagar, Haridwar, Dehradun, Selaqui, Kotdwar, Sitarganj and Kashipur, and says it has secured investment from around 2,000 companies across them.

Pantnagar, in Udham Singh Nagar district, is the largest SIIDCUL estate and one of the country's notable automobile manufacturing hubs. The Haridwar estate is located near BHEL, close to Shivalik Nagar. Selaqui, on the edge of Dehradun, includes a dedicated Pharma City, and Sitarganj was developed as a later phase of SIIDCUL's estates.

Tourism and pilgrimage are the other main economic pillar, supporting hotels, dharamshalas, travel agencies, taxi operators, adventure tour companies and food businesses across the hill districts.

## Main business areas and markets
Dehradun is the state's administrative, education and services centre, with government offices, schools, hospitals, retail and professional firms. Selaqui, on the edge of the city, is its main industrial area.

Haridwar combines a major pilgrimage economy on the Ganga with the SIIDCUL estate and industrial units. Rishikesh, a short distance upstream, is known for pilgrimage, yoga and adventure tourism. Roorkee, in Haridwar district, is an education and trading town. In the Kumaon region, Haldwani is the main commercial gateway to the hills, and Nainital is a hill resort with a tourism-led economy.

Udham Singh Nagar district, with Pantnagar, Kashipur and Sitarganj, forms the state's other industrial cluster in the southern plains.

## Tourism and pilgrimage
Pilgrimage and leisure travel shape business in much of Uttarakhand. Haridwar and Rishikesh draw large numbers of pilgrims and visitors throughout the year, and hill towns such as Nainital and the trekking and pilgrimage routes of Garhwal and Kumaon support seasonal businesses. For many firms in the hills, demand is concentrated in the travel season, and hotels, homestays, transport operators and guides plan around it. In the plains, by contrast, the industrial estates provide steadier demand for contractors, logistics companies and industrial suppliers. Businesses that serve both markets, such as transport operators and food suppliers, often shift their focus between the hills and the plains over the course of the year.
