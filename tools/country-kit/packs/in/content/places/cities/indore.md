Indore is the largest commercial centre of Madhya Pradesh, although the state capital is Bhopal. Provisional 2011 census figures put the population of the Indore Municipal Corporation at 1,964,086, and the area under the corporation and its outgrowths at 1,994,397.

The city's trading role is centred on the old Rajwada area, where thousands of wholesale and retail shops supply Indore and the towns of western Madhya Pradesh. Manufacturing is concentrated outside the city, at Pithampur, the Sanwer industrial belt and Dewas. Indore has also been ranked India's cleanest city in the national Swachh Survekshan survey eight times in a row.

## Key facts
- **State:** Madhya Pradesh (the state capital is Bhopal)
- **Population (2011 census, provisional, municipal corporation):** 1,964,086
- **Population (2011 census, municipal corporation and outgrowths):** 1,994,397
- **Telephone STD code:** 0731
- **Industrial areas:** Pithampur, Sanwer and Dewas
- **Special economic zone:** Indore SEZ, about 3,000 acres
- **Cleanliness ranking:** top city in Swachh Survekshan for the eighth consecutive time in the 2024–25 results

## Economy and main industries
Trade and distribution have long been the core of Indore's economy, with wholesalers supplying a large part of western Madhya Pradesh. Manufacturing has grown around the city. Pithampur is an industrial town within the Indore Metropolitan Region, and together with the Sanwer industrial belt, about 1,000 acres, and Dewas, it forms the area's main manufacturing base. The Indore SEZ covers about 3,000 acres.

Indore's consistent performance in the Swachh Survekshan cleanliness survey has also made it a reference point for urban services. In the 2024–25 survey, announced in July 2025, it retained the top position for the eighth time in a row, ahead of Surat and Navi Mumbai.

## Main business areas and markets
Rajwada, the historic centre of the city, is Indore's main market area. A smart city planning document counted about 1,500 wholesale and 9,000 retail shops in the Rajwada core.

The Ujjain–Indore road begins at Rajwada and runs north towards Ujjain, with a major junction at the Sanwer Road Industrial Area. That industrial area, along with Pithampur and Dewas, is where most factories, fabricators and industrial suppliers are based.

## Industry around Indore
Because Indore's main industrial areas lie outside the city, many firms keep sales offices in Indore and production units in Pithampur, Sanwer or Dewas. Buyers should check where a plant is actually located before arranging visits or transport. Industrial suppliers, transporters and warehousing firms serve the corridors between the city and these towns.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the STD code for Indore?
Indore landlines use the STD code 0731. From abroad, dial +91 731 before the number.

### Is Indore the capital of Madhya Pradesh?
No. Bhopal is the state capital, but Indore is the state's main commercial centre.

### Where is the main industrial area near Indore?
Pithampur is the largest industrial area, with further industry at the Sanwer Road industrial area and Dewas.

### How many times has Indore been ranked India's cleanest city?
Indore topped the Swachh Survekshan survey for the eighth consecutive time in the 2024–25 results.
