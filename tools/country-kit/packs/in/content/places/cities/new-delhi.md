Delhi is the National Capital Territory of India and contains New Delhi, the seat of the central government. At the 2011 census the territory had 16,787,941 residents, of whom more than 11 million lived in the city proper, making it one of the largest urban areas in the country.

Commercially, Delhi is a city of wholesale markets and small manufacturers. The lanes of the old walled city around Chandni Chowk supply traders across northern India, specialised markets such as Nehru Place serve whole trades, and a ring of government-developed industrial estates holds thousands of small and medium factories.

## Key facts
- **Status:** National Capital Territory; New Delhi is the national capital
- **Population (2011 census, NCT of Delhi):** 16,787,941 (8,987,326 male, 7,800,615 female)
- **Telephone STD code:** 011
- **Industrial estates:** 29 estates maintained by DSIIDC, the municipal corporation, PWD and other agencies
- **Oldest planned estate:** Okhla Industrial Estate, developed from 1956–57
- **Main produce market:** Azadpur Mandi, for fruit and vegetables

## Economy and main industries
Government, services and trade dominate Delhi's economy, but manufacturing remains significant. Most of it is small and medium-scale and spread across planned industrial areas. The Delhi State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation (DSIIDC) developed and largely administers estates such as Okhla, and runs relocation schemes for units moved out of residential areas.

Wholesale distribution is the other pillar. Markets in the old city and at Azadpur act as distribution points for goods sent on to retailers in Delhi and neighbouring states.

## Main business areas and markets
Chandni Chowk is one of the oldest and largest wholesale markets in Delhi. At its western end, Khari Baoli is the city's spice market and is often described as the largest wholesale spice market in Asia. Sadar Bazaar is another large wholesale market, supplying retailers across India with household goods, clothing, accessories and toys.

Nehru Place in south Delhi is the city's main market for computers, laptops and IT accessories. In Karol Bagh, Gaffar Market is known for mobile phones, and Tank Road for jeans and denim. Connaught Place in New Delhi holds offices, banks, showrooms and restaurants. Azadpur Mandi in north Delhi is the main wholesale market for fruit and vegetables, and Okhla also has a wholesale produce mandi.

Industry is spread across several zones. Okhla Industrial Area in the south is divided into phases; in Phase III alone, 199 of 230 plots are in possession and 177 factories are running. Patparganj in east Delhi is another government industrial area. To the west are Naraina and Mayapuri, and to the north Wazirpur, Mangolpuri, Narela and Bawana.

## Wholesale trade in the old city
The old city markets specialise strongly by street and product. Buyers looking for spices, dry fruit and grocery staples head to Khari Baoli, while general merchandise and festive goods are concentrated in Sadar Bazaar. Many traders sell only in bulk, so it is worth calling ahead to confirm minimum order quantities, trading days and whether a shop also sells retail.

## Finding businesses in Delhi
Common searches in Delhi include wholesale suppliers, spice and dry fruit traders, computer and mobile dealers, garment manufacturers, printers, and factories in the industrial areas. Lawyers, chartered accountants and consultants dealing with central government departments are also in demand. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the STD code for Delhi?
Delhi landlines use the STD code 011. From abroad, dial +91 11 before the number.

### Which market in Delhi is best for computers?
Nehru Place is the main market for computers and IT accessories. For mobile phones, Gaffar Market in Karol Bagh is better known.

### Where is the wholesale spice market in Delhi?
Khari Baoli, at the western end of Chandni Chowk, is Delhi's wholesale spice market.

### Who manages Delhi's industrial areas?
Many estates, including Okhla, were developed and are largely administered by DSIIDC. In total Delhi has 29 industrial estates maintained by DSIIDC, the municipal corporation, PWD and other agencies.
