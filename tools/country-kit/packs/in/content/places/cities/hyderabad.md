Hyderabad is the capital of Telangana. At the 2011 census the area under the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) had 6,731,790 residents, rising to 6,993,262 when outgrowths are included. Secunderabad Cantonment, a separate civic body, had a further 217,910.

The city's business life runs on two tracks. The old city around Charminar is a dense network of bazaars for bangles, textiles and groceries. To the west, HITEC City and the surrounding districts hold software and business services firms, while the city's pharmaceutical and life sciences industry is spread across clusters on the outskirts.

## Key facts
- **State:** Telangana (state capital)
- **Population (2011 census, GHMC municipal corporation):** 6,731,790
- **Population (2011 census, GHMC including outgrowths):** 6,993,262
- **Telephone STD code:** 040
- **Main IT district:** HITEC City
- **Life sciences cluster:** Genome Valley, in three phases
- **Main industries:** software and IT services, pharmaceuticals and bulk drugs, biotechnology, trade

## Economy and main industries
Hyderabad is often called India's pharma capital, and more than 100 pharmaceutical companies operate in and around the city. Genome Valley, a life sciences cluster developed in three phases, covers suburbs such as Shamirpet, Medchal, Patancheru and Jeedimetla, and houses research laboratories, biotech firms and drug manufacturers.

Information technology is the other main pillar. HITEC City is one of the city's most important technology and business districts, home to software developers, IT service companies and back-office operations. Around these two sectors sit large numbers of suppliers: chemical traders, laboratory equipment dealers, packaging firms, facility management companies and recruitment agencies.

## Main business areas and markets
Begum Bazaar is one of the oldest and largest markets in Hyderabad, with both wholesale and retail shops dealing in groceries, dry fruit, household goods and utensils. Top Khana Road in Begum Bazaar has its own cluster of glass and plastic bangle dealers.

Laad Bazaar, also called Choodi Bazaar, runs close to Charminar and is the city's best-known bangle market. Its name refers to lac, the resin used to make the bangles, many of which are still made locally.

HITEC City in the west is the main office district for technology firms. Industrial and pharmaceutical units are concentrated further out, in areas such as Patancheru, Jeedimetla, Medchal and Shamirpet within the Genome Valley belt.

## Pharmaceuticals and life sciences
Because so much of the industry is clustered in Hyderabad, the city has a deep supply chain for drug makers. Buyers can find contract manufacturers, bulk drug producers, laboratory testing services, solvent and chemical suppliers and specialised logistics in the same region. When searching for a manufacturer, it helps to check which industrial area the plant is in, as head offices in the city are often separate from production sites on the outskirts.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the STD code for Hyderabad?
Hyderabad landlines use the STD code 040. From abroad, dial +91 40 before the number.

### Where is Laad Bazaar?
Laad Bazaar is next to Charminar in the old city and is known mainly for lac bangles.

### Which is the main wholesale market in Hyderabad?
Begum Bazaar is one of the oldest and largest markets in the city, with many wholesale dealers in groceries, dry fruit and household goods.

### Where are Hyderabad's pharma companies located?
Many are in the Genome Valley belt, which covers areas such as Shamirpet, Medchal, Patancheru and Jeedimetla.
