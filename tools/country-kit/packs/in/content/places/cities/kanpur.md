Kanpur is one of the largest cities in Uttar Pradesh and the administrative centre of Kanpur Nagar district. At the 2011 census the Kanpur Municipal Corporation area had 2,765,348 residents in 522,242 households, the urban agglomeration about 2.92 million, and the district as a whole 4,581,268.

The city grew as an industrial centre, and leather remains its best-known industry. The tannery cluster at Jajmau processes hides for leather goods makers in Kanpur and elsewhere. The industry has contracted sharply in the past decade, which has changed how and where buyers find suppliers.

## Economy and main industries
Leather tanning and leather products have been Kanpur's signature industry for generations. Jajmau, an industrial suburb, holds the city's largest tannery cluster. A 2024 report counted about 250 operational tanneries at Jajmau, with around 50 more active in Unnao, about 35 km away.

The sector has been under pressure. Pollution controls on discharges into the Ganga, a 2015 ruling by the National Green Tribunal, periodic closures during major religious gatherings on the river, and changes in the supply of raw hides have all reduced the number of working units. Estimates of how many tanneries operated at the cluster's peak vary widely, from about 400 to 700. The raw hide market at Pech Bagh, once busy, has also declined.

Beyond leather, Kanpur's economy depends on trade, small-scale manufacturing, education and services for the surrounding districts.

## Main business areas and markets
Jajmau is the address for tanneries, finishing units and suppliers of tanning chemicals and machinery. Leather goods makers, including footwear and accessories manufacturers, are spread through the city and often source hides and finished leather from Jajmau or Unnao.

Pech Bagh has historically been the raw hide market, though trade there has fallen off. Wholesale traders, transporters and service businesses are spread across the city and serve the surrounding districts as well as Kanpur itself.

## Leather and environmental compliance
For anyone sourcing leather in Kanpur, compliance now matters as much as price. Tanneries must meet effluent treatment requirements and may be required to suspend operations at certain times of year. Buyers, particularly exporters, often ask suppliers for evidence of pollution control clearances and treatment arrangements. Consultants, testing labs and effluent treatment service providers have grown up around this demand.

### Where are Kanpur's tanneries?
Most are at Jajmau, an industrial suburb of the city. A smaller cluster operates at Unnao, about 35 km away.
