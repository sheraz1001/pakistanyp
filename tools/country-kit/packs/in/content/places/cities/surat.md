Surat is a major industrial city in south Gujarat. At the 2011 census the Surat Municipal Corporation area had 4,467,797 residents, of whom 2,543,623 were men and 1,924,174 women.

The city is best known for diamonds. Around 3,000 cutting and polishing units, from small workshops to large factories, process rough stones for export, and in 2023 the Surat Diamond Bourse opened at Khajod to bring trading closer to manufacturing. Surat has also been ranked among India's cleanest cities in the national Swachh Survekshan survey.

## Key facts
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Population (2011 census, Surat Municipal Corporation):** 4,467,797
- **Population (2011 census, urban agglomeration):** 4,591,246
- **Telephone STD code:** 0261
- **Main industry:** diamond cutting and polishing
- **Diamond trading centre:** Surat Diamond Bourse, Khajod, part of the DREAM City project
- **Chamber of commerce:** Southern Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry, active for more than 75 years

## Economy and main industries
Diamond cutting and polishing dominates Surat's economy. During the Covid period, estimates put the workforce at more than 5 lakh people in about 3,000 small and large units, and other estimates are higher. The industry has been through a difficult period since 2024: reports describe a prolonged slowdown in demand, job losses and wage pressure, and many units have shifted part of their work to polishing laboratory-grown diamonds, which pay workers less per stone.

Around the diamond units sits a wide network of support businesses: tool and machinery suppliers, assortment and grading offices, brokers, couriers, security firms, certification labs and jewellery makers.

## Main business areas and markets
The established diamond markets are in Varachha and Mahidharpura, where brokers and traders have long done business face to face.

The Surat Diamond Bourse at Khajod, on the outskirts of the city, is the new centre for diamond trading. Construction was completed on 26 July 2023, and the building was inaugurated by the Prime Minister on 17 December 2023. Designed by the architecture firm Morphogenesis, it is described as the world's largest office building, larger than the Pentagon. Uptake by traders has been gradual: some find the location on the outskirts less convenient than the established markets, and the bourse has held sessions in Varachha and Mahidharpura to encourage firms to move.

## The diamond trade
For buyers, Surat's diamond industry works as a chain. Rough stones are bought, assorted and planned, then cut and polished in workshops, and finally sorted and certified before sale to jewellers and exporters. Firms specialise in one or more of these steps, and the same building may hold a polishing unit on one floor and a trading office on another. Labour contractors, machine suppliers and certification labs are therefore as much part of the market as the traders themselves.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the STD code for Surat?
Surat landlines use the STD code 0261. From abroad, dial +91 261 before the number.

### Where is the Surat Diamond Bourse?
The Surat Diamond Bourse is at Khajod, on the outskirts of the city, within the DREAM City project.

### Where are Surat's traditional diamond markets?
Varachha and Mahidharpura are the established diamond markets, with many traders and brokers.

### Which chamber of commerce represents Surat businesses?
The Southern Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry is the main business chamber for Surat and south Gujarat.
