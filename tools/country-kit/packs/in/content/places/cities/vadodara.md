Vadodara, also known as Baroda, is a major city in Gujarat and the headquarters of Vadodara district. At the 2011 census the Vadodara Municipal Corporation area had 1,670,806 residents, and the district as a whole 4,165,626.

The city has a strong industrial base. Indian Oil's Gujarat Refinery at Koyali has operated since 1965, the Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation (GIDC) runs large estates at Makarpura and Nandesari, and Savli has become another major industrial area. Chemicals, petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals and engineering are the main sectors.

## Key facts
- **State:** Gujarat
- **District:** Vadodara (district headquarters)
- **Population (2011 census, Vadodara Municipal Corporation):** 1,670,806
- **Population (2011 census, Vadodara district):** 4,165,626
- **Telephone STD code:** 0265
- **Refinery:** Gujarat Refinery, Koyali, operated by Indian Oil Corporation since 1965, capacity 13.7 million tonnes a year
- **Industrial estates:** Makarpura (about 716 hectares), Nandesari (about 1,000 hectares) and Savli
- **Airport:** Vadodara Airport at Harni (IATA code BDQ)

## Economy and main industries
The Gujarat Refinery at Koyali, run by Indian Oil Corporation, is the anchor of Vadodara's petroleum and petrochemical industry. Opened in 1965, it is Indian Oil's third-largest refinery after Paradip and Panipat, with a capacity of 13.7 million tonnes a year and plans to expand to 18 million tonnes.

Chemicals and pharmaceuticals are concentrated at Nandesari, about 15 km from the city, where the GIDC estate covers around 1,000 hectares and has a common effluent treatment plant. Makarpura, established in the mid-1960s and spread over about 716 hectares, is one of the oldest and largest estates in the city and a hub for engineering and small and medium enterprises. Savli is another large industrial area in the district.

## Main business areas and markets
For industrial buyers, the main addresses are the GIDC estates: Makarpura for engineering, fabrication and MSME suppliers, and Nandesari for chemicals, pharmaceuticals and plastics. Savli is a further large industrial area, and Koyali is home to the refinery.

Within the city, offices, hotels and showrooms are found in central areas such as Alkapuri and Sayajigunj. Vadodara Airport at Harni, about 6 to 8 km north-east of the city centre, is usually 20 to 30 minutes by road from these areas, and city buses run by VITCOS serve the Harni route.

## Chemicals and engineering
Many of Vadodara's businesses work as suppliers or contractors to the refinery and chemical plants: process equipment fabricators, instrumentation firms, pipe and valve dealers, laboratories, effluent treatment specialists and industrial safety suppliers. Buyers looking for engineering job work often start at Makarpura, while those looking for chemical intermediates or contract manufacturing are more likely to look at Nandesari.

The Gujarat Refinery is at Koyali near Vadodara and is operated by Indian Oil Corporation.
