Noida is a planned city in Gautam Buddh Nagar district of Uttar Pradesh, next to Delhi. Its name comes from the New Okhla Industrial Development Authority, the body that plans and administers it. Provisional 2011 census figures put the population of Noida at 642,381, and the district as a whole had 1,648,115.

Noida is laid out in numbered sectors, with residential, commercial and industrial land in separate areas. It has grown into a significant location for offices, media companies and manufacturing in the National Capital Region, and a new international airport at Jewar, in the same district, is intended to extend that role.

## Key facts
- **State:** Uttar Pradesh
- **District:** Gautam Buddh Nagar
- **Population (2011 census, provisional, Noida):** 642,381
- **Population (2011 census, Gautam Buddh Nagar district):** 1,648,115
- **Telephone STD code:** 0120, shared with Ghaziabad
- **Administration:** New Okhla Industrial Development Authority (NOIDA)
- **New airport:** Noida International Airport, Jewar, first phase designed for about 12 million passengers a year

## Economy and main industries
As its name suggests, Noida was planned around industrial development, and manufacturing, offices and services now sit side by side. The NOIDA Authority allots land by sector, so businesses of a similar kind tend to cluster. Media and entertainment companies are concentrated in Film City in Sector 16A, while other sectors are given over to factories, IT and corporate offices.

The Noida International Airport at Jewar, in Gautam Buddh Nagar district, has a first phase with one runway and one passenger terminal designed to handle about 12 million passengers a year. In May 2026 reports said commercial flights were scheduled to begin on 15 June 2026, after several earlier target dates had passed.

## Main business areas and markets
Sector 18 is Noida's main commercial sector, with shops, restaurants, clothing and electronics retailers clustered around the Noida Sector 18 metro station.

Atta Market, opposite the Sector 18 metro station on the Sector 27 side, is a market known for low-priced clothing and is often used for bulk purchases of apparel and accessories.

Film City, in Sector 16A and a short walk from Sector 18, is home to many media and production companies.

Factories and corporate offices are spread across Noida's industrial and institutional sectors. Searching by sector number is the quickest way to find businesses in a particular part of the city.

## Living and working in the sectors
Because Noida's land use is planned, sector numbers matter. A business listed in a residential sector is likely to be a shop or local service, while one in an industrial or institutional sector may be a factory, warehouse or office. Many businesses also operate across the boundary with Delhi and Ghaziabad, and the shared 0120 code with Ghaziabad means telephone numbers alone do not indicate which side of the boundary a firm is on.
