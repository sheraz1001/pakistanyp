Bengaluru is the capital of Karnataka. At the 2011 census the city, administered by the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP), had 8,443,675 residents, and the BBMP area of about 712 square kilometres made it one of the largest municipal corporations in the country.

The city is best known for software and electronics, but its business base is broader than that. Peenya in the north-west is one of the largest clusters of small and medium engineering units in Asia, and the old pete area around Chickpet and KR Market still handles wholesale trade in textiles, flowers and produce for much of the state.

## Economy and main industries
Information technology and electronics drive much of Bengaluru's economy. Electronic City in the south of the city was set up by Keonics, the Karnataka State Electronics Development Corporation, in 1978 on 332 acres in Konappana Agrahara and Doddathogur villages. It has since grown to over 800 acres in three phases, and Keonics says it houses more than 100 IT and electronics companies employing over 60,000 people.

Manufacturing is concentrated in Peenya. Karnataka's investment promotion agency describes it as the largest of more than 16 industrial areas in Bengaluru Urban district, and it is made up mainly of micro, small and medium enterprises. The state has since notified Peenya as its 18th Special Investment Region under the Karnataka Special Investment Region Act, 2022, which is expected to bring infrastructure upgrades.

## Main business areas and markets
The old commercial core lies around KR Market (Krishna Rajendra Market), one of the city's oldest and largest wholesale markets. It is best known for its flower trade and for fruit, vegetables and spices, and supplies florists, caterers and retailers across the city.

Chickpet, next to KR Market, is the main textile area, with wholesalers selling sarees, dress materials and accessories to retailers from across Karnataka and neighbouring states. SP Road, short for Spare Parts Road, is the place for electronic components, cables, tools and inexpensive electronic goods.

Peenya's phases in the north-west hold the bulk of the city's small factories and job-work shops, while Electronic City in the south is dominated by software, electronics and business services companies.

## Small manufacturers and suppliers
Bengaluru's large companies depend on thousands of small suppliers. Peenya's micro, small and medium units do machining, sheet-metal work, moulding, surface treatment and assembly for larger manufacturers. For buyers, this means it is often possible to source both a component and a local job-work partner within the same industrial area. Firms in SP Road and Chickpet, by contrast, are mainly traders rather than makers.
