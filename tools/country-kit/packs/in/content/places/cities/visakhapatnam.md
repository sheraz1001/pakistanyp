Visakhapatnam, often shortened to Vizag, is a coastal city in Andhra Pradesh and one of the main industrial and port centres on India's east coast. At the 2011 census the Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC) area had 1,728,128 residents.

Heavy industry and shipping define the city's economy. The Visakhapatnam Steel Plant, an oil refinery operated by HPCL and two large ports, Visakhapatnam and Gangavaram, anchor a wide network of suppliers, contractors and logistics firms. The Andhra Pradesh Special Economic Zone at Atchutapuram adds a further manufacturing base.

## Key facts
- **State:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Population (2011 census, GVMC):** 1,728,128
- **Telephone STD code:** 0891
- **Steel plant:** Visakhapatnam Steel Plant, run by Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL), installed capacity 7.3 million tonnes of liquid steel a year
- **Refinery:** Hindustan Petroleum Corporation (HPCL) refinery
- **Ports:** Visakhapatnam Port and Gangavaram Port
- **Special economic zone:** Andhra Pradesh SEZ, Atchutapuram and Rambilli mandals, about 3,213 acres

## Economy and main industries
The Visakhapatnam Steel Plant is the city's largest industrial complex. Run by Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL), a Navratna central public sector enterprise wholly owned by the Government of India, it was the country's first shore-based integrated steel plant and has an installed capacity of 7.3 million tonnes of liquid steel a year. The plant is in the southern part of the city. In 2021 the central cabinet gave in-principle approval for full disinvestment of RINL, a decision that has met local protests.

Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) operates one of its main refineries in Visakhapatnam. Visakhapatnam Port is one of the largest cargo-handling ports in India, and Gangavaram Port adds further capacity.

The Andhra Pradesh Special Economic Zone, a multi-product SEZ in the Atchutapuram and Rambilli mandals of the district, covers about 3,213 acres and has access to both ports. Pharmaceutical and other manufacturing units operate in the Gajuwaka and Atchutapuram areas.

## Main business areas and markets
For industrial buyers, the main areas are the steel plant zone in the south of the city, Gajuwaka, and the APSEZ at Atchutapuram, where manufacturers, fabricators, chemical suppliers and contractors are concentrated.

The port areas hold shipping agents, freight forwarders, customs brokers, stevedores and warehouse operators serving both Visakhapatnam and Gangavaram ports.

## Ports and heavy industry
Many of Visakhapatnam's smaller businesses work as vendors to the large public sector units. Fabrication, maintenance, scaffolding, refractory supply, transport and manpower contracting are common lines of work. Vendors typically need registration with the relevant company, and tender notices from RINL, HPCL and the port authorities are an important source of work for local firms.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the STD code for Visakhapatnam?
Visakhapatnam landlines use the STD code 0891. From abroad, dial +91 891 before the number.

### Who runs the Visakhapatnam Steel Plant?
The plant is run by Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL), a central public sector enterprise wholly owned by the Government of India.

### Where is the Andhra Pradesh SEZ?
The APSEZ is in the Atchutapuram and Rambilli mandals of Visakhapatnam district and covers about 3,213 acres.

### Which ports serve Visakhapatnam?
Visakhapatnam Port and Gangavaram Port both serve the city.
