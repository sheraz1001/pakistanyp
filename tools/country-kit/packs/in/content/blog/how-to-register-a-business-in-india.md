If you are wondering how to register a business in India, the short answer is this: choose a legal structure first, then collect the registrations that structure and your activity need. A sole proprietor can often start with a PAN, a bank account, a free Udyam certificate and a state Shops and Establishments registration. A private limited company is incorporated online with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) through a single form, SPICe+.

GST registration is a separate step. It becomes compulsory once turnover crosses the threshold for your state and activity, or straight away if you sell goods across state lines. Food, trade and other local licences depend on what you sell and where.

:::takeaways
- Pick your structure first: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or private limited company. It decides which forms you file.
- Udyam registration for micro, small and medium enterprises is free, online and based on Aadhaar and PAN.
- Companies are incorporated through MCA's SPICe+ form, which also issues PAN, TAN, EPFO and ESIC registrations.
- GST is compulsory above the turnover threshold, and for inter-state sellers of goods regardless of turnover.
- Shops and Establishments, trade licence and FSSAI rules are set by your state and city, so check them separately.
:::

## Step 1: Choose the right business structure

Your structure decides your liability, your tax position and your yearly paperwork. Changing it later usually means re-registering, so spend an hour on this now.

| Structure | Who it suits | Registered with | Personal liability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sole proprietorship | One owner, shop or freelancer | No central registration; use PAN, Udyam, GST and local licences | Unlimited |
| Partnership firm | Two or more owners, family businesses | Optional, state Registrar of Firms | Unlimited, shared |
| LLP | Professional firms, small partnerships | MCA, Form FiLLiP | Limited to agreed contribution |
| Private limited company | Businesses planning to raise funds or scale | MCA, SPICe+ | Limited to share capital |
| One Person Company | Solo founder wanting limited liability | MCA, SPICe+ | Limited |

A proprietorship has no separate legal identity: the business is you. It is fast and cheap, but your personal savings are exposed to business debts. Compliance rises as you move down the table, with companies facing board meetings, statutory audits and annual returns.

> [!TIP]
> If you expect outside investment, a private limited company is usually the cleaner choice from day one. Investors rarely fund a proprietorship or partnership.

## Step 2: Get your PAN, bank account and Udyam certificate

As a proprietor you use your personal PAN (Permanent Account Number, the income-tax ID). Partnerships, LLPs and companies get their own. Banks want proof that the business exists before opening a current account, typically a Shops and Establishments, GST or Udyam certificate.

### Udyam registration for MSMEs

Udyam is the government registration for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs). It is free and paperless on the [official Udyam portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in), using the owner's Aadhaar and the business PAN. Investment and turnover figures are pulled from income-tax and GST records, so you upload nothing.

From 1 April 2025 the classification limits were raised. An enterprise must stay within both the investment and the turnover limit of its category.

:::bars MSME turnover ceilings from April 2025 (₹ crore)
- Micro | 10 | Up to ₹10 crore
- Small | 100 | Up to ₹100 crore
- Medium | 500 | Up to ₹500 crore
:::

Investment limits are ₹2.5 crore (micro), ₹25 crore (small) and ₹125 crore (medium). A Udyam certificate helps with priority-sector loans, some tenders and protection against delayed payments from larger buyers.

> [!WARNING]
> Udyam registration is free on the government portal. Look-alike websites charging a "registration fee" are private agents. You never need to pay for a Udyam number.

## Step 3: Incorporate a private limited company through SPICe+

SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) is filed on the [MCA portal](https://www.mca.gov.in) and bundles several registrations into one application.

:::steps How SPICe+ incorporation works
- Get digital signatures | Each proposed director needs a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
- Reserve the name | SPICe+ Part A checks and reserves your company name.
- File Part B | Submit director, office and capital details; Director Identification Numbers are allotted here.
- Attach MoA and AoA | File the memorandum and articles electronically.
- Add linked forms | AGILE-PRO-S covers EPFO, ESIC, bank account, optional GSTIN and, in some states, professional tax.
- Receive the certificate | The Certificate of Incorporation arrives with the company's PAN and TAN.
:::

Companies with authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh pay no MCA incorporation fee, though state stamp duty still applies. With complete documents, approval commonly takes one to two weeks.

:::checklist
- PAN and Aadhaar of every director and shareholder
- Photograph and recent address proof (bank statement or utility bill) for each director
- Registered office proof: rent agreement or ownership document, plus a recent utility bill
- Owner's no-objection certificate if the office is rented
- Digital Signature Certificates for directors
- Two or more proposed names and a short note on the business activity
:::

## Step 4: Register an LLP or partnership firm

**LLP:** reserve a name through RUN-LLP, then file Form FiLLiP with MCA, giving partner and office details. You need at least two designated partners. Fees depend on total contribution, so check the current MCA fee table, and file the LLP agreement within the deadline after incorporation.

**Partnership firm:** it is formed by a deed on stamp paper. Registration with the state Registrar of Firms is optional under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, but an unregistered firm cannot easily sue third parties to enforce contracts. Banks and many clients also prefer registered firms.

## Step 5: Apply for GST registration when you need it

In most states, suppliers of goods must register once annual turnover crosses ₹40 lakh, and service providers at ₹20 lakh. Several special category states have lower limits, and inter-state suppliers of goods must register whatever their turnover. E-commerce selling can also trigger registration.

Since 1 November 2025, applicants whose monthly output tax on supplies to registered businesses stays within ₹2.5 lakh can opt for a simplified route, with approval due within three working days of Aadhaar authentication. Our coming guide to GST registration for small businesses covers the application in detail.

> [!NOTE]
> GST rates were rationalised from 22 September 2025 into mainly 5% and 18%, with 40% for a short list of sin and luxury goods. Check your product's rate on the CBIC website before printing price lists.

## Step 6: Collect state and local licences

This is where requirements differ most between cities:

- **Shops and Establishments registration** from the state labour department for shops, offices and commercial premises.
- **Trade licence** from the municipal corporation for many trades, especially food, workshops and hazardous goods.
- **FSSAI registration or licence**, compulsory for any food business.
- **Professional tax**, levied by some states.
- **Specialist approvals** such as pollution consent, drug licence or fire NOC, depending on your activity.

A local professional can handle most of this. Compare [business registration consultants](/categories/business-registration), [licence and compliance specialists](/categories/licences-compliance) or [chartered accountants and tax advisers](/categories/chartered-accountants-tax) near you.

## Costs and timelines to register a business in India

If you are working out how to register a business in India on a tight budget, note how many government fees are nil. Professional fees vary widely, so treat quotes as indicative and compare at least two.

:::stats Registration at a glance
- ₹0 | Udyam registration fee
- ₹15 lakh | authorised capital limit for nil MCA fee
- 3 working days | GST approval after Aadhaar authentication
- 30 days | maximum when GST site verification is needed
:::

Founders in [Bengaluru](/cities/bengaluru), [Mumbai](/cities/mumbai) and [New Delhi](/cities/new-delhi) will find most steps fully online, with occasional physical verification of premises. The usual delays are mismatched names between PAN, Aadhaar and bank records, weak office proof, and names too close to an existing brand.

## Final word on how to register a business in India

When you plan how to register a business in India, sequence matters most. Decide the structure, get PAN and Udyam, incorporate if needed, then add GST and local licences as your activity requires. Keep scans of every certificate in one folder, because banks, clients and marketplaces will ask for them.

:::faq
### Can I start a business in India without registration?
A sole proprietor can start trading without any central registration, using a personal PAN. You will still need some registrations quickly: banks want business proof for a current account, many states require Shops and Establishments registration for premises, and GST becomes compulsory above the turnover threshold or for inter-state sales of goods. Food businesses need FSSAI registration before starting.

### Is Udyam registration free?
Yes. Udyam registration on the official government portal is free and needs only the owner's Aadhaar and the business PAN. The portal draws turnover and investment data from income-tax and GST records, so nothing is uploaded. Websites that charge a fee are private intermediaries. Updating your Udyam details later is also free.

### What is the cheapest way to register a business in India?
A sole proprietorship is cheapest because there is no incorporation fee. You use your PAN, take a free Udyam certificate and pay your state's Shops and Establishments fee where it applies. The trade-off is unlimited personal liability. A private limited company with authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh pays no MCA fee, though stamp duty and professional charges apply.

### How long does company registration take in India?
With complete documents and an approved name, incorporation through SPICe+ commonly takes one to two weeks, including time to obtain Digital Signature Certificates. Delays usually come from rejected names, unclear office proof or mismatched director details. The Certificate of Incorporation is issued with the company's PAN and TAN.

### What is SPICe+?
SPICe+ is the Ministry of Corporate Affairs' integrated web form for incorporating a company. Part A reserves the name; Part B handles incorporation, Director Identification Numbers, PAN, TAN, EPFO and ESIC. Linked forms let you apply for GSTIN, a bank account and, in some states, professional tax. It replaced the older SPICe form in 2020.

### Is GST registration mandatory for a new business?
Not always. In most states it is compulsory once turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services, with lower limits in several special category states. It is compulsory from the start for inter-state supplies of goods, and e-commerce selling can also trigger it. Some businesses register voluntarily to claim input tax credit.

### Which is better, LLP or private limited company?
An LLP has lighter compliance and suits professional firms and small partnerships that want limited liability. A private limited company carries more paperwork, including board meetings and audits, but is preferred by investors and supports employee stock options. If you plan to raise equity, choose a company; otherwise an LLP is often enough.

### Can I register a business from my home address?
Usually, yes. MCA accepts a residential address as a registered office with ownership or rent proof, a recent utility bill and the owner's no-objection certificate. GST also accepts home addresses, though officers may verify the premises. Check your housing society rules and your state's Shops and Establishments law before relying on a home address.

### What documents are needed to register a proprietorship?
There is no single proprietorship certificate. Authorities and banks accept a mix of proofs: PAN and Aadhaar, a Udyam certificate, a GST certificate if registered, a Shops and Establishments certificate and utility bills for the premises. RBI's KYC rules generally require banks to collect two business proofs for a proprietorship account.

### Do I need a CA to register my business?
No law requires a chartered accountant for Udyam, GST or proprietorship registration, and many owners file these themselves. Company and LLP forms often need certification by a practising CA, company secretary or cost accountant, so most founders hire one for incorporation and keep them for annual filings.
:::
