When you search "AC repair near me" or "plumber near me", the fastest way to find someone reliable is to shortlist two or three nearby providers with recent reviews, confirm their visit charge and warranty on the phone, and ask for a written estimate before any work begins. A good technician will diagnose first, explain the fault in plain words and quote before touching anything.

Most problems with home services in India come from three things: no estimate, no bill and no warranty. Get all three and you avoid most disputes. This guide covers what to check for plumbers, electricians and AC technicians, indicative charges, the red flags to watch for, and your rights if a job goes wrong.

:::takeaways
- Shortlist two or three local providers with recent, detailed reviews.
- Agree the visit or inspection charge and ask for an estimate before work starts.
- Insist on a bill that lists parts, labour and any warranty period.
- For wiring and AC gas work, ask about training, licences and the parts used.
- If a job goes wrong, complain to the provider first, then to the National Consumer Helpline (1915).
:::

## How to shortlist a reliable home service provider

Searching is easy; choosing well takes five minutes of checking.

1. **Look close to home.** A provider in your locality can come quickly and return if the problem recurs. Use your locality or city page to compare options.
2. **Read recent reviews, not just ratings.** Look for reviews that mention the specific job (a leaking concealed pipe, a split AC not cooling) and how the provider handled problems.
3. **Prefer verified businesses.** A business that has submitted registration documents is easier to trace if something goes wrong.
4. **Call two or three.** Compare how clearly they explain charges and how soon they can come.

Our guide to [finding trusted local services](/blogs/find-trusted-local-services-indiayp) explains how to use reviews and verification badges when comparing businesses.

> [!TIP]
> Describe the problem precisely on the phone: the brand and age of your AC, whether a leak is from a visible pipe or inside a wall, and when the fault started. A good provider will ask follow-up questions, which is itself a sign of competence.

## Finding a plumber near me: what to check

Plumbing jobs range from a two-minute washer change to tracing a concealed leak behind tiles. Before you book:

- **Ask about visit charges.** Many plumbers charge a fixed visit fee that may be adjusted against the job.
- **Ask how they will find hidden leaks.** Breaking tiles at random is expensive. Experienced plumbers check pressure, look for damp patches and narrow down the spot first.
- **Know what parts are used.** Ask for branded taps, valves and pipes, and keep the packaging of replaced parts.
- **Check the water supply.** A good plumber will advise on tank cleaning or water pressure issues while there.

For larger jobs such as bathroom renovation, ask for a written quote with a material list, and pay in stages. You can compare [plumbing and water sanitation services](/categories/water-sanitation) or general [home repair services](/categories/repairs) in your area.

## Hiring an electrician safely

Electrical work carries real safety risk, so be stricter here than anywhere else.

- **Ask about licensing for wiring jobs.** States license electrical contractors and wiremen under electricity safety rules. For new wiring, rewiring or meter-side work, prefer a licensed contractor.
- **Ask about safety devices.** An RCCB (residual current circuit breaker) cuts power if current leaks to earth and can prevent electrocution. Ask whether your home has one and proper earthing.
- **Insist on certified materials.** Wires, MCBs and switches should carry the ISI mark. Cheap unbranded wire is a common cause of electrical fires.
- **Switch off and verify.** A careful electrician turns off the supply and tests before working, even for small jobs.

For bigger projects, browse [electrical works contractors](/categories/electrical-works). If you are adding solar panels, the installer should also check your earthing and wiring; see our coverage of [how PM Surya Ghar is changing homes and installers](/news/pm-surya-ghar-rooftop-solar-progress).

> [!WARNING]
> Never let anyone work on live wiring without proper insulated tools, or bypass an MCB or RCCB because it "keeps tripping". Repeated tripping is a warning of a fault that needs to be traced, not ignored.

## AC repair near me: servicing, gas refills and common scams

AC repair is where customers feel most at risk of overcharging, because faults are hard to see. A few facts help.

### Routine service versus repair

A routine service cleans the filters, coils and drain line. Many technicians recommend servicing before summer and after the monsoon, depending on use. A repair addresses a specific fault such as low cooling, water leakage, noise or an error code.

### Gas refills

An AC in good condition should not need regular gas top-ups. If yours needs gas, there is a leak somewhere. Ask the technician to find and fix the leak before refilling, and to tell you which refrigerant your unit uses. Ask for the charge per job in writing.

### Questions to ask an AC technician

:::checklist
- What is the visit or inspection charge, and is it adjusted against the repair?
- What exactly is the fault, and can you show me?
- Is the part being replaced new, and which brand?
- If gas is low, where is the leak and how will you fix it?
- What warranty do you give on parts and labour?
- Will I get a bill with your business name and contact details?
:::

### Energy-saving tips after a repair

Since January 2020, room ACs covered by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency's star-labelling programme must ship with a default temperature of 24°C. Keeping it around that setting, cleaning filters regularly and choosing a higher BEE star rating when you replace a unit all reduce your electricity bill.

## Red flags to watch for

- **No address, no bill.** Reliable providers give a bill with their business name and contact number.
- **Big upfront payment.** For small repairs, pay after the work is done and tested.
- **Unexplained part replacement.** Ask to see the old part; you are entitled to keep it.
- **"Gas finished" every season.** This points to an unrepaired leak.
- **Pressure to decide immediately.** Good technicians let you compare quotes for big jobs.

## What to do if a home service job goes wrong

First, contact the provider in writing (WhatsApp or email works) with photos, the bill and what you want: a repeat visit, a refund or a replacement part. Most reputable businesses fix genuine problems quickly to protect their reviews.

If that fails, you can contact the **National Consumer Helpline** by dialling 1915 or through the [official consumer helpline portal](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in), which tries to resolve disputes before they reach court. As a last resort, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 lets you file a complaint with the district consumer commission, including online. Keep every bill, message and photo as evidence. You can also leave a factual review of your experience on the provider's listing.

Treat every first job as a test. Hire a provider for something small, see how they behave, and if they are honest and careful, save their number and use them again. A trusted local plumber, electrician and AC technician on your phone is worth more than any search result.

### How do I find a good AC repair near me?
Shortlist two or three nearby AC service providers with recent, detailed reviews. Call each, describe your AC brand, type and fault, and ask about their visit charge and warranty. Choose the one who asks sensible questions and agrees to diagnose before quoting. Ask for a bill listing parts and labour after the job.

### How much does AC servicing cost in India?
Charges vary by city, AC type and the kind of service. A basic split AC service in a large city typically costs a few hundred rupees, while a deep jet or foam wash costs more. Gas refills and part replacements are charged separately. Treat any figure as indicative and confirm the price before the technician starts.

### How often should an AC be serviced?
Many technicians and manufacturers suggest servicing at least once a year, ideally before summer, and more often if you use the AC heavily or live in a dusty area. Clean the filters yourself every two to four weeks during heavy use. Check your manufacturer's manual for the recommended schedule.

### Why does my AC need gas refilling every year?
An air conditioner works in a sealed system, so refrigerant should not run out under normal use. If it needs topping up every season, there is a leak. Ask the technician to find and repair the leak before refilling, otherwise you will keep paying for gas that escapes again within months.

### How do I find a reliable plumber near me?
Look for plumbers in your own locality with reviews describing similar jobs. Ask about visit charges, how they will trace the problem and which branded parts they use. For concealed leaks or renovation, ask for a written estimate. Pay for small jobs after you have checked that the leak or blockage is fixed.

### What should I check before hiring an electrician?
For wiring work, ask whether the electrician or contractor holds a licence under your state's electrical safety rules. Insist on ISI-marked wires, switches and MCBs, and ask about earthing and an RCCB. A careful electrician switches off the supply and tests before working. Avoid anyone who suggests bypassing safety devices.

### Is it safe to give an advance to a home service provider?
For small repairs, it is safest to pay after the work is done and tested. For larger jobs such as renovation or new wiring, an advance for materials is normal, but agree it in writing, pay digitally so there is a record, and link further payments to completed stages of work.

### What can I do if a technician overcharges me?
First, ask the provider in writing for a breakdown and a correction, with your bill and photos. If they refuse, contact the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or through its online portal, which mediates disputes. You can then file a complaint with the district consumer commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, including online.

### What does a BEE star rating mean for an AC?
The Bureau of Energy Efficiency's star label shows how efficient an AC is compared with others of the same type, based on a seasonal efficiency measure suited to Indian temperatures. More stars mean lower electricity use. Ratings are revised periodically, so compare labels valid for the same period.

### Should I choose a company service centre or a local technician?
For ACs and appliances under warranty, use the brand's authorised service centre so the warranty stays valid. After warranty, a skilled local technician can be cheaper and quicker, especially for common faults. Check reviews, ask about parts and warranty on repairs, and keep the bill either way.
