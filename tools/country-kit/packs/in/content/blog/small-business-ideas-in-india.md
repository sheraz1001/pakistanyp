The best small business ideas in India with low investment are local services people need every week: home food and tiffin, tuition, mobile and appliance repair, beauty services, tailoring, event décor and small-scale digital services. Most can start from home or a small rented space for under a few lakh rupees, and some for much less.

What makes an idea work is not the idea itself but local demand, a skill you already have and a way to reach customers. This guide lists twelve ideas with indicative start-up costs, the registrations each needs, where funding comes from, and how to get your first paying customers.

:::takeaways
- Service businesses based on a skill you already have need the least capital.
- Food businesses need FSSAI registration; from April 2026 basic registration covers turnover up to ₹1.5 crore.
- Mudra loans go up to ₹20 lakh, without collateral for eligible micro enterprises.
- Udyam registration is free and helps with loans and some government benefits.
- Free listings, WhatsApp and word of mouth bring in most early customers for local businesses.
:::

## How to choose the right small business idea

Before you spend money, test any idea against four questions:

1. **Is there demand within 3–5 km of you?** Most small businesses in India serve their neighbourhood. Look at how many similar businesses already operate and whether customers wait for them.
2. **Do you have the skill, or can you learn it quickly?** Repair, beauty and tuition need proven competence before customers trust you.
3. **What does a customer pay, and how often?** A weekly tiffin customer is worth more than a one-off job.
4. **How much can you afford to lose?** Start small, prove demand, then invest more.

> [!TIP]
> Spend a week before launching asking 20 potential customers what they pay now and what annoys them about their current provider. That answer often becomes your selling point.

## 12 small business ideas in India with low investment

Investment figures below are **indicative** ranges for a modest start, based on typical equipment and set-up needs. Actual costs vary widely by city, scale and the quality of equipment you choose.

:::bars Indicative start-up cost by idea (₹, lower end of typical range)
- Tuition classes | 10000 | from ~₹10k
- Digital services | 30000 | from ~₹30k
- Home tiffin | 40000 | from ~₹40k
- Mobile repair | 60000 | from ~₹60k
- Home salon | 75000 | from ~₹75k
- Event décor | 150000 | from ~₹1.5 lakh
:::

### 1. Home tiffin and meal service

Office-goers, students and PG residents in every big city want home-style meals. You can start from your own kitchen with good containers and a delivery arrangement. You need **FSSAI registration** before you sell food. Start with a fixed weekly menu and WhatsApp orders; see how established [tiffin services](/categories/catering-tiffin) present themselves.

### 2. Tuition and coaching classes

Home tuition needs little more than a room, a whiteboard and study material. Group classes for board exams or spoken English earn more per hour. Parents choose on results and word of mouth, so track your students' improvement.

### 3. Mobile and electronics repair

Smartphones break constantly, and a neighbourhood repair counter with fair pricing builds loyal customers. You need training, a toolkit, spare parts stock and a small counter in a busy market. Offer a short written warranty on repairs to stand out.

### 4. Home salon and beauty services

Trained beauticians can start with home visits, carrying a kit and products, before renting a small parlour. Bridal and festive bookings pay best; hygiene and punctuality matter as much as skill.

### 5. Tailoring, alterations and boutique

Alterations are steady year-round work; blouse stitching, kurta tailoring and festive wear peak around weddings and festivals. A good sewing machine, an overlock machine and a small space are enough to start.

### 6. Event décor and party rentals

Birthday, housewarming and small-function décor is in constant demand. Start with balloon and flower décor and a few rental items such as backdrops and lights, then add tents or furniture as bookings grow. This needs more capital and storage space.

### 7. Digital services for local shops

If you can design, write or manage social media, local shops need help with WhatsApp catalogues, Google listings, simple websites and posts. A laptop and reliable internet are the main costs. Charge a monthly retainer rather than per task.

### 8. Homemade food products

Pickles, papads, snacks, masalas and sweets sell well locally and at festivals, and through online marketplaces once you scale. FSSAI registration and proper labelling are compulsory. Check packaging rules before selling through shops.

### 9. Solar cleaning and maintenance

Rooftop solar installations are growing fast under PM Surya Ghar, and dusty panels lose output. A cleaning and basic maintenance service needs little equipment and can tie up with installers. Read our [rooftop solar subsidy guide](/blogs/rooftop-solar-subsidy-india-guide) to understand the market.

### 10. Pet grooming and boarding

Grooming, walking and boarding are in demand in cities. Start with home-visit grooming; add boarding only with suitable space.

### 11. Photography and video

Weddings, birthdays and product shoots for small brands all need photographers. A camera body, two lenses and editing software are the core costs.

### 12. Cleaning and home services

Deep cleaning and sofa shampooing peak before festivals and house moves. Rent equipment at first; reliable, trained staff win repeat work.

## Registrations and licences for a new small business

Most small businesses start as sole proprietorships. The registrations you need depend on your activity:

| Registration | Who needs it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Udyam (MSME) | Any micro or small enterprise wanting MSME benefits | Free, online, Aadhaar and PAN based |
| FSSAI | Every food business, including home kitchens | Basic registration up to ₹1.5 crore turnover from April 2026 |
| GST | Above ₹40 lakh (goods) or ₹20 lakh (services) in most states, or inter-state goods sellers | Free on the GST portal |
| Shops and Establishments | Shops, offices and commercial premises, as per state rules | Issued by the state labour department |
| Trade licence | Many trades, especially food and workshops | Issued by the municipal corporation |

Our guide to [how to register a business in India](/blogs/how-to-register-a-business-in-india) explains each step, and our [GST registration guide](/blogs/gst-registration-guide-small-business) covers thresholds and the application. For changes in personal and business taxation under the new law, see our explainer on the [new Income-tax Act](/news/new-income-tax-act-2025-what-changes).

## Funding a low-investment business: Mudra and PMEGP

Savings and family support fund most small starts, but two government-backed routes are worth knowing.

### Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana

Mudra loans are given by banks, NBFCs and microfinance institutions to non-farm micro enterprises. They come in four categories:

:::stats Mudra loan categories
- ₹50,000 | Shishu: up to this amount
- ₹5 lakh | Kishore: above ₹50,000
- ₹10 lakh | Tarun: above ₹5 lakh
- ₹20 lakh | Tarun Plus: for borrowers who repaid a Tarun loan
:::

Interest rates and terms are set by each lender. A simple written business plan, KYC documents and a Udyam certificate make the application stronger.

### PMEGP

The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme gives a margin-money subsidy on bank loans for new micro enterprises, with higher rates for rural applicants and special categories such as women and SC/ST entrepreneurs. Applications are online through the KVIC's PMEGP portal, and training is part of the process.

> [!WARNING]
> Never pay an agent to "guarantee" a Mudra or PMEGP loan. You can apply directly at a bank branch or on official portals. Loan approval depends on the lender's assessment.

## How to get your first customers

The first ten customers usually come from people near you. A few low-cost steps make a big difference:

:::checklist
- Create a free business listing with your exact locality, phone and WhatsApp
- Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue and quick replies
- Accept UPI from day one and display your QR code clearly
- Ask every happy customer for a review and a referral
- Join local RWA and community WhatsApp groups, following their rules
- Keep consistent hours and reply to enquiries within the hour
:::

Digital payments help small businesses look professional and keep clean records; our news piece on [UPI's newest features for shopkeepers](/news/upi-new-features-shopkeepers) covers recent changes. Local grocery and food sellers can also look at [ONDC for online orders](/news/ondc-explained-small-sellers).

## Mistakes to avoid with small business ideas in India

- **Renting a shop before proving demand.** Start from home or with home visits.
- **Underpricing.** Cover materials, travel, your time and a margin.
- **Mixing personal and business money.** Open a separate account so you know what you earn.
- **Ignoring compliance.** Registration is cheap compared with a fine or forced closure.

## Final word: start small, prove it, then grow

The strongest small business ideas in India solve a frequent local problem with a skill you already have. Pick one idea, test demand for a month with minimal spend, then register properly and reinvest your early earnings. ### Which small business is most profitable in India?
No single business is most profitable everywhere. Profit depends on local demand, your skill, pricing and costs. Service businesses such as tuition, repair, beauty and digital services often have high margins because they need little stock. Food businesses can do well at volume but have tighter margins and higher compliance needs.

### Which business can I start with ₹50,000 in India?
With around ₹50,000, you could start home tuition, digital services for local shops, a home tiffin service on a small scale, alterations tailoring or home-visit cleaning. These rely mainly on your skill and basic equipment. Costs vary by city and quality, so price your equipment locally before you commit.

### What business can a housewife start from home?
Popular home-based options include tiffin services, homemade pickles and snacks, tuition, tailoring and alterations, home salon services, crafts and online reselling. Choose something that fits your available hours. Food businesses need FSSAI registration, and you should check your housing society's rules on running a business from home.

### Do I need a licence to start a small business in India?
It depends on the activity. Food businesses need FSSAI registration. Shops and offices often need Shops and Establishments registration from the state, and some trades need a municipal trade licence. GST applies above the turnover threshold. Udyam registration is optional but free and useful for loans and MSME benefits.

### How can I get a loan to start a small business?
The most common route is a Mudra loan from a bank, NBFC or microfinance institution, available up to ₹20 lakh across four categories for micro enterprises. PMEGP offers a subsidy on bank loans for new projects. Prepare KYC documents, a short business plan, cost quotes and a Udyam certificate to strengthen your application.

### Is Udyam registration required for a Mudra loan?
Udyam registration is not always mandatory for a Mudra loan, but many lenders ask for it or prefer it, especially for Kishore and higher categories. It is free, online and takes minutes using Aadhaar and PAN, so registering before you apply is sensible. It also helps with priority-sector lending and some government schemes.

### What is the FSSAI limit for basic registration in 2026?
From 1 April 2026, FSSAI basic registration covers food businesses with annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore, up from ₹12 lakh earlier, under revised thresholds notified by FSSAI. Larger businesses need a state or central licence, and some categories need a licence regardless of turnover. Apply on FSSAI's FoSCoS portal.

### Can I start a business without GST registration?
Yes, if your turnover stays below the threshold, which in most states is ₹40 lakh for goods and ₹20 lakh for services, and you do not make inter-state supplies of goods. Some marketplaces and business customers still ask for a GSTIN. You must register within 30 days of becoming liable.

### How do I promote a new small business for free?
Create free listings on business directories and Google, set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue, share before-and-after photos, and ask satisfied customers for reviews and referrals. Local community groups and RWA notice boards help too. Reply quickly to every enquiry, because speed often decides who wins the job.

### Which small business ideas work well in small towns?
Repair services, tuition, tailoring, beauty services, mobile recharge and accessories, photography, tiffin services and agri-input or dairy-related businesses often work well in smaller towns. Rents are lower, but customers rely heavily on trust and personal recommendation, so reputation and consistent service matter even more than in big cities.
