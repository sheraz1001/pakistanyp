The cost of living in Bangalore (Bengaluru) usually sits between Mumbai and Delhi NCR. Mumbai is the most expensive of the three, almost entirely because of housing. Delhi NCR is generally the cheapest for a comparable flat, while Bengaluru's rents have climbed sharply in the tech corridors and its upfront deposits remain the highest.

For most people, rent and the deposit decide the answer. Food, groceries and phone bills differ far less between the three cities than many expect. Commuting is the wild card: a long daily cab ride in Bengaluru or Gurugram can cost more than the gap in rent.

All figures below are indicative ranges for 2026, drawn from typical listings and household spending patterns. They move quickly and vary hugely by locality, so treat them as a starting point and check current listings in your target neighbourhood.

:::takeaways
- Mumbai is the costliest city overall, driven by rent; Delhi NCR is usually the cheapest of the three for housing.
- Bengaluru landlords have traditionally asked for the largest deposits, often several months' rent.
- Metro fares were revised in 2025: Bengaluru's maximum is ₹90 and Delhi's ranges from ₹11 to ₹64.
- Food, groceries and mobile plans cost roughly the same across all three cities.
- Living near your office is the single biggest saving in Bengaluru and Gurugram.
:::

## Cost of living in Bangalore: the quick verdict

Compared with Mumbai, Bengaluru gives you noticeably more space for the same rent, a milder climate that cuts air-conditioning bills, and a large supply of new apartments. Compared with Delhi NCR, it is usually dearer for housing and has weaker public transport coverage, though the metro network is expanding.

Here is how the three cities compare on the main monthly costs for a working professional.

| Cost head | Bengaluru | Mumbai | Delhi NCR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rent level | Medium to high | Highest | Lowest to medium |
| Typical deposit | Often 3–10 months | Often 3–6 months | Often 1–3 months |
| Public transport | Metro and buses, gaps in coverage | Suburban rail, metro, buses | Largest metro network |
| Electricity use | Lower (mild climate) | Medium (humid) | High in summer |
| Groceries and eating out | Similar | Slightly higher | Similar |

*Indicative, based on typical market practice. Terms vary by landlord and locality.*

## Rent and deposits: the biggest difference

### Bengaluru

Rents are highest around the IT corridors such as Whitefield, Outer Ring Road, Electronic City surroundings, HSR Layout and Koramangala. Older areas in the south and west, and newer suburbs further out, are cheaper. The deposit is the shock for newcomers: landlords have long asked for several months' rent upfront, sometimes up to ten, though this is negotiable and lower deposits are increasingly common in larger gated projects.

### Mumbai

Space is the luxury. The same rent that gets a two-bedroom flat in Bengaluru may get a one-bedroom in the western suburbs. Many people save by living in Thane, Navi Mumbai or further along the suburban rail lines. In Maharashtra, every leave and licence agreement must be registered, which adds stamp duty and registration charges to your moving cost.

### Delhi NCR

Delhi, Noida and Ghaziabad generally offer the best rent-to-space ratio, and deposits of one to three months are the norm. Gurugram is closer to Bengaluru's price levels in its newer sectors. Builder floors are common and often cheaper than apartment societies.

:::bars Indicative monthly rent for a 2BHK in a mid-range area (₹)
- Delhi NCR | 32000 | ₹22k–45k
- Bengaluru | 38000 | ₹28k–55k
- Mumbai suburbs | 55000 | ₹40k–80k
:::

> [!TIP]
> Before paying a large deposit, insist on a written rent agreement that states the deposit amount, the refund timeline and what deductions are allowed. A brokerage-free listing is not a reason to skip the paperwork.

## Commuting costs in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi

Transport is where your choice of locality matters most.

- **Bengaluru:** Namma Metro revised fares from February 2025, the first revision since 2017, raising the maximum fare to ₹90. Buses fill gaps, but many professionals still rely on cabs, autos or two-wheelers, and traffic can turn a short distance into a long ride.
- **Mumbai:** the suburban railway remains the cheapest way to move long distances, and monthly passes cost far less than daily cabs. Metro lines now cover more of the suburbs.
- **Delhi NCR:** Delhi Metro revised fares from 25 August 2025, the first change in about eight years. The minimum fare is now ₹11 and the maximum ₹64 on regular lines, with smart card discounts available.

:::stats Metro fares after the 2025 revisions
- ₹90 | maximum Namma Metro fare (Bengaluru)
- ₹11 | minimum Delhi Metro fare
- ₹64 | maximum Delhi Metro fare, excluding Airport Express
- 2017 | year both networks last revised fares before 2025
:::

If your office and home are on the same metro line, your monthly commute can stay modest. If not, budget generously for cabs and autos, especially in Bengaluru and Gurugram.

## Food, groceries and household help

Groceries and everyday food cost broadly the same in all three cities, with Mumbai slightly higher for eating out. Local markets and neighbourhood kirana shops are usually cheaper than premium supermarkets, and many now take orders on WhatsApp or through ONDC; see our explainer on [how ONDC works for small sellers](/news/ondc-explained-small-sellers).

Single professionals often save by subscribing to a tiffin service rather than ordering in. You can compare [tiffin and catering services](/categories/catering-tiffin) or [mess and meal services](/categories/mess-meal-services) in your area. For families, part-time [domestic help](/categories/domestic-help) is common in all three cities, with rates set locally by locality and number of tasks.

## Utilities, internet and bills

Electricity bills depend on climate as much as tariffs. Bengaluru's mild weather means many homes rarely run an AC, while Delhi summers push bills up sharply. Mumbai's humidity keeps fans and ACs running much of the year.

State schemes affect bills too. Karnataka's **Gruha Jyothi** scheme gives eligible households free electricity up to a usage limit of 200 units a month, calculated against each household's past average consumption; tenants can apply on the state's Seva Sindhu portal. Delhi has its own long-running subsidy for low-consumption homes. Eligibility rules change, so check with your electricity distributor after you move.

Broadband, mobile plans and cooking gas cost much the same across the three cities. Society maintenance charges vary widely and are sometimes included in rent, so always ask.

## Sample monthly budgets for 2026 (indicative)

These rough budgets assume a mid-range locality and moderate lifestyle. They are not averages from any survey; use them to sense-check your own plan.

| Monthly cost | Single professional, Bengaluru | Single professional, Mumbai | Single professional, Delhi NCR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rent (1BHK or shared 2BHK) | ₹18k–35k | ₹25k–50k | ₹15k–30k |
| Food and groceries | ₹8k–15k | ₹9k–16k | ₹8k–15k |
| Transport | ₹3k–8k | ₹2k–6k | ₹2k–6k |
| Utilities and internet | ₹2k–4k | ₹2.5k–5k | ₹2.5k–6k |

A family of four needs a 2BHK or 3BHK, school fees and more household help, so its budget is driven even more by rent and schooling. On the tax side, check with your employer how house rent allowance is treated for your city under the current income-tax rules, as the list of cities qualifying for the higher exemption has been under revision.

> [!NOTE]
> The deposit is a one-time cost but can equal several months of your budget in Bengaluru. Plan your savings for the move, not just the monthly spend.

## How to cut your costs in any of the three cities

:::checklist
- Shortlist localities on the same metro or rail line as your office
- Compare deposit terms, not just rent, before committing
- Ask whether maintenance, parking and water charges are included
- Register or notarise the rent agreement as your state requires
- Use a tiffin service or local mess for weekday meals
- Choose a society with power backup if outages matter for remote work
- Buy appliances with a good energy star rating to manage bills
:::

## Final word on choosing your city

The cost of living in Bangalore is manageable if you live close to work and negotiate the deposit. Mumbai rewards people who accept smaller homes or longer train rides, and Delhi NCR offers the most space for the money. Before signing anything, visit the locality at peak hour and price the commute.

:::faq
### Is Bangalore cheaper than Mumbai?
Generally, yes. The main reason is rent: a similar budget usually gets a larger flat in Bengaluru than in Mumbai, particularly compared with Mumbai's western suburbs and central areas. Food, groceries, mobile plans and internet cost roughly the same in both. Bengaluru's upfront deposits are often higher, so the move itself can need more savings.

### Is Delhi cheaper than Bangalore?
For housing, Delhi NCR is usually cheaper than Bengaluru, and deposits are much smaller, often one to three months' rent. Gurugram's newer sectors are an exception and can match Bengaluru prices. Delhi's extensive metro also keeps commuting costs down. Electricity bills can be higher in Delhi summers because of air-conditioning use.

### How much salary is enough to live in Bangalore?
It depends on your locality, household size and lifestyle. A single person sharing a flat near work can manage on a much lower salary than a family renting a 3BHK near an IT corridor and paying private school fees. Add up indicative rent, food, transport and bills for your situation, then keep a margin for the deposit and emergencies.

### What is the cost of living in Bangalore for a family?
For a family of four, the big costs are rent for a 2BHK or 3BHK, school fees, transport and household help. Rent varies most: homes near Whitefield, Outer Ring Road or Koramangala cost far more than in outer suburbs. Budget separately for the security deposit, which in Bengaluru can be several months' rent.

### Why are rent deposits so high in Bangalore?
High deposits are a long-standing local custom in Bengaluru rather than a legal requirement. Landlords have often asked for several months' rent, sometimes up to ten. Deposits are negotiable, and many larger apartment projects now accept lower amounts. Always record the deposit and refund terms in a written rent agreement.

### What is the Namma Metro fare in 2026?
Bengaluru Metro Rail Corporation revised fares in February 2025, the first revision since 2017, and set the maximum fare at ₹90. The fare you pay depends on distance travelled, and smart cards and QR tickets may carry discounts. Check BMRCL's official channels for the current fare table, as further revisions have been proposed.

### Are groceries cheaper in Delhi or Bangalore?
Grocery prices are broadly similar in Delhi and Bengaluru. The bigger differences come from where you shop: neighbourhood kirana stores and local markets are usually cheaper than premium supermarkets, and seasonal produce from local mandis costs less than imported or packaged goods. Quick-delivery apps may add convenience fees and small-order charges.

### Is Mumbai worth the higher cost of living?
For many people, yes, if their career depends on industries concentrated in Mumbai such as finance, media and shipping. The trade-off is less living space for the same rent and, often, longer commutes. Living along the suburban rail network in Thane or Navi Mumbai can bring housing costs closer to Bengaluru levels.

### Do I need to register my rent agreement?
It depends on your state and the length of the agreement. Leases longer than one year must be registered under the Registration Act, which is why many agreements run for 11 months. Maharashtra is stricter: every leave and licence agreement, whatever its length, must be registered. Check local rules before signing.

### Which city has better public transport: Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi?
Delhi has the largest metro network of the three, and Mumbai's suburban railway carries the most commuters over long distances at low fares. Bengaluru's metro is growing but still leaves gaps, so many residents rely on buses, cabs and two-wheelers. Choosing a home near a metro or rail station helps in all three cities.
:::
