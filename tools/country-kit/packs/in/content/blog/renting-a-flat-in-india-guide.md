Renting a flat in India safely comes down to three documents and checks: a written rent agreement that both sides sign, a clear receipt for the security deposit, and tenant police verification where your city requires it. Most residential agreements are made for 11 months, because leases of more than a year must be registered under the Registration Act, 1908. Some states go further: Maharashtra requires every leave and licence agreement to be registered, whatever its length.

This rent agreement India guide covers what the agreement should contain, typical deposits, stamp duty and registration, police verification, and how to get your deposit back when you move out.

:::takeaways
- Always sign a written agreement and pay rent and deposit digitally.
- Leases over one year need compulsory registration; 11-month agreements usually do not, except in some states.
- Maharashtra requires registration of every leave and licence agreement; Tamil Nadu requires registration with the Rent Authority.
- The Model Tenancy Act suggests a two-month deposit cap, but it applies only where a state has adopted it.
- Tenant police verification is required by police orders in many cities.
:::

## Before you sign: inspect the flat and the owner

Visit at different times of day to check water supply, noise, parking and mobile signal. Look for damp patches and check whether the society restricts bachelors, pets or commercial use.

Confirm that the person renting to you has the right to do so. Ask to see an ownership document such as the sale deed or a recent property tax receipt, and match the name to the owner's ID. If a relative or agent is signing, ask for a power of attorney or the owner's written authorisation.

> [!TIP]
> Take a dated video walk-through of the flat on the day you move in, showing walls, floors, fittings and appliances. Share it with the owner on WhatsApp. It is the simplest protection against unfair deductions later.

## The renting process, step by step

:::steps From flat hunt to move-in
- Shortlist and visit | Compare localities, commute and society rules; visit at least twice.
- Agree the terms | Settle rent, deposit, maintenance, lock-in, notice period and annual increase.
- Draft the agreement | Use a clear draft that lists every term and the fittings provided.
- Pay stamp duty | Buy e-stamp paper or pay online at your state's rate.
- Sign and register | Sign with two witnesses; register where the law requires it.
- Pay and get receipts | Pay deposit and first rent digitally and get written acknowledgement.
- Complete police verification | Submit tenant details to the local police as your city requires.
:::

## Rent agreement India: what the document must include

A rent agreement in India should be specific enough that a stranger could read it and understand exactly what was agreed. Check that yours covers:

:::checklist
- Full names, addresses and ID details of owner and tenant
- Complete address of the flat, including floor and flat number
- Monthly rent, due date and mode of payment
- Security deposit amount and the timeline and conditions for refund
- Who pays maintenance, electricity, water, gas and property tax
- Term of the agreement, lock-in period and notice period for each side
- Annual rent increase, if any, as a percentage
- List of furniture, fittings and appliances provided, with their condition
- Rules on repairs: what the tenant handles and what the owner handles
- Owner's right of entry, with reasonable advance notice
- Signatures of both parties and two witnesses
:::

Avoid vague clauses such as "deductions as decided by the owner". If the owner insists on a lock-in, make sure it applies to both sides.

## Why most rent agreements are for 11 months

This is the most common rent agreement India question, and the answer is legal. Under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908, a lease from year to year, for more than one year or reserving a yearly rent must be registered with the sub-registrar. Registration means stamp duty based on the rent and deposit, a registration fee and a visit or online process. An 11-month agreement falls outside that rule in most states, so it is usually just printed on stamp paper of the right value and signed, sometimes with notarisation.

Three cautions:

- **Notarisation is not registration.** A notary only attests signatures. It does not make an unregistered long lease valid as evidence of a tenancy.
- **Repeated renewals can blur the line.** If you renew an 11-month agreement year after year, take advice on whether registration has become advisable.
- **Some states override the 11-month approach**, as below.

## State rules: Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and the Model Tenancy Act

| State or law | Key rule for tenants and owners |
| --- | --- |
| Maharashtra | Every leave and licence agreement must be registered (Section 55, Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999); online registration is available through the state IGR system |
| Tamil Nadu | Tenancy agreements must be registered with the Rent Authority under the 2017 tenancy law, within 90 days |
| Uttar Pradesh | Urban tenancies are governed by the 2021 Urban Premises Tenancy Act, which sets up a Rent Authority |
| Model Tenancy Act, 2021 | Central template suggesting a deposit cap of two months' rent for homes; applies only after a state adopts it |

In Maharashtra, the duty to register lies with the owner, and if the agreement is not registered, the tenant's version of the terms can prevail in a dispute. Tenants in [Mumbai](/cities/mumbai), [Pune](/cities/pune) and the rest of [Maharashtra](/maharashtra) should insist on registration. In [Tamil Nadu](/tamil-nadu), the Madras High Court has held that the rent authorities will not hear disputes without a registered tenancy agreement, so registration matters in [Chennai](/cities/chennai) too.

> [!NOTE]
> Reports in late 2025 about "new rent rules" with deposit caps and online registration largely restate the Model Tenancy Act. Housing is a state subject, so these rules bind only where your state has adopted them. Check your state's notification or ask a lawyer.

- 11 months | common term for unregistered agreements
- 1 year | lease length above which registration is compulsory
- 2 months | deposit cap suggested by the Model Tenancy Act
- 90 days | to register with Tamil Nadu's Rent Authority
:::

## Security deposit: what is normal and how to protect it

Deposits vary more by city than by law. Bengaluru has long been known for asking several months' rent, while Delhi NCR and many other cities commonly ask one to three months. Our comparison of the [cost of living in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi](/blogs/cost-of-living-bengaluru-mumbai-delhi) covers typical local practice.

To protect your deposit, pay it by bank transfer or UPI, never cash; state the amount and refund timeline in the agreement; record the condition of the flat at move-in and move-out; and give notice exactly as the agreement requires, in writing. Normal wear and tear, such as faded paint after a year, should not be deducted. Damage beyond that, unpaid bills and unpaid rent can be.

## Tenant police verification

In many cities, police orders require owners to report tenant details to the local police station. Failing to comply can lead to action under Section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), which replaced Section 188 of the IPC for disobeying orders of a public servant. The duty usually falls on the owner, but tenants should cooperate and keep a copy.

Most big-city police forces let you submit tenant information online. Mumbai Police, for example, offers an online tenant information form, and Delhi Police and Karnataka State Police provide tenant verification through their portals or local stations. You typically need the tenant's photo ID, a photograph, permanent address and the owner's details.

> [!WARNING]
> Never hand over original ID documents. Share self-attested photocopies marked with the purpose, such as "for rent agreement only", and the date.

## Moving out and getting your deposit back

Give written notice as per the agreement and agree an inspection date with the owner. Clear all utility bills and get final readings. Repair any damage you caused, and clean the flat or book a professional [cleaning service](/categories/cleaning-maintenance). Compare against your move-in video, return all keys and get a written handover note.

If the owner delays the refund unfairly, send a written reminder citing the agreement. Then consider a legal notice through a [lawyer](/categories/legal-services). Many disputes settle at that stage.

## Final word on renting safely

A clear agreement, digital payments and good records protect both tenant and owner. Read every clause, register where your state requires, and complete police verification before you move in. :::faq
### Is a rent agreement mandatory in India?
No central law forces every tenancy to have a written agreement, but leases of more than one year must be in writing and registered. Some states, such as Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu, require registration more broadly. In practice, a written agreement is essential for address proof, bank and gas connections, police verification and resolving disputes.

### Why are rent agreements made for 11 months?
Under the Registration Act, 1908, leases from year to year, for more than one year or reserving a yearly rent must be registered, which involves higher stamp duty and a registration fee. An 11-month agreement avoids compulsory registration in most states. It does not avoid registration rules in states such as Maharashtra, where all leave and licence agreements must be registered.

### Is registration of rent agreement compulsory in Maharashtra?
Yes. Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, every leave and licence agreement must be registered, whatever its length. The responsibility lies with the owner, and if the agreement is unregistered, the tenant's account of the terms can prevail in a dispute. Registration can be done online through the state's IGR system.

### How much security deposit is legal in India?
There is no single national cap that applies everywhere. The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests a maximum of two months' rent for residential property, but it applies only in states that have adopted it. Elsewhere, the deposit is whatever owner and tenant agree. Always record the amount and refund terms in the agreement.

### Is a notarised rent agreement valid?
A notarised agreement is valid as a written record of an 11-month or shorter tenancy in most states, but notarisation is not registration. A notary only attests signatures. For leases of more than a year, or in states that require registration of all agreements, a notarised document is not a substitute for registration with the sub-registrar or rent authority.

### Is police verification of tenants mandatory?
In many cities, police orders make it compulsory for owners to submit tenant details to the local police. Non-compliance can attract action under Section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Requirements vary by city, so check your local police website. Many forces, including Mumbai Police, offer online tenant information forms.

### Who pays stamp duty on a rent agreement?
The law in each state sets stamp duty rates, but who pays is usually agreed between owner and tenant and written into the agreement. Often it is shared equally or paid by the tenant. Registration fees, where registration applies, are handled the same way. Confirm the current rate on your state's e-stamping or registration portal.

### Can an owner increase rent during the agreement?
Only if the agreement allows it. Most agreements fix rent for the term and specify any increase at renewal, often as a percentage. If your agreement does not mention an increase, the owner cannot raise rent mid-term without your consent. State rent laws and adopted tenancy acts may add further rules on notice for increases.

### How soon must a landlord return the security deposit?
The agreement should state the refund timeline, and that is what usually applies. Where a state has adopted a Model Tenancy Act-based law, it may set rules on refunds at handover. If the agreement is silent, ask for the refund at handover after deducting only documented unpaid dues and damage beyond normal wear and tear.

### Can a landlord evict a tenant without notice?
An owner should follow the notice period and termination terms in the agreement, and state rent laws set out the grounds and procedure for eviction. Cutting off water or electricity, changing locks or removing belongings to force a tenant out is not a lawful substitute. Tenants facing this should seek legal advice promptly.
:::
