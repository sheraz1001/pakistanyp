Every wedding planning checklist India families rely on starts with three decisions made together: the total budget, the guest count and the date. Everything else, from venue to caterer to photographer, follows from those. Book the venue and caterer first, then photographers, décor and make-up artists, and leave smaller purchases for the final two months.

Indian weddings involve more events, more family decision-makers and more vendors than most. This guide gives you a month-by-month timeline, an indicative budget split, the order in which to book vendors, questions to ask before signing, and how to register the marriage afterwards.

:::takeaways
- Fix budget, guest count and dates before contacting any vendor.
- Popular venues and dates in the peak season get booked many months ahead.
- Venue and catering usually take the largest share of the budget.
- Get every vendor commitment in writing, with payment stages and cancellation terms.
- Register the marriage after the ceremony; the process depends on the law that applies.
- Adapt this wedding planning checklist India-wide to your own rituals, city and family.
:::

## Start with budget, guest list and dates

Sit down with both families early and agree three numbers: the maximum budget, the approximate guest count for each function, and a preferred window of dates. Decide who pays for what if costs are being shared, and write it down to avoid confusion later.

Guest count drives cost more than anything else, because catering is charged per plate and venue size follows headcount. Trimming a function's list by a fifth often saves more than haggling with every vendor.

If your community follows a muhurat (an auspicious date and time chosen by a priest or astrologer), get a few options early. Popular muhurat dates in the main wedding season can push up demand and prices for venues and vendors.

## Indicative wedding budget split

No two weddings spend the same way, but most budgets follow a similar pattern. The split below is **indicative**, a starting point for planning, not data from a survey.

:::bars How a wedding budget is often split (% of total, indicative)
- Venue and catering | 40 | ~40%
- Clothes and jewellery | 20 | ~20%
- Photography and video | 10 | ~10%
- Décor and lighting | 10 | ~10%
- Music and entertainment | 5 | ~5%
- Invitations and gifts | 5 | ~5%
- Contingency | 10 | ~10%
:::

Jewellery can dominate the budget in some families, so decide early whether it sits inside the wedding budget or separately. Keep at least a tenth aside for contingencies: extra guests, last-minute transport and price rises are almost certain.

> [!TIP]
> Track every quote, advance and balance in one shared spreadsheet or notes file with columns for vendor, amount, paid, due date and contact. It prevents double payments and missed balances in the final week.

## Wedding planning checklist India: the 12-month timeline

Use this timeline as your master plan. If you have less time, compress it, but keep the order.

:::steps Month-by-month wedding timeline
- 10–12 months before | Fix budget, guest count and dates; shortlist and book the venue.
- 8–10 months before | Book caterer, photographer and videographer, and décor team.
- 6–8 months before | Book make-up artist, mehendi artist, DJ or band, and priest; start outfit shopping.
- 4–6 months before | Send save-the-dates; book guest accommodation and transport.
- 2–3 months before | Order invitations, finalise menus, plan each function's run-sheet.
- 1 month before | Final fittings, confirm every vendor, collect RSVPs and prepare payment envelopes.
- Wedding week | Brief a family coordinator, hand over timings and keep emergency kits ready.
:::

## Which wedding vendors to book first

Book vendors in order of scarcity. The best options in each category are limited on peak dates.

| Priority | Vendor | Why book early |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Venue (banquet hall, lawn, hotel, farmhouse) | Fixes date, capacity and often catering rules |
| 2 | Caterer | Some venues insist on in-house or empanelled caterers |
| 3 | Photographer and videographer | Top teams book out first on popular dates |
| 4 | Décor and lighting | Needs venue details to design |
| 5 | Make-up and mehendi artists | Bridal slots are limited per day |
| 6 | Music: DJ, band, dhol or baraat band | Popular groups are booked for peak nights |
| 7 | Priest or officiant | Depends on muhurat and rituals |
| 8 | Transport, accommodation, invitations | Easier to arrange later |

## Questions to ask before you sign any vendor

Verbal promises are the main source of wedding disputes. Before paying an advance, ask for written answers:

:::checklist
- Exactly what is included, and what is charged extra?
- Is the price per plate, per hour or per event, and what are minimum numbers?
- How much advance is due now, and when are the remaining payments due?
- What happens if we cancel or change the date?
- Who will actually turn up on the day, and how many staff?
- What are the timings, and what does overtime cost?
- For photographers: how many edited photos, which deliverables, and by when?
- For venues: rules on outside caterers, décor, music and closing time?
- Are taxes included in the quoted price?
:::

Pay advances digitally so there is a record, and keep a signed copy of each agreement. Ask past clients about punctuality and whether the vendor delivered what was promised.

> [!WARNING]
> Avoid paying the full amount upfront for any wedding service. Link the balance to delivery: on the day for catering and décor, and on delivery of albums and films for photographers.

## Outfits, jewellery, beauty and the smaller details

Bridal outfits often need several fittings, so start early. Groom's wear and family outfits can follow later. Browse [wedding shopping](/categories/wedding-shopping) and [jewellery stores](/categories/jewellery-accessories) to compare options, and book trials with make-up artists from [beauty services](/categories/beauty) a few weeks before the main function.

Small details are where stress builds up in the final week. Assign one trusted person to each: gifts and return gifts, guest pick-ups, ritual items, first aid, phone chargers and a small cash float. Neighbourhood shops and quick-delivery apps can help with last-minute needs; our news piece on [quick commerce and kirana stores](/news/quick-commerce-and-kirana-stores) looks at how local retail is changing.

## Registering your marriage after the wedding

Registering your marriage gives you a certificate that you will need for passports, visas, bank nominations and many other records.

- **Hindu, Sikh, Jain and Buddhist marriages** solemnised by religious ceremony can be registered under the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 with the marriage registrar or sub-registrar, as per your state's rules. Both partners usually appear with witnesses, age and address proofs, photographs and evidence of the ceremony such as the invitation card.
- **Marriages under the Special Marriage Act, 1954**, used by couples of any religion including interfaith couples, require a notice to the Marriage Officer of the district where one partner has lived for at least 30 days. The notice is displayed for 30 days for objections before the marriage is solemnised and registered.

Documents and fees vary by state, and many states now accept online applications with an appointment for verification. Check your state's registration or revenue department portal, or ask a [legal services provider](/categories/legal-services) for help.

:::stats Marriage registration basics
- 30 days | notice period under the Special Marriage Act
- 30 days | minimum residence in the district before giving notice
- 1955 | Hindu Marriage Act, used for most religious ceremonies
:::

:::faq
### How many months before should you start planning an Indian wedding?
Ideally, start 10 to 12 months ahead, especially if you want a popular venue on a peak-season date. Smaller weddings can be planned in three to six months, but your choice of venues and top vendors will be narrower. Fix budget, guest count and dates first, then book venue and caterer immediately.

### What should be included in a wedding planning checklist in India?
A complete checklist covers budget and guest list, dates and muhurat, venue, caterer, photographer, décor, make-up and mehendi, music, priest, outfits and jewellery, invitations, guest accommodation and transport, gifts, a function-wise run-sheet, vendor payments and marriage registration after the ceremony. Assign an owner and deadline to each item.

### How do I make a wedding budget in India?
Agree a maximum total with both families, then allocate shares to venue and catering, clothes and jewellery, photography, décor, music, invitations and contingency. Venue and catering usually take the biggest share. Collect quotes, track every advance and balance in one sheet, and keep around a tenth of the budget for surprises.

### Which vendor should be booked first for a wedding?
Book the venue first, because it fixes the date and capacity and often decides catering rules. Next, book the caterer, then the photographer and videographer, décor team and bridal make-up artist. These are the vendors whose best options sell out earliest on popular dates. Transport and invitations can be arranged later.

### How can I reduce wedding costs in India?
The biggest saving usually comes from a smaller guest list, because catering is charged per plate. Other options include a weekday or off-peak date, fewer functions, combining venues for multiple events, choosing seasonal flowers and local décor, and negotiating packages with a single vendor for related services. Avoid cutting the contingency reserve.

### Is marriage registration compulsory in India?
Rules differ by state. Several states have laws making registration of marriages compulsory, and the Supreme Court has directed that marriages be registered. Even where not strictly enforced, a marriage certificate is needed for passports, visas, name changes and many financial records, so registering soon after the wedding is strongly advisable.

### What documents are needed for marriage registration?
Typically, both partners need age proof, address proof, passport-size photographs, and evidence of the marriage such as the invitation card and ceremony photographs, along with witnesses carrying their own ID. Under the Special Marriage Act, a notice of intended marriage is also required. Exact lists and fees vary by state, so check your state portal.

### How long does the Special Marriage Act process take?
The couple gives notice to the Marriage Officer of a district where at least one partner has lived for 30 days or more. The notice is then displayed for 30 days so objections can be raised. If no valid objection is made, the marriage can be solemnised and registered, with witnesses, after that period.

### How much advance do wedding vendors usually take?
Advance terms vary by vendor and city. Many ask for a booking advance to block the date, with further payments before and on the day. Always agree the advance, payment schedule and cancellation terms in writing, pay digitally for a record, and avoid paying the full amount before the service is delivered.

### Should we hire a wedding planner?
A planner helps most with large, multi-day or destination weddings, or when family members cannot spare time to coordinate vendors. They charge a fee or percentage, but may secure better vendor rates and reduce stress. For smaller weddings, a well-organised family coordinator with a clear checklist and shared budget sheet can manage well.
:::
