The winter **wedding season India** looks forward to every year is now under way. In the Hindu calendar, auspicious wedding dates resume after Dev Uthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi, which fell on 20 November 2026 in most panchangs and 21 November in some traditions. From then until mid-December, caterers, banquet halls, decorators, photographers, salons, tailors and jewellers see their busiest weeks of the year.

The scale is large. For the 2025 season, the Confederation of All India Traders (CAIT) estimated about **46 lakh weddings** between 1 November and 14 December, generating business of around **₹6.5 lakh crore**. At the time of writing, CAIT had not published a 2026 estimate, so treat last year's figures as a guide. This explainer sets out what the numbers show and how local service businesses can prepare.

:::takeaways
- Wedding dates in many North Indian communities resume after Dev Uthani Ekadashi, which fell on 20–21 November 2026.
- CAIT estimated 46 lakh weddings and ₹6.5 lakh crore of business for the 1 November–14 December 2025 season.
- Delhi was expected to be the largest single market in 2025, with about 4.8 lakh weddings.
- GST cuts from September 2025 lowered tax on hotel rooms up to ₹7,500 a night, salon services and many garments.
- Vendors who confirm bookings in writing, take part-payments by UPI and keep their listings current win the most repeat referrals.
:::

## Wedding season India in numbers

CAIT, a national traders' body, publishes estimates each season based on surveys of traders and service providers in many cities. They are not official statistics, but they are the most widely cited figures.

:::stats CAIT's 2025 wedding season estimate (1 Nov–14 Dec 2025)
- 46 lakh | weddings estimated nationwide
- ₹6.5 lakh crore | estimated business generated
- 4.8 lakh | weddings estimated in Delhi
- 1 crore+ | temporary jobs CAIT said the season would support
:::

CAIT's figures show spending per wedding rising even when the number of weddings dips. Its 2024 study put that season at about 48 lakh weddings and around ₹5.9 lakh crore of business; for 2025 it expected fewer weddings but more spending.

:::bars CAIT estimates of winter wedding season business (₹ lakh crore)
- 2023 season | 4.25 | ₹4.25 lakh crore (35 lakh weddings)
- 2024 season | 5.9 | ₹5.9 lakh crore (48 lakh weddings)
- 2025 season | 6.5 | ₹6.5 lakh crore (46 lakh weddings)
:::

> [!NOTE]
> Wedding dates vary by community, region and calendar. Many South Indian, Bengali, Sikh, Muslim and Christian families follow their own calendars or simply choose a convenient weekend. Always confirm dates with clients rather than relying on a national muhurat list.

## What changed for vendors this year: GST and payments

This is the first full winter season under the two-slab GST structure that took effect on 22 September 2025. According to the GST Council's decisions:

- **Hotel rooms** with a tariff of up to ₹7,500 a night moved from 12% to 5%, without input tax credit, which lowers the cost of guest accommodation.
- **Salon and beauty services** moved from 18% to 5%, without input tax credit.
- **Garments and footwear** priced up to ₹2,500 per piece are taxed at 5%.
- **Gold and jewellery** stayed at 3%.

Our explainer on the [new GST rates](/news/gst-rate-changes-two-slab-explained) covers the details. Most clients now pay advances by UPI; read about [UPI's new rules for shopkeepers](/news/upi-new-features-shopkeepers) and NPCI's merchant fee plan, which was deferred beyond Diwali 2026.

> [!WARNING]
> Wedding season is also peak season for payment scams. Never approve a UPI request to "receive" an advance; receiving money never needs your PIN. Confirm large transfers in your bank app before releasing bookings.

## How local service businesses can prepare

### Lock in staff and suppliers early

The most common failure in peak season is not demand but capacity. Confirm extra cooks, helpers, drivers and make-up assistants for your busiest dates now, and agree rates with suppliers of flowers, vegetables and rental items before prices rise.

### Put every booking in writing

A one-page booking confirmation on WhatsApp or paper, with date, venue, guest count, menu or package, price, advance and cancellation terms, prevents most disputes. Families planning their budgets often use a checklist like our [Indian wedding planning checklist](/blogs/indian-wedding-planning-checklist-budget), so match your quote to the way they compare.

:::steps A two-week plan for the peak season
- Review your calendar | List every confirmed date and the staff and stock each needs.
- Confirm suppliers | Agree quantities and rates with key suppliers for each date.
- Send confirmations | Message every client a written summary of what is booked.
- Collect advances | Take part-payments by UPI or bank transfer and issue receipts.
- Plan logistics | Check travel times between venues, parking and power backup.
- Ask for reviews | After each event, request a review and photos for your listing.
:::

### Plan logistics around traffic

On peak muhurat dates, roads near popular venues can jam for hours. Plan routes, allow extra time and keep a backup vehicle. In cities with growing metro networks, staff can sometimes travel faster by train; see our explainer on [metro expansion in Indian cities](/news/metro-expansion-indian-cities-local-business).

### Keep your listings current

Families search for vendors by area and category, often late at night. Make sure your online listing has current photos, starting prices or packages, your service area and a working phone and WhatsApp number. Vendors in [Delhi](/delhi) and other big markets compete on fast replies as much as on price.

:::checklist
- Extra staff confirmed for every peak date
- Supplier rates agreed in writing
- Booking confirmations sent to all clients
- Advances received and receipts issued
- GST rates on invoices checked against current rates
- Backup generator, vehicle and key equipment arranged
- Online listing updated with photos, packages and availability
- Review requests ready to send after each event
:::

## Beyond the big cities

Wedding demand is not limited to metros. CAIT's estimates cover weddings across tier-two and tier-three cities and towns, where local caterers, tent houses and bands often handle several functions a day. Smaller vendors can compete by specialising, such as regional cuisine, a style of decor or bridal make-up for a particular community, and by building a reputation through reviews.

Planning help is also in demand; families search for [wedding planners and wedding services](/categories/wedding-services) and [jewellers](/categories/jewellery-accessories) months ahead.

### When does the wedding season start in India in 2026?
In many North Indian Hindu communities, wedding dates resume after Dev Uthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi, which fell on 20 November 2026 in most panchangs and 21 November in some traditions. The winter season usually runs until mid-December. Other communities follow their own calendars, so families and vendors should confirm dates with a local pandit or calendar.

### How many weddings happen in India's wedding season?
For the season from 1 November to 14 December 2025, the Confederation of All India Traders estimated about 46 lakh weddings nationwide, generating around ₹6.5 lakh crore of business. Its 2024 estimate was about 48 lakh weddings. At the time of writing, CAIT had not published a 2026 estimate. These are trade-body estimates, not official statistics.

### Which businesses earn the most during wedding season?
Caterers, banquet halls, decorators, tent and lighting rentals, photographers, bands, beauty and bridal make-up artists, tailors and jewellers see the most demand. Hotels, transport services and florists also benefit. CAIT's estimates include spending on clothing, jewellery, food, venues and services, which together make the season one of the busiest periods for local trade.

### What is the GST on wedding services after the 2025 changes?
Since 22 September 2025, salon and beauty services are taxed at 5% without input tax credit, hotel rooms up to ₹7,500 a night at 5%, and garments up to ₹2,500 per piece at 5%. Gold and jewellery remain at 3%. Many other event services are at 18%. Check the exact rate for your service code with a chartered accountant.

### How far ahead should families book wedding vendors?
For popular dates, venues and caterers are often booked several months ahead, photographers two to four months ahead, and decorators, bands and make-up artists one to three months ahead. Tailors and alteration services may be booked weeks ahead. Booking early gives more choice and better rates, especially for peak muhurat dates.

### How can a small caterer prepare for wedding season?
Confirm extra cooks and helpers for each booked date, agree ingredient prices with suppliers in advance, and send every client a written summary of the menu, guest count, price and advance. Plan transport and equipment for each venue, keep backup gas and power, and ask satisfied clients for reviews after each event.

### How should wedding vendors take advance payments safely?
Take advances by UPI or bank transfer and issue a receipt with the booking details. Confirm each transfer in your bank app before you release a date, and never approve a UPI request to receive money, because receiving never needs your PIN. For large amounts, a written agreement with cancellation terms protects both sides.

### Why do wedding dates depend on muhurat?
Many Hindu families choose wedding dates according to auspicious times, or muhurat, set out in the panchang. Weddings usually pause during the Chaturmas period and resume after Dev Uthani Ekadashi. Dates vary by region and tradition, so pandits and local calendars give different lists. This is why demand clusters around certain dates.

### Is Delhi the biggest wedding market in India?
In CAIT's 2025 estimate, Delhi was expected to be the largest single market, with about 4.8 lakh weddings generating nearly ₹1.8 lakh crore of business. These figures are trade estimates based on surveys rather than official counts, and CAIT revises them each season. Check its latest release for 2026 numbers.

### How can wedding vendors get more bookings online?
Keep an up-to-date listing with real photos, starting prices or packages, your service area and a working phone and WhatsApp number. Reply quickly to enquiries, especially in the evenings, and ask happy clients for reviews. Families often compare several vendors before calling, so a complete, verified listing helps you make the shortlist.
