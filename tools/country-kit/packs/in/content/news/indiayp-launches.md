**New Delhi, 2 November 2026.** IndiaYP (indiayp.com), a new business directory built for India, is now live. It helps people find, compare and contact businesses near them, and gives shops, professionals and companies a free, trustworthy place to be found online.

## Built around how India is organised

Most people don't search for "a plumber in India". They search for a plumber in Andheri, a dentist in Koramangala or a property dealer in Lajpat Nagar. IndiaYP is organised the same way:

- **{{REGIONS}} states and union territories**, from Maharashtra, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu to Delhi, Chandigarh and Ladakh.
- **{{CITIES}} cities and towns**, each with its own page.
- **{{LOCALITIES}} localities** in the big cities, from Bandra and Indiranagar to Connaught Place, T. Nagar, Salt Lake and Banjara Hills.

Every business is listed under a state or union territory, a city and a locality, so a search for a neighbourhood shows the businesses that are actually there. Place names are also shown in Hindi.

## Categories written for India

The directory has more than {{CATEGORIES}} categories in {{SECTORS}} sectors, written in the words Indians use: CA and GST services, packers and movers, coaching classes for JEE and NEET, AC repair, tiffin services, PG and hostels, wedding planners and banquet halls, tailors, mobile repair, property dealers, and many more. Every main category opens into sub-categories, so customers land on exactly the right list.

## What customers get

- **Search by category and area**, then filter by state, city and locality.
- **Call, WhatsApp or get directions** straight from a listing. Indian numbers are shown in a clear +91 format.
- **Request a quote once** and have it sent to up to five matching businesses.
- **Verified badges** that show which businesses have proved who they are.
- **Reviews and ratings** from other customers.

## What businesses get

- **A free listing.** Any business can add its profile at no cost: name, description, categories, location, phone, WhatsApp, website, working hours and logo.
- **Verification.** Businesses can upload documents such as a GSTIN certificate, Udyam registration, Shop & Establishment licence, MCA certificate of incorporation or professional registration (ICAI, Bar Council, medical council) to earn a verified badge.
- **Enquiries and leads** in the owner dashboard, including quote requests from customers.
- **Claim an existing listing** if your business is already in the directory.
- **Pages that work on any phone**, which is how most customers in India search.

## Built for search engines

Each state, city and locality has its own page, and every listing has a clean, shareable address. The site's map for search engines updates automatically whenever a business, city or article is added, so new listings can be found on Google and Bing quickly.

## How to get listed

Adding a business takes a few minutes: create a free account, choose your main category and sub-category, pick your state, city and locality, and add your contact details. Listings are reviewed before they go live, to keep the directory accurate.

Visit **indiayp.com** to search, or choose **List your business** to add yours for free.

## About IndiaYP

IndiaYP is an Indian business directory. Its aim is simple: make it easy to find a trustworthy local business anywhere in the country, and easy for every business to be found. Contact: info@indiayp.com.
