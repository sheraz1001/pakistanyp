**Metro expansion India** reached another milestone this month. On 3 November 2026, Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated Pune Metro Line 3, a 23.3 km corridor between Hinjewadi and Shivajinagar, by video link, with passenger services starting the next day. The country's operational metro network, which stood at about 248 km in five cities in 2014, was put at more than 1,185 km across 26 cities by the Union government in October 2026, according to news reports.

For local businesses, a new line changes where people walk, wait and shop. Shops near stations often gain footfall, rents near busy stations tend to rise, and years of construction can hurt trade before the benefits arrive. This explainer covers the lines that opened in 2025 and 2026 and what to do if a metro is coming to your street.

## Metro expansion India: the big picture in 2026

Metro rail is now part of daily life in many Indian cities, not just Delhi. Official and reported figures show steady growth:

:::bars India's operational metro network (km, as reported)
- 2014 | 248 | about 248 km
- January 2025 | 1000 | crossed 1,000 km
- March 2026 | 1143 | about 1,143 km
- October 2026 | 1185 | 1,185+ km
:::

Reports in 2026 described India's network as the third-largest operational metro system in the world, with China far ahead. Delhi Metro remains the largest in India. City counts differ between sources because of how regional rail and newly opened lines are counted, so check the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs for official figures.

### Pune Line 3: a new model

Pune's Line 3 was built under the Centre's 2017 metro policy as a public-private partnership, the first project of its kind under that policy, according to reports at its opening. The line serves the Hinjewadi IT parks, where traffic congestion has long been a complaint. Reports put the project cost at about ₹8,300 crore.

### Mumbai's Aqua Line

The Prime Minister inaugurated the final phase of Mumbai Metro Line 3 on 8 October 2025, and the entire 33.5 km underground corridor opened the next day, carrying more than 1.5 lakh passengers on its first full day, according to reports. It links south Mumbai's business districts with BKC, the airport area and Aarey.

## What a new metro line means for local businesses

### More walk-in customers near stations

Station exits create new walking routes. Cafés, [street food and quick bites](/categories/street-food-quick-bites), pharmacies, mobile shops and print-and-copy centres within a few minutes' walk often see more customers once a line settles in. Businesses facing away from exits, or across busy roads without crossings, may see little change.

### Rents and property values

Rents for shops and flats near busy stations generally rise once a line opens, and property near planned stations often rises in anticipation. If your lease is due, negotiate early. Tenants and young professionals moving near new lines look for [rentals and PGs](/categories/rentals-pg); the area's [property dealers and real estate services](/categories/real-estate-services) are usually the first to notice demand.

### New services for commuters

Last-mile transport, bike parking, tiffin services and coaching centres near stations all benefit. [Transport services](/categories/transport-services) such as e-rickshaws and shared cabs often regroup around exits.

> [!TIP]
> Add the station name to your business listing and Google profile, for example "2 minutes from Shivajinagar metro". Many people now search for a service "near" a metro station rather than by street name.

## The hard part: years of construction

Before a line opens, there are usually years of barricades, diversions and dust. Businesses on construction routes often report lower sales. If your street is on a planned corridor:

- Keep **clear, visible signage** above barricades; [signage and outdoor media](/categories/signage-outdoor-media) firms can help.
- Offer **home delivery or phone orders** so regulars can still buy; our explainer on [how kiranas are adapting to quick commerce](/news/quick-commerce-and-kirana-stores) has practical ideas.
- Tell customers where to park and which entrance is open.
- Ask the metro corporation's public grievance cell about access arrangements for shops.

### How many km of metro does India have in 2026?
According to news reports of an October 2026 government statement, India's operational metro network was more than 1,185 km across 26 cities, up from about 248 km in five cities in 2014. Figures differ slightly between sources because of how regional rail and new sections are counted. Check the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs for official numbers.

### When did Pune Metro Line 3 open?
Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated Pune Metro Line 3 by video link on 3 November 2026, and passenger services were scheduled to start on 4 November. The 23.3 km line connects the Hinjewadi IT area with Shivajinagar. Reports describe it as the first metro project built under the Centre's 2017 public-private partnership policy.

### Is Mumbai Metro Line 3 fully open?
Yes. The Prime Minister inaugurated the final phase on 8 October 2025, and the full 33.5 km underground corridor between Cuffe Parade and Aarey JVLR opened on 9 October 2025. It carried more than 1.5 lakh passengers on its first day of full operation, according to reports. It is Mumbai's first fully underground metro line.

### Which is the largest metro network in India?
Delhi Metro is the largest metro network in India, with more than 400 km of operational lines according to reports in 2026. It continues to expand under Phase 4, including a Magenta Line section between Deepali Chowk and Majlis Park that opened on 8 March 2026. Mumbai and Bengaluru have the next largest networks.

### Does a metro station increase shop rents?
Usually, yes. Rents for shops and flats close to busy stations tend to rise after a line opens, and property near planned stations often rises in anticipation. The effect varies with the station's ridership, the exits and the local road layout. Negotiate lease renewals early and compare similar properties near existing stations.

### How do metro projects affect businesses during construction?
Construction often brings barricades, road diversions, dust and reduced parking, and many shops on the route report lower sales for a period. Clear signage, home delivery, phone orders and directions for customers can reduce the impact. Contact the metro corporation's grievance cell if access to your shop is blocked.

### What is India's rank among world metro networks?
Reports in 2026 described India's metro network as the third largest in the world by operational length, with China far ahead. Rankings depend on how lines are counted, including whether suburban and regional rail are included. Check government statements for the official comparison and current length.

### Which cities opened new metro lines in 2026?
Verified openings in 2026 include a Delhi Metro Magenta Line section on 8 March, Indore Metro's Yellow Line extension on 5 September and Pune Metro Line 3 on 3 November. Other cities added stations and extensions during the year. Check your city's metro corporation for the latest openings and station lists.

### How can my business attract metro commuters?
Add the nearest station name and walking time to your listings, place signage visible from the exit, and keep opening hours that match the morning and evening peaks. Quick, take-away and convenience services do especially well. Offers for regular commuters and fast service help turn passers-by into repeat customers.

### What is transit-oriented development?
Transit-oriented development means planning denser homes, offices and shops within walking distance of metro and transit stations. India's central government issued a national transit-oriented development policy in 2017, and several cities allow higher building limits near stations. Check your city's development plan for rules that apply near your property.
