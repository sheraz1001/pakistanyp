**Quick commerce India** has grown from a big-city convenience into a national retail force. Platforms such as Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto deliver groceries and household goods in minutes from small warehouses called dark stores, and they are expanding into smaller cities. For neighbourhood kirana stores, that means new competition for the top-up shopping that used to walk through their door.

The short answer to "are kiranas finished?" is no. Traditional stores still sell most of India's groceries by industry estimates, and many are adapting with home delivery, WhatsApp ordering, credit for regular customers and listings on open networks. This explainer sets out what has changed in 2025 and 2026, and what is working for neighbourhood shops.

:::takeaways
- Blinkit alone had 2,027 stores at the end of December 2025 and now targets more than 3,500 by March 2027, according to parent company Eternal.
- After the Labour Minister intervened, Blinkit dropped its "10-minute delivery" claim on about 13 January 2026; other platforms reportedly followed.
- Distributors' body AICPDF claimed in October 2024 that around 2 lakh kiranas had closed because of quick commerce; the figure is disputed.
- India's labour codes, in force from 21 November 2025, bring gig and platform workers under social security provisions.
- Kiranas compete best on trust, credit, range for local tastes and fast delivery within a few streets.
:::

## How fast quick commerce India is growing

The clearest numbers come from listed companies. Eternal, Blinkit's parent, reported **2,027 Blinkit stores** at the end of December 2025 after adding 211 in that quarter. With its results for the July–September 2026 quarter, Eternal said it expected to cross **3,500 stores by March 2027** and reach about **5,000 by March 2028**, as it moves into smaller cities.

Market-size estimates vary widely because research firms measure different things, from gross order value to net revenue. Treat any single figure with caution. What is not in doubt is the direction: more dark stores, in more cities, carrying more products, from electronics to medicines.

## What changed in 2026: the end of "10 minutes"

On or around **13 January 2026**, Blinkit removed its "10-minute delivery" branding after Union Labour Minister Mansukh Mandaviya met quick commerce platforms over delivery workers' safety. The meeting followed protests by gig workers, including a strike on New Year's Eve 2025. Reports said Zepto and others also dropped or began dropping similar claims.

Deliveries are still fast; platforms simply stopped promising a fixed number of minutes. For kiranas, the change matters less than the trend behind it: authorities are paying closer attention to how platforms treat workers and suppliers.

### Gig workers and the labour codes

The government brought India's four labour codes into force on **21 November 2025**. The Code on Social Security, 2020 recognises gig and platform workers and provides for social security schemes funded partly by contributions from aggregators. Detailed schemes and rules continue to be notified, so check the Ministry of Labour and Employment's website for what applies now.

## The kirana side: closures, claims and what the data shows

In October 2024, the All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation (AICPDF) said about 2 lakh kirana stores had closed in the previous year, and blamed quick commerce. It said 45% of closures were in metros, 30% in tier-one cities and 25% in smaller cities. Platforms and some analysts disputed the figure, and no official count exists.

What shop owners report is more nuanced. Top-up purchases such as milk, bread, snacks and cold drinks are most at risk, especially in apartment-heavy parts of cities like [Gurugram](/cities/gurugram) and [Bengaluru](/cities/bengaluru). Monthly stock-up, loose staples, credit purchases and local brands remain strengths for neighbourhood shops.

| Factor | Quick commerce platforms | Neighbourhood kirana |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | Very fast in covered areas | Fast within a few streets; instant for walk-ins |
| Range | Wide, branded, standard pack sizes | Local brands, loose staples, regional products |
| Price | Discounts, plus delivery and platform fees | Fewer discounts, no delivery fee for regulars |
| Credit | Rare | Monthly khata (credit book) for known customers |
| Trust | App ratings and refunds | Personal relationship with the owner |

## How kirana stores are adapting

Shop owners who are holding their ground tend to do several of these things at once.

### Take orders on WhatsApp and the phone

A WhatsApp number on the shop board, a simple price list and a delivery boy on a bicycle or scooter can match the platforms within a short radius. Our guide on [how to grow a local business online](/blogs/grow-local-business-online-india) covers listings, Google and WhatsApp in detail.

### Join an open network

Through the government-backed Open Network for Digital Commerce, a kirana can appear on several buyer apps via one seller app. A November 2026 report said a pilot called DigiDukaan had connected more than 21,000 kirana stores with over 450 brands in three cities. Read our explainer on [how small sellers can use ONDC](/news/ondc-explained-small-sellers).

### Stock what the apps do not

Regional snacks, festival items, loose grains, [sweets and snacks](/categories/sweets-snacks) from local makers and small pack sizes give customers a reason to visit. Pharmacies can do the same with advice and repeat prescriptions; customers search for [pharmacies and medical supplies](/categories/pharmacies-medical-supplies) nearby.

### Make payment easy

Accept UPI and cards, and keep the khata for trusted customers. Our explainer on [UPI's new rules for shopkeepers](/news/upi-new-features-shopkeepers) covers what NPCI's merchant fee plan means for small stores.

> [!TIP]
> Track your ten best-selling items each week. If sales of milk, bread or cold drinks fall, that is where quick commerce is hurting you; push delivery offers on exactly those items.

## Data, prices and the rules to watch

As you build a customer list, remember that India's data protection rules will soon apply to small businesses. Collect phone numbers with consent and keep them secure; our guide to the [DPDP Rules for small businesses](/news/dpdp-rules-what-small-businesses-must-do) explains the May 2027 deadline.

Prices also shifted after the [new GST rates](/news/gst-rate-changes-two-slab-explained) of September 2025, when many packaged foods and personal care items moved to 5%. Check that your billing matches the current rates, as customers compare prices with apps.

> [!NOTE]
> Distributors and trade bodies have raised concerns with regulators about deep discounting by platforms. No final regulatory action on pricing had been announced at the time of writing. Follow updates from the Competition Commission of India and the Department of Consumer Affairs.

:::checklist
- Display a WhatsApp number and delivery radius at the shop and online
- Keep a weekly price list for your top 30 items
- Offer credit only to known customers, with a clear limit
- Stock local and regional products that apps rarely carry
- Accept UPI and confirm credits before handing over goods
- Ask customers before adding them to broadcast lists
- Keep your online listing's hours and photos up to date
:::

## Opportunities for other local businesses

Quick commerce also creates work. Dark stores need [warehousing and cold chain](/categories/warehousing-cold-chain) services, local delivery fleets and repair shops for scooters and e-bikes. Kiranas that deliver can partner with a local [courier and delivery service](/categories/courier-delivery) for peak hours.

### What is quick commerce in India?
Quick commerce is a form of online retail that delivers groceries and household products within minutes from small local warehouses called dark stores. Leading platforms include Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto. It began in large cities and is now expanding into smaller ones, with a widening range that includes electronics, beauty products and some medicines.

### Is quick commerce killing kirana stores?
It is putting pressure on some, especially for top-up items like milk and snacks in apartment-heavy city areas. In 2024, the distributors' body AICPDF claimed about 2 lakh kiranas had closed, but the figure is disputed and there is no official count. Many kiranas are adapting with delivery, WhatsApp orders and credit for regular customers.

### How many dark stores does Blinkit have?

### How can a kirana store compete with quick commerce?
Focus on what apps cannot easily copy: personal trust, monthly credit for known customers, local and loose products, and delivery within a few streets. Publish a WhatsApp number, share a weekly price list, accept UPI and keep your online listing updated. Joining an open network seller app can also bring online orders.

### Can kirana stores sell on ONDC?
Yes. Kirana stores can join the Open Network for Digital Commerce through an approved seller app and appear on several connected buyer apps. You will typically need PAN, bank details, FSSAI registration and a way to deliver. A November 2026 report said a pilot had connected more than 21,000 kiranas with brands in three cities.

### Do gig workers get social security in India now?
The Code on Social Security, 2020, one of four labour codes brought into force on 21 November 2025, recognises gig and platform workers and provides for social security schemes funded partly by aggregators. Detailed schemes and contribution rules continue to be notified, so check the Ministry of Labour and Employment for current benefits.

### Are quick commerce prices cheaper than kirana prices?
Not always. Platforms offer discounts on many items, but delivery, handling and platform fees can add to the bill, especially on small orders. Kiranas may not discount as much but usually charge no delivery fee to regulars. Customers who compare the final amount often find the difference smaller than headline prices suggest.

### Which cities have quick commerce?
Quick commerce started in metros such as Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Bengaluru and has expanded to many tier-two and tier-three cities. Blinkit has said its expansion to 3,500 stores by March 2027 includes smaller cities. Availability depends on whether a dark store serves your pin code, which each app shows.

### What should a kirana owner do about customer data?
If you keep customer phone numbers and addresses for delivery or WhatsApp lists, collect them with consent and store them securely. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules phase in, with most duties expected around May 2027. Delete old lists you no longer need and let customers opt out of messages easily.
