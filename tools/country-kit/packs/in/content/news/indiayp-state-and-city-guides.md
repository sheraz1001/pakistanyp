**IndiaYP city guides** are now live. State and city pages on the directory carry a written guide that explains how business works in that place: the key facts, the main industries, the markets and business districts where traders cluster, and answers to common questions. Guides cover **25 cities** and **21 states and union territories**, from Mumbai and Bengaluru to Guwahati, Ladakh and Lakshadweep.

The guides answer a simple problem. A list of businesses tells you who is there; it does not tell you where a city's jewellers, cloth wholesalers or engineering units are concentrated. Each guide appears on the place page alongside the business listings, so you can understand an area and then contact the businesses in it.

:::takeaways
- Written guides now appear on 25 city pages and 21 state and union territory pages.
- Each guide has a key facts panel, sections on the economy and main business areas, and a short FAQ.
- Across all 46 guides there are 182 frequently asked questions, each shown on the page.
- Guides sit alongside the listings, so you can read about an area and then call or WhatsApp businesses there.

## What the IndiaYP city guides include

Every guide follows the same structure, so you know where to look whichever city you open.

| Section | What you will find | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | Where the place is, its civic body and what it is known for | Mumbai's Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation |
| Key facts | A panel with population (2011 census), telephone code, status, airport or industrial agency | Mumbai's STD code, 022 |
| Economy and main industries | The sectors that drive local business | Rajasthan's zinc mining, marble and textiles |
| Main business areas and markets | Wholesale bazaars, industrial estates and business districts | Zaveri Bazaar and Lamington Road in Mumbai |
| Topic sections | One or two sections specific to the place | The tea trade in Guwahati |
| Finding businesses | What people commonly search for there | Marble suppliers and hotels in Rajasthan |
| FAQs | Short answers to common questions | "What is the STD code for Mumbai?" |

The key facts panel is shown separately from the main text, so the STD code, population or capital is easy to find at a glance. Every city guide lists the city's telephone STD code.

## What you can learn from a guide

The guides are written for people who need to find suppliers, services or customers in a place they may not know well. A few examples of what they cover:

- **Surat:** India's main diamond cutting and polishing centre, and the Surat Diamond Bourse at Khajod.
- **Ludhiana:** woollen hosiery and knitwear, and a large share of India's bicycle manufacturing.
- **Kanpur:** the leather tanning cluster at Jajmau on the Ganga.
- **Guwahati:** the Fancy Bazaar wholesale district and the Guwahati Tea Auction Centre.
- **Jaipur:** coloured gemstones and jewellery in the Walled City bazaars, and the export park at Sitapura.
- **Rajasthan:** Makrana marble, the Kishangarh stone-processing centre and RIICO industrial areas such as Neemrana.
- **Ladakh:** a union territory formed in 2019, with an economy shaped by tourism and seasonal access.

Facts such as population figures are taken from the 2011 census, the most recent full census, and each guide says so where it gives a figure.

## How to use a guide to find the right business

:::steps Using a state or city guide
- Open the place | Choose a state or city from the states or cities pages.
- Read the key facts | Check the telephone code, capital or main industries in the panel.
- Find the right area | Use the markets section to see where a trade is concentrated.
- Pick a category | Open the category and filter by locality.
- Contact and compare | Call, WhatsApp or request quotes from several businesses.
:::

> [!TIP]
> Wholesale trades often cluster in one market. If a guide says a city's cloth or electronics trade is centred on a particular bazaar, search that locality first. You will usually find more choice and better prices than in scattered shops.

:::faq
### What are IndiaYP city guides?
They are written guides on IndiaYP's state and city pages that explain how business works in each place. Each guide includes a key facts panel, sections on the economy and main industries, the markets and business districts where trades are concentrated, what people commonly search for, and a short set of frequently asked questions about the place.

### Which cities have guides on IndiaYP?
Guides are live for 25 cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kanpur, Noida, Gurugram, Chandigarh, Ludhiana, Indore, Bhopal, Nagpur, Patna, Guwahati, Kochi, Coimbatore and Visakhapatnam. Each guide appears on that city's page, next to the city's business listings and categories.

### Which states and union territories have guides?
Guides cover 21 states and union territories, including Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal and the north-eastern states of Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim and Tripura. Union territories include Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and Lakshadweep. Other states and cities still have their own pages with listings.

### Where do the facts in the guides come from?
The guides are written from public sources. Population figures come from the 2011 census, the most recent full census, and each guide labels them that way. Other facts, such as telephone codes, civic bodies, industrial areas and markets, are drawn from official and published sources. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact page.

### What is in the key facts panel?
The key facts panel lists the most useful reference information for a place at a glance. For cities, it usually includes the state, population from the 2011 census and the telephone STD code, plus items such as the civic body, airport or industrial estates. For states, it typically shows status, capital, area and population.

### Do the guides show businesses as well?
Yes. The guide sits on the same page as the state's or city's businesses, categories and localities. You can read about an area, then open a category, filter by locality and call, WhatsApp or request quotes from the businesses listed there. The guide adds context but does not replace the listings.

### Are the guides free to read?
Yes. All state and city guides are free to read, like the rest of the directory. You can browse states and cities, read the guides and contact businesses at no cost. Businesses can also list themselves free, with optional paid upgrades for more visibility.

### How do I get my business shown on a city page?
Add your business free from the list your business page, choosing the correct state, city, locality and categories. Once your listing is live, it appears with other businesses in that place. You can then apply for a verified badge by confirming your phone number and uploading registration documents such as a GSTIN or Udyam certificate.

### Does every guide have FAQs?
Yes. Every guide ends with a short set of frequently asked questions, usually three or four, covering things like telephone codes, main markets and industrial areas. Across the 46 guides there are 182 questions in all. The questions and answers are shown on the page and are also marked up so that search engines can understand them.

### Can I suggest a correction to a guide?
Yes. If you find a fact that is out of date or wrong, such as a market that has moved or a changed telephone code, send the details and a source through the contact page. Guides can be updated by the IndiaYP team where the new information can be checked.
