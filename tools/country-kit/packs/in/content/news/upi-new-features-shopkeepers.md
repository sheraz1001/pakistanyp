The **UPI new rules** that matter most to a shopkeeper in late 2026 come from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), which runs UPI. Over the past 15 months NPCI has capped background API calls such as balance checks, stopped person-to-person "collect" requests, raised limits for some merchant categories, allowed fingerprint or face approval as an alternative to the UPI PIN, and published a merchant discount rate (MDR) plan for larger payments.

The short answer for most small shops: accepting UPI is still free. NPCI's MDR plan exempts payments up to ₹2,000 and small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month on QR codes. The plan was due to start on 15 October 2026 but NPCI deferred it until after Diwali, and at the time of writing no new date had been notified.

:::takeaways
- UPI remains free for customers, and merchant payments up to ₹2,000 stay free under NPCI's MDR plan.
- The 0.4% MDR on specified merchant payments above ₹2,000 (capped at ₹300) has been deferred beyond Diwali 2026; watch for NPCI's new date.
- Since 1 October 2025, UPI apps no longer process person-to-person collect requests, a common fraud route.
- Higher per-transaction limits for categories such as insurance, capital markets and jewellery took effect on 15 September 2025.
- Balance-check and autopay API limits from August 2025 mainly affect apps, not your counter, but explain slow status updates.
:::

NPCI data for September 2026 showed 24.07 billion UPI transactions worth ₹29.37 lakh crore in one month, as widely reported. That scale is why NPCI has tightened how apps use the system and how its costs are paid for.

## The MDR plan: who would pay and who stays exempt

In September 2026 NPCI published FAQs on a revised UPI merchant discount rate framework for person-to-merchant (P2M) payments. MDR is the fee a merchant's bank charges for accepting a payment; UPI merchant payments have carried zero MDR since 2020.

As reported from NPCI's FAQs, the framework would work like this:

- **Payments up to ₹2,000:** no MDR. NPCI said this covers more than 95% of P2M transactions.
- **Specified P2M payments above ₹2,000:** 0.4% of the amount, capped at ₹300 per transaction.
- **Some thin-margin sectors**, reportedly including railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and agricultural inputs: a flat ₹5 per transaction, according to news reports.
- **Small merchants in the P2PM category** (a "person-to-person-merchant" category for small, often unregistered sellers who accept UPI on a personal-style QR) receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month: no MDR at all, and no GST registration needed to qualify.
- **Customers:** no charge. Apps may not add platform fees, and merchants may not pass the MDR on to buyers.

> [!NOTE]
> On the start date: the framework was announced to take effect on 15 October 2026. In mid-October NPCI said implementation would happen after Diwali, with the date to be notified separately. Until NPCI publishes that date, nothing has changed at your counter. Check announcements at https://www.npci.org.in or ask your acquiring bank.

### Will a kirana store pay MDR?

Most will not. A typical [neighbourhood grocery or daily-needs store](/categories/daily-needs) takes many payments well below ₹2,000, and a shop with UPI receipts under ₹1 lakh a month in the P2PM category is fully exempt. The businesses likely to feel it are those with frequent larger bills: [electronics and appliance dealers](/categories/electronics-appliances), [jewellers](/categories/jewellery-accessories), furniture stores and some clinics.

## Higher UPI limits for big-ticket merchant payments

From 15 September 2025, NPCI raised UPI limits for selected merchant categories, while leaving the general person-to-person limit at ₹1 lakh. The changes, as widely reported, included:

- **Capital markets and insurance:** up to ₹5 lakh per transaction and ₹10 lakh a day.
- **Jewellery:** up to ₹2 lakh per transaction and ₹6 lakh a day, according to most reports.
- **Credit card bill payments:** up to ₹5 lakh per transaction, ₹6 lakh a day.
- Travel, government e-Marketplace payments and some other categories also moved up.

:::bars Reported UPI daily limits after 15 September 2025 (₹ lakh per day)
- Capital markets | 10 | ₹10 lakh
- Insurance | 10 | ₹10 lakh
- Jewellery | 6 | ₹6 lakh
- Credit card bills | 6 | ₹6 lakh
- Person to person | 1 | ₹1 lakh
:::

> [!TIP]
> If a customer's large payment fails at your jewellery or electronics counter, the cause is often the customer's own bank limit or your merchant category, not UPI itself. Ask your acquiring bank to confirm your category code and per-transaction ceiling.

## End of P2P collect requests: a fraud route closed

Fraudsters used fake "collect requests" to trick people into paying when they thought they were receiving money. NPCI's circular of 29 July 2025 told banks and apps to stop initiating, routing or processing person-to-person collect requests from **1 October 2025**.

Merchant collect requests were not affected. For a shop, the lesson is simple: if anyone asks you to "approve a request to receive money", it is a scam. Receiving money never needs your PIN.

## Biometric approval and other new UPI features

In October 2025, UPI apps began offering fingerprint or face authentication for payments as an option alongside the UPI PIN, within limits set by NPCI. The PIN remains available, and customers choose which method to use.

Behind the scenes, NPCI's 2025 API guidelines also changed how apps behave:

- **Balance checks:** capped at 50 a day per app per customer, as reported.
- **Autopay mandates:** executed outside peak hours (reported as 10 am to 1 pm and 5 pm to 9:30 pm), with limited retries.
- **Status checks:** apps must wait about 90 seconds after a payment before checking its status, and check only a few times.

This is why a "pending" payment can take a minute or two to resolve.

## What shopkeepers should do now

Nothing changes today, but a few steps will help when the MDR date arrives.

:::checklist
- Check whether your QR is a P2PM (small merchant) or full P2M merchant QR with your bank or app
- Note your average bill size and how many UPI payments exceed ₹2,000 each month
- Keep the soundbox or SMS alert switched on, and never release goods on a "pending" status
- Train staff that receiving money never needs a UPI PIN
- Confirm your merchant category code if you sell jewellery, insurance or other high-value items
- Watch NPCI and your bank for the notified MDR start date
:::

### Should you get GST registration to use UPI?

No. NPCI's FAQs, as reported, say small merchants do not need GST registration to stay in the zero-MDR P2PM category. GST registration depends on your turnover and type of supply, not on UPI. ## Final word

The UPI new rules of 2025 and 2026 are about safety and sustainability: fewer background calls, no P2P collect fraud, higher limits and a fee only for larger merchant payments. For a small shop, UPI is still free. Check your QR type, keep an eye on NPCI's notified date for MDR, and make sure customers can find your shop online by [adding your business to IndiaYP](/add-business).

:::faq
### Is UPI still free for customers in 2026?
Yes. Under NPCI's MDR framework, as reported from its September 2026 FAQs, customers pay nothing extra for UPI payments, and UPI apps are not allowed to charge platform fees. Person-to-person transfers carry no MDR either. Any fee under the plan falls on the merchant, who should not pass it on.

### What is the new UPI MDR for merchants?
NPCI's framework sets a 0.4% merchant discount rate on specified person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000, with a cap of ₹300 per transaction. Reports say some essential sectors would pay a flat ₹5 instead. Payments up to ₹2,000 remain free. The plan was due on 15 October 2026 but NPCI deferred it beyond Diwali, with a new date to be notified.

### When will UPI merchant charges start?
At the time of writing, there is no confirmed date. NPCI had announced 15 October 2026, then said in October that implementation would take place after Diwali and that the date would be notified separately. Until NPCI publishes that date, merchants pay no MDR on UPI. Check npci.org.in or ask your bank.

### Do small shopkeepers have to pay MDR on UPI?
Most do not. Merchants in the small-merchant (P2PM) category who receive up to ₹1 lakh a month through UPI QR codes stay at zero MDR on all UPI payments, according to NPCI's FAQs as reported. Payments up to ₹2,000 are also free for every merchant.

### Do I need GST registration to accept UPI?
No. UPI acceptance does not depend on GST registration. NPCI's FAQs, as reported, also say small merchants do not need GST registration to qualify for zero MDR in the P2PM category. Whether you need GST depends on your turnover, what you sell and whether you sell across states, so check with a chartered accountant.

### Why did UPI stop collect requests?
Fraudsters used person-to-person collect requests to trick people into approving payments while believing they were receiving money. NPCI's circular dated 29 July 2025 told banks and apps to stop processing P2P collect requests from 1 October 2025. Merchant collect requests continue. Receiving money never needs your PIN.

### How many times can I check my UPI balance in a day?
Under NPCI's API guidelines that took effect in August 2025, balance enquiries are capped at 50 per day per customer on each UPI app, according to widely reported figures. If you use two apps, each has its own limit. The cap reduces load and helps prevent outages.

### What is the UPI limit for jewellery shops?
From 15 September 2025, NPCI raised limits for selected merchant categories. Most reports put jewellery at ₹2 lakh per transaction and ₹6 lakh per day, though one outlet reported a different per-transaction figure. Your shop must also carry the right merchant category code.

### Can customers pay with fingerprint instead of a UPI PIN?
Yes, on supported apps. From October 2025, UPI apps began offering fingerprint or face authentication as an alternative to the PIN, within NPCI's limits. The PIN remains available and customers can choose either method. Check your app for the current biometric limit.

### What should I do if a UPI payment shows pending?
Do not release goods on a pending status. NPCI's rules make apps wait about 90 seconds before checking a payment's status, so updates can take a short while. Wait for your soundbox, SMS or bank app to confirm the credit. If money is debited from the customer but not credited to you, it is usually reversed automatically.
