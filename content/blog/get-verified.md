Customers are more likely to call a business they trust. The PakistanYP verified badge is a simple signal that a business is genuine and that its listing is managed by the right people. This article explains which documents you can use and how to get approved quickly.

## Documents you can use

You only need one document that matches your business name. The more official it is, the faster the check.

- **NTN certificate.** Issued by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) to individuals and businesses registered for income tax. Most businesses can download it from FBR's online IRIS system.
- **Sales tax registration (STRN).** For businesses registered for sales tax.
- **SECP certificate of incorporation.** For private limited, single member and public companies registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan.
- **Chamber of Commerce membership certificate.** From your city's chamber of commerce and industry.
- **Trade or municipal licence.** Issued by your city or cantonment authority.
- **Food authority licence.** For restaurants, bakeries, caterers and food producers, from the provincial food authority.
- **Professional registration.** For example PMDC for doctors and dentists, PEC for engineers, or a Bar Council licence for lawyers.

## Tips for a quick approval

- **Match the names.** The name on the document should match the name on your listing. If they differ (for example a brand name versus the registered company name), mention both in your listing description.
- **Upload a clear copy.** A sharp photo or PDF of the whole document, with no parts cut off.
- **Confirm your phone number** from the dashboard. It is part of verification.
- **Keep it current.** Expired licences can't be used.

## What happens to your documents

Documents are used only to verify your business and are never shown on your public listing. You can see the status of each document in **My verification** in your dashboard.

## Is verification required?

No. Listing on PakistanYP is free and does not require verification. But the badge helps customers choose you over unverified competitors, and on province pages customers can filter to show verified businesses only.

## Already verified elsewhere?

Even if you are registered with FBR or SECP, customers can't see that unless you show it. The badge does that for you in one glance.
