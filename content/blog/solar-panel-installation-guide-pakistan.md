The solar panel price in Pakistan moved within a wide band during 2026. Retail price lists put mainstream A-grade panels at roughly Rs 28 to Rs 32 per watt early in the year, and premium N-type Tier-1 panels at around Rs 42 to Rs 48 per watt by mid-year. A complete 10kW on-grid system, installed, typically costs somewhere around Rs 10 lakh to Rs 18 lakh depending on brands, structure and wiring. A hybrid system with batteries costs considerably more.

Panels are now only part of the bill. Inverters, batteries, mounting structures, cabling and installation quality decide how much you pay and how long the system lasts. NEPRA's 2026 move from net metering to net billing for new users has also changed the maths. This guide explains each piece, so you can compare quotes properly and choose a reliable installer.

:::takeaways
- Panel prices are quoted per watt; multiply by system size to estimate panel cost only.
- Inverter, structure, cabling and labour often cost as much as the panels or more.
- New grid-connected users now come under NEPRA's net billing rules, with a lower buyback rate for exported units.
- Size your system to daytime use and evening backup needs, not only to your total bill.
- Use a certified installer and get a written, itemised quote with warranty terms.
:::

## Solar panel price in Pakistan per watt

Most dealers quote panels in rupees per watt. A 585-watt panel at Rs 40 per watt therefore costs about Rs 23,400. Prices depend on brand, cell technology, wattage, whether the panel is bifacial and the warranty that comes with it.

:::bars Indicative panel prices in 2026 (PKR per watt, indicative)
- A-grade mono, early 2026 | 29 | Rs 28–30
- N-type A-grade, early 2026 | 32 | Rs 30–33
- N-type Tier-1, mid-2026 | 43 | Rs 42–45
- Premium back-contact, mid-2026 | 46 | Rs 43–48
:::

These figures come from dealer price lists published during 2026 and are indicative only. Prices move with the exchange rate, import costs and demand, and they can change weekly in peak season. The 2025-26 budget added a 10% sales tax on imported solar panels and parts from July 2025, although the exact scope of the tax was debated at the time.

> [!WARNING]
> Be careful with "A-grade" claims on unfamiliar brands. Ask to see the panel's datasheet, serial numbers and the importer's warranty card. Cheap panels with inflated wattage ratings are a common complaint.

## Types of solar system: on-grid, hybrid and off-grid

| System type | How it works | Best for | Main drawback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On-grid | Feeds your home and exports surplus to the grid; no batteries | Homes and shops with reliable grid supply and daytime use | No power during load shedding |
| Hybrid | Grid-connected with batteries for backup | Homes that need evening backup and face outages | Batteries add significant cost |
| Off-grid | Panels and batteries only, no grid link | Farms, tube wells and remote sites | Large battery bank needed |

Most urban homes choose on-grid or hybrid. Since net billing pays less for exported units, more buyers are now looking at hybrid systems with modest batteries to use more of their own power at night.

### Batteries: lithium vs tubular

Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries cost more upfront but last longer, charge faster and need no maintenance. Tubular lead-acid batteries are cheaper but heavier and wear out sooner. If you need backup, compare cost per usable kilowatt-hour over the expected life, not just the sticker price. [Power backup suppliers](/categories/power-backup) can explain options for your load.

## Complete solar system cost by size

Installed system prices vary even more than panel prices. For a 10kW on-grid system, published 2026 estimates from installers range from about Rs 9.5 lakh at the budget end to around Rs 18 lakh for premium brands and better structures. Hybrid systems of the same size, with batteries, can exceed Rs 25 lakh depending on battery capacity.

A typical quote should break down:

- panels (brand, model, wattage, quantity)
- inverter (brand, model, on-grid or hybrid, single or three phase)
- batteries, if any (type, capacity, warranty)
- mounting structure (galvanised steel or aluminium; elevated or flush)
- DC and AC cabling, breakers, surge protection and earthing
- labour, transport and commissioning
- net billing paperwork, if included

> [!TIP]
> Ask every installer to quote the same panel and inverter brands. Then you can compare the structure, wiring and service, which is where cheap quotes usually cut corners.

## How to size a solar system for your home

Start with your last 12 electricity bills. Note your highest and lowest monthly units, and when you use most power.

As a rough guide, each installed kilowatt produces somewhere around 120 to 150 units a month in much of Pakistan, depending on season, panel angle, dust and shading. This is an indicative range, so ask your installer for a site-specific estimate.

- A household using about 500 units a month might start with a 3kW to 5kW system.
- A family running two or three air conditioners in summer often looks at 8kW to 12kW.
- Shops, clinics and small factories need a load survey, not a rule of thumb.

Your sanctioned load also matters. Under the 2026 rules, a grid-connected system cannot exceed your sanctioned load, so you may need to apply for a load increase first. Our [cost of living guide](/blogs/cost-of-living-in-pakistan-city-comparison) explains how electricity slabs and fixed charges affect household bills.

## Net metering vs net billing in 2026

NEPRA notified the Prosumer Regulations, 2026 on 9 February 2026, replacing the 2015 net metering framework with **net billing** for new users. Under net billing, units you import from the grid are charged at your normal tariff, while units you export are bought at a much lower rate. Reports put the buyback rate for new users at around Rs 10 to Rs 11 per unit. New agreements run for five years instead of seven.

The treatment of existing net-metered users was contested. The Prime Minister directed the Power Division to seek a review, and NEPRA moved to protect existing contracts for their remaining term. Read our news update on [net metering policy in Pakistan](/news/solar-net-metering-pakistan-policy-update) and check the latest position on the [NEPRA website](https://www.nepra.org.pk) before you sign.

What this means for buyers: a system sized to export lots of surplus earns less than it used to. Size closer to your daytime use, and consider batteries if your evening load is high.

## How to choose a reliable solar installer

Grid-connected systems need an installer certified by the Private Power and Infrastructure Board (PPIB), which took over this work from the former Alternative Energy Development Board (AEDB). You can check certified installer lists on the [PPIB website](https://www.ppib.gov.pk).

:::checklist
- Ask for the installer's PPIB certificate number and check its validity
- Visit at least two systems they installed more than a year ago
- Get an itemised written quote with brands and models
- Confirm who handles net billing paperwork with your DISCO
- Ask about panel, inverter and workmanship warranties in writing
- Check who provides after-sales service and how quickly
- Agree a payment schedule linked to delivery and commissioning
:::

:::steps From quote to switch-on
- Load survey | The installer checks your bills, roof, shading and sanctioned load.
- Itemised quote | Compare at least three quotes using the same brands.
- Installation | Structure, panels, inverter and wiring are fitted, usually within days.
- Testing | The installer commissions the system and shows you the monitoring app.
- Grid connection | For on-grid systems, the DISCO processes the net billing application.
:::

## Financing a solar system

Several banks offer solar financing, and some installers offer instalment plans. Compare the total amount payable, not just the monthly instalment. Look for [loans and financing providers](/categories/loans-financing) in your city, and ask whether the loan covers batteries and installation.

## Final word: buy the system, not the panel

Watching the solar panel price in Pakistan is useful, but the cheapest panels rarely make the cheapest system. A good inverter, a solid structure, safe wiring and an installer who answers the phone after a year matter more over 20-plus years of use.

Your next step: collect your last 12 electricity bills and ask three certified installers for itemised quotes on the same brands.

### What is the solar panel price in Pakistan today?
Panel prices are quoted per watt and change often. During 2026, dealer price lists showed mainstream A-grade panels at roughly Rs 28 to Rs 32 per watt early in the year, and premium N-type Tier-1 panels at around Rs 42 to Rs 48 per watt by mid-year. Check current rates with two or three local dealers before buying.

### How much does a 10kW solar system cost in Pakistan?
Published 2026 estimates for a complete 10kW on-grid system range from about Rs 9.5 lakh to around Rs 18 lakh, depending on panel and inverter brands, structure and installation quality. A 10kW hybrid system with batteries can cost well over Rs 25 lakh. Always ask for an itemised quote so you can see what is included.

### How many solar panels do I need for a 5kW system?
Divide the system size by the panel wattage. With 585-watt panels, a 5kW system needs about nine panels, giving roughly 5.3kW. With smaller panels you need more. Your installer may slightly oversize the panel array relative to the inverter, which is common practice. Check that your roof has enough unshaded space.

### Is net metering still available in Pakistan?
New grid-connected solar users now come under NEPRA's Prosumer Regulations, 2026, which replaced net metering with net billing. Exported units are bought at a lower rate than you pay for imported units, reported at around Rs 10 to Rs 11 for new users. The treatment of existing users was contested, so check NEPRA's latest notifications.

### Which is better: on-grid or hybrid solar?
On-grid systems are cheaper because they have no batteries, but they shut down during load shedding. Hybrid systems add batteries, so you keep power during outages and use more of your own solar at night. With lower buyback rates under net billing, hybrid systems make more sense for homes with high evening use or frequent outages.

### How long do solar panels last?
Good-quality panels usually come with performance warranties of 25 years or more, promising they will still produce a stated percentage of their original output. Inverters typically last less and may need replacing during the panels' life. Lithium batteries last longer than lead-acid. Keep warranty cards and buy from importers with a local presence.

### Do I need a certified installer for solar in Pakistan?
For grid-connected systems that export power, yes. Applications are processed through your DISCO, and installers should be certified by the Private Power and Infrastructure Board, which took over from AEDB. Even for off-grid systems, a qualified installer reduces fire and safety risks. Ask for the certificate number and check it on the PPIB website.

### How much electricity does a 1kW solar system produce in Pakistan?
As an indicative guide, each installed kilowatt produces somewhere around 120 to 150 units a month in much of Pakistan. Output is higher in summer and lower in winter, and it drops with dust, shading and poor panel angles. Ask your installer for a site-specific estimate based on your location and roof.

### Can I install solar on a rented house?
You can, but only with the landlord's written permission, because panels and structures are fixed to the roof and grid connection is in the meter holder's name. Agree in writing who owns the system and what happens when you move out. Many tenants choose a small, easily removable system for this reason.

### How long does it take to recover the cost of a solar system?
Payback depends on system price, your tariff, how much solar you use yourself and the buyback rate for exported units. Higher grid tariffs shorten payback, while net billing's lower export rate lengthens it for systems that export a lot. Ask installers to show their payback calculation using your actual bills, and check their assumptions.
