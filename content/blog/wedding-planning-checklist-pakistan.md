Good wedding planning in Pakistan starts six to twelve months before the barat. Agree the number of functions and a total budget with both families first. Then book the venue and caterer, which fill up fastest in the winter wedding season. Bridal wear, photography, décor and beauty come next, followed by invitations, nikah arrangements and final confirmations in the last month.

Most stress comes from decisions made late or without a budget. This checklist walks through each stage: who to book and when, what to ask vendors, the legal paperwork, and the rules on timings and food that apply in parts of the country. It works whether you are planning a small nikah at home or three days of mehndi, barat and walima.

:::takeaways
- Fix the guest list and total budget before you speak to any vendor.
- Book venues and caterers first; good dates go months ahead in the winter season.
- Get every vendor quote in writing, with what is included, advance paid and cancellation terms.
- Register the nikah with the Union Council and get the NADRA computerised marriage certificate.

:::

## Step 1 of wedding planning in Pakistan: agree budget and guests

Before anyone visits a marquee, sit down with both families and agree three things:

1. **The functions**: dholki, mayun, mehndi, nikah, barat, walima, or a combined event.
2. **Who pays for what**: traditionally the bride's family hosts the barat and the groom's family the walima, but arrangements vary.
3. **The guest count per function**: this drives venue size and catering cost more than anything else.

Write the total budget down and keep a contingency of around 10% for last-minute costs. The split below is an example, not a rule; your priorities may be very different.

:::bars Example budget split for a mid-size wedding (% of total, indicative)
- Venue and catering | 40 | 40%
- Bridal and groom wear | 15 | 15%
- Jewellery | 15 | 15%
- Photography and video | 8 | 8%
- Décor and lighting | 8 | 8%
- Beauty and makeup | 5 | 5%
- Invitations, transport, other | 9 | 9%
:::

> [!TIP]
> Cut guests before you cut quality. Reducing the guest list by 100 usually saves more than switching to a cheaper photographer or skipping a function decoration.

## Step 2: Book the venue and caterer first

Winter is peak wedding season in much of Pakistan, and popular halls and marquees are booked months in advance. Shortlist [wedding venues](/categories/venues) by location, capacity, parking and power backup, then visit each at the time of day your function will be held.

Many halls include catering; others let you bring an outside caterer. Compare [event caterers](/categories/catering-food-for-events) on menu, per-head price, food tasting, serving staff and what happens to leftover food.

### Know the rules on timing and food

In Punjab, the Punjab Marriage Functions Act, 2016 limits the main course to one dish and requires venues to end functions by 10pm. The provincial government has run repeated enforcement drives, including late 2025 and March 2026, with halls sealed and fines imposed. The law also restricts lavish displays such as fireworks and showing dowry. Other provinces and districts set their own rules on timings, so ask your venue what applies locally.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not let a venue or caterer promise to "manage" a rule violation. When halls are sealed or fined during a function, it is the families who lose the event. Plan your menu and timings within the law.

## Step 3: Book your key wedding vendors

Once the venue and dates are fixed, book the vendors who have limited availability.

| Vendor | When to book | What to confirm in writing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photographer and videographer | 4–6 months ahead | Number of hours, team size, album, edited video, delivery date |
| Bridal makeup artist | 3–6 months ahead | Trial session, products used, travel, timing on the day |
| Bridal and groom wear designer | 4–8 months ahead | Fittings, delivery date, alterations, final price |
| Decorator | 2–4 months ahead | Stage design, flowers, lighting, set-up and removal times |
| Jeweller | 3–6 months ahead | Weight, purity, making charges, buy-back policy |
| Music and entertainment | 1–3 months ahead | Sound levels, timings, equipment, permissions |

### Questions to ask every vendor

:::checklist
- What exactly is included in the price, and what costs extra?
- How much advance do you need, and when is the balance due?
- What is your cancellation and date-change policy?
- Who will actually do the work on the day, and how many staff will come?
- Can I see work from a recent wedding similar in size to ours?
- What is the delivery date for photos, videos, outfits or jewellery?
- Will you sign a simple written agreement or receipt?
:::

## Step 4: Follow a month-by-month wedding timeline

:::steps Wedding planning timeline
- 9–12 months before | Agree the budget, guest list and dates; book the venue and caterer.
- 6–8 months before | Book the photographer, designer, makeup artist and decorator.
- 3–5 months before | Finalise outfits, buy jewellery, plan the menu and order invitations.
- 2 months before | Send invitations, book transport and arrange guest accommodation.
- 1 month before | Confirm every vendor, do the makeup trial and final fittings.
- Wedding week | Reconfirm timings, prepare payments and assign a family point person.
:::

For guests travelling from other cities, look at nearby [hotels and guest houses](/categories/hotels-stay) early, especially in December and around public holidays.

## Step 5: Handle the nikah and the legal paperwork

Under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961, a nikah must be registered. In practice:

1. The **nikah khwan** or nikah registrar completes the nikah nama, with the bride's and groom's details, the haq mehr and the witnesses' signatures.
2. The nikah nama is registered with the **Union Council** where the nikah took place, which issues a registration number.
3. You can then obtain the **NADRA computerised marriage registration certificate**, which is needed for many later tasks such as updating CNICs, family registration, passports and visas.

Read the nikah nama carefully before signing, especially the clauses on haq mehr and the delegated right of divorce. Fees and procedures for the certificate vary by Union Council, so confirm locally. [Legal documentation services](/categories/legal-documentation) can help if documents are lost or details need correcting.

## Step 6: Plan the wedding day itself

Even a well-planned wedding goes wrong without a clear run-sheet. Write down:

- arrival times for each vendor and family group
- who holds the cash and pays vendors on the day
- who handles the barat's arrival, rukhsati timing and transport
- a backup plan for power, sound and rain at outdoor venues

Assign one calm family member to deal with vendors on the day, so the bride, groom and parents are not taking phone calls.

## Ways to save money without cutting corners

- **Choose a weekday or off-peak date**: some venues and vendors offer lower rates.
- **Combine functions**: a joint mehndi or a nikah on the barat day saves a venue booking.
- **Limit printed invitations**: digital invites for distant relatives and friends cut printing and postage.
- **Rent rather than buy**: some outfits and jewellery for secondary functions can be rented.
- **Book vendor packages**: photographers and decorators often discount multi-function bookings.

If you are planning in a big city such as [Lahore](/cities/lahore) or [Karachi](/cities/karachi), compare at least three vendors in each category; prices vary widely between areas.

## Final word: decide early, confirm often

Successful wedding planning in Pakistan is less about spending more and more about deciding early, getting everything in writing and confirming every booking twice. With the budget, guest list and venue settled early, the rest of the plan falls into place.

### How do I start wedding planning in Pakistan?
Start with the budget and guest list. Agree with both families how many functions you will hold, who pays for each and how many guests each will have. Then book the venue and caterer, as good dates fill early in the winter season. Photographers, designers, makeup artists and decorators come next, followed by invitations and final confirmations.

### How far in advance should I book a wedding hall in Pakistan?
For popular dates in the winter wedding season, many families book halls and marquees six to twelve months ahead. Off-peak dates and weekdays may be available at shorter notice. Visit the venue before paying an advance, check parking, power backup and capacity, and get the booking terms and cancellation policy in writing.

### What is the one-dish rule in Pakistan?
The one-dish rule comes from the Punjab Marriage Functions Act, 2016. It limits the main course served at wedding functions in Punjab to a single dish and requires venues to end functions by 10pm. The Punjab government has run repeated enforcement drives, including sealing halls and imposing fines. Other provinces have their own rules, so check locally.

### What time do weddings have to end in Punjab?
Under the Punjab Marriage Functions Act, 2016, venues must end wedding functions by 10pm. Enforcement teams have inspected halls, marquees and farmhouses, and halls that ran late have been sealed or fined. Plan your timings with a buffer, especially for the barat's arrival and the rukhsati, so the event finishes on time.

### How do I register my nikah in Pakistan?
After the nikah, the nikah nama is registered with the Union Council where the ceremony took place, as required under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961. The Union Council issues a registration number. You can then apply for the NADRA computerised marriage registration certificate. Confirm documents and fees with your local Union Council.

### What documents are needed for a NADRA marriage certificate?
You usually need the registered nikah nama, CNIC copies of the bride and groom and, in many areas, CNIC copies of the witnesses or parents. The Union Council where the nikah was registered processes the application. Requirements and fees can differ between Union Councils, so call ahead or visit before you go with the documents.

### How much does a wedding cost in Pakistan?
Costs vary enormously with city, guest numbers, number of functions, venue type and vendor choices. Venue and catering usually take the largest share, followed by outfits and jewellery. Rather than relying on averages, set your own budget, get written quotes from at least three vendors in each category and keep a contingency of about 10%.

### When is wedding season in Pakistan?
The main wedding season runs through the cooler months, roughly from October or November to March, with a strong peak in December. Many families avoid Muharram and Ramadan for weddings. Venues, decorators and makeup artists are in highest demand during peak months, so book early if your date falls in this period.

### What should I check before paying a wedding vendor advance?
Check recent work from a real event, read reviews, and confirm exactly what is included in writing. Ask about the advance amount, balance due date, cancellation and date-change terms, and who will actually work on the day. Pay by bank transfer where possible and keep receipts. Avoid vendors who refuse any written confirmation.

### How can I reduce wedding costs in Pakistan?
Cut the guest list first, as it drives venue and catering costs. Consider combining functions, choosing an off-peak date, sending digital invitations and booking vendor packages across functions. Renting outfits or jewellery for secondary events also helps. Compare at least three quotes for each vendor and stick to your agreed budget and contingency.
