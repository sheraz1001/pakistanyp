Here is how to become a filer in Pakistan: register yourself on the Federal Board of Revenue's IRIS portal with your CNIC, file your income tax return and wealth statement for the latest tax year, and your name appears on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL). Being on that list is what makes you a "filer" in the eyes of banks, property registrars and vehicle excise offices.

You do not need a separate NTN (National Tax Number) as an individual. Your 13-digit CNIC number works as your NTN. The whole process can be done online from a phone or laptop, and many salaried people finish it in an evening once their documents are ready.

:::takeaways
- A filer is anyone whose name is on FBR's Active Taxpayer List, not just anyone with an NTN.
- Individuals register on IRIS with their CNIC, which then serves as their NTN.
- You must file both an income tax return and a wealth statement.
- Since late 2024, FBR updates the ATL daily, so filing on time puts you on the list quickly.
- Late filers can still join the ATL, but only after paying a surcharge.
:::

:::stats Becoming a filer at a glance
- 13 digits | your CNIC number doubles as your NTN
- 9966 | FBR's SMS number for checking ATL status
- Daily | how often the ATL is updated since 2024
- 1 July–30 June | Pakistan's tax year
:::

## What being a filer in Pakistan actually means

Pakistan's tax laws treat people on the **Active Taxpayer List** differently from people who are not. The ATL is maintained by FBR under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001. You are "active" if you filed your return for the latest tax year by the due date, or filed late and paid the ATL surcharge.

Having an NTN alone does not make you a filer. Many people registered years ago, stopped filing and now pay higher withholding taxes without realising it.

The tax year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Tax Year 2026, for example, covers income earned from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. For most individuals the statutory due date is 30 September after the year ends, though FBR often extends it. For Tax Year 2026, FBR extended the date twice, reportedly to 31 October 2026.

## Why it is worth learning how to become a filer in Pakistan

Non-filers pay higher rates of withholding tax on a long list of everyday transactions. Exact rates change with every Finance Act, so check FBR's current withholding tax card, but the pattern is consistent:

| Transaction | Filer | Non-filer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash withdrawals above the daily limit | No advance tax | Advance tax deducted by the bank |
| Buying or selling property | Lower withholding rates | Much higher rates |
| Registering a new vehicle | Lower advance tax | Higher advance tax |
| Profit on bank deposits | Lower deduction | Higher deduction |
| Dividends and some contracts | Standard rates | Higher rates |

Recent budgets have gone further and restricted what non-filers can buy at all, including higher-value property and vehicles. The direction of policy is clear: staying off the list gets more expensive every year.

> [!TIP]
> If you plan to buy a plot, a flat or a car in the next year, become a filer first. The difference in withholding tax on a single property deal can be far larger than any tax you pay on your income. Ask [property dealers](/categories/property-dealers-estate-agents) and [car dealers](/categories/car-dealers) to quote taxes for both cases.

## Step 1: Register on FBR IRIS as a new user

Go to FBR's [IRIS portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk) and choose **Registration for Unregistered Person**. You will need:

:::checklist
- Your original CNIC
- A mobile number registered in your own name
- A personal email address you can access
- Your current residential address
- Employer details (if salaried) or business name and address (if self-employed)
- Bank account details
:::

Enter your details exactly as they appear on your CNIC. FBR sends verification codes to your mobile and email; enter them to complete registration, and you then receive your password and PIN. FBR's **Tax Asaan** mobile app offers the same registration route if you prefer a phone.

> [!WARNING]
> The mobile number must be in your own name. A number registered to a parent or spouse is the most common reason the verification code never arrives.

If you are running a business as a sole proprietor, add the business name and activity to your IRIS profile. If you have a company, it has its own NTN; our guide on [how to register a company in Pakistan](/blogs/how-to-register-a-company-in-pakistan) explains how that is issued.

## Step 2: File your income tax return on IRIS

Once logged in, open the return form for the relevant tax year (Form 114(1) for individuals). FBR has introduced simplified forms for salaried people in recent years. Other individuals complete the full return. You will report:

1. **Income** from salary, business, property and other sources
2. **Tax already deducted**, such as tax on salary, mobile phone bills, electricity bills, bank profit and vehicle tokens
3. **Adjustments**, such as Zakat paid or eligible pension contributions

IRIS pulls in many withholding tax deductions automatically. Check them against your salary certificate and bank statements. If the return shows tax payable, you pay it through a payment slip (PSID) before submitting.

### Documents to gather before you file

Keep your employer's salary and tax deduction certificate, bank tax certificates, property and vehicle papers, and business summaries if you are self-employed. Utility and phone bills show advance tax already paid.

## Step 3: Submit your wealth statement

Individuals must also file a **wealth statement** (Form 116) with the return. It lists your assets and liabilities on 30 June: property, vehicles, bank balances, investments, cash, gold and loans.

The key part is **reconciliation**. FBR expects the increase in your wealth to match your declared income minus your expenses. If you bought a car but declared modest income and no savings, the numbers will not reconcile, and that can trigger questions.

First-time filers should declare what they own now at cost and keep purchase documents. A qualified [tax adviser](/categories/accounting-tax) can help if you have several properties, foreign income or business assets.

:::steps How to become a filer in Pakistan in 5 steps
- Register on IRIS | Sign up as an unregistered person with your CNIC, mobile and email.
- Gather documents | Collect salary, bank, property and vehicle papers for the tax year.
- File the return | Complete the income tax return and pay any tax due by PSID.
- Add the wealth statement | Declare assets and liabilities, and check that they reconcile.
- Check ATL status | Send ATL and your CNIC to 9966, or search on the FBR website.
:::

## Step 4: Check your Active Taxpayer List status

Once you file on time, you should appear on the ATL quickly. Through SRO 1638(I)/2024, FBR moved to daily updates of the list from late 2024, replacing the old weekly cycle.

You can check your status in two simple ways:

- **SMS:** type `ATL` followed by a space and your 13-digit CNIC (no dashes) and send it to 9966. For a company or AOP, use the 7-digit NTN.
- **Online:** use the ATL search on the [FBR website](https://www.fbr.gov.pk).

If you still show as inactive several days after filing, contact FBR's helpline or your Regional Tax Office. Our listing of [revenue and taxation offices](/categories/revenue-taxation) shows where they are.

## If you miss the deadline: becoming a late filer

You can still file after the due date. However, under section 182A of the Income Tax Ordinance, your name is added to the ATL only after you pay a **surcharge**. For years this was Rs 1,000 for individuals. The Finance Act 2026 increased it sharply, and reports cite Rs 25,000 for individuals, with higher amounts for AOPs and companies. Confirm the current figure on IRIS or with FBR before paying, because the amount applies by tax year.

Late filing can also attract penalties under section 182, separate from the ATL surcharge. Filing on time is far cheaper than catching up.

## Staying a filer year after year

You stay on the ATL only by filing every year. A simple routine helps: request tax certificates from your employer and bank in July, keep documents for big purchases, and file in August or September rather than waiting for extensions.

If you run a shop or a service business, filing also builds the paper trail that banks and digital payment providers ask for. Our news piece on [Raast and QR payments](/news/raast-digital-payments-what-businesses-need-to-know) explains why documented income is becoming more important for small merchants.

## Final word: file once, then keep it simple

Learning how to become a filer in Pakistan takes one careful evening. Staying a filer takes an hour or two each year. If your affairs are simple, do it yourself on IRIS. If you own property, run a business or have income from abroad, a local tax consultant in [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) or your own city is worth the fee.

Your next step: register on IRIS today, even if you file later, so the account is ready when your documents are.

:::faq
### What is the difference between a filer and a non-filer in Pakistan?
A filer is a person whose name appears on FBR's Active Taxpayer List because they filed their income tax return for the latest tax year. A non-filer is not on that list, even if they have an NTN. Non-filers pay higher withholding taxes on banking, property, vehicles and many other transactions, and face growing restrictions on some purchases.

### How can I become a filer in Pakistan online?
Register on FBR's IRIS portal as an unregistered person using your CNIC, a mobile number in your name and an email address. After verification, log in, file your income tax return and wealth statement for the latest tax year, and pay any tax due. Once filed, your name is added to the Active Taxpayer List, usually quickly.

### Is NTN the same as being a filer?
No. An NTN, or your CNIC for individuals, only shows that you are registered with FBR. You become a filer when you actually file your return and appear on the Active Taxpayer List. Many people with NTNs are non-filers because they stopped filing returns. You must file every year to stay active.

### How do I check if I am a filer?
Send an SMS with ATL, a space and your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes to 9966. FBR replies with your status. For a company or association of persons, use the 7-digit NTN instead. You can also search the Active Taxpayer List on the FBR website by CNIC or NTN.

### What is the fee to become a filer in Pakistan?
FBR charges nothing to register on IRIS or to file a return on time. You only pay any income tax you owe. If you file after the due date, you must pay a surcharge to join the Active Taxpayer List. Tax consultants charge their own fees, which vary with how complex your income and assets are.

### Can a student or unemployed person become a filer?
Yes. Anyone with a CNIC can register on IRIS and file a return, even with little or no income. Some people do this to avoid higher withholding taxes or to support visa and bank applications. You still need to file a wealth statement, so keep a record of any assets and of who supports your expenses.

### What is the ATL surcharge for late filers?
Under section 182A of the Income Tax Ordinance, a late filer joins the Active Taxpayer List only after paying a surcharge. It was Rs 1,000 for individuals for several years. The Finance Act 2026 raised it substantially, with reports citing Rs 25,000 for individuals. Check the current amount on IRIS or with FBR before paying.

### What is the last date to file income tax returns in Pakistan?
For most individuals, the statutory due date is 30 September following the end of the tax year on 30 June. FBR often extends it. For Tax Year 2026, FBR extended the deadline to 15 October and then, according to reports, to 31 October 2026. Always confirm the current date on the FBR website.

### Do I need a wealth statement to become a filer?
Yes. Individuals filing a return must also file a wealth statement listing assets and liabilities at the end of the tax year. It should reconcile with your declared income and expenses. Missing or inconsistent wealth statements can lead to notices from FBR, so keep records of property, vehicles, bank balances and loans.

### How long does it take to appear on the Active Taxpayer List?
FBR moved to daily updates of the Active Taxpayer List in late 2024. If you file on time, your name should appear quickly, often by the next day. Late filers appear after paying the surcharge. If your status still shows inactive after several days, contact the FBR helpline or your Regional Tax Office.
:::
