The best small business ideas in Pakistan with low investment are service and skills businesses: home food and tiffin services, tuition, mobile phone repair, AC servicing, tailoring, cleaning, digital marketing for local shops and freelancing. They need little stock or premises, you can start from home or a small shop, and demand comes from everyday needs that do not disappear when the economy slows.

The trick is not finding an idea but choosing one that fits your skills, your neighbourhood and your cash. This guide lists 15 ideas that work in Pakistani cities and towns. It explains what each needs, how to register, where to find funding and how to win the first customers.

:::takeaways
- Service businesses need the least capital: your skill is the main asset.
- Start small from home or a shared space, and prove demand before renting a shop.
- Register with FBR early; some trades also need a food, trade or provincial licence.
- Government-backed loans such as the PM Youth Business scheme can fund growth once you have a plan.
- A free business listing and a WhatsApp number bring the first local enquiries.
:::

## How to choose between small business ideas in Pakistan

Before you spend anything, answer four questions honestly:

1. **What can you already do well?** A business built on an existing skill starts earning sooner.
2. **Who will pay, and how often?** Repeat customers, such as tiffin clients or tuition students, make cash flow predictable.
3. **How much can you lose?** Never invest money you need for rent or family expenses.
4. **What does your area lack?** Walk your neighbourhood. A missing service is a better opportunity than a crowded one.

The Small and Medium Enterprises Development Authority (SMEDA) publishes free pre-feasibility studies for many trades on its [official website](https://smeda.org.pk). They are a useful starting point for estimating costs and margins, though you should update prices locally.

## Home-based small business ideas in Pakistan

These ideas can start from your home with modest equipment.

### 1. Home food and tiffin service

Office workers, students in hostels and elderly people living alone need reliable home-cooked meals. Start with a fixed weekly menu for a few clients, then grow by word of mouth. Food businesses usually need registration with the provincial food authority, such as the Punjab Food Authority. Browse [home food and catering businesses](/categories/home-food-catering) to see how others present their menus.

### 2. Home bakery and desserts

Birthday cakes, cupcakes, desi sweets and dessert boxes sell well online through Instagram and WhatsApp. Invest in a good oven and consistent recipes before you invest in packaging.

### 3. Home tuition and online classes

Qualified teachers and university students can tutor school children, O and A level students, or Quran classes. Online classes widen your reach beyond your neighbourhood. Look at how [academies and tuition centres](/categories/academies-tuition) price and advertise their courses.

### 4. Tailoring and alterations

Stitching and alterations are needed year-round, with peaks before Eid and the wedding season. A sewing machine and skill are enough to start. Specialise in bridal alterations or children's wear to stand out among [tailoring businesses](/categories/tailoring).

### 5. Freelancing and remote services

Writing, design, video editing, bookkeeping, software development and virtual assistance can be sold to clients worldwide. Our news piece on [PSEB registration for freelancers](/news/pakistan-freelancing-it-exports-pseb-registration) explains the benefits of registering as an IT exporter.

## Skilled trade ideas with steady local demand

### 6. Mobile phone repair

Screens, batteries and charging ports break every day. Training, a tool kit and a small counter in a market are enough to start. Build relationships with parts wholesalers and offer a short warranty to win trust. See how established [mobile repair shops](/categories/mobile-repair) present their services.

### 7. AC installation and servicing

Air conditioner servicing, gas refilling and installation peak from March to September. A trained technician with tools and a motorcycle can serve a whole area. Offering annual maintenance contracts smooths out the winter slowdown.

### 8. Solar panel cleaning and maintenance

Dust cuts the output of rooftop panels, and many owners do not clean or check their systems. A cleaning and basic inspection service needs water-fed brushes, safety gear and a clear price list. Our [solar panel price guide](/blogs/solar-panel-installation-guide-pakistan) explains why system owners care about output.

### 9. Home cleaning and pest control

Deep cleaning for homes, offices and move-outs has grown in big cities. Pest control needs proper training and safe chemicals. Both businesses depend on punctuality and trusted staff.

### 10. Electrical and plumbing repairs

Reliable electricians and plumbers are always in demand. If you have a recognised vocational certificate, highlight it in your listing.

## Retail and trading ideas with low investment

### 11. Online reselling

Buy from [wholesale markets](/categories/wholesale-hubs) and sell clothing, home décor, cosmetics or electronics accessories online. Start with a narrow product range and keep stock low until you know what sells.

### 12. Neighbourhood grocery or general store

A small shop in an underserved residential area can do well, but margins are thin, and you need capital for stock. Shopkeepers should read our news explainer on [FBR retailer registration and Tajir Dost](/news/fbr-retailer-registration-tajir-dost-explained), FBR's scheme for documenting retailers.

### 13. Printing and branding

Visiting cards, flex banners, stickers and custom mugs serve local businesses, schools and events. A small print shop near a market area gets steady walk-in demand.

## Digital and agricultural ideas

### 14. Digital marketing for local businesses

Many shops, clinics and restaurants want help with social media posts, Google profiles and WhatsApp catalogues but cannot hire a full-time person. Offer a simple monthly package. Look at how [digital marketing agencies](/categories/digital-marketing) package their services.

### 15. Small-scale poultry, dairy or kitchen gardening

In towns and peri-urban areas, a small poultry unit, a few dairy animals or a vegetable plot can supply local households directly. These need space, veterinary support and patience, as returns take longer.

## Registration and taxes for a new small business

You can start as a sole proprietor. Register on FBR's IRIS portal with your CNIC, add your business details and file a return every year. Our guide on [how to become a filer](/blogs/how-to-become-a-filer-in-pakistan) explains the process. If you have partners or want limited liability, read about [registering a company](/blogs/how-to-register-a-company-in-pakistan).

> [!NOTE]
> Food, health and some trading businesses need extra licences from provincial or local authorities. Check with your local municipal office or food authority before you open to the public.

## Funding your small business

Most small businesses in Pakistan start with personal savings or family support. Once you have a plan and some track record, other options include:

- the **Prime Minister's Youth Business and Agriculture Loan Scheme**, offered through partner banks, with tiered loans of up to Rs 7.5 million on concessional terms
- microfinance banks, which lend smaller amounts with less paperwork
- bank SME financing for established businesses

Loan terms and eligibility change, so confirm them directly with participating banks or [loans and financing providers](/categories/loans-financing).

:::bars PM Youth Business Loan: maximum loan by tier (PKR million)
- Tier 1 (0% markup) | 0.5 | Rs 0.5m
- Tier 2 (5% markup) | 1.5 | Rs 1.5m
- Tier 3 (7% markup) | 7.5 | Rs 7.5m
:::

> [!WARNING]
> Be wary of agents who promise guaranteed government loans for an upfront fee. Applications for the PM Youth scheme go through participating banks and the official programme channels.

## Winning your first customers

:::steps From idea to first paying customer
- Test demand | Offer your service to ten people you know and ask for honest feedback.
- Set prices | Check what nearby competitors charge and price clearly.
- Get listed | Add your business to a directory with your locality, hours and WhatsApp.
- Collect reviews | Ask happy customers to leave a review after each job.
- Reinvest | Put early profits into tools, training or stock, not overheads.
:::

- A WhatsApp Business number with a catalogue or price list
- A free directory listing with your exact locality
- Photos of your real work or products

- A basic record of income and expenses from day one
:::

### Which business is best in Pakistan with low investment?
Service businesses built on a skill usually need the least money: tuition, home food, tailoring, mobile repair, AC servicing, cleaning, digital marketing and freelancing. They need few tools and no large stock. The best choice is one that matches your skills and fills a gap in your area, so test demand with a few customers before spending more.

### What business can I start from home in Pakistan?
Popular home-based options include tiffin and home food services, home bakeries, tuition and online classes, tailoring, freelancing, online reselling and digital marketing for local businesses. Check whether your area allows commercial activity in homes, especially if customers will visit, and register with FBR and any relevant food or trade authority.

### Do I need to register my small business in Pakistan?
You should at least register with FBR as a sole proprietor using your CNIC and file a yearly return. Food businesses typically need provincial food authority registration, and some trades need local licences. Registration helps you open business bank accounts, accept digital payments, get loans and earn a verified badge on business directories.

### How can I get a loan to start a business in Pakistan?
Options include the Prime Minister's Youth Business and Agriculture Loan Scheme, offered through partner banks with tiered loans up to Rs 7.5 million, microfinance banks for smaller amounts, and bank SME products. You will usually need a CNIC, a business plan and, for larger loans, collateral or guarantors. Confirm current terms directly with banks.

### What are the interest rates on the PM Youth Business Loan?
As announced, the scheme has three tiers: loans up to Rs 0.5 million at 0% markup, from Rs 0.5 million to Rs 1.5 million at 5%, and from Rs 1.5 million to Rs 7.5 million at 7%. Larger loans may need collateral under bank policy. Check the latest terms with participating banks before applying.

### Is freelancing a good business in Pakistan?
For people with digital skills, yes. Freelancing needs little more than a laptop, reliable internet and a skill clients pay for, such as writing, design, development or bookkeeping. Income is earned in foreign currency, and registering with PSEB and FBR can bring tax and banking benefits. Building a portfolio and reviews takes time at first.

### Which small business is most profitable in Pakistan?
Profit depends more on execution than on the idea. Businesses with repeat customers and low running costs, such as tuition, servicing contracts, tiffin subscriptions and digital marketing retainers, often keep more of what they earn. Retail can have high turnover but thin margins. Use SMEDA's pre-feasibility studies and local price checks to compare.

### How much money do I need to start a small business in Pakistan?
It varies widely. A tuition service or freelancing business can start with almost nothing beyond a phone or laptop. Mobile repair or AC servicing needs tools and training. A shop needs rent, advance, fittings and stock, which is far more. Prepare a simple budget covering setup and three months of running costs before you start.

### How do I find customers for a new local business?
Start with people you know, then make yourself easy to find. Create a free directory listing with your exact locality, phone and WhatsApp, use WhatsApp Business with a price list, and ask every satisfied customer for a review. Local flyers, school and mosque notice boards, and partnerships with nearby businesses also help.

### Can women start a small business from home in Pakistan?
Yes, and many do. Home food, baking, tailoring, boutiques, tuition, beauty services, online reselling and freelancing are common choices. Register with FBR as you would for any business, use digital payments to keep records, and consider women-focused financing schemes offered by some banks. Listing online helps reach customers without a shopfront.
