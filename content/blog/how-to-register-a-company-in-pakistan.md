If you want to know how to register a company in Pakistan, the short answer is this: you reserve a name and file your incorporation online with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), and your National Tax Number (NTN) follows through the SECP–FBR link. You then register for sales tax if your business needs it, open a business bank account and keep up with yearly filings.

Most small founders can do the SECP part themselves in a few sittings, without visiting an office. The harder part is making the right choices before you start: which structure to use, how much authorised capital to declare, and whether you need federal sales tax, provincial sales tax on services, or both.

:::takeaways
- Company incorporation is done online through SECP's eZfile portal, which replaced the older eServices system.
- Since 2018, an NTN is issued automatically after incorporation through the SECP–FBR one-window link.
- Sales tax is separate: goods are registered with FBR, services with your provincial revenue authority.
- A single member company suits solo founders who want limited liability.
- SECP fees depend on authorised capital, so check the official fee calculator before you pay.
:::

## Step 1: Choose the right business structure in Pakistan

You do not always need a company. Pick the structure that fits your risk, your partners and the clients you serve.

| Structure | Registered with | Liability | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sole proprietorship | FBR (NTN on your CNIC) | Unlimited, personal | Freelancers, small shops, home businesses |
| Partnership firm | Provincial Registrar of Firms | Unlimited, shared | Family businesses, small trading firms |
| Limited liability partnership (LLP) | SECP | Limited | Professional firms with two or more partners |
| Single member company (SMC) | SECP | Limited | Solo founders who want a corporate entity |
| Private limited company | SECP | Limited | Startups with co-founders or investors |

A **sole proprietorship** is the simplest option. Your CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card) number works as your NTN, but your personal assets are exposed if the business runs into debt.

A **private limited company** needs at least two members and suits startups raising investment. A **single member company** gives one person limited liability, with a nominee named in case the sole member dies.

> [!TIP]
> Many corporate clients, multinationals and government tenders only work with registered companies. If that is your market, incorporate early. It is easier than changing structure once contracts are signed.

## Step 2: Reserve your company name on SECP eZfile

SECP's corporate registry runs on **eZfile**, part of its LEAP digitisation programme. You create an account with your CNIC, email address and mobile number, then apply for name reservation before anything else.

SECP checks that your proposed name is not too similar to an existing company and avoids restricted words, so prepare two or three alternatives. SECP eased its name reservation guidelines in 2025, but names implying government links or regulated activities, such as "bank" or "insurance", still need extra approvals.

A reservation is valid only for a limited time, so file your incorporation promptly.

## Step 3: File incorporation documents with SECP

Incorporation is filed on eZfile after name reservation. You will provide:

- the memorandum and articles of association (SECP offers standard templates)
- the names, CNICs, addresses, occupations and nationalities of subscribers and directors
- the registered office address
- the authorised share capital and the shares each subscriber takes
- details of the nominee (for a single member company)

You then pay the fee through the portal or a bank challan. **SECP fees are based on your authorised (nominal) capital**, so keep that figure realistic, and the schedule has been revised more than once in recent years. Rather than rely on figures quoted online, use the fee calculator on eZfile or check the current notification on the [SECP website](https://www.secp.gov.pk) before you pay.

After approval, you download the certificate of incorporation from eZfile. Complete applications are often approved within a few working days; mismatched director details are the usual cause of delay.

:::steps How to register a company in Pakistan in 6 steps
- Choose a structure | Decide between a proprietorship, partnership, SMC or private limited company.
- Reserve the name | Apply for name reservation on SECP eZfile with two or three alternatives.
- File incorporation | Submit the memorandum, articles and director details, and pay the fee.
- Receive the NTN | FBR issues the company NTN through the SECP–FBR link.
- Register for sales tax | Apply on FBR IRIS for goods or with your province for services.
- Open a bank account | Use your incorporation certificate, NTN and board resolution.
:::

## Step 4: Get your company NTN from FBR

Since March 2018, SECP and the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) have run a one-window facility. When your company is incorporated, its details go to FBR, which verifies them and issues the NTN to the company's email address. You do not need to visit a tax office for this.

Log in to FBR's [IRIS portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk) to confirm the company profile, principal activity, bank details and directors. A company must file an income tax return every year, even in a loss-making or dormant year, to stay on the Active Taxpayer List.

If you are setting up as a sole proprietor instead, you register yourself on IRIS, and your CNIC serves as your NTN. If you prefer face-to-face help, look up [tax offices and revenue services](/categories/revenue-taxation) near you.

## Step 5: Register for sales tax with FBR or your province

Sales tax in Pakistan is split between two levels of government:

- **Sales tax on goods** is federal. Manufacturers, importers, wholesalers, distributors and many retailers register with FBR on IRIS using Form 14(1). The standard rate is 18% at the time of writing, though many goods carry reduced or special rates.
- **Sales tax on services** is provincial. You register with the Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA), Sindh Revenue Board (SRB), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority (KPRA) or Balochistan Revenue Authority (BRA), depending on where you provide the service.

For FBR registration, the applicant or directors must complete **biometric verification at a NADRA e-Sahulat centre**, and FBR now applies pre-verification checks before final approval. Expect a premises check, a utility bill, a bank certificate and sometimes a balance sheet.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not register for sales tax "just in case". Once registered, you must file a monthly return even when you have no sales. Missed returns bring penalties and can make you inactive on the sales tax list.

## Step 6: Open a bank account and set up compliance

Banks will typically ask for:

:::checklist
- Certificate of incorporation and company NTN
- Memorandum and articles of association
- Board resolution authorising the account and signatories
- CNIC copies of all directors and signatories
- Proof of registered office address (rent agreement or utility bill)
- Company letterhead and stamp
:::

Requirements differ, so ask several [banks and financial institutions](/categories/banks-financial-institutions). Account opening can take longer than incorporation itself.

### Yearly obligations after you register a company

Learning how to register a company in Pakistan is only half the job. To stay in good standing, you will usually need to:

1. file an annual return with SECP each year
2. hold an annual general meeting (private companies) and keep minutes
3. keep proper books of account, and have them audited where the law requires
4. file the company's income tax return with FBR every year
5. file monthly sales tax returns if registered
6. register with EOBI and your provincial social security institution once you hire staff

A good [accounting and tax adviser](/categories/accounting-tax) usually costs less than the late-filing penalties you avoid.

## How to register a company in Pakistan yourself or with a consultant

Many founders register an SMC or private limited company on their own. It makes sense to pay a professional when:

- you have foreign shareholders or directors
- your business is in a regulated sector such as lending, insurance or securities
- you need a non-profit (Section 42) company
- you want shareholder agreements and investor-ready articles

In those cases, speak to [corporate lawyers](/categories/lawyers-legal-services) or chartered accountants, and ask for a quote that lists SECP fees separately. Advisers are concentrated in [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) and [Islamabad](/cities/islamabad), but most work remotely.

:::faq
### How much does it cost to register a company in Pakistan?
SECP's government fee depends on your authorised share capital and whether you file online. The schedule has changed several times, so figures quoted on blogs are often out of date. Use the fee calculator on SECP eZfile or check SECP's latest notification. Professional fees, if you hire a consultant, are charged on top and vary widely between firms and cities.

### How long does SECP company registration take?
If your name is available and your documents are complete, SECP often approves an online incorporation within a few working days. Name reservation is usually quicker. Delays mostly come from rejected names, mismatched CNIC details or missing nominee information. Bank account opening and sales tax registration usually take longer than incorporation itself, so plan for several weeks overall.

### Can I register a company in Pakistan online?
Yes. SECP runs name reservation, incorporation and most later filings through its eZfile portal. You sign up with your CNIC, email and mobile number, then file and pay online. A standard private limited or single member company needs no office visit, although regulated businesses need extra approvals.

### What is a single member company in Pakistan?
A single member company (SMC) is a private limited company with only one shareholder. It gives a solo founder limited liability and a separate legal identity. The member must name a nominee who would take over in case of death. An SMC can later convert into a private limited company with more shareholders if you bring in partners.

### Do I get an NTN automatically when I register a company?
Yes. Since 2018, SECP and FBR have run a one-window facility. Once your company is incorporated, its details pass to FBR, which issues an NTN to the company's registered email. You should still log in to FBR's IRIS portal to check the profile, add business activity details and file returns every year.

### Is sales tax registration compulsory for every company?
No. Whether you need it depends on what you do. Manufacturers, importers, wholesalers and many retailers must register with FBR for sales tax on goods. Service providers usually register with their provincial authority, such as PRA in Punjab or SRB in Sindh. Check the rules for your sector before registering, because registration brings monthly filing duties.

### Can a foreigner register a company in Pakistan?
Yes, foreign nationals can be shareholders and directors of a Pakistani company. Their participation usually involves security clearance through the Ministry of Interior, which SECP coordinates, and some sectors have extra conditions. Because of this, foreign-owned incorporations take longer. Most foreign founders use a local law firm or chartered accountant to handle the filings and clearance.

### What is the difference between SECP and FBR registration?
SECP registration creates your company as a legal entity under the Companies Act 2017. FBR registration relates to tax: your NTN, income tax returns and, if applicable, sales tax on goods. A company needs both. A sole proprietor, by contrast, only registers with FBR, because there is no separate company to incorporate with SECP.

### Do I need an office address to register a company?
Yes. Every company must have a registered office address in Pakistan, where official notices are sent. It can be a rented office, and some small founders use a home address. Banks and FBR may ask for a utility bill or rent agreement for the premises. If you move, you must notify SECP of the change.

### What happens if I do not file annual returns with SECP?
Late or missing annual returns attract penalties that increase with the delay. Directors can also be held responsible. If a company stays inactive and non-compliant for long enough, SECP can strike it off the register. Reviving a struck-off company requires extra filings and costs, so it is cheaper to stay compliant or close the company properly.
:::
