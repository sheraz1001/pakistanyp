Getting your business onto PakistanYP takes about ten minutes and costs nothing. This guide walks through each step and explains the choices that make the biggest difference to how customers find you.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your business name exactly as customers know it (and in Urdu, if you use an Urdu name on your signboard).
- A short description of what you do, who you serve and what makes you different.
- Your phone and WhatsApp numbers, email and website (if you have one).
- Your full address and working hours.
- Your logo or a clear photo of your shopfront.

## Step 1: Create a free account

Choose **List your business** at the top of any page and sign up with your email. You will use this account to manage your listing, reply to enquiries and request verification later.

## Step 2: Choose your main category and sub-category

Categories decide where your business appears, so take a moment here.

- First choose the **main category**, the broad sector such as Property & Construction, Health & Medical or Education & Training.
- Then choose the **sub-category** that describes what you actually do, for example Property Dealers & Estate Agents, Dental Clinics or Academies & Tuition.
- Where available, add the **specialised category** too, such as Commercial Property Dealers or MDCAT Coaching.

Both a main category and a sub-category are required. Main categories group related businesses; customers browse them to reach the sub-category lists where businesses actually appear.

## Step 3: Choose your province, city and locality

PakistanYP is organised the way people search: by province, city and neighbourhood.

- Pick your **province or territory**.
- Pick your **city**. For Islamabad, the city is selected automatically.
- Pick your **locality**, such as Gulberg, Clifton, F-7 or Hayatabad. If your town has no separate localities listed, your business is filed under the town itself.

Choosing the right locality matters: your business will appear on that locality's page and on its city page, which is where nearby customers look.

## Step 4: Add contact details customers can use

Add your phone number in the usual Pakistani format, for example 0300 1234567. PakistanYP shows it as +92 300 1234567 and turns it into a click-to-call button. Add a WhatsApp number if you take orders or questions there; many customers prefer it.

## Step 5: Write a description people will read

Write two or three short paragraphs:

1. What you do, in plain words.
2. Who you serve and where (for example "families in DHA and Clifton").
3. Why customers choose you: experience, guarantees, delivery, timings.

Avoid writing in all capital letters or repeating keywords. A clear description helps both customers and search engines.

## Step 6: Hours, logo and photos

Add your working hours, including Friday timings if they differ, and upload your logo. Listings with a logo and accurate hours get noticeably more attention.

## Step 7: Submit and get verified

Submit the listing. Our team reviews new listings before they go live to keep the directory accurate. While you wait, you can start verification from your dashboard by confirming your phone number and uploading a document such as your NTN certificate or SECP registration. Verified businesses get a badge that customers trust.

## After you're listed

- Reply quickly to enquiries and quote requests in your dashboard.
- Ask happy customers to leave a review.
- Keep your hours and phone number up to date, especially around Ramadan and public holidays.

That's it. Your business now has a page that customers in your city and locality can find, call and message.
