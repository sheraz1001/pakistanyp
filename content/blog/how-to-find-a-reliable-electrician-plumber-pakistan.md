When you search "electrician near me" or "plumber near me" in Pakistan, you want someone who turns up on time, fixes the problem safely and charges a fair price. The most reliable way to find one is to shortlist local technicians with a verifiable address and reviews, call two or three, describe the job clearly and agree the visit charge and labour rate before anyone starts work.

Most problems with hired technicians come from skipping those steps: hiring the first number on a wall poster, not asking about materials, or paying in full before checking the work. This guide explains where to look, what to ask, how pricing usually works and the red flags to watch for, whether you need a quick fix or a full rewiring job.

:::takeaways
- Look for technicians with a fixed shop or office, a working phone number and reviews from real customers.
- Describe the job with photos on WhatsApp before the visit, so the technician brings the right parts.
- Agree the visit charge, labour rate and who buys materials before work begins.
- For electrical work, ask about training such as a NAVTTC vocational certificate.
- Pay after checking the work, and keep receipts for parts with warranties.
:::

## How to find a reliable plumber near me

Plumbing problems range from a dripping tap to a burst pipe inside a wall. For anything beyond a simple fix, look for a plumber who:

- explains the cause before quoting
- can show photos of similar jobs, such as bathroom fittings or water tank connections
- knows the difference between pipe types used in your building, such as PPR or GI
- offers to source branded fittings and shows you the receipts

For water pressure problems, motors and underground tanks, you may need a specialist in [boring and water pumps](/categories/boring-pumps) rather than a general plumber.

## Finding a good AC technician before summer

AC technicians are busiest from March to September, especially in [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) and [Faisalabad](/cities/faisalabad), where summers are long. Book servicing in late winter or early spring, when technicians have more time and you can compare quotes.

A proper AC service usually includes cleaning filters and coils, checking the drain line, checking gas pressure and testing the electrical connections. Gas refilling should only follow a leak check: a unit that keeps losing gas has a leak that needs fixing. Find [AC and appliance repair specialists](/categories/ac-appliances) in your area, and ask whether they service inverter ACs, as their electronics need specific skills.

> [!WARNING]
> Be cautious if a technician insists on a full gas refill without testing pressure or finding the leak first. Ask them to show you the gauge reading before and after.

## Questions to ask before you hire

Use this list on the phone or WhatsApp before agreeing a visit.

:::checklist
- What is your visit or inspection charge, and is it adjusted against the final bill?
- Do you charge per job, per point or per day?
- Who buys the materials, and will I get the receipts?
- Which brands of cable, breakers or fittings do you recommend, and why?
- How long will the job take, and will you work alone or with a helper?
- Do you give any warranty on your workmanship?
- Can you share photos of a similar job or a customer I can speak to?
:::

### How to describe the problem

Send clear photos or a short video of the fault, the main distribution board or the plumbing under the sink. Mention when the problem started, what you have already tried and whether it is getting worse. A technician who understands the job before arriving is more likely to bring the right tools and parts.

## How pricing usually works

Rates vary by city, locality, urgency and the technician's experience, so there is no single fair price. In general:

- **Small jobs** such as replacing a switch, fixing a tap or changing a fan capacitor are charged per job.
- **Wiring and fittings** are often charged per point (each socket, switch or light) or per day.
- **Emergency or late-night visits** cost more.
- **Materials** are usually extra, and you should see what you are paying for.

Get at least two quotes for any job bigger than a quick fix. If one quote is far lower than the rest, ask what is missing, such as earthing, proper cable size or a waterproofing step.

## Red flags and safety basics

Some warning signs are worth walking away from:

- refusing to tell you the price until the job is finished
- asking for full payment before starting
- using thinner cable than recommended or skipping earthing to "save money"
- disconnecting safety devices, such as breakers, that keep tripping instead of finding the fault
- pressuring you to replace an appliance without diagnosis

Electrical faults can cause fires, and poor gas connections can be deadly. If you smell gas, open windows, avoid switches and flames, leave the area and call your gas company's emergency line on 1199. For fire or serious injury, call Rescue 1122. Report power faults outside your house to your electricity company using the helpline printed on your bill.

## Checking qualifications and experience

Pakistan's National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC) sets national vocational qualifications for trades including general and domestic electricians. A certificate from a recognised training institute shows a technician has had formal training, though many skilled tradespeople learned on the job.

### How do I find a good electrician near me in Pakistan?
Ask neighbours and local electrical shops for names, then check online directories for technicians with a real address, photos of past work and recent reviews. Call two or three, describe the job with photos and agree the visit charge before they come. Prefer technicians who explain the fault before quoting and give receipts for materials.

### How much does an electrician charge in Pakistan?
Charges vary by city, area, urgency and experience. Small jobs are usually priced per job, wiring is often charged per point or per day, and emergency visits cost more. Materials are normally extra. Ask for the rate before work starts and get at least two quotes for larger jobs, so you can spot prices that are unusually high or low.

### What should I check before hiring a plumber?
Ask the plumber to explain the cause of the problem and how they will fix it. Confirm the visit charge, labour rate and who will buy fittings, and ask for receipts. For bigger jobs, request photos of similar work. Check the work before paying, especially for leaks, by running water for a while and looking for drips.

### When is the best time to service an AC in Pakistan?
Late winter or early spring, before the summer rush that usually starts around March. Technicians have more time, prices are easier to compare and your AC is ready for the first hot days. Many households also arrange a second check mid-summer, particularly in hot, dusty cities where filters and coils clog quickly.

### Is it safe to hire technicians from wall posters?
It can be, but it is harder to verify them. Posters rarely show an address, reviews or a track record. If you use one, ask for a shop location and references, agree prices in advance and avoid paying in full upfront. A listing with a verifiable address and reviews is generally a safer choice.

### What should I do if I smell gas at home?
Open doors and windows, do not switch lights or appliances on or off, avoid flames and leave the area. Then call your gas company's emergency helpline on 1199, which serves both SNGPL and SSGC areas. Do not try to find the leak with a flame, and wait for the gas company before using appliances again.

### Do electricians in Pakistan need a licence?
Individual household electricians are not generally required to hold a licence for small domestic jobs, but formal training is available. NAVTTC sets national vocational qualifications for electricians at several levels. Contractors working on larger construction projects may need registration with bodies such as the Pakistan Engineering Council. Ask about training and experience before hiring.

### How can I tell if an AC technician is overcharging for gas?
Ask the technician to show you the gas pressure reading before refilling and after. A unit only needs gas if it has leaked, so a proper technician should look for and fix the leak first. Repeated refills point to an unresolved leak. Compare quotes from two technicians if the price seems high.

### Should I buy the materials myself or let the technician buy them?
Either can work. Buying yourself gives you control over brands and price, but you need the right specifications. If the technician buys them, ask for itemised receipts and branded products, and keep packaging for warranty claims. For wiring, agree the cable size and breaker brand in advance, as cheap substitutes are a common shortcut.

### How do I leave a useful review for a technician?
Describe the job, whether the technician arrived on time, how clearly they explained the problem, the price compared with the quote and whether the fix lasted. Keep it factual and fair. Specific reviews help other households choose and help good technicians stand out, while vague one-line reviews are far less useful.
