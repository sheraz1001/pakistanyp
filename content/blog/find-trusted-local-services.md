When the AC stops working in June or you need a reliable tutor before exams, you want someone good, close by and quick to respond. PakistanYP is built to make that search short. Here is how to use it well.

## Start with where you are

Every business on PakistanYP belongs to a province, a city and a locality. You can start at any level:

- **Province pages**, such as Punjab or Sindh, list every city in the province with the number of businesses in each.
- **City pages**, such as Lahore or Karachi, list the city's localities, each with its own business count, plus all businesses in the city.
- **Locality pages**, such as Gulberg in Lahore or Clifton in Karachi, show the businesses in that neighbourhood.

If you know your area, open your city from **Cities** in the menu and choose your locality from the list.

## Or start with what you need

Open **Categories** and choose a main category such as Home Services or Health & Medical. Each main category lists its sub-categories, for example Repairs & Maintenance or AC & Appliances in Home Services, with the specialised categories inside them such as Plumbers, Electricians and AC Repair & Service. Open the one you need, then narrow the results by province and locality.

You can also type what you need into the search box on the home page and choose your province.

## How to choose between businesses

- **Look for the verified badge.** It means the business has proved it is genuine with documents such as an NTN or SECP certificate.
- **Read the reviews**, especially the most recent ones.
- **Check the details:** working hours, address, and whether the business serves your area.
- **Compare two or three options** before you decide.

## Ask several businesses at once

On category pages you can **request a quote**: describe what you need once, and it is sent to up to five matching businesses, who reply to you directly. It is the quickest way to compare prices for jobs such as renovation, catering or event planning.

## Contact the business

Every listing has a call button and, where the business uses it, WhatsApp. Numbers are shown in the +92 format, so they work from any phone, in Pakistan or abroad.

## Help others

After the job is done, leave a short, honest review. It helps your neighbours choose, and it helps good businesses grow.
