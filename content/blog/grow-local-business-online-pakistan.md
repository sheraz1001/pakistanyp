If you are wondering how to grow business online in Pakistan, start with the basics that bring customers who are already searching: accurate listings on business directories and Google, a WhatsApp Business number that answers quickly, a steady flow of genuine reviews and easy digital payment options. Social media and paid ads help, but they work far better once those foundations are in place.

For a local shop, clinic, workshop or service business, "online" mostly means being found at the moment someone nearby needs you. With more than 160 million mobile broadband users in Pakistan, according to figures shared with the National Assembly in October 2026, most of your customers already search on their phones. This guide sets out a simple, low-cost plan you can follow in a few hours a week.

:::takeaways
- Get found first: complete, consistent listings on directories and Google bring high-intent customers.
- WhatsApp Business is the main sales channel for many Pakistani small businesses; answer fast.
- Reviews build trust more than any advert; ask every satisfied customer.
- Accept bank transfers, wallets and Raast QR to make paying easy.
- Measure what brings calls and orders, then spend more time or money there.
:::

## How to grow business online in Pakistan, step 1: complete your listings

Directory listings are the cheapest, fastest way to appear when people search for a business type and area. On our directory, a free listing puts you on your category page, your city page and your locality page.

To make it work:

- **Use the exact name** on your signboard and registration documents.
- **Choose the most specific category** and the correct locality.
- **Add a phone and WhatsApp number** that someone actually answers.
- **Write a description for customers**: what you do, for whom and where.
- **Upload real photos** of your shop, team and work.
- **Keep hours accurate**, including Friday prayers and Ramadan timings.

> [!TIP]
> Keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere online: directories, Google, Facebook and your website. Consistent details help search engines and customers trust that you are a real, active business.

## Step 2: Set up your Google Business Profile

A free Google Business Profile lets your shop appear on Google Maps and in local search results. Verify your location, add categories, opening hours, photos and services, and post updates when you have offers or new products.

Watch for three common mistakes: a pin placed in the wrong spot, a personal number that no one answers, and hours that were never updated after Ramadan or a move. Check your profile once a month.

## Step 3: Use WhatsApp Business to sell and serve

For many Pakistani customers, sending a WhatsApp message is easier than calling. The free WhatsApp Business app gives you:

- a business profile with address, hours and website
- a **catalogue** of products or services with prices
- **quick replies** for common questions such as prices, delivery and location
- **labels** to organise new enquiries, pending orders and paid orders
- an **away message** for after hours

Reply within minutes during working hours. Quote requests often go to several businesses at once, and the first clear, polite answer frequently wins.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not add customers to broadcast lists or groups without their consent. Unwanted messages lead to blocks and reports, which can restrict your WhatsApp account.

## Step 4: Build trust with reviews

Reviews are often the deciding factor between two similar businesses. Make asking a habit:

1. Ask at the right moment, just after a job is completed or a customer says thank you.
2. Send a short WhatsApp message with a direct link to your listing.
3. Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and specifically.
4. Fix the problems that reviews point out, and say so in your reply.

Never buy reviews or post fake ones. Customers spot them, and platforms remove them.

## Step 5: Choose social media that fits your business

You do not need to be everywhere. Pick one or two platforms where your customers spend time:

| Platform | Works well for | Effort level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | Local services, community groups, older customers | Medium |
| Instagram | Fashion, food, beauty, décor, weddings | High (needs good photos) |
| TikTok | Restaurants, salons, youth-focused brands | High (needs regular video) |
| YouTube | Tutorials, product reviews, real estate tours | High |
| WhatsApp Status | Daily offers to existing customers | Low |

Post real work: before-and-after photos, short videos of products being made, customer stories with permission. Consistency matters more than polish. If you lack time, [digital marketing agencies](/categories/digital-marketing) and [designers](/categories/design-creative) can manage content for a monthly fee.

## Step 6: Make paying easy with digital payments

Customers increasingly expect to pay by bank transfer, mobile wallet or QR code. Raast, the State Bank of Pakistan's instant payment system, lets customers pay directly from their bank or wallet app. Ask your bank about merchant QR acceptance and any fees, which vary by provider. Our news piece on [Raast and QR payments](/news/raast-digital-payments-what-businesses-need-to-know) explains what businesses need to know.

Digital payments also create a record of sales, which helps when you apply for loans or a business bank account.

## Step 7: Sell online beyond your area

If your products can be shipped, consider selling to other cities through your own website, social media shops or marketplaces. Start with a few best-selling items, clear photos, honest descriptions and reliable courier partners. Cash on delivery remains common, so plan for returns and refusals. Our news analysis of [e-commerce in Pakistan](/news/ecommerce-growth-pakistan-local-businesses) looks at what online growth means for local shops.

## Step 8: Track what works, then invest

The last part of learning how to grow business online in Pakistan is measurement. Ask every new customer, "How did you hear about us?" and note the answer. Over a month, you will see which channels bring real business.

:::steps Your first 30 days online
- Week 1 | Complete your directory listing and Google Business Profile with photos and hours.
- Week 2 | Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue, quick replies and labels.
- Week 3 | Ask your last 20 happy customers for reviews and reply to each one.
- Week 4 | Start posting on one social platform and track where enquiries come from.
:::

Once free channels are working, paid options can speed things up. A featured listing or banner on a directory puts you above competitors in your category and city. See [our pricing page](/pricing) for options.

### How can I grow my small business online in Pakistan for free?
Start with free tools: a complete directory listing, a Google Business Profile, the WhatsApp Business app and one social media page. Add real photos, keep hours accurate and reply to enquiries quickly. Ask every satisfied customer for a review. These steps cost nothing but time and bring customers who are already searching for your service.

### Is WhatsApp Business free in Pakistan?
Yes. The WhatsApp Business app is free to download and use on a smartphone. It adds a business profile, product catalogue, quick replies, labels and away messages. Larger businesses that need automated messaging or several agents can use the paid WhatsApp Business Platform through approved providers, but most small businesses do not need it.

### How do I get my business on Google Maps in Pakistan?
Create a free Google Business Profile with your business name, category, address and phone number, then place the map pin at your exact location. Google will ask you to verify the business, often by phone, video or postcard. Once verified, add photos, hours and services and keep them updated.

### Which social media platform is best for business in Pakistan?
It depends on your customers. Facebook works well for local services and community reach. Instagram suits fashion, food, beauty and weddings. TikTok can work for restaurants and youth brands with regular video. Choose one or two platforms you can post on consistently rather than spreading yourself thin across all of them.

### How do I get more reviews for my business?
Ask at the right moment, just after a job is finished or a customer thanks you. Send a WhatsApp message with a direct link to your listing so leaving a review takes seconds. Reply to every review. Never buy or fake reviews; they damage trust and are removed by platforms when detected.

### Should I accept digital payments in my shop?
Yes, if you can. Many customers prefer bank transfers, mobile wallets or QR payments, especially for larger amounts. Raast lets customers pay instantly from their bank or wallet app. Digital payments also create a sales record that helps with loans and tax filing. Ask your bank about merchant fees before signing up.

### Do I need a website for my small business?
Not always. Many local businesses do well with a directory listing, a Google profile, WhatsApp and one social page. A simple website helps if you sell online, serve corporate clients or want to rank in search for specific services. Start with the free channels and add a website once you know what customers ask for.

### How much does digital marketing cost for a small business in Pakistan?
Costs vary widely. Doing it yourself costs only time. Freelancers and agencies usually charge monthly fees depending on platforms, number of posts and whether ad management is included, and ad budgets are extra. Ask for a written scope listing posts per month, platforms and reporting, and compare at least two proposals.

### How long does it take to see results from online marketing?
Directory listings and Google profiles can bring enquiries within days of going live, especially for urgent services. Reviews build trust over weeks. Social media usually takes a few months of consistent posting to grow an audience. Track how each new customer found you so you know which efforts are paying off.

### What is the fastest way to get customers online?
Be visible where people are already searching. Complete your directory listing and Google Business Profile with accurate details, photos and a WhatsApp number, and answer enquiries quickly. A featured listing or local ad can add visibility immediately. Social media is valuable but slower, because you have to build an audience first.
:::
