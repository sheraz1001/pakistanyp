Renting a house in Pakistan comes down to three things done properly: a written rent agreement on stamp paper, a clear understanding of the advance and security deposit, and registering the tenant with the local police. A good rent agreement in Pakistan names both parties, describes the property, fixes the rent, deposit, term and annual increase, and sets out who pays utilities and repairs. Both sides sign, with two witnesses.

Each province has its own rent law, and tenant registration with the police is mandatory in Punjab, Sindh and Islamabad. This guide explains what to put in the agreement, how deposits usually work, what the law expects in each region and the steps to follow from the first viewing to moving in.

:::takeaways
- Always sign a written rent agreement on stamp paper, with two witnesses.
- Agree the advance, security deposit, term and yearly increase in writing before paying anything.
- Tenant registration with the police is a legal duty in Punjab, Sindh and Islamabad.
- Each province has its own rent law; Punjab's 2009 Act requires written tenancy agreements.
- Photograph the property's condition on move-in to protect your security deposit.
:::

## What a rent agreement in Pakistan should include

A rent agreement, also called a tenancy agreement or lease deed, is a contract between landlord and tenant. Property dealers often use a standard format, but read every line and add what is missing.

| Clause | What it should say |
| --- | --- |
| Parties | Full names, CNIC numbers and addresses of landlord and tenant |
| Property | Full address, portion rented (whole house, upper portion, flat), and fixtures included |
| Rent | Monthly amount, due date and payment method (bank transfer preferred) |
| Term | Start date and duration, commonly 11 months or one year |
| Annual increase | Fixed percentage increase on renewal |
| Security deposit | Amount, conditions for deductions and refund timeline |
| Advance | Months of rent paid upfront, if any |
| Utilities | Who pays electricity, gas, water and maintenance charges |
| Repairs | Who handles minor and major repairs |
| Use | Residential or commercial; limits on subletting |
| Notice period | Notice required by either side to end the tenancy |
| Signatures | Both parties and two witnesses, with CNIC numbers |

Attach an inventory of fittings, such as fans, lights, geysers and kitchen fixtures, and note their condition.

> [!TIP]
> Pay rent by bank transfer and keep the receipts. It gives both sides a clear payment record and avoids disputes over cash payments.

## Advance and security deposit: how they usually work

Two different upfront payments are common in Pakistan, and people often confuse them:

- **Security deposit:** held by the landlord and refundable at the end of the tenancy, minus agreed deductions for damage or unpaid bills. It is commonly equal to a few months' rent, depending on city and property.
- **Advance rent:** rent paid ahead for one or more months, adjusted against future rent.

Amounts are negotiable and vary by city, area and landlord. Write both figures separately in the agreement, with the refund terms for the deposit. Never pay a deposit before the agreement is signed.

If you rent through a property dealer, a commission is usually charged, often to the tenant, the landlord or both. Agree the amount before viewings start. ## Stamp paper and registration of the rent agreement

Rent agreements are usually written on stamp paper. Stamp duty depends on your province and on the rent and term, so ask your stamp vendor, property dealer or lawyer what applies. In Punjab, e-stamp papers are widely used and can be generated online.

**Punjab** goes further. The Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009 says a landlord may not let premises except through a written tenancy agreement, and it provides for agreements to be presented to the Rent Registrar. A registered agreement is strong evidence in any later dispute. Ask your lawyer or dealer about the current registration procedure and deadline, as practice varies by district.

For help drafting or checking an agreement, look for [legal documentation services](/categories/legal-documentation) or [property lawyers](/categories/lawyers-legal-services) in your city.

> [!NOTE]
> An agreement typed on plain paper is still a contract, but stamp paper and proper witnessing make it far easier to rely on in court or before a rent controller.

## Rent agreement Pakistan law: Punjab, Sindh and Islamabad

Rent disputes are heard by rent tribunals or rent controllers under provincial laws:

- **[Punjab](/punjab):** the Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009 covers tenancy agreements, rent, eviction and the role of the Rent Registrar and Rent Tribunals.
- **Sindh:** the Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance, 1979 governs tenancies in [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Hyderabad](/cities/hyderabad) and other Sindh cities, with rent controllers deciding disputes.
- **Islamabad:** the Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance, 2001 applies in the [capital territory](/islamabad).
- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan** have their own rent laws.

If a dispute arises over rent, eviction or the deposit, speak to a lawyer early. Keep copies of the agreement, payment receipts and any written notices.

## Police verification and tenant registration

Landlords, tenants and property dealers share a legal duty to register tenants with the police in several regions.

### Punjab

Under the Punjab Information of Temporary Residents Act, 2015, tenant details must be registered with the local police. You can register at the local police station or a Police Khidmat Markaz. The Punjab Police runs a Tenant Registration System for this. Typically you need copies of both CNICs, recent photographs of owner and tenant, and the rent agreement.

### Sindh

In Sindh, the Sindh Information of Temporary Residents Act, 2015 requires owners, tenants and property dealers to register tenant details with the concerned police station. Karachi Police accepts registration at police stations and facilitation centres, and Sindh Police has introduced an online tenant registration system.

### Islamabad

Islamabad Capital Territory Police registers tenants at its Police Khidmat Markaz in F-6/1, at H-11 Police Lines, or at the nearest police station. Bring original and copies of the tenant's and owner's CNICs.

> [!WARNING]
> Failing to register a tenant can lead to police action against the landlord and, in some cases, the tenant and dealer. Register within days of the tenant moving in and keep the registration slip.

Find your local police station under [law and order services](/categories/law-order).

## Step-by-step: renting a house in Pakistan

:::steps From viewing to moving in
- Shortlist | Compare areas, rents and commute; view at least three properties.
- Check | Verify the owner's CNIC and ownership documents, and test water, power and gas.
- Agree terms | Settle rent, deposit, advance, term, increase and notice period.
- Sign | Sign the agreement on stamp paper with two witnesses; pay by bank transfer.
- Register | Register the tenancy with the police and, in Punjab, the Rent Registrar.
- Move in | Photograph the property, note meter readings and hire movers.

## Tips for landlords

Landlords benefit from the same discipline:

- Verify the tenant's CNIC and employer, and ask for a previous landlord's reference.
- Register the tenant with the police promptly; the legal duty falls on you as well.
- Keep the property in the condition described in the agreement and fix major repairs quickly.
- Return the security deposit promptly at the end, with a written list of any deductions.

## Final word: put everything in writing

A clear rent agreement in Pakistan protects both landlord and tenant. Write down every term, sign on stamp paper with witnesses, pay by bank transfer and register with the police. Most rental disputes start with something that was only agreed verbally.

:::faq
### What should a rent agreement in Pakistan include?
A rent agreement should include the full names, CNIC numbers and addresses of landlord and tenant, the property address, monthly rent and due date, term, annual increase, security deposit and advance, utilities, repairs, permitted use, notice period and signatures of both parties with two witnesses. Attach an inventory of fittings and their condition.

### Is a rent agreement on stamp paper necessary in Pakistan?
It is strongly recommended. A rent agreement on stamp paper, properly signed and witnessed, is much easier to rely on before a rent controller or court. Stamp duty depends on the province, rent and term, so check with a stamp vendor or lawyer. In Punjab, e-stamp papers can be generated online and are widely used for rent agreements.

### How much advance and security deposit do landlords ask for?
It varies by city, area and landlord. A refundable security deposit equal to a few months' rent is common, and some landlords also ask for one or more months of rent in advance. Both are negotiable. Write each amount separately in the agreement, with clear conditions for refunding the security deposit.

### Is police verification of tenants compulsory?
Yes, in several regions. In Punjab and Sindh, temporary residents laws passed in 2015 require owners, tenants and property dealers to register tenant details with the police. Islamabad Police also registers tenants at its Khidmat Markaz and police stations. Failing to register can lead to police action, so register as soon as the tenant moves in.

### How do I register a tenant with the police in Punjab?
Visit your local police station or a Police Khidmat Markaz with copies of the landlord's and tenant's CNICs, recent photographs and the signed rent agreement. The details are entered into the Punjab Police Tenant Registration System. Keep the registration confirmation with your agreement, as police may ask for it during checks.

### How long is a typical rent agreement in Pakistan?
Many residential rent agreements run for 11 months or one year, with the option to renew. The term should be stated clearly, along with the annual increase on renewal and the notice period either side must give to end the tenancy. Longer terms can be agreed, especially for commercial premises.

### Can a landlord evict a tenant without notice in Pakistan?
A landlord should follow the notice period in the agreement and the provincial rent law. Disputes over eviction are decided by rent tribunals or rent controllers, not by force. If a landlord tries to remove a tenant without due process, or a tenant refuses to leave after the term, either side should seek legal advice and keep all documents.

### Who pays the property dealer's commission?
Practice varies by city and dealer. Commission may be charged to the tenant, the landlord or both, and amounts differ. Agree the commission in writing before you start viewing properties, and ask exactly what the dealer will do, such as drafting the agreement or helping with police registration, for that fee.

### Do I need to register my rent agreement in Punjab?
The Punjab Rented Premises Act, 2009 requires tenancies to be made through a written agreement and provides for the agreement to be presented to the Rent Registrar. A registered agreement is strong evidence if a dispute arises. Ask a lawyer or your property dealer about the current registration procedure and timeline in your district.

### How can a tenant protect the security deposit?
Photograph and video the property on move-in, attach an inventory of fittings to the agreement and note meter readings. Pay rent by bank transfer, report damage in writing and leave the property in the same condition. At the end, ask for a written list of any deductions before handing back the keys.
:::
