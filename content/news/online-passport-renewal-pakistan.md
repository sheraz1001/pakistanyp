**Passport renewal online in Pakistan** is handled by the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGI&P), which lets eligible citizens at home and abroad apply, pay and track a renewal without first queuing at a passport office. In most cases you can use the online route if your current passport has expired or has less than a year left, and your personal details have not changed.

One big change took effect last month. DGI&P announced that from **23 October 2026** it would stop issuing machine-readable passports (MRPs), so every new passport, including renewals, is now an e-passport. This explainer covers what that means, the fees reported on DGI&P's site, and the steps to follow.

:::takeaways
- DGI&P's online renewal portal serves applicants in Pakistan and overseas.
- Online renewal is generally for passports that have expired or have less than one year of validity, where your details match your old passport and NADRA record.
- DGI&P stopped issuing machine-readable passports from 23 October 2026; all new passports are e-passports.
- Existing machine-readable passports remain valid until their printed expiry date.
- Fees are paid online by card, are reported to be non-refundable, and vary by page count, validity and speed.
:::

## The switch to e-passports in October 2026

Pakistan began issuing e-passports, which carry a contactless chip holding the holder's biometric and personal data, in March 2022, starting with diplomats and Islamabad residents. The rollout then widened to other cities and missions.

In late September 2026, several outlets reported that MRP issuance would end on 15 October. That date moved. On 22 October 2026, DGI&P posted a public announcement on its official X account: from 23 October 2026, machine-readable passports would be discontinued, and only e-passports would be issued at passport offices across Pakistan and at Pakistani missions abroad. The News reported on 24 October that the change had taken effect.

:::stats Pakistan's passport changes at a glance
- March 2022 | e-passport issuance began
- 23 Oct 2026 | machine-readable passports discontinued
- Under 1 year | validity left to qualify for online renewal
- 100% | of new passports now issued as e-passports
:::

> [!NOTE]
> If you hold a valid machine-readable passport, you do not need to replace it early. It stays valid until its expiry date. When you renew, you will receive an e-passport.

## Who can use passport renewal online in Pakistan

DGI&P's guidance, as summarised by several 2026 guides, sets out the basic conditions for the online route:

| Situation | Can you renew online? (general guide) |
| --- | --- |
| Passport expired, or less than 1 year validity left | Generally yes |
| Details match your old passport and NADRA record | Required |
| Change of name, date of birth or other personal details | Usually no; visit a passport office |
| First passport for an adult | Usually no; apply in person |
| Lost or damaged passport | Usually no; follow the lost passport procedure |
| Living abroad with a valid NICOP or CNIC | Generally yes, through the overseas option |

If your CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card) details have changed, for example after marriage, update your NADRA record first. Mismatches between NADRA and passport data are the most common reason applications are held.

## Passport fees in 2026: what DGI&P lists

Fees depend on the number of pages, the validity period and the processing speed. A guide that checked DGI&P's fee page on 8 October 2026 reported the following inland fees for a 36-page e-passport. DGI&P has also listed 72- and 100-page options at higher fees.

:::bars Reported e-passport fees, 36 pages, inland (Rs, indicative)
- 5 years, normal | 9000 | Rs9,000
- 5 years, urgent | 15000 | Rs15,000
- 10 years, normal | 13500 | Rs13,500
- 10 years, urgent | 22500 | Rs22,500
:::

> [!WARNING]
> Fees can change without much notice, and overseas fees are charged differently from inland fees. Always check the current fee on DGI&P's official fee page before paying. Guides note that online fees are not refundable or transferable, so make sure you are eligible first.

## Passport renewal online in Pakistan: step by step

:::steps Online passport renewal in 6 steps
- Check eligibility | Confirm your passport has expired or has under a year left, and your details are unchanged.
- Open the portal | Go to DGI&P's online renewal portal and choose the inland or overseas option.
- Enter your details | Fill in your CNIC or NICOP and current passport details, then choose pages, validity and speed.
- Upload photo and documents | Upload a recent photograph and any scans requested, following DGI&P's photo rules.
- Pay online | Pay the fee by debit or credit card and save the receipt and tracking ID.
- Track and receive | Track progress online; the new passport is delivered by courier to your address.
:::

### Documents to keep ready

:::checklist
- Your current or expired passport, with its number and dates
- Valid CNIC or NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis)
- A recent passport-style photograph meeting DGI&P's size and background rules
- A debit or credit card that can make online payments
- An accurate postal address and an active mobile number and email
- Any extra document the portal requests, such as a signature or fingerprint scan
:::

> [!TIP]
> Use a well-lit, plain background for your photo and remove glasses. Photo rejections are a common cause of delay, and some studios and [visa and travel document services](/categories/visas-travel-documents) offer DGI&P-compliant photos.

## Overseas Pakistanis: renewing from abroad

The online service is open to Pakistanis living abroad, who apply through the overseas option on DGI&P's site rather than visiting an embassy queue. Fees for overseas applicants are set separately from inland fees and are not the same as the rupee amounts above, so check the overseas table on DGI&P's site or with your nearest Pakistani mission.

During the 23 October transition, one outlet reported a temporary technical suspension of passport services at the embassy in Muscat. If you are abroad, check your mission's notices before booking travel around a renewal. For related paperwork, such as attestation or NICOP queries, PakistanYP lists [overseas Pakistani services](/categories/overseas-pakistani-services) and [document attestation](/categories/documents-attestation) providers.

## Processing times and delivery

DGI&P offers normal, urgent and, in some cases, fast-track processing. Reported timelines vary, and some guides say fast-track options differ for e-passports, so treat any quoted number of days as a guide only. Courier time is added on top of processing.

Plan around real deadlines. If you need a passport for Hajj, Umrah, a study visa or a job abroad, apply early and choose urgent processing if the dates are tight. Travel agents and [Hajj and Umrah operators](/categories/hajj-umrah) usually need a passport with at least six months' validity for visa processing. ## When you still need to visit a passport office

The online route does not cover every case. You will generally need an office appointment for a first adult passport, a lost or damaged passport, changes to personal details, and some cases involving minors. Passport offices operate in all major cities, including [Islamabad](/cities/islamabad), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) and [Karachi](/cities/karachi). Search [identity document offices](/categories/identity-documents) on PakistanYP for local listings, and confirm hours before you go.

## Final word

The October 2026 switch means every renewal now produces an e-passport, but passport renewal online in Pakistan works the same way: check eligibility, apply on DGI&P's portal, pay by card and wait for the courier. Fees and timelines change, so rely on the official pages, not on screenshots shared on social media.

Your next step: check your passport's expiry date today, and if it is within a year, start the application on [DGI&P's official website](https://dgip.gov.pk).

:::faq
### Can I renew my Pakistani passport online?
Yes, if you are eligible. DGI&P's online renewal service lets citizens in Pakistan and abroad renew a passport that has expired or has less than one year of validity, provided their details match the old passport and NADRA record. You apply, pay and track online, and the new passport is delivered by courier.

### Is the machine-readable passport still issued?
No. DGI&P announced on 22 October 2026 that machine-readable passports would be discontinued from 23 October 2026, and only e-passports would be issued at passport offices in Pakistan and at missions abroad. Existing machine-readable passports remain valid until their printed expiry date, after which you renew to an e-passport.

### What is the e-passport fee in Pakistan in 2026?
A guide that checked DGI&P's site on 8 October 2026 reported Rs9,000 normal and Rs15,000 urgent for a 36-page, five-year e-passport, and Rs13,500 or Rs22,500 for ten years. Larger booklets cost more, and overseas fees differ. Check DGI&P's official fee page before paying, as fees can change.

### How long does online passport renewal take?
It depends on whether you choose normal, urgent or fast-track processing, plus courier time. Reported timelines vary and some services differ for e-passports, so treat quoted days as indicative. If you have a travel or visa deadline, apply well ahead and choose a faster category. Track progress through the DGI&P portal using your tracking ID.

### Can I renew my passport online if my name has changed?
Generally no. The online route assumes your details are unchanged and match your NADRA record. If your name or other details have changed, update your CNIC with NADRA first, then apply at a passport office, where officials can verify documents. Check DGI&P's guidance for your specific case before applying.

### Is the online passport fee refundable?
Guides citing DGI&P's online service say the fee paid online is not refundable or transferable. That makes it important to confirm eligibility before paying, especially if your details have changed or your passport still has more than a year of validity. Keep your payment receipt and tracking ID until the passport arrives.

### Can overseas Pakistanis renew passports online?
Yes. DGI&P's online service includes an overseas option for Pakistanis living abroad, using a CNIC or NICOP. Overseas fees are set separately from inland rupee fees. Check the overseas fee table on DGI&P's website or with your nearest Pakistani mission, and follow its notices about local delivery and collection.

### Do I need to replace my valid machine-readable passport?
No. Machine-readable passports remain valid until their printed expiry date, according to reports of DGI&P's transition. You only need an e-passport when you renew, or earlier if a destination or employer specifically asks for one. Check your passport's expiry, as many countries require at least six months' validity to issue a visa.

### What photo is required for passport renewal?
DGI&P sets specific rules for online photographs, typically a recent colour photo with a plain light background, a neutral expression, no glasses and your face clearly visible. The portal shows the size and file requirements. Photos that do not meet the rules are a common reason for delay, so check them before uploading.

### How do I track my passport renewal?
After paying, you receive a tracking ID by SMS or email. You can enter it on DGI&P's website to see the application status, from processing to printing and dispatch. Once the courier collects it, you may also receive a courier tracking number. Contact DGI&P's helpline if the status does not change for an unusually long time.
:::
