**PakistanYP city guides** are now live. Alongside the business listings on each province and city page, PakistanYP has added 31 written guides: one for each of Pakistan's seven provinces and territories, and one for each of 24 major cities, from Karachi and Lahore to Gilgit, Quetta and Mirpur.

Each guide answers the questions people ask before they search for a business in a place they do not know well. What is the area code? Where are the main markets? Which industries drive the local economy, and which chamber of commerce represents traders? The guides sit next to the listings, so you can read about a city and then find the shop, clinic or contractor you need.

:::takeaways
- PakistanYP has published 31 guides: 7 for provinces and territories and 24 for major cities.
- Every city guide lists the local telephone area code, and 23 of the 24 cite 2023 census population figures.
- Guides cover key facts, the economy and main industries, main business areas and markets, and how to find businesses locally.
- Each guide ends with four frequently asked questions, 124 answers in total.
- The guides appear on the province and city pages, alongside the businesses listed there.
:::

## What the PakistanYP city guides cover

Every guide follows the same structure, so you can compare places quickly.

| Section | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| Key facts | Province or status, population, telephone area code, airports, chambers of commerce, main industries |
| Economy and main industries | What the local economy is built on, from textiles and sports goods to tourism and government |
| Main business areas and markets | The bazaars, industrial estates and commercial districts where trade happens |
| Finding businesses | The services people most often search for locally |
| Frequently asked questions | Four short answers per guide, such as dialling codes and where to find markets |

Some guides add sections that matter for that place. The Islamabad guides explain the capital's sector system and federal institutions, and other guides cover topics such as ports and logistics, trade with Afghanistan and seasonal travel.

## Province and territory guides

There is one guide for each of the four provinces and three territories:

- [Punjab](/punjab), the most populous province, with a manufacturing base across Faisalabad, Sialkot, Gujranwala and Multan
- [Sindh](/sindh), home to Karachi's ports and large industrial estates such as SITE and Korangi
- [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](/khyber-pakhtunkhwa), with Peshawar's border trade through Torkham and industrial estates at Hattar, Gadoon and Rashakai
- [Balochistan](/balochistan), the province with the smallest population of the four, centred on Quetta
- [Islamabad Capital Territory](/islamabad), including how the sector system shapes where businesses are
- [Gilgit-Baltistan](/gilgit-baltistan), with Gilgit as its main city and the Karakoram Highway as its trade route
- [Azad Jammu and Kashmir](/azad-kashmir), with Muzaffarabad as its capital and Mirpur's industrial estates

Population figures come from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics' 2023 census where it covers the area. For Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir, the guides explain which official figures are available.

## Examples from the guides

A few facts from the guides show the kind of detail they include:

- **[Karachi](/cities/karachi):** area code 021, Karachi Port and Port Qasim, and a Karachi Division population of 20,382,881 in the 2023 census.
- **[Lahore](/cities/lahore):** area code 042, a Lahore district population of 13,004,135, and the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry, founded in 1923.
- **[Sialkot](/cities/sialkot):** area code 052, known for sports goods, surgical instruments and leather, with an international airport built with funds from the local business community.
- **[Quetta](/cities/quetta):** area code 081, the Chaman crossing to Afghanistan, and markets known for fresh fruit, dry fruit, carpets and handicrafts.

> [!NOTE]
> The guides use official sources where they exist, such as census tables, and say so when an official figure is not available. If you spot something out of date, contact the PakistanYP team through the [Contact us page](/contact-us).

### What are PakistanYP city guides?
They are written guides published on PakistanYP's province and city pages. Each one summarises a place's key facts, economy, main business areas and markets, and the services people most often look for there, followed by four frequently asked questions. They sit alongside the business listings, so you can read about a city and find local businesses in one place.

### How many guides has PakistanYP published?
PakistanYP has published 31 guides: seven covering the provinces and territories of Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and 24 covering major cities. Twelve of the city guides are in Punjab, four in Sindh, three in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and two in Azad Jammu and Kashmir.

### Which cities have guides on PakistanYP?
The 24 city guides cover Abbottabad, Bahawalpur, Faisalabad, Gilgit, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Hyderabad, Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Larkana, Mardan, Mirpur, Multan, Muzaffarabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Rahim Yar Khan, Rawalpindi, Sahiwal, Sargodha, Sheikhupura, Sialkot and Sukkur. Each has its own city page with local listings.

### What information is in each city guide?
Each guide includes key facts such as the province, population, telephone area code, airports and chambers of commerce, followed by sections on the economy and main industries, the main business areas and markets, and finding businesses locally. Some guides add sections on topics such as logistics, heritage or getting around.

### Where does the population data come from?
Where available, the guides use the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics' 2023 census results. Twenty-three of the 24 city guides cite 2023 census figures. For Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan, the guides explain which official figures are available, because coverage of the 2023 census results differs for those territories.

### Do the guides list telephone area codes?
Yes. Every city guide lists the local telephone area code in its key facts, such as 021 for Karachi, 042 for Lahore, 091 for Peshawar and 081 for Quetta. Most also explain how to dial the number from abroad. You can use the code to check that a landline number belongs to the city you expect.

### Are the guides free to read?
Yes. The guides are free to read on the province and city pages, like the rest of the directory. You do not need an account to read a guide, search listings or contact a business. Businesses can also list themselves free of charge.

### How can my business appear on a city page?
Add your business through the free listing form, choosing your province, city and locality, your main category and your contact details. Listings are reviewed before they go live. Once approved, your business appears in the relevant city and category pages, beside the city guide.

### Can I suggest a correction to a guide?
Yes. If you notice a fact that is out of date or incorrect, such as a market that has moved or a new chamber of commerce, contact our team through the Contact us page with the details and, if possible, an official source. This helps keep the guides accurate for everyone.

### How do the guides help me find a trustworthy business?
The guides help you understand where to look, for example which market or industrial estate is known for what. You can then compare local listings on the same page, check reviews and look for verified badges, which show that a business has confirmed its phone number and supplied registration documents.
