**PTA mobile registration** is the process of recording a phone's IMEI (its unique 15-digit identity number) with the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority's Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System (DIRBS). A phone bought from an authorised dealer in Pakistan is already registered. A phone you bring from abroad must be registered, and the applicable duties and taxes paid, or it will eventually stop working on Pakistani networks.

The second half of the picture is SIM verification. Every SIM in Pakistan must be issued against a biometrically verified CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card), and PTA offers simple ways to check how many SIMs are registered in your name. This explainer covers both, including the changes to phone taxes in the 2026-27 budget.

:::takeaways
- PTA launched DIRBS on 10 May 2018; it identifies, registers and blocks mobile devices by IMEI.
- You can check any phone's status by sending its IMEI by SMS to 8484 before you buy.
- From 1 July 2026, the Finance Act allows the taxes on an imported phone to be paid in instalments, as reported.
- Overseas Pakistanis and foreigners can register a phone free for temporary use of up to 120 days per visit.
- You can check how many SIMs are registered on your CNIC through PTA's 668 service.
:::

## How DIRBS and PTA mobile registration work

PTA launched DIRBS on 10 May 2018 under the Telecom Policy 2015. Its aim was to cut smuggling and theft by making every device on Pakistani networks traceable through its IMEI. Phones with non-standard or duplicate IMEIs were to be blocked from 1 December 2018, as PTA announced at the time, although deadlines for some categories were later moved.

A device falls into one of three broad states:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliant / registered | IMEI registered and duties paid | Nothing; the phone works normally |
| Non-compliant (unregistered imported phone) | Imported device without duties paid | Register on DIRBS and pay taxes |
| Blocked | Lost, stolen, cloned or non-standard IMEI | Do not buy; report to PTA if it is yours |

:::stats PTA mobile and SIM checks at a glance
- 8484 | SMS your IMEI to check a phone's status
- 668 | SMS service to check SIMs on your CNIC
- 120 days | free temporary use for overseas visitors
- 2018 | year PTA launched DIRBS
:::

> [!TIP]
> Before buying a used phone, dial *#06# to see its IMEI, then send it by SMS to 8484. If the reply says the device is not compliant or is blocked, walk away or negotiate the tax into the price.

## Registering a phone you bring from abroad

If you buy a phone abroad or receive one as a gift, you can register it through PTA's Device Registration System (DRS). Guides commonly state that you should register within 60 days of arrival; check the exact window on the DIRBS portal, because unregistered devices are eventually blocked.

:::steps Registering an imported phone with PTA
- Find the IMEI | Dial *#06#; dual-SIM phones show two IMEIs, and both must be registered.
- Sign up on DIRBS | Create an account on PTA's Device Registration System using your CNIC, NICOP or passport.
- Submit the device | Enter the IMEIs and choose permanent registration (or temporary, if eligible).
- Pay the taxes | Generate the payment slip (PSID) and pay through a bank or app, now with an instalment option.
- Confirm compliance | Check the IMEI again on 8484 after payment; the phone should show as compliant.
:::

Many [mobile phone shops](/categories/mobile-phones) in markets across [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) and [Rawalpindi](/cities/rawalpindi) will also help with registration for a fee. Ask for a receipt showing the PSID and amount paid.

## PTA tax in the 2026-27 budget: instalments and changes

The taxes on imported phones are set by the federal government in the budget, not by PTA, and they are collected through DIRBS. Two changes for 2026-27 were reported in June 2026:

1. **Instalments.** The Finance Act 2026 allows individuals to pay the taxes on an imported phone, new or used, in instalments from 1 July 2026, with the full amount due by the end of the financial year of import, as reported by ProPakistani and The Nation.
2. **Mid-range phones.** ProPakistani reported that duties and taxes on phones with a customs value of $101 to $200 were reduced from 1 July 2026, while the 18% sales tax on that bracket stayed the same.

Not everything changed. TechJuice reported that proposals to cut sales tax on premium phones did not make it into the final Finance Bill. Geo News reported that relief for overseas Pakistanis was "under review".

> [!WARNING]
> The tax on a specific phone depends on its customs value, which FBR sets, and on whether you register with a CNIC or passport. Unofficial "PTA tax calculators" often give conflicting figures. Use the amount shown on the DIRBS portal when you generate the payment slip.

## Overseas Pakistanis: free temporary use for 120 days

PTA allows overseas Pakistanis and foreign nationals to register a personal phone for temporary use of up to 120 days per visit, free of charge, through the DRS portal. PTA announced the facility in an April 2025 notice. Pakistanis use their CNIC or NICOP, and foreigners use their passport.

After the period ends, the phone is blocked on local networks unless you pay the taxes for permanent registration. The facility can be used again on later visits. ## SIM verification: checking SIMs on your CNIC

Every SIM in Pakistan must be issued after biometric verification of the buyer's CNIC at a franchise or authorised retailer. This is meant to stop SIMs being issued in someone's name without their knowledge, but misuse still happens, so check regularly.

:::checklist
- Send your CNIC number (without dashes) by SMS to 668 to see how many SIMs are registered in your name
- Compare the count with the SIMs you actually use, including data SIMs
- If a number is not yours, visit the relevant operator's franchise with your original CNIC to block it
- Never give your thumbprint to street vendors offering "free" SIMs or prizes
- Keep the SIMs you use registered in your own name, not a relative's
:::

### How many SIMs can you have on one CNIC?

Reports conflict. In 2015, the Supreme Court allowed operators to issue up to eight SIMs per person, including data SIMs. More recently, Daily Ausaf reported that the PTA chairman told the National Assembly Standing Committee on Interior that a maximum of five SIMs can be registered against one CNIC. We could not find a PTA notification settling whether data SIMs are counted separately, so check with PTA or your operator.

> [!NOTE]
> PTA's consumer complaint portal is the place to report SIMs or devices you believe are misused. Keep a record of your complaint number.

## Final word

PTA mobile registration protects buyers from stolen and smuggled phones, but it also means every imported phone needs to be registered and taxed. The 2026-27 instalment option makes that easier, and 8484 and 668 let you check a phone or your SIMs in seconds.

Your next step: check the IMEI of any phone you plan to buy, and send your CNIC to 668 today to confirm the SIMs in your name. For official guidance, use the [PTA DIRBS portal](https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk) and [PTA's website](https://www.pta.gov.pk).

:::faq
### What is PTA mobile registration?
It is the registration of a phone's IMEI number with PTA's DIRBS system, so the device can work on Pakistani mobile networks. Phones sold by authorised dealers in Pakistan are already registered. Phones brought from abroad must be registered through PTA's Device Registration System, and the applicable duties and taxes paid, or they will be blocked.

### How do I check if my phone is PTA approved?
Dial *#06# to see the phone's IMEI, then send the 15-digit IMEI by SMS to 8484. PTA replies with the device's status, such as compliant, non-compliant or blocked. Dual-SIM phones have two IMEIs, so check both. You can also check on the DIRBS website before buying a used phone.

### Can I pay PTA tax in instalments?
Yes, from 1 July 2026. The Finance Act 2026 allows individuals to pay the taxes on imported mobile phones, new or used, in instalments, with the full amount due by the end of the financial year in which the phone was imported, as reported in June 2026. The payment options appear when you generate the payment slip on DIRBS.

### Was PTA tax reduced in the 2026-27 budget?
Partly. ProPakistani reported that duties and taxes on phones with a customs value of $101 to $200 were reduced from 1 July 2026, though the 18% sales tax on that bracket was unchanged. TechJuice reported that proposed cuts on premium phones were dropped. Check the amount shown on DIRBS for your specific phone.

### How long can overseas Pakistanis use a phone without paying tax?
PTA allows overseas Pakistanis and foreign nationals to register a personal phone free for temporary use of up to 120 days per visit, through the DIRBS Device Registration System. After that, the phone is blocked unless taxes are paid for permanent registration. The facility can be used again on future visits.

### How many days do I have to register an imported phone?
Guides commonly say a phone brought from abroad should be registered within 60 days of arrival, after which it can be blocked. Because rules and grace periods can change, check the current window on the DIRBS portal or PTA's website as soon as you arrive, and keep your travel documents handy.

### How do I check how many SIMs are on my CNIC?
Send your CNIC number without dashes by SMS to 668. The reply shows how many SIMs are registered in your name with each operator, but not the phone numbers. If the count is higher than expected, visit the operator's franchise with your original CNIC to identify and block SIMs you do not use.

### How many SIMs are allowed on one CNIC in Pakistan?
Sources differ. A 2015 Supreme Court decision allowed up to eight SIMs per person, including data SIMs. Recent reports quote the PTA chairman telling a National Assembly committee that five SIMs is the maximum per CNIC. Confirm the current limit with PTA or your mobile operator before trying to register another SIM.

### What happens if I buy a non-PTA phone?
A non-compliant phone may work with a SIM for a short period, but it will be blocked on Pakistani networks unless its taxes are paid and it is registered on DIRBS. Many buyers use non-PTA phones only on Wi-Fi. Factor the registration tax into the price, and check the IMEI on 8484 before paying.

### Is PTA registration different for a CNIC and a passport?
Yes. The registration route and tax amount can differ depending on whether you register with a CNIC or a passport, and some reports say certain taxes do not apply to passport-based registration. The DIRBS portal shows the applicable amount for your case when you generate the payment slip, which is the figure to rely on.
