**Ecommerce in Pakistan** is growing because the way people pay has changed. The State Bank of Pakistan's (SBP) Annual Payment Systems Review for 2025-26, released in October 2026, reported that digital channels handled 92% of retail payments, and mobile banking and wallet transactions rose sharply. For a local shop, that means customers increasingly expect to find you online, message you on WhatsApp and pay without cash.

The direct answer for small businesses is that you do not need to become an online-only brand. You need to be findable, accept digital payments, and understand the tax rules that now apply to online and cash-on-delivery (COD) sales. This explainer covers what the official data shows, what changed in the 2025 and 2026 budgets, and practical steps for neighbourhood shops.

:::takeaways
- The SBP reported that digital channels accounted for 92% of retail payments in 2025-26, up from 88% the year before.
- Mobile-based payments led the growth, with more than 11 billion transactions in the year, as reported.
- Account-based payments made up 96% of e-commerce payments through banking channels, according to the SBP review.
- Since the 2025 budget, couriers deduct tax on cash-on-delivery sales; the 2026-27 budget made this tax adjustable for sellers with turnover above Rs200 million.
- Local shops gain most by combining a physical presence with online discovery, WhatsApp ordering and Raast QR payments.
:::

## What the SBP data says about ecommerce in Pakistan

The SBP does not publish a single "e-commerce market size", but its payment data is the most reliable official indicator. As reported by Profit, ProPakistani and other outlets in early October 2026, the 2025-26 review showed:

:::stats Digital payments in 2025-26 (SBP review, as reported)
- 92% | share of retail payments made digitally
- 13.2bn | digital payment transactions, up about 65%
- 11.1bn | mobile banking and wallet transactions, up about 79%
- 96% | of e-commerce payments via banking channels were account-based
:::

The same review reported about 300 million internet banking transactions, up around 15%, and roughly 1.5 million card payments a day at shop terminals, up from about 1 million a year earlier.

> [!NOTE]
> Figures for e-commerce market size quoted by private reports vary widely and are not official. If a supplier or platform quotes a market size, ask for the source. For payment data, use the SBP's own publications.

## Why the growth matters for local shops

The numbers point to a shift in habits rather than a move away from local shops. Most Pakistanis still buy groceries, clothes, medicines and hardware near home. What has changed is how they find shops and pay.

- **Search before visiting.** Customers check opening hours, location and phone numbers online.
- **WhatsApp ordering.** Many shops now take orders and send photos of stock on WhatsApp, then deliver locally.
- **Cashless payment.** With [Raast QR payments](/news/raast-digital-payments-what-businesses-need-to-know), customers can pay instantly from any bank or wallet app.
- **Social selling.** Boutiques and home businesses in cities such as [Lahore](/cities/lahore) and [Karachi](/cities/karachi) sell through Instagram and Facebook.

## Taxes on online and cash-on-delivery sales

Tax rules have followed the growth. Under the Finance Act 2025, courier companies were designated as collection agents and, as Business Recorder reported, began deducting 2% withholding income tax and 2% sales tax on COD sales from online sellers. Payment gateways also deduct tax on digital e-commerce payments.

In the 2026-27 budget, the FBR's salient features stated that tax deducted on e-commerce transactions would be adjustable for sellers with turnover above Rs200 million. Commentators have noted that for smaller sellers, the deduction generally remains a final liability.

| Sale type | What happens, as reported | Who should check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash on delivery via courier | Courier deducts withholding income tax and sales tax before paying you | Any online seller using COD |
| Digital payment via gateway | Gateway or bank deducts tax on the receipt | Sellers with online checkout |
| In-store sale paid by Raast QR | Normal income and sales tax rules for your business apply | All shops |
| Seller turnover above Rs200 million | E-commerce deductions adjustable from 2026-27 | Larger sellers |

> [!WARNING]
> Rates differ for filers and non-filers and have changed in each recent budget. Ask your courier and gateway for their deduction statements, and confirm current rates with the FBR or a tax adviser. Small retailers should also check the [2026 fixed tax for shopkeepers](/news/fbr-retailer-registration-tajir-dost-explained).

## How a local shop can start selling online

You can start small and test demand before spending on a full online store.

:::steps Getting a local shop online in 5 steps
- Get listed | Create free listings with accurate address, hours, phone and photos.
- Open WhatsApp Business | Set up a catalogue with prices and a greeting that explains delivery areas.
- Accept digital payments | Get a Raast QR from your bank or wallet provider and display it at the counter.
- Arrange delivery | Use your own rider for nearby orders and a courier for other cities.
- Track what sells | Note which products get online enquiries and stock accordingly.
:::

### How big is ecommerce in Pakistan?
There is no single official market size figure. The best official indicator is the SBP's payment data: its 2025-26 review reported that digital channels handled 92% of retail payments and about 13.2 billion digital transactions. Private estimates of the e-commerce market vary widely, so check the source and method behind any figure quoted to you.

### Is cash on delivery still common in Pakistan?
Yes, cash on delivery remains widely used for online orders, especially for first-time buyers and outside the largest cities, although there is no official figure for its share. Digital payment through Raast, wallets and cards is growing quickly. Many sellers offer both options and encourage prepayment with small discounts or free delivery.

### What tax do online sellers pay on cash on delivery?
Under the Finance Act 2025, couriers act as collection agents and, as reported by Business Recorder, deduct 2% withholding income tax and 2% sales tax on cash-on-delivery sales. Rates can differ for non-filers. From 2026-27, the deduction is adjustable for sellers with turnover above Rs200 million. Confirm current rates with the FBR or a tax adviser.

### Do I need to register with FBR to sell online?
Most businesses that earn regular income should have an NTN and file returns, whether they sell online or in store. Sales tax registration depends on your type of business and turnover. Being a filer also lowers the withholding rates you pay. Check the FBR's current rules or ask a tax practitioner about your situation.

### How can a small shop start selling online?
Start with a free business listing, a WhatsApp Business catalogue and a Raast QR for payments. Deliver nearby orders yourself and use a courier for other cities. Track which products get online interest before investing in a full website. This lets you test demand with very little upfront cost.

### What is the best payment method for online sellers in Pakistan?
Many sellers offer a mix: Raast QR or Raast ID transfers for instant bank payments, mobile wallets, cards through a payment gateway, and cash on delivery. Raast payments settle in seconds and are free for customers. Compare each provider's fees and settlement times, and keep records for tax filing.

### What did the SBP's 2025-26 payment review show?
Released in October 2026, the review reported that digital payments made up 92% of retail payments, up from 88%, with about 13.2 billion digital transactions. Mobile-based payments exceeded 11 billion transactions, and account-based payments made up 96% of e-commerce payments through banking channels, as reported in the business press.

### Should my shop be on social media or a directory?
Ideally both. Social media is good for showing new stock and offers, while a directory listing helps people who are searching for a specific type of shop in a specific area. Keep your name, address, phone and hours consistent everywhere, so customers and search engines see the same details.

### How do couriers handle cash-on-delivery payments?
The courier collects cash from the buyer, deducts its delivery charges and any taxes it must withhold, and pays the balance into your account on an agreed schedule. Settlement times and return charges vary between companies. Ask for a written rate card and regular statements showing every deduction before you start.

### Can home-based businesses sell online legally?
Yes. Many home-based businesses, such as bakers, tailors and resellers, sell online. You should still keep records, consider getting an NTN and filing returns, and check any local rules, such as food authority requirements for food businesses. Clear pricing and delivery terms also help avoid disputes with customers.
