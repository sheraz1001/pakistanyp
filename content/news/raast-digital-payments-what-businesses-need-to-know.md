A **Raast payment** is an instant bank-to-bank transfer made through Raast, the payment system built and run by the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP). For a business, the part that matters is Raast P2M (person to merchant): a customer scans your Raast QR code with any bank or wallet app, and the money lands in your account within seconds, at no charge to the customer.

For shopkeepers, Raast is now mainstream and the pressure to accept it is rising. The SBP's latest annual review, published in October 2026, shows digital channels handled the large majority of retail payments in 2025-26, and fuel stations have been told to accept Raast QR by 31 January 2027. This explainer sets out what has been announced, by whom and when, and what you should check before signing up.

:::takeaways
- Raast is the SBP's own instant payment system; customers pay you by scanning a Raast QR code or using your Raast ID (usually your mobile number).
- The SBP rolled Raast out in stages: bulk payments in January 2021, person-to-person in February 2022 and person-to-merchant in late 2023.
- A government-funded Rs3.5 billion subsidy for Raast P2M QR payments covered 1 September 2025 to 30 June 2026. We found no official notice extending it.
- The SBP has told all fuel stations to accept Raast QR payments by 31 January 2027.
- A proposed law that would make retailers accept at least one digital payment mode was still a draft at the time of writing.
:::

## What a Raast payment is and how it differs from cards and wallets

Raast is a national payment rail, not an app. Banks, microfinance banks and electronic money institutions (EMIs, such as wallet providers) connect to it, so a customer at one bank can pay a merchant at another instantly through their usual app.

Each account can be linked to a **Raast ID**, usually a mobile number. A merchant can also display a Raast QR code: the customer scans it, confirms the amount, and the payment is final in seconds.

### How Raast P2M compares with other ways to get paid

| Method | Who pays fees | Speed of settlement | Hardware needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | None, but handling and security costs | Instant | None |
| Raast QR (P2M) | Free for the customer; merchant terms set by your bank or EMI | Seconds | A printed QR or phone |
| Debit/credit card on POS | Merchant pays a merchant discount rate (MDR) | Usually next working day | POS terminal |
| Wallet-to-wallet transfer | Depends on provider | Instant within the wallet | Phone |

> [!NOTE]
> Merchant pricing on Raast QR is set by the bank or EMI that signs you up, within SBP rules. Ask for the fee schedule in writing before you print a code.

## Raast timeline: how the SBP rolled out the system

Raast launched in phases, which is why many shops only started displaying Raast QR codes recently.

:::steps How Raast reached Pakistani shop counters
- January 2021 | The SBP went live with the first phase, letting accounts receive bulk payments such as salaries.
- February 2022 | Person-to-person (P2P) transfers launched, using mobile numbers as Raast IDs.
- September to December 2023 | The person-to-merchant (P2M) service was deployed in September and formally launched by SBP circular in December 2023.
- September 2025 | A Rs3.5 billion government subsidy for Raast P2M QR transactions took effect.
- October 2025 | The SBP said it planned to route all government payments through Raast by the end of 2025-26.
- August 2026 | The SBP set a 31 January 2027 deadline for fuel stations to accept Raast QR.
:::

## The Rs3.5 billion Raast P2M subsidy: what it covered

In 2025 the federal government approved a Rs3.5 billion subsidy to push Raast QR payments at retail outlets, and the SBP set out how it would work in PSPOD Circular No. 03 of 2025. The subsidy is paid to the participating financial institutions, not directly to shops.

According to the circular, the subsidy covered Raast P2M QR transactions made between **1 September 2025 and 30 June 2026**, at 0.5% of each transaction's value or Rs100, whichever is lower. The aim was to let banks and EMIs offer merchants low or zero acceptance fees.

> [!WARNING]
> Some reports described the subsidy as a three-year programme, but we found no SBP notice allocating funds beyond 30 June 2026. If a provider promises "subsidised" or zero-fee terms now, ask which notice they rely on and get the terms in writing.

## How big Raast has become

The SBP's Annual Payment Systems Review for 2025-26, released in early October 2026, shows how quickly Pakistanis have moved away from cash at the counter. Raast's own volume roughly doubled from about 1.28 billion payments the year before.

:::stats Digital payments in 2025-26 (SBP review, as reported)
- 92% | of retail payments made digitally, up from 88%
- 13.2bn | digital transactions, up about 65% in a year
- 2.76bn | payments processed by Raast itself
- 3.8m | QR-enabled merchants registered with banks (provisional)
:::

The SBP marks some of these figures as provisional, so check the published review if you need exact numbers.

## Fuel stations: the 31 January 2027 Raast QR deadline

The clearest compulsory step so far is for petrol pumps. In a circular reported on 3–4 August 2026, the SBP directed all fuel stations nationwide to implement Raast QR code payments by **31 January 2027**.

The same circular extended concessional pricing for card payments at fuel stations, capping the merchant discount rate at Re1 per litre until that date. If you run a fuel station, talk to your bank now: QR displays and staff training take time across several pumps.

## Is accepting Raast payment compulsory for every shop?

Not yet, nationally. In 2026 the Finance Division drafted an amendment to the Payment Systems and Electronic Fund Transfers Act, 2007 that would require retail outlets to accept at least one digital payment mode. When reported, the draft was with the Prime Minister's Office for in-principle approval. We have not seen it passed by Parliament.

Tax policy points the same way: FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) schemes increasingly rely on documented sales, and a Raast QR leaves a clean record. If you are setting up formally, our guide on [how to register a company in Pakistan](/blogs/how-to-register-a-company-in-pakistan) covers NTN and sales tax steps.

> [!TIP]
> Even where it is optional, a visible Raast QR reduces change problems, fake notes and the cash you carry home at night. Many [grocery and daily-needs shops](/categories/grocery-daily-needs) now display one next to the till.

## How to start accepting Raast QR payments

Any business with a bank or EMI account can usually get a Raast merchant QR. The process varies slightly by provider.

:::checklist
- An account in the business's name (or your own name for a sole trader) at a bank or EMI that offers Raast P2M
- Your CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card) and, for registered businesses, NTN or SECP documents
- Proof of business, such as a shop address, utility bill or trade licence
- The provider's written merchant fee schedule and settlement terms
- A plan for where the QR will be displayed and who checks incoming payment alerts
:::

1. Ask your current bank first; many banks and wallet providers offer Raast QR to merchants.
2. Complete merchant onboarding in the app or at a branch.
3. Print the static QR, or use a dynamic QR that carries the bill amount, if your provider offers it.

If you need help choosing, compare providers among [banks and financial institutions](/categories/banks-financial-institutions) or speak to a local [accountant or tax adviser](/categories/accounting-tax) about record-keeping.

## Risks and good practice for merchants

Raast payments are final, which protects you from chargebacks but makes mistakes hard to reverse. Fraudsters sometimes show a fake "payment sent" screen.

- Only release goods after the alert arrives on your own phone or terminal.
- Never share your app PIN or one-time passwords with "bank staff" who call you.
- Check your statement daily against sales, especially in busy markets in [Karachi](/cities/karachi), [Lahore](/cities/lahore) or [Islamabad](/cities/islamabad). [Fuel stations](/categories/fuel-stations-gas) with several pumps should reconcile per shift.

### What is a Raast payment?
A Raast payment is an instant transfer through Raast, the payment system owned and operated by the State Bank of Pakistan. Money moves between accounts at different banks and wallet providers within seconds. Customers pay a person using their Raast ID, usually a mobile number, or pay a shop by scanning its Raast QR code.

### Is Raast free to use?
For customers, the SBP has kept Raast transfers free. For merchants, the bank or wallet provider that signs you up sets the acceptance terms within SBP rules. A government subsidy helped keep merchant fees low between September 2025 and June 2026. Ask your provider for its current merchant fee schedule in writing before you start accepting payments.

### What is Raast P2M?
P2M stands for person to merchant. It is the part of Raast that lets customers pay businesses, mainly by scanning a QR code at the counter or online. The SBP deployed P2M in September 2023 and formally launched it by circular in December 2023. Payments settle into the merchant's account in seconds, and the merchant receives an alert.

### How do I get a Raast QR code for my shop?
Ask your bank or a wallet provider that offers Raast merchant services. You will usually need an account, your CNIC, and some proof of the business such as an address or trade licence. Registered businesses may also be asked for NTN or SECP documents. The provider then issues a static QR you can print, or a dynamic QR in its app.

### Is it mandatory for shops to accept Raast in Pakistan?
Not nationally at the time of writing. Fuel stations have been directed by the SBP to accept Raast QR by 31 January 2027. A Finance Division draft amendment would require retailers to accept at least one digital payment mode, but it was still awaiting approval when reported. Check SBP and Finance Division announcements for any change.

### Did the Raast merchant subsidy end?
The SBP circular allocating the Rs3.5 billion subsidy covered Raast P2M QR transactions from 1 September 2025 to 30 June 2026, at 0.5% of the transaction value or Rs100, whichever is lower. We found no official notice extending it beyond June 2026. The SBP circulars page is the place to confirm whether a new allocation has been made.

### How long does a Raast transfer take?
Raast is designed for real-time payments, so a transfer normally arrives within seconds, at any hour, including weekends and holidays. Delays can occur if a participating bank's systems are down, so merchants should wait for the alert on their own phone before handing over goods.

### Can a Raast payment be reversed?
Raast payments are final once completed, so there are no card-style chargebacks. If you send money to the wrong account, contact your bank immediately; it can raise the issue with the receiving bank, but recovery is not guaranteed. Always check the name shown before confirming.

### What is a Raast ID?
A Raast ID is an alias linked to your bank or wallet account, usually your mobile number. Instead of sharing a long IBAN, you share the number, and the sender's app shows your registered name for confirmation. You set it up through your banking app.

### How many merchants use Raast?
Reports of the SBP's Annual Payment Systems Review for 2025-26, released in October 2026, put QR-enabled merchants registered with banks at roughly 3.8 million by June 2026, up from about 1.1 million a year earlier. Some figures are provisional, so check the published review.
