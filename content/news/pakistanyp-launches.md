**Islamabad, 2 November 2026.** PakistanYP (pakistanyp.com), a new business directory built for Pakistan, is now live. It helps people find, compare and contact businesses near them, and gives shops, professionals and companies a free, trustworthy place to be found online.

## Built around how Pakistan is organised

Most people don't search for "a plumber in Pakistan". They search for a plumber in G-11, a dentist in Gulberg or a property dealer in Clifton. PakistanYP is organised the same way:

- **7 provinces and territories:** Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir.
- **463 cities and towns**, each with its own page.
- **821 localities** in the big cities, from Saddar and DHA to F-7, Johar Town and Hayatabad.

Every business is listed under a province, a city and a locality, so a search for a neighbourhood shows the businesses that are actually there. Place names are also shown in Urdu.

## Categories written for Pakistan

The directory has more than 1,500 categories in 22 sectors, written in the words Pakistanis use: property dealers and housing societies, solar installers and UPS dealers, academies and MDCAT coaching, marquees and caterers, Hajj and Umrah operators, darzi and boutiques, mobile repair, tax consultants for FBR and SECP registration, and many more. Every main category opens into sub-categories, so customers land on exactly the right list.

## What customers get

- **Search by category and area**, then filter by province, city and locality.
- **Call, WhatsApp or get directions** straight from a listing. Pakistani numbers are shown in a clear +92 format.
- **Request a quote once** and have it sent to up to five matching businesses.
- **Verified badges** that show which businesses have proved who they are.
- **Reviews and ratings** from other customers.

## What businesses get

- **A free listing.** Any business can add its profile at no cost: name, description, categories, location, phone, WhatsApp, website, working hours and logo.
- **Verification.** Businesses can upload documents such as an NTN certificate, STRN, SECP incorporation certificate, chamber of commerce membership or professional registration (PMDC, PEC, Bar Council) to earn a verified badge.
- **Enquiries and leads** in the owner dashboard, including quote requests from customers.
- **Claim an existing listing** if your business is already in the directory.
- **Pages that work on any phone**, which is how most customers in Pakistan search.

## Built for search engines

Each province, city and locality has its own page, and every listing has a clean, shareable address. The site's map for search engines updates automatically whenever a business, city or article is added, so new listings can be found on Google and Bing quickly.

## How to get listed

Adding a business takes a few minutes: create a free account, choose your main category and sub-category, pick your province, city and locality, and add your contact details. Listings are reviewed before they go live, to keep the directory accurate.

Visit **pakistanyp.com** to search, or choose **List your business** to add yours for free.
