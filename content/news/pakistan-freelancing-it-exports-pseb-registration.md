If you earn from clients abroad, **PSEB registration as a freelancer** is one of the cheapest tax decisions you can make. The Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB), which sits under the Ministry of IT and Telecommunication, issues certificates to individual IT and IT-enabled services (ITeS) freelancers, and registered exporters have been able to pay a reduced final tax of 0.25% on export proceeds received through banks, compared with 1% for those who are not registered.

Registration is online, needs only a few documents, and is renewed every year. This explainer sets out how it works, what the 2026-27 budget changed, and what Pakistan's record IT export figures for 2025-26 mean for individual freelancers.

:::takeaways
- PSEB registers individual freelancers in IT and IT-enabled services through its online portal at techdestination.com.
- The core documents are a personal NTN, both sides of your CNIC and a personal bank account certificate.
- PSEB-registered exporters have paid a 0.25% final tax on export proceeds, against 1% without registration; the 2026-27 budget proposed extending the regime to 2029.
- PSEB reported record IT and ITeS exports of about $4.6 billion in 2025-26, with freelancers contributing more than $1.1 billion.
- Renewal requires your previous year's income tax return, so filing on time matters.
:::

## What PSEB registration means for a freelancer

PSEB is the government body that promotes Pakistan's software and IT services exports. Its registration certificate tells the FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) and your bank that your foreign income comes from IT or ITeS exports, such as software development, web design, digital marketing, data services or content work delivered online.

Registration does not make you a company, and it is not a licence to work. It is a certificate that lets you claim the concessional tax treatment and some other facilities linked to IT exporters.

## How much tax PSEB registration saves

The tax benefit is the main reason most freelancers register. As widely reported, export proceeds of a PSEB-registered IT exporter, received through normal banking channels, have been taxed at 0.25% as a final tax, while unregistered exporters pay 1%.

| Annual foreign receipts (example) | Tax at 0.25% (registered) | Tax at 1% (not registered) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rs1,000,000 | Rs2,500 | Rs10,000 |
| Rs3,000,000 | Rs7,500 | Rs30,000 |
| Rs6,000,000 | Rs15,000 | Rs60,000 |

These are simple worked examples of the two rates, not tax advice. Conditions apply: proceeds generally need to arrive through banks or approved channels, and you need to file your annual return.

> [!NOTE]
> Business Recorder reported in June 2026 that the Finance Bill 2026-27 proposed keeping the 0.25% final tax regime for IT exporters and freelancers until 2029. Confirm the enacted wording in the Finance Act 2026 or with a tax adviser before you file.

## Pakistan's IT exports and freelancers in 2025-26

The policy matters because the sector is growing. In July 2026, PSEB said IT and ITeS exports reached a record of about $4.6 billion in 2025-26, with freelancers contributing more than $1.1 billion. Reports noted this was still about $400 million short of the government's $5 billion target. Some outlets quoted a slightly different total of $4.5 billion, depending on the data used.

:::stats Pakistan IT exports 2025-26 (as reported)
- $4.6bn | IT and ITeS exports, per PSEB
- $1.1bn+ | contributed by freelancers, per PSEB
- 0.25% | final tax for PSEB-registered exporters

State Bank of Pakistan data for the first nine months (July to March) showed ICT export remittances of about $3.38 billion, up 19.7%, and freelancer remittances of about $856 million, against $567 million a year earlier, as reported. PSEB's freelancer figure is higher than the remittances the SBP records directly, which analysts attribute to how payments are reported.

PSEB also opened nominations for a new Top Freelancer Award in mid-2026, with an August deadline.

## Documents for PSEB registration as a freelancer

PSEB's freelancer registration page lists three core documents. Guides sometimes add extra items, so check the portal for anything requested in your case.

:::checklist
- Personal NTN (National Tax Number) certificate, in your own name with no business name
- Scanned CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card), front and back
- A personal bank account letter or account maintenance certificate from your bank
- An active email address and mobile number for the portal account
- For renewal: your income tax return for the previous year
:::

If you do not have an NTN yet, our guide on [how to become a filer in Pakistan](/blogs/how-to-become-a-filer-in-pakistan) explains how to register on IRIS and get onto the Active Taxpayer List.

> [!TIP]
> Use the same name, CNIC and bank account across PSEB, FBR and your freelancing platforms. Mismatches are a common reason for delays at the bank or in tax filing.

## How to register with PSEB step by step

:::steps PSEB freelancer registration in 5 steps
- Get your NTN | Register on the FBR's IRIS portal if you do not already have a personal NTN.
- Create a portal account | Sign up on PSEB's TechDestination portal using your email and mobile number.
- Fill in your profile | Choose the freelancer category and add your skills and service areas.
- Upload documents | Attach your NTN, CNIC and bank certificate, then pay the fee shown.
- Download the certificate | Once approved, download it and share it with your bank and tax adviser.
:::

Freelancer certificates are annual. Guides commonly quote a registration fee of around Rs1,000 and a higher renewal fee, but PSEB's portal is the only reliable source for the current amount. Processing times reported by applicants range from a day to about two weeks.

## Getting paid: banks, proceeds and records

Your tax rate depends on proving the money is export income. Receive client payments into your own bank account, through banks, licensed exchange companies or approved payment services, and keep the bank's proceeds realisation certificate (PRC) or credit advice.

- Keep invoices or platform statements that match each receipt.
- Ask your bank to mark foreign receipts correctly as IT or freelance export income.
- Keep local payments separate. If you also receive payments locally, a [Raast QR or Raast ID](/news/raast-digital-payments-what-businesses-need-to-know) keeps them traceable.

A local [accountant or tax consultant](/categories/accounting-tax) can check whether your filings claim the correct rate. For help with payment gateways or online storefronts, compare providers listed under [freelancing and e-commerce](/categories/freelancing-e-commerce).

## Who should register, and when to form a company

PSEB registration suits solo freelancers in [IT and digital services](/categories/it-digital), [software development](/categories/software-development), [digital marketing](/categories/digital-marketing) and [design and creative work](/categories/design-creative). If you hire staff, sign larger contracts or want investors, a registered company may be a better structure; PSEB also registers companies separately. For most freelancers earning from abroad, PSEB registration is cheap, quick and worth it: the gap between 0.25% and 1% grows with every rupee you earn. Keep your NTN, bank records and annual return in order, because renewal depends on them.

:::faq
### What is PSEB registration for freelancers?
It is a certificate issued by the Pakistan Software Export Board to individuals who export IT or IT-enabled services, such as software, web design, digital marketing or data work. It confirms to the FBR and banks that your foreign income is IT export income, which lets you claim the reduced final tax rate available to registered exporters.

### Is PSEB registration mandatory for freelancers?
It is not required to work as a freelancer, but without it you generally cannot claim the 0.25% final tax rate on IT export proceeds and pay 1% instead. Many banks also ask for the certificate when classifying foreign receipts. For most freelancers who earn regularly from abroad, registration pays for itself quickly.

### What documents are required for PSEB freelancer registration?
PSEB's freelancer page lists a personal NTN certificate with no business name, both sides of your CNIC, and a personal bank account letter or certificate. You also need an email address and mobile number for the portal. For renewal, you must provide your income tax return for the previous year and pay the renewal fee.

### What is the PSEB registration fee?
Guides commonly report a registration fee of about Rs1,000 for a new freelancer certificate and a higher fee for renewal, but figures quoted online vary. PSEB shows the current fee on its TechDestination portal during the application. Pay only through the portal's official payment method, not to agents who claim to speed up approval.

### How long does PSEB registration take?
Processing times reported by applicants vary from about one working day to around two weeks, depending on whether documents are complete and clear. Uploading a correct NTN in your own name and a bank certificate that matches your CNIC details usually avoids delays. Track the status in your portal account rather than applying twice.

### What tax do PSEB-registered freelancers pay?
PSEB-registered IT exporters have paid a final tax of 0.25% on export proceeds received through banking channels, compared with 1% for unregistered exporters. The 2026-27 Finance Bill proposed extending this regime to 2029, as Business Recorder reported in June 2026. Conditions apply, including filing your annual return, so confirm details with a tax adviser.

### Do I need an NTN for PSEB registration?
Yes. A personal NTN issued by the FBR is one of the required documents, and it should be in your own name without a business name. You can get one free on the FBR's IRIS portal using your CNIC, mobile number and email. Filing your return each year also keeps you on the Active Taxpayer List.

### How often must I renew PSEB registration?
Freelancer certificates are issued for a year and must be renewed annually through the same portal. Renewal requires your previous year's income tax return and the renewal fee. Renew before expiry so that your bank and tax filings continue to show you as a registered IT exporter throughout the year.

### How much did Pakistan earn from IT exports in 2025-26?
PSEB reported record IT and IT-enabled services exports of about $4.6 billion for 2025-26, with freelancers contributing more than $1.1 billion. The figure was about $400 million below the government's $5 billion target. Some outlets reported $4.5 billion, depending on the data source used, so check PSEB and SBP releases for final numbers.

### Can a freelancer register a company with PSEB instead?
Yes. PSEB registers IT companies as well as individuals. A company first needs SECP incorporation and its own NTN, then applies under the company category with its own documents and fee. A company structure can suit freelancers who hire staff or sign larger contracts, but it brings more compliance work and costs.
