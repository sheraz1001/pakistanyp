Trust is the first question customers ask before calling a business they found online. From today, businesses listed on PakistanYP can earn a **verified badge** that answers it.

## What the badge means

A verified badge shows that the PakistanYP team has checked that the business is real and that the person managing the listing is entitled to do so. It appears next to the business name in search results and on the business profile.

## How a business gets verified

1. **Add or claim the listing.** Listing is free.
2. **Confirm the phone number** shown on the listing.
3. **Upload one or more documents** from the owner dashboard. Accepted documents include:
   - NTN certificate (Federal Board of Revenue)
   - Sales tax registration (STRN)
   - SECP certificate of incorporation
   - Chamber of Commerce membership
   - Trade or municipal licence
   - Food authority licence
   - Professional registration such as PMDC, PEC or Bar Council
4. **Our team reviews the documents** and the badge is added once they match the listing.

Documents are used only for verification and are never shown publicly.

## Why it matters for customers

Customers can filter for verified businesses on province pages, and the badge helps them choose with more confidence, especially for services where trust matters most: builders, property dealers, clinics, travel agents and education providers.

## Why it matters for businesses

A verified listing stands out in a list of similar businesses and tells customers that the details are current. It is one of the simplest ways for a small business to look as credible online as it is in person.

To get started, open **List your business** on pakistanyp.com, or go to **My verification** in your dashboard if your business is already listed.
