Sindh is the province where most of Pakistan's sea trade enters and leaves. Its capital, Karachi, sits on the Arabian Sea and holds both of the country's main commercial ports, Karachi Port and Port Qasim, which together handle the bulk of Pakistan's external trade. That makes Sindh the natural base for importers, exporters, shipping agents, freight forwarders and the banks and insurers that serve them.

Beyond Karachi, the province follows the Indus. Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana and Nawabshah are trading and agricultural centres for the irrigated lands along the river, while the Thar Desert in the south-east has become a centre for coal mining and power generation.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Province of Pakistan
- **Capital:** Karachi
- **Population (2023 census, province):** 55,696,147, the second largest of any province
- **Area:** 140,914 sq km
- **Administration:** 6 divisions and 30 districts
- **Telephone area code (Karachi):** 021, with eight-digit local numbers
- **Seaports:** Karachi Port and Port Qasim
- **Main airport:** Jinnah International Airport, Karachi

## Economy and main industries
Karachi's economy rests on trade, finance and manufacturing. Textiles and garments, food and beverages, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, steel, automotive assembly and packaging all have a large presence in the city's industrial areas. Many banks, insurers and listed companies are headquartered in Karachi, and the city is the hub for customs clearing, warehousing and road haulage to the rest of the country.

Inland Sindh is agricultural. The Indus canal system supports wheat, rice, cotton and sugarcane, and towns such as Hyderabad, Sukkur and Nawabshah host rice mills, sugar mills and ginning factories, along with wholesale grain and vegetable markets.

Energy is the third strand. In Tharparkar district, the Sindh Engro Coal Mining Company, a joint venture of the Government of Sindh and Engro, mines lignite at Thar Block II near Islamkot. The mine feeds a 660 MW power plant whose first unit was connected to the national grid in 2019, and a second 330 MW plant, majority-owned by Hub Power Company, is linked to the mine's expansion.

## Main business areas and markets
**Karachi** has several large, long-established industrial areas. SITE, in the west of the city, is the oldest and largest industrial estate and holds thousands of factories. Korangi Industrial Area and Landhi Industrial Area in the east are dense with textile, leather, food and engineering units, and North Karachi Industrial Area serves the northern districts. Near Port Qasim, the Bin Qasim industrial zone hosts heavy industry and oil terminals.

Two Karachi sites have special economic zone status under the SEZ Act 2012: Korangi Creek Industrial Park, on 240 acres in the Korangi Industrial Area, and Bin Qasim Industrial Park, on 930 acres near Port Qasim. Further out, the Dhabeji Special Economic Zone has been planned on about 1,530 acres in Thatta district, linked to CPEC.

**Kotri and Nooriabad**, on the routes between Karachi and Hyderabad, are the main industrial locations in the interior. SITE Kotri dates from 1962, and SITE Nooriabad is being extended with a third phase.

**Hyderabad** is the second city of the province and the main trading centre for lower Sindh, while **Sukkur** serves upper Sindh and the routes north into Punjab and Balochistan.

## Ports and logistics
Most of the province's commercial activity connects to the ports. Karachi Port, in Keamari, is the older of the two; Port Qasim, to the east, now handles a larger share of the country's exports than Karachi Port. Businesses that move goods through either port typically deal with customs clearing agents, shipping lines, container terminals and transporters clustered around Keamari and the Port Qasim industrial belt. The National Highway and the M-9 motorway carry cargo towards Hyderabad and on up-country.

## Finding businesses in Sindh
In Karachi, common searches include clearing and forwarding agents, importers and wholesalers, textile and garment manufacturers, packaging suppliers, logistics companies and corporate service firms. In Hyderabad, Sukkur and the smaller towns, people more often look for agricultural inputs, rice and sugar traders, transport operators and local retail. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Karachi?
Karachi landlines use 021. Local numbers have eight digits, so a full number reads 021 followed by eight digits, or +92 21 from abroad.

### Which are the main industrial areas in Karachi?
The best known are SITE, Korangi Industrial Area, Landhi Industrial Area, North Karachi Industrial Area and the Bin Qasim zone near Port Qasim.

### Which authority collects tax on services in Sindh?
Sales tax on services in Sindh is administered by the Sindh Revenue Board under the Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act, 2011. Sales tax on goods remains with the Federal Board of Revenue.

### Where can I register a company in Sindh?
Companies are registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan, which has company registration offices in Karachi and Sukkur and also accepts applications online.
