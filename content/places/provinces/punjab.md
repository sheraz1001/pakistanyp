Punjab is Pakistan's most populous province, home to more than half of the country's people according to the 2023 census. Its capital, Lahore, is the provincial seat of government and a major centre for retail, services, education and media, but the province's commercial life is spread across a network of large cities rather than concentrated in one place.

For anyone looking for suppliers, manufacturers or service providers, Punjab matters because so much of Pakistan's textile, sports goods, surgical instrument, engineering and food processing capacity sits here. Faisalabad, Sialkot, Gujranwala and Multan each have their own industrial character, and the motorway network links them to Lahore, Islamabad and the ports in Sindh.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Province of Pakistan
- **Capital:** Lahore
- **Population (2023 census, province):** 127,688,922, the largest of any province
- **Area:** 205,344 sq km
- **Districts:** 41 (as of 2024)
- **Telephone area code (Lahore):** 042; other cities have their own codes, such as 051 for Rawalpindi
- **Main airports:** Allama Iqbal International Airport (Lahore) and Sialkot International Airport, which was built with funding from the local business community
- **Notable industries:** Textiles, sports goods, surgical instruments, leather goods, light engineering and agro-processing

## Economy and main industries
Agriculture remains the base of the provincial economy. The canal-irrigated plains of central and southern Punjab grow wheat, cotton, rice and sugarcane, and these crops feed a large network of flour mills, ginning factories, rice mills and sugar mills in the surrounding towns.

Manufacturing is the other pillar. Faisalabad is a major textile centre, with spinning, weaving, finishing and garment units. Sialkot is known for sports goods, surgical instruments, leather products and gloves; its surgical instrument trade dates back to the late nineteenth century, when local blacksmiths repaired instruments for a mission hospital. Gujranwala's workshops and factories make electric fans and other home appliances, sanitary fittings, cutlery and ceramics. Lahore adds a large services sector, including software houses, banking, advertising, publishing and wholesale trade.

## Main business areas and markets
**Lahore** is the province's largest commercial city. Its industrial capacity is concentrated in Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate at Kot Lakhpat and in Sundar Industrial Estate, both managed by the Punjab Industrial Estates Development and Management Company (PIEDMC). Office and retail activity clusters around Gulberg, the Mall Road, Johar Town and DHA.

**Sheikhupura**, next to Lahore, hosts Quaid-e-Azam Business Park, a special economic zone close to the M-2 motorway.

**Faisalabad** has two large zones on the motorway near the Sahianwala Interchange: M-3 Industrial City and Allama Iqbal Industrial City, a priority special economic zone under the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC). Both are run by the Faisalabad Industrial Estate Development and Management Company.

**Sialkot** is an export city with a small industrial estate and many workshops along the Daska, Wazirabad and Sambrial roads. **Multan** has a PIEDMC industrial estate and serves as the trading centre for southern Punjab. PIEDMC estates at Rahim Yar Khan, Bhalwal and Vehari have also been declared special economic zones.

## Trade bodies and support
Most large cities have their own chamber of commerce and industry. The Lahore, Faisalabad, Gujranwala and Sialkot chambers are the usual first contact for membership, trade delegations and export documentation. Sialkot's business community also financed Sialkot International Airport, which opened for flights in 2007 so exporters would not have to travel to Lahore.

For tax on services, businesses in the province register with the Punjab Revenue Authority, while sales tax on goods and income tax are handled by the Federal Board of Revenue. The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has company registration offices in Lahore, Faisalabad and Multan.

People searching in Punjab often want manufacturers and exporters: textile mills and garment makers in Faisalabad, sports goods and surgical instrument firms in Sialkot, or engineering workshops in Gujranwala. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the dialling code for Lahore?
Lahore landlines use the area code 042, and local numbers have eight digits. From abroad, dial +92 42 followed by the number.

### Where are the main industrial estates in Punjab?
The largest include Sundar Industrial Estate and Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate in Lahore, Quaid-e-Azam Business Park in Sheikhupura, M-3 Industrial City and Allama Iqbal Industrial City in Faisalabad, and the Multan Industrial Estate.

### Which city in Punjab is known for sports goods and surgical instruments?
Sialkot. Its sports goods, surgical instruments, leather products and gloves are mostly made for export.

### Where do I register a service business for provincial tax in Punjab?
Sales tax on services in Punjab is collected by the Punjab Revenue Authority. Company incorporation itself is handled nationally by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan.
