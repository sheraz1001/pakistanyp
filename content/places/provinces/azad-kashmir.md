Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) is an administrative territory of Pakistan with its own government, legislative assembly and courts, and Muzaffarabad as its capital. It lies along the eastern edge of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the north of Punjab, and its three divisions run from the mountain valleys around Muzaffarabad and Neelum in the north to the lower-lying districts of Mirpur, Kotli and Bhimber in the south.

Commercially the territory has two centres of gravity. Muzaffarabad is the seat of government and the trading town for the northern valleys, while Mirpur division in the south holds most of the territory's industry and is closely linked to Britain through a large overseas community whose remittances support construction, property and retail.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Administrative territory of Pakistan with its own elected government
- **Capital:** Muzaffarabad
- **Population (2017 census, territory):** 4,045,366; the AJK Planning and Development Department projects about 4.46 million for 2023. The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics' 2023 census results do not cover AJK
- **Area:** 13,297 sq km
- **Administration:** 3 divisions (Muzaffarabad, Poonch and Mirpur) and 10 districts
- **Telephone area codes:** 05822 for Muzaffarabad, 05827 for Mirpur
- **Major infrastructure:** Mangla Dam on the Jhelum near Mirpur, and the 969 MW Neelum-Jhelum hydropower project, in operation since 2018
- **Notable sectors:** Construction and property, hydropower, small manufacturing, tourism and remittance-driven retail

## Economy and main industries
Hydropower is one of the territory's main economic assets. Mangla Dam, near Mirpur, and the Neelum-Jhelum project both draw on AJK's rivers. The building of Mangla submerged more than 280 villages and displaced over 110,000 people in the Mirpur area, and the displacement added to a migration to Britain that had begun in the 1950s.

That migration still shapes the economy. Mirpur, Kotli and Bhimber receive large flows of money from relatives abroad, which keeps demand high for construction, property and travel services, and makes local spending sensitive to the value of the pound sterling. The State Bank of Pakistan does not publish remittance figures by region of origin, so there is no official total for AJK.

The AJK government also promotes small and cottage industry through the AJK Small Industries Corporation, and has announced incentive packages for new investors.

## Main business areas and markets
**Mirpur** is the main industrial city. The AJK Department of Industries lists two large estates there: the Small Industrial Estate Mirpur, covering 869 kanals, and the New Industrial Area Mirpur, covering 6,196 kanals. A new industrial estate at Dadyal in Mirpur district is in progress. The Mirpur Special Economic Zone, one of the zones planned under CPEC, has had about 1,185 acres identified, though its development has moved slowly. The office of the Joint Director Industries is based in Mirpur.

**Muzaffarabad** is the administrative capital and the main market for the Neelum and Jhelum valleys, with government offices, banks and wholesale trade. A special economic zone at Muzaffarabad is also planned.

**Kotli**, **Bhimber**, **Rawalakot** and **Dadyal** have smaller industrial estates that have received government funding for development works. Kotli's is a mini estate of 94 kanals.

**Bagh**, **Palandri** and **Hattian Bala** serve as district trading towns for the Poonch and Muzaffarabad divisions.

## Trade bodies
Business representation is split by region. The Mirpur Chamber of Commerce and Industry describes itself as the apex body for the business community of AJK and is active on industrial policy, including keeping the industries office in Mirpur. The Muzaffarabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry represents traders in the capital and has met counterparts such as the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry. The Azad Jammu and Kashmir Chamber of Commerce and Industry is a further territory-wide body.

## Finding businesses in Azad Jammu and Kashmir
In Mirpur and Dadyal, people commonly look for builders, property dealers, furniture makers, travel agents and money transfer services. In Muzaffarabad and the northern valleys, searches more often cover hotels and guesthouses, transport, building materials and government contractors. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the dialling code for Muzaffarabad?
Muzaffarabad landlines use 05822 and Mirpur uses 05827. From abroad, drop the first zero and dial +92 5822 or +92 5827.

### Where are the industrial estates in Azad Kashmir?
The largest are the Small Industrial Estate and the New Industrial Area in Mirpur. Smaller estates are in Kotli, Bhimber, Rawalakot, Dadyal and Muzaffarabad.

### Which chamber of commerce should I contact in AJK?
For Mirpur division, the Mirpur Chamber of Commerce and Industry; for the capital, the Muzaffarabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

### How many districts does Azad Jammu and Kashmir have?
Ten districts, grouped into the Muzaffarabad, Poonch and Mirpur divisions.
