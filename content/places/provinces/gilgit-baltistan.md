Gilgit-Baltistan is an administrative territory of Pakistan in the far north of the country, on the border with China and among the high peaks of the Karakoram. Gilgit is the capital and administrative centre, and Skardu is the main town of Baltistan to the east.

Its business life is shaped by geography. The Karakoram Highway carries Pakistan's overland trade with China through the Khunjerab Pass, tourism fills hotels from Hunza to Skardu in the summer season, and orchards in the valleys produce cherries, apricots and other fruit, with exports of fresh cherries to China now under way.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Administrative territory of Pakistan
- **Capital:** Gilgit
- **Population (2017 census, territory):** 1,492,924; the 2023 census results published by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics do not include Gilgit-Baltistan
- **Administration:** 14 districts in three divisions: Gilgit, Baltistan and Diamer-Astore
- **Telephone area codes:** 05811 for Gilgit, 05815 for Skardu
- **Airports:** Gilgit Airport and Skardu International Airport, which was designated an international airport in December 2021
- **Border trade point:** Sost Dry Port, serving the Khunjerab Pass crossing to China
- **Notable sectors:** Tourism and hospitality, fruit, minerals, border trade and hydropower

## Economy and main industries
Tourism is a mainstay of the private economy in many valleys. Hunza, Nagar, Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu and Astore draw domestic visitors, trekkers and mountaineering expeditions, which support hotels, guesthouses, transport operators, guides and handicraft sellers. Skardu's upgrade to international status was intended in part to bring in foreign visitors more directly.

Fruit is the main commercial crop. Cherries are grown across a belt from Diamer to the Chinese border, particularly in Hunza and Nagar. After a plant health agreement between Pakistan and China in 2022, the first consignments of fresh cherries were exported to China by road in June 2024. Dried apricots and other dry fruit are seen as the next export opportunity, and growers and officials point to cold storage and processing as the main gaps.

Minerals and stone, including marble and granite, are a further strand, and they are among the target industries for the planned special economic zone at Moqpondass.

## Main business areas and markets
**Gilgit** is the main commercial centre, with wholesale trade, government offices, banks and suppliers for the whole territory. Danyor, Nomal and Jaglot nearby share Gilgit's 05811 dialling code.

**Moqpondass Special Economic Zone**, about 40 km from Gilgit near the Gilgit-Skardu road, is the territory's CPEC economic zone. It is being developed on about 250 acres under a public-private partnership, with planned industries including marble and granite, mineral and iron ore processing, fruit processing, steel and leather.

**Skardu** is the commercial centre of Baltistan and a starting point for treks and expeditions in the Karakoram. **Aliabad** and **Karimabad** in Hunza are the main service towns for visitors to the Hunza valley.

**Sost**, near the Chinese border, holds the dry port where goods arriving through the Khunjerab Pass are cleared by customs.

## China trade through Khunjerab
Trade with China through Khunjerab has been uneven. In 2025, traders staged a long sit-in at Sost Dry Port over taxes and stuck consignments, halting traffic for weeks until a settlement that included tax relief on Chinese imports for local consumption. By March 2026, customs reported 1,774 consignments from China cleared at Sost, and machinery and electric vehicles were being routed through Khunjerab rather than Karachi. Anyone importing through this route usually works with clearing agents in Sost or Gilgit and checks current customs rules first. The Gilgit-Baltistan Chamber of Commerce and Industry has been one of the main voices for local traders in these disputes.

## Finding businesses in Gilgit-Baltistan
Most searches in Gilgit-Baltistan are for hotels, guesthouses, tour operators, jeep and vehicle hire, and trekking outfitters, especially in Hunza and Skardu. Local residents and contractors more often look for building material suppliers, clearing agents and dry fruit wholesalers in Gilgit. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the telephone code for Gilgit?
Gilgit landlines use 05811 and Skardu uses 05815. From abroad, drop the first zero and dial +92 5811 or +92 5815.

### How does trade with China work in Gilgit-Baltistan?
Goods cross the border at the Khunjerab Pass and are cleared at Sost Dry Port. The route has been disrupted at times by protests and policy disputes, so it is worth checking its current status before shipping.

### Is there an economic zone in Gilgit-Baltistan?
Yes. The Moqpondass Special Economic Zone near Gilgit is being developed under CPEC for minerals, marble, fruit processing and other industries.

### Can I register a company from Gilgit-Baltistan?
Companies are incorporated through the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan, which lists a company registration office in Gilgit-Baltistan and also accepts applications online.
