Balochistan covers roughly 44 percent of Pakistan's land area but holds a small share of its people, so distances between towns are long and each city serves a wide hinterland. Quetta, the provincial capital, sits in the north near the Afghan border, while the Makran coast in the south runs to Gwadar and the border with Iran.

For businesses the province matters for what lies under and around it: natural gas, copper and gold deposits, a deep-sea port at Gwadar, fruit orchards in the northern valleys, date palms in Makran and the industrial town of Hub on the edge of Karachi. Border crossings at Chaman and Taftan also make Quetta a trading point for Afghanistan and Iran.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Province of Pakistan
- **Capital:** Quetta
- **Population (2023 census, province):** 14,894,402, the smallest of the four provinces
- **Area:** 347,190 sq km, the largest province by area
- **Telephone area code (Quetta):** 081
- **Seaport:** Gwadar, plus the Gadani ship-breaking yard and fish harbour in Hub district
- **Main airport:** Quetta International Airport
- **Notable sectors:** Natural gas, mining, fruit and dates, fisheries, and industry around Hub

## Economy and main industries
Natural gas has shaped the province's role in the national economy since the Sui gas field in Dera Bugti district was discovered in 1952; gas from Sui was being supplied to other parts of Pakistan by 1955.

Mining is the other large resource sector. In Chagai district, Saindak is a copper-gold project run with Chinese investment, and Reko Diq, also in Chagai, is one of the world's largest copper deposits, now being developed by Barrick as a copper-gold mine. These projects create demand for engineering, transport, catering and security contractors.

Farming is concentrated in the northern valleys and the Makran belt. Pishin is the main apple-growing district, with orchards also around Quetta, Killa Abdullah, Killa Saifullah and Kalat, and the crop is sent to Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Peshawar from late summer to autumn. Panjgur and Kech are major date-growing districts. Along the coast, fishing and seafood are the main livelihood, with considerable room for processing and cold storage.

## Main business areas and markets
**Quetta** is the commercial and administrative centre, where wholesalers supply the rest of the province and traders handle goods moving to and from Afghanistan and Iran. The Quetta Chamber of Commerce and Industry is the main business body.

**Hub**, just across the provincial boundary from Karachi, is the province's industrial town. The Hub Industrial and Trading Estate (HITE) began with a handful of factories and now has more than 300 industrial units. The Lasbela Chamber of Commerce and Industry represents businesses here and has asked for HITE's second phase to be declared a special economic zone. Hub is also a CPEC special economic zone location.

**Bostan**, in Pishin district north of Quetta, is the site of the Bostan Special Economic Zone, a CPEC priority zone whose first phase covers about 200 acres. A handful of factories had started operating there by late 2025, with more under construction.

**Gwadar** is the province's port city. Gwadar Port is run by the Gwadar Port Authority with China Overseas Port Holding Company, and about 2,000 acres have been leased for the Gwadar Special Economic Zone. The port was inaugurated in 2016 and became fully operational in 2021.

**Turbat**, **Khuzdar**, **Sibi** and **Chaman** serve as regional centres for trade and services.

## Border trade
Chaman, on the Afghan border north-west of Quetta, and Taftan, on the Iranian border, are the main land crossings. Closures at Chaman have repeatedly disrupted traders, and the Quetta chamber has gone to the Balochistan High Court and petitioned the government over them. Anyone planning to move goods across these borders should check the current status with clearing agents in Quetta.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Quetta?
Quetta landlines use 081. From outside Pakistan, dial +92 81 followed by the local number.

### Where are the industrial zones in Balochistan?
The main locations are the Hub Industrial and Trading Estate near Karachi, the Bostan Special Economic Zone in Pishin district, and the Gwadar Special Economic Zone next to the port.

### Which authority collects tax on services in Balochistan?
The Balochistan Revenue Authority collects sales tax on services under the Balochistan Sales Tax on Services Act, 2015.

### Is there a company registration office in Quetta?
Yes. The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has a company registration office in Quetta, and companies can also be registered online.
