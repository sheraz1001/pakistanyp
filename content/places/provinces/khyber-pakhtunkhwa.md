Khyber Pakhtunkhwa runs along Pakistan's north-western border with Afghanistan, from the mountains of Chitral and Swat in the north to the plains around Dera Ismail Khan in the south. Its capital, Peshawar, has long been a trading city on the route to the Khyber Pass, and cross-border commerce through Torkham remains a defining part of the provincial economy.

The province took its current shape in 2018, when the former Federally Administered Tribal Areas were merged into it and the seven tribal agencies became districts. For businesses this widened the province's market and brought the border districts under the same provincial laws and tax system as the rest of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

## Key facts
- **Status:** Province of Pakistan
- **Capital:** Peshawar
- **Population (2023 census, province):** 40,856,097, the third largest of the four provinces
- **Merged districts (since 2018):** Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Orakzai, Kurram, North Waziristan and South Waziristan
- **Telephone area code (Peshawar):** 091
- **Main airport:** Bacha Khan International Airport, Peshawar
- **Main border crossing:** Torkham, on the road to Afghanistan
- **Notable industries:** Tobacco, marble and minerals, chemicals, steel, food products and tourism

## Economy and main industries
Agriculture is central, and tobacco stands out. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa grows almost all of Pakistan's flue-cured Virginia tobacco, mostly in Swabi, Mardan, Charsadda, Buner and Mansehra, which supports leaf buyers, curing barns and transport businesses across the Peshawar valley.

Mining and stone processing are the second strand. Buner and Mohmand hold large marble deposits, and a Marble City is being built on 350 acres in Mohmand district for processing units. In the industrial estates, chemicals, steel, food products and pharmaceuticals are among the main lines of manufacture.

Trade with Afghanistan affects many businesses in Peshawar and the Khyber district. Transporters, clearing agents, wholesalers and money changers depend on traffic through Torkham, and the Sarhad Chamber of Commerce and Industry regularly lobbies the federal government when the crossing closes or trade rules change.

## Main business areas and markets
**Peshawar** is the main commercial city, with wholesale trade in the old city bazaars, offices and retail in Saddar, University Town and Hayatabad, and the long-established Hayatabad Industrial Estate.

**Nowshera** district hosts the Rashakai Special Economic Zone, a CPEC priority zone of about 1,000 acres near Peshawar, developed by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Economic Zones Development and Management Company (KPEZDMC) with China Road and Bridge Corporation. Nowshera also has an older industrial estate.

**Haripur** district is home to the Hattar Special Economic Zone, the province's oldest and best-known industrial estate, which received SEZ status in 2016. Its units include chemicals, steel, food and pharmaceutical producers, though a number of older factories there have closed.

**Swabi** has the Gadoon Amazai industrial estate, covering about 1,116 acres, where many units have struggled in recent years.

**Mardan**, **Abbottabad**, **Mingora** in Swat, **Kohat** and **Dera Ismail Khan** serve as regional trading centres for their divisions, with wholesale markets, transport depots and services for the surrounding districts.

## Border trade and transit
Torkham, in Khyber district, carries both bilateral trade with Afghanistan and Afghan transit cargo. A round-the-clock terminal operates there under the Integrated Transit Trade Management System. Trade volumes have swung sharply with border closures and policy changes, so anyone sourcing goods or arranging transport for this route usually works with established clearing agents in Peshawar who track the current rules.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the dialling code for Peshawar?
Peshawar landlines use 091. From outside Pakistan, dial +92 91 followed by the local number.

### Which economic zones operate in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?
The main ones are the Rashakai Special Economic Zone in Nowshera, the Hattar Special Economic Zone in Haripur and the Gadoon Amazai estate in Swabi. KPEZDMC also manages the older industrial estates, including Hayatabad and Nowshera.

### Who represents the business community in Peshawar?
The Sarhad Chamber of Commerce and Industry is the main chamber in Peshawar and is active on Pakistan-Afghanistan trade issues.

### Which authority collects tax on services in the province?
The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority collects provincial sales tax on services. Companies are incorporated through the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan, which has a registration office in Peshawar.
