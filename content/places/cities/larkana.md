Larkana is a city in upper Sindh and the headquarters of Larkana district. It serves a farming region whose main commercial crop is rice, and much of the city's trade revolves around buying, milling and moving that harvest.

The city is also the nearest large town to Mohenjo-daro, the Indus Valley Civilisation site, which brings researchers and visitors through Larkana. Its old city bazaars remain the main shopping destination for people from the surrounding towns and villages.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Sindh
- **Population (2023 census, district):** 1,784,453
- **City size:** one of ten cities with a population between half a million and one million in the 2023 census
- **Telephone area code:** 074
- **Main crop:** rice, particularly the IRRI-6 variety
- **Airport:** Moenjo Daro Airport, about 28 km from the city
- **Industrial zone:** Larkana Industrial Zone, granted special economic zone status in September 2024
- **Business body:** Larkana Chamber of Commerce and Industry (LCCI)

## Economy and main industries
Rice dominates the local economy. According to the president of the Larkana Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Larkana division exports around 342,000 tonnes of IRRI-6 rice a year. Rice millers, traders, transporters and exporters form the core of the business community, and the volume of rice trade was one of the reasons given for opening a State Bank of Pakistan branch in the city.

The Larkana Industrial Zone was given special economic zone status in September 2024, which makes investors there eligible for the incentives offered to such zones. The chamber has also campaigned on railway services, a fairer share of federal revenues for the province, and trade with Bangladesh, whose deputy high commissioner has visited the chamber to discuss commercial ties.

## Main business areas and markets
Shahi Bazaar, in the centre of the old city, is the main and most important market in Larkana. Resham Gali, or Silk Alley, is the centre for textiles and fine needlework, and Sonarki Bazaar is the jewellers' market. The city also has separate fish, meat and vegetable markets, and Station Road is another busy commercial street.

Beyond the old city, business addresses are found along Bunder Road and VIP Road and around Pakistan Chowk. Lahori Muhalla and Ali Goharabad are other localities used in business addresses. Crowds are heavy in Resham Gali and on Station Road, so visitors are advised to watch for pickpockets.

## Mohenjo-daro and travel
The archaeological site of Mohenjo-daro is served by Moenjo Daro Airport, operated by the Pakistan Airports Authority. Scheduled services have been limited and at times suspended, so most visitors and business travellers reach Larkana by road. The chamber has also lobbied for better train services, including the Qalandar Express. Hotels, transport operators and guides in the city serve those visiting the site, as well as officials and traders on business in the district.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Larkana?
The telephone area code is 074, or +92 74 from abroad.

### Where is the main market in Larkana?
Shahi Bazaar in the old city is the main market, with Resham Gali for textiles and needlework and Sonarki Bazaar for jewellery nearby.

### What is Larkana's main business?
The rice trade. Larkana division exports around 342,000 tonnes of IRRI-6 rice a year, according to the local chamber.

### How far is Mohenjo-daro airport from Larkana?
Moenjo Daro Airport is about 28 km from Larkana city.
