Sialkot is a city in north-eastern Punjab and the headquarters of Sialkot district. Although it is smaller than Lahore or Faisalabad, it is one of Pakistan's most export-oriented cities, with thousands of manufacturers producing goods mainly for foreign buyers.

The city is also known for doing things through its business community. Local industrialists funded Sialkot International Airport, which opened in 2007 as the first privately owned airport in Pakistan, and the same business community later launched an airline, AirSial.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Punjab; headquarters of Sialkot district (tehsils include Sialkot, Daska and Sambrial)
- **Population (2023 census, Sialkot district):** about 4.5 million
- **Telephone area code:** 052
- **Airport:** Sialkot International Airport, privately owned and built with funds from the local business community
- **Chamber of commerce:** Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and Industry, with more than 6,500 members
- **Main industries:** sports goods, surgical instruments, leather goods, textiles
- **Motorway:** Lahore–Sialkot Motorway (M-11), about 91.5 km

## Economy and main industries
Sports goods are Sialkot's best-known product. The city makes hand-stitched footballs and other inflatable balls, along with other sports equipment, and is commonly cited as meeting around 70 per cent of world demand for hand-stitched balls. Footballs account for about half of Pakistan's sports goods exports.

Surgical instruments are the second pillar. Sialkot's makers produce stainless steel instruments for hospitals and distributors abroad, and the sector's exports were estimated at around 449 million dollars in 2024. Leather products and textiles make up most of the rest. According to the chamber, most of its members work in the sports goods, leather and surgical instruments sectors.

Besides the larger exporters, press reports put the number of small industrial units in and around the city at more than ten thousand.

## Main business areas and markets
Sialkot has relatively little formal industrial land, and most of its manufacturing units are spread across the city and its suburbs rather than in large estates. The Small Industrial Estate is the main planned industrial area, and local business groups have pressed the Punjab government for more estates.

Our listings cover localities including Ugoki, Daska Road, Wazirabad Road, Sambrial Road, Pasrur Road and Kashmir Road, as well as the towns of Daska and Sambrial. Paris Road has the General Post Office and many courier offices, and Saddar Bazaar and Trunk Bazaar are part of the older city. Residential growth has moved to Model Town, Citi Housing and other schemes.

## Exports and connectivity
Because most of Sialkot's output is shipped abroad, logistics matter. Sialkot Dry Port allows exporters to clear consignments locally before they move to the seaports in Karachi. Sialkot International Airport handles passenger flights, and AirSial began commercial flights in December 2020. The Lahore–Sialkot Motorway, opened in 2020, connects the city to Lahore and the national motorway network at Kala Shah Kaku.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Sialkot?
Sialkot landlines use the code 052. From abroad, dial +92 52.

### What is Sialkot famous for?
Sialkot is known for hand-stitched footballs and other sports goods, surgical instruments and leather products, most of which are exported.

### Who owns Sialkot International Airport?
The airport was built with funds from Sialkot's business community and opened in 2007. It is described as the first privately owned airport in Pakistan.

### Which organisation represents Sialkot's exporters?
The Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and Industry represents more than 6,500 members, mostly exporters. Most of its members work in the sports goods, leather and surgical instruments sectors.
