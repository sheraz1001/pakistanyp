Rahim Yar Khan is the main city of a district of more than 5.5 million people in the south of Punjab. The district's economy is built on agriculture, and its fields of sugarcane and cotton supply mills, ginning factories and traders across the region.

For business seekers, the city combines an agricultural market town with an industrial and institutional centre. It has an international airport, a public engineering university and a district industrial estate, alongside the bazaars that serve a large rural population.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Punjab
- **Population (2023 census, district):** 5,564,703
- **Telephone area code:** 068
- **Airport:** Sheikh Zayed International Airport (upgraded from domestic to international status in 2009)
- **Main crops and industries:** sugarcane and sugar milling, cotton ginning, flour milling, fertiliser
- **Notable institutions:** Khwaja Fareed University of Engineering and Information Technology (2014); Sheikh Zayed Medical College
- **Business body:** Rahim Yar Khan Chamber of Commerce and Industry (incorporated 2007)

## Economy and main industries
The district has shifted from being one of the country's leading cotton areas to a major supplier of sugarcane. It has six sugar mills, and during the crushing season long queues of tractor trolleys loaded with cane form on roads such as Shahi Road. Hundreds of flour mills and cotton ginning factories also operate in the district, and two fertiliser companies have plants here.

A district industrial estate of about 455 acres lies around 20 km from the city on the national highway. The Rahim Yar Khan Chamber of Commerce and Industry has worked with the Multan, Bahawalpur and Dera Ghazi Khan chambers on southern Punjab trade issues, and a business delegation from the city has visited the headquarters of the airline AirSial to discuss air services.

## Main business areas and markets
Shahi Road is one of the busiest roads in the city, linking Khanpur Road and Canal Road with the Abbasia flyover and Circular Road. Hospital Road and Railway Road are other important commercial streets. The main shopping centres include Zaffar Market, New Sadiq Bazaar, Sadar Bazaar and the Grain Market, the centre of wholesale produce trading. Jinnah Bazaar is another established market, where traders have pushed back against municipal anti-encroachment drives.

Newer development has spread along Abu Dhabi Road, home to the Khwaja Fareed University campus, and into residential localities such as Model Town and Satellite Town.

## Education and air links
Khwaja Fareed University of Engineering and Information Technology was set up under a Punjab act in 2014 and moved to its own campus on Abu Dhabi Road in January 2016. Sheikh Zayed Medical College is the city's other major institution, and the two together bring a sizeable student population to the city. Sheikh Zayed International Airport gives the city direct air access, although scheduled flights to Lahore and Islamabad have been interrupted at times, leaving the district without direct links to those cities for a period of three years.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Rahim Yar Khan?
The telephone area code is 068, or +92 68 from abroad.

### Which are the main markets in Rahim Yar Khan?
Zaffar Market, New Sadiq Bazaar, Sadar Bazaar, Jinnah Bazaar and the Grain Market are the main shopping and trading centres, with Shahi Road as the main commercial road.

### Does Rahim Yar Khan have an airport?
Yes. Sheikh Zayed International Airport serves the city and has had international status since 2009.

### What industries are found in Rahim Yar Khan?
Sugar milling, cotton ginning and flour milling are the most widespread, along with fertiliser production and agricultural trade.
