Rawalpindi is a major city in northern Punjab and the older half of the Islamabad–Rawalpindi twin cities. It is a cantonment city: the General Headquarters of the Pakistan Army is located in Rawalpindi Cantonment, and the military presence has shaped the city's layout and economy since the British period.

For buyers and traders, Rawalpindi is the bazaar city of the capital region. Where Islamabad is planned around sector markets, Rawalpindi's old city still runs on dense specialist bazaars, and many Islamabad residents come here for wholesale prices, wedding shopping and spare parts.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Punjab; headquarters of Rawalpindi district and division
- **Population (2023 census, Rawalpindi district):** 5,745,964, excluding Murree, which became a separate district in 2022
- **Telephone area code:** 051, shared with Islamabad
- **Airport:** served by Islamabad International Airport; the old Benazir Bhutto International Airport at Chaklala closed to civil flights in 2018 and is now part of PAF Base Nur Khan
- **Chamber of commerce:** Rawalpindi Chamber of Commerce and Industry
- **Transport:** on the Grand Trunk Road; linked to Islamabad by the 24 km Rawalpindi–Islamabad Metrobus

## Economy and main industries
Trade and services make up most of Rawalpindi's economy. The city's wholesale and retail markets serve its own population, Islamabad and the surrounding districts. The army cantonment creates steady demand for contractors, suppliers, transport, catering and housing. Food production, oil products, transport and tourism are among the main industries, and the city's position on the Grand Trunk Road and near the airport makes it a logistics point for goods moving to the north.

Residential growth in Bahria Town, DHA and the schemes along Adiala Road and GT Road has made construction, building materials and property dealing large sources of business.

## Main business areas and markets
Raja Bazaar is the commercial heart of the old city, with markets selling clothing, books, bags, household goods and food. Around it are specialist markets including Moti Bazaar, which is the city's main market for women's clothing, fabric and wedding shopping, and Bara Market, a crowded central bazaar long known for fabric and general goods. Kohati Bazaar and Bohar Bazaar are other old city bazaars nearby.

Saddar developed as the cantonment bazaar between the old city and the Mall, and remains a major area for retail, banking and offices. Murree Road, the main artery through the city, is lined with shops and commercial plazas, and the Metrobus runs along it. Commercial Market in Satellite Town is a key shopping area for the residential districts around it, with garment and household stores. 6th Road and Committee Chowk are other busy commercial points.

Newer business areas have grown in Bahria Town, DHA Phase 1 and Phase 2 and along Peshawar Road and Adiala Road.

## Getting around the twin cities
Murree Road and the Metrobus connect Rawalpindi's markets with Islamabad's Blue Area and sector markets, and many businesses serve customers in both cities. Traffic in the old city is heavy and parking is limited, so allowing extra time, or using the Metrobus along Murree Road, makes market visits easier. Islamabad International Airport, west of the twin cities, handles all scheduled flights.

## Finding businesses in Rawalpindi
Common searches in Rawalpindi include cloth and garment wholesalers, wedding services, auto parts dealers, building material suppliers, private hospitals and clinics, and property dealers in the housing schemes. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Rawalpindi?
Rawalpindi uses 051, the same landline code as Islamabad.

### Which is the main market in Rawalpindi?
Raja Bazaar in the old city is the main commercial centre. Moti Bazaar, beside it, is the best-known market for women's clothing and bridal shopping.

### Which airport serves Rawalpindi?
Islamabad International Airport serves both cities. The former airport at Chaklala stopped civil operations in 2018.

### How does Rawalpindi connect to Islamabad?
The cities adjoin each other. The 24 km Metrobus runs along Murree Road into Islamabad, and several highways link the two.
