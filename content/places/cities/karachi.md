Karachi is the capital of Sindh and the country's principal seaport. The city's administrative area, Karachi Division, is split into seven districts: Central, East, South, West, Korangi, Malir and Keamari. Most national banks, insurers, shipping lines and trading houses keep their head offices here.

For anyone sourcing goods or services, Karachi matters because so much of Pakistan's trade physically passes through it. Imports arrive at its two ports before travelling up-country, and exporters from Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa often route cargo through local clearing and forwarding agents.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Sindh (provincial capital)
- **Population (2023 census, Karachi Division):** 20,382,881
- **Telephone area code:** 021
- **Ports:** Karachi Port and Port Qasim
- **Airport:** Jinnah International Airport
- **Chamber of commerce:** Karachi Chamber of Commerce and Industry, formed in 1959, on Aiwan-e-Tijarat Road
- **Stock exchange:** Pakistan Stock Exchange, in the Stock Exchange Building on I.I. Chundrigar Road

## Economy and main industries
Karachi's economy rests on port trade, finance and manufacturing. Karachi Port and Port Qasim together handle the bulk of Pakistan's seaborne cargo, which supports a large cluster of shipping agents, customs brokers, warehousing and road haulage firms. I.I. Chundrigar Road is the city's financial street, with the stock exchange, bank head offices and brokerage houses.

Manufacturing covers textiles and garments, leather and tanneries, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, edible oil, glass, engineering and vehicle assembly. Retail, wholesale distribution, media, software and business services employ large numbers of people across the city.

## Main business areas and markets
The city has several large industrial estates. SITE Area, the Sindh Industrial and Trading Estate, was set up shortly after independence and is one of the oldest in the country, with textile, chemical and pharmaceutical units. Korangi Industrial Area is known for tanneries and pharmaceutical firms, and Landhi Industrial Area sits beside the Landhi Export Processing Zone. North Karachi Industrial Area, established in 1974, has a mix of small and medium manufacturers. The Bin Qasim industrial zone near Port Qasim hosts heavy industry and vehicle plants.

For wholesale buying, the old city around Kharadar and Mithadar is the starting point. Jodia Bazaar deals in grain, rice, sugar and pulses, and Bolton Market, dating from 1883, trades in spices, dry fruit, groceries and imported goods. Saddar has Empress Market, Zainab Market on Abdullah Haroon Road and electronics traders, while Bohri Bazaar and Zaib-un-Nisa Street are long-established Saddar shopping streets.

Retail and office activity has spread south and east. Tariq Road in PECHS is a major clothing and footwear street, Shahrah-e-Faisal is lined with corporate offices and showrooms, and Clifton, Zamzama and the commercial areas of DHA Phase 5 and 6, including Badar Commercial and Bukhari Commercial, host restaurants, boutiques and professional firms.

## Ports and logistics
Two ports serve the city. Karachi Port sits next to Keamari and the old city, while Port Qasim lies to the east near Bin Qasim. Freight forwarders, container terminals, bonded warehouses and transport companies cluster along the routes between the ports and the industrial estates, particularly around Keamari, Mauripur and the Korangi and Landhi belt. Businesses importing machinery or raw materials usually deal with a customs clearing agent based near one of the two ports.

## Finding businesses in Karachi
People most often search Karachi for importers and wholesalers, clearing and forwarding agents, textile and garment manufacturers, pharmaceutical distributors, and professional services such as accountants, lawyers and IT firms. Searching by locality helps because the city is large and traffic is heavy: an industrial supplier in SITE Area is a long way from a client in DHA. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the telephone area code for Karachi?
Karachi's landline code is 021. From abroad, dial +92 21 followed by the subscriber number.

### Which is the main chamber of commerce in Karachi?
The Karachi Chamber of Commerce and Industry, formed in 1959, represents the city's traders and industrialists. Its office is on Aiwan-e-Tijarat Road.

### Where are the main wholesale markets in Karachi?
Most wholesale trade is concentrated in the old city: Jodia Bazaar for food grains and commodities, Bolton Market for spices, dry fruit and groceries, and the Saddar area for electronics and general goods.

### What are Karachi's main industrial areas?
The largest are SITE Area, Korangi Industrial Area, Landhi Industrial Area, North Karachi Industrial Area and the Bin Qasim industrial zone near Port Qasim.
