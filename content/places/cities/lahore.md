Lahore is the capital of Punjab and, by the 2023 census, the most populous district in Pakistan. It is the seat of the provincial government and the Lahore High Court, and the base for many of the country's textile groups, media houses, universities and software companies.

Commercially, the city works on two levels. The Walled City and the streets around it still hold the wholesale markets that supply traders across Punjab, while newer districts such as Gulberg, Johar Town and DHA carry offices, showrooms and modern retail.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Punjab (provincial capital)
- **Population (2023 census, Lahore district):** 13,004,135, all classed as urban
- **Telephone area code:** 042
- **Airport:** Allama Iqbal International Airport, about 15 km east of the city centre
- **Chamber of commerce:** Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry, founded in 1923 as the Northern India Chamber of Commerce
- **Technology park:** Arfa Software Technology Park, Ferozepur Road
- **Main industries:** textiles and garments, software and IT, publishing and printing, home appliances, food processing

## Economy and main industries
Textiles and garments are the largest export sector, supported by spinning, weaving, dyeing and stitching units in and around the city. Lahore is also one of Pakistan's main IT centres. Arfa Software Technology Park on Ferozepur Road, developed by the Punjab government, houses software and IT companies, and many more firms operate from Gulberg and Johar Town.

Other important sectors include publishing and printing, home appliances, pharmaceuticals, food processing, banking and real estate. A large share of employment is in small workshops and cottage industries that make garments, footwear, metal goods and furniture.

## Main business areas and markets
The old city is where many traders still buy in bulk. Shah Alam Market, near Shah Alami Gate, is one of Punjab's main wholesale markets for general goods, and Azam Cloth Market, inside Delhi Gate, is a large wholesale cloth market. Akbari Mandi is the wholesale market for spices, grains, pulses and dry fruit. Urdu Bazaar, next to Anarkali, is the centre of the book, publishing and paper trade.

Anarkali Bazaar, one of the city's oldest markets, sells textiles, clothing and jewellery, with its old section known for food. Hall Road is the main market for imported electronics, including CCTV equipment, cables, UPS units and solar products. Hafeez Centre in Gulberg is the best-known building for computers, mobile phones and accessories.

Modern retail and office space is concentrated in Gulberg, particularly MM Alam Road and Liberty Market on Main Boulevard, and in the commercial areas of DHA, Johar Town and Model Town. Mall Road and Jail Road hold government offices, banks and older corporate buildings.

Manufacturing is spread across several zones. Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate at Kot Lakhpat began in 1960 as the Township Industrial Estate, and Sundar Industrial Estate is another estate managed by the Punjab Industrial Estates Development and Management Company. Older industry lines Ferozepur Road, Multan Road and the Shahdara and Badami Bagh areas.

## Education and services
Lahore has a large concentration of universities, colleges and teaching hospitals, which creates steady demand for printing, stationery, hostel accommodation, catering and medical supplies. Professional services, including law firms near the High Court, accountancy practices, advertising agencies and recruitment firms, are a significant part of the local economy.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Lahore?
Lahore landlines use the code 042. Callers from abroad dial +92 42 before the number.

### Where is the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry?
The LCCI head office is on Shahrah-e-Aiwan-e-Sanat-o-Tijarat in central Lahore. The chamber dates from 1923.

### Which market in Lahore is best for electronics and computers?
Hall Road is the main market for electronics and electrical goods, while Hafeez Centre in Gulberg specialises in computers, laptops and mobile phones.

### Where are Lahore's industrial estates?
The main planned estates are Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate at Kot Lakhpat and Sundar Industrial Estate. Many factories also sit along Ferozepur Road and Multan Road.
