Abbottabad is a hill city in the Hazara region of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It was laid out under British rule in 1853, and the old town and cantonment still form its commercial centre. The city sits on the Karakoram Highway corridor and is now also served by the Hazara Motorway (M-15), which links it with Hasan Abdal, Haripur, Mansehra and Thakot.

Commercially, Abbottabad is shaped less by heavy industry than by education, the armed forces, health care, retail and tourism. Large student and military populations, plus visitors heading to the Galyat hill resorts and further north, support a steady demand for shops, hotels, restaurants, transport and professional services.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Hazara region)
- **Population (2023 census, district):** 1,419,072
- **Telephone area code:** 0992
- **Road links:** Karakoram Highway and Hazara Motorway (M-15)
- **Notable institutions:** Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul; Ayub Medical College (established 1978); a COMSATS University campus
- **Business bodies:** Abbottabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry; Abbottabad Women Chamber of Commerce and Industry

## Economy and main industries
The local economy rests on services. The Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul, which began operating in 1948, and the cantonment create demand for tailoring, uniforms, catering, housing and transport. Ayub Medical College and its teaching hospital, along with private hospitals and clinics, make health care one of the larger employers, and a cluster of pharmacies, laboratories and diagnostic centres has grown around them.

Education is a second pillar. Boarding and day schools, colleges and university campuses draw students from across Hazara and beyond, which keeps hostels, bookshops, stationers, coaching centres and food outlets busy for most of the year. Tourism adds a seasonal layer: hotels, guest houses, car rental firms and tour operators serve visitors travelling to Nathia Gali and the other Galyat resorts, and to the Kaghan valley and the north.

## Main business areas and markets
Saddar Bazaar, in the old cantonment town, is the traditional shopping street for groceries, clothing, shoes and jewellery, and is at its busiest on weekends and before Eid. Supply Bazaar is another long-established market area. Fawara Chowk, named after the fountain that once stood at the roundabout, is a central junction surrounded by shops where bargaining is normal.

Mandian, on the Karakoram Highway north of the centre, takes its name from its fruit and vegetable markets and has grown into a commercial area in its own right, close to Ayub Medical College. Newer shopping plazas such as Italian Mall and Jadoon Plaza cater to fashion, footwear and electronics buyers. Residential and mixed-use localities including Jinnahabad, Nawanshehr, Kehal, Bilal Town, Habibullah Colony, Link Road and Shimla Hill have their own neighbourhood shops and services, while Kakul and Cantt are centred on the military establishment.

## Getting around and access
The Hazara Motorway runs from the Burhan interchange near Hasan Abdal through Haripur, Havelian, Abbottabad, Mansehra, Shinkiari and Battagram to Thakot. Its Havelian–Mansehra section, which includes tunnels near Abbottabad, was inaugurated in November 2019, and the extension from Mansehra to Thakot was completed in July 2020. For businesses this has made the city easier to reach for suppliers and customers from Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Punjab. Within the city, wagons, rickshaws and ride-hailing services connect the bazaars with Mandian, Kakul and the outlying localities.

## Finding businesses in Abbottabad
People most often search for schools and tuition centres, hospitals and doctors, hotels and guest houses, car rental, property dealers and restaurants. Students and their families also look for hostels, stationers and computer shops. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the telephone area code for Abbottabad?
The area code is 0992. From outside Pakistan, dial +92 992 followed by the local number.

### Where are the main markets in Abbottabad?
Saddar Bazaar, Supply Bazaar and the streets around Fawara Chowk are the main traditional shopping areas. Mandian, on the Karakoram Highway, is the main commercial area on the northern side of the city.

### Is there a chamber of commerce in Abbottabad?
Yes. The Abbottabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry represents local traders and industrialists, and a separate Abbottabad Women Chamber of Commerce and Industry is registered with the Ministry of Commerce.

### How do you get to Abbottabad from Islamabad?
Most travellers drive via the Hazara Motorway (M-15) from the Burhan interchange near Hasan Abdal, or take a coach or wagon from Rawalpindi or Islamabad.
