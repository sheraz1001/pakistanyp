Mirpur is a city in the south of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, an administrative territory of Pakistan. Its modern form was shaped by the Mangla Dam, completed in 1967, whose reservoir submerged more than 280 villages and displaced over 110,000 people in the Mirpur area.

Migration that accelerated around the time of the dam's construction created strong links between Mirpur and the United Kingdom. Remittances and spending by overseas families are visible across the local economy, especially in property and construction.

## Key facts
- **Status:** City and district headquarters in Azad Jammu and Kashmir, an administrative territory of Pakistan
- **Population (2017 census, district):** about 456,200
- **Telephone area code:** 05827
- **Landmark:** Mangla Dam and reservoir (completed 1967)
- **Industrial areas:** Small Industrial Estate Mirpur; New Industrial Area Mirpur; new estates at Dudyal
- **Business body:** Mirpur Azad Jammu and Kashmir Chamber of Commerce and Industry (MCCI)

## Economy and main industries
The overseas connection is the single biggest factor in the economy. An estimated 60 to 70 per cent of British Pakistanis in England trace their roots to Azad Jammu and Kashmir, mainly to Mirpur, Kotli and Bhimber, with large communities in Birmingham, Bradford, Manchester, Leeds and Luton. Remittances are the most visible source of wealth in the city and fund much of its house building and property market. Because these flows follow the British economy and the exchange rate, local businesses have felt the effects of changes in the value of the pound.

Mirpur is also the main industrial location in the territory. The AJK industries department lists a Small Industrial Estate Mirpur and a larger New Industrial Area Mirpur, with new estates at Dudyal in Mirpur district and a special economic zone in progress. The Mirpur chamber, which describes itself as the apex body of the business community in Azad Jammu and Kashmir, has pressed the government to repair infrastructure in the industrial estates and to keep the joint director of industries office in Mirpur, which serves firms in the estate. Its proposals have included a large bridge linking the city with the New Industrial Estate.

## Main business areas and markets
Business addresses in the city are commonly given by locality: Allama Iqbal Road, Chowk Shaheedan, Mian Muhammad Road, Kotli Road, Sector F-1 and New City are the localities used in PakistanYP listings for Mirpur. Outside the residential city, the Small Industrial Estate and the New Industrial Area are the main locations for manufacturing and workshops.

Because so much local spending comes from remittances, retail in Mirpur leans towards property, construction and household goods rather than towards supplying factories elsewhere.

## Remittances and the local economy
One local businessman has estimated remittances to Mirpur at around 200 million dollars a year. Observers have also pointed out the limits of this model: much of the money goes into building large houses, which adds little to productive activity, or stays uninvested for lack of business opportunities. After the 2016 Brexit referendum, the fall in the pound reduced the value of money sent home, and remittances dropped by almost 10 per cent according to State Bank figures reported at the time. Mirpur University of Science and Technology (MUST), based in the city, runs a business incubation centre that has engaged with firms in the New Industrial Estate.

## Finding businesses in Mirpur
People commonly look for property dealers and builders, travel agents, money exchange, jewellers, wedding halls and caterers, lawyers, and suppliers to the industrial estates. PakistanYP listings can be filtered by locality, such as Allama Iqbal Road or Chowk Shaheedan.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the telephone area code for Mirpur, AJK?
Mirpur's area code is 05827, or +92 5827 from abroad.

### Why does Mirpur have strong links with the UK?
Large-scale migration to Britain, which accelerated around the time Mangla Dam was built in the 1960s, means a large share of British Pakistanis have family roots in Mirpur and nearby districts.

### Is there an industrial estate in Mirpur?
Yes. Mirpur has a Small Industrial Estate and a New Industrial Area, with further estates being developed at Dudyal.

### Which chamber of commerce covers Mirpur?
The Mirpur Azad Jammu and Kashmir Chamber of Commerce and Industry represents traders and industrialists in the city.
