Gujranwala is an industrial city in northern Punjab, on the Grand Trunk Road between Lahore and the Sialkot and Gujrat area. It is the headquarters of Gujranwala district and division, and is traditionally known as the City of Wrestlers for the pehlwans it has produced.

The city's business identity is small and medium manufacturing. Thousands of family-run workshops and factories make metal goods, electrical appliances and ceramics, and buyers from across Pakistan come here to source products directly from makers.

## Key facts
- **Province:** Punjab; headquarters of Gujranwala district and division
- **Population (2023 census, Gujranwala district):** 4,966,338, about 65 per cent urban
- **Telephone area code:** 055
- **Chamber of commerce:** Gujranwala Chamber of Commerce and Industry, established in 1978
- **Main road:** Grand Trunk Road; linked to the Lahore–Sialkot Motorway
- **Main industries:** electric fans, cookware and utensils, cutlery, ceramics and sanitary fittings, home appliances, rice processing
- **Airports:** Sialkot International Airport and Allama Iqbal International Airport, Lahore, are the usual options

## Economy and main industries
The Gujranwala Chamber of Commerce and Industry lists a wide range of local sectors: rice and agro processing, cookware and utensils, cutlery and knives, textiles and apparel, ceramics, sanitary fittings, plastic products, pipes and fittings, electric and gas home appliances, automobile parts, heavy engineering and metal processing.

Electric fans are one of the city's signature products. The national trade development authority identifies Gujranwala and Gujrat as the centre of Pakistan's fan manufacturing cluster, which grew out of the city's older tradition of metal working. Ceramics and sanitary ware are another significant cluster, with around 100 units producing tiles, crockery and sanitary products.

Rice and agro processing, which the chamber lists first among local sectors, link the city's industry with farming in the surrounding district.

## Main business areas and markets
GT Road is the city's commercial spine, and much of its trade and industry is located along or close to the highway. The older city centre, with localities such as Ghanta Ghar, Rail Bazaar and Sheranwala Bagh, holds traditional bazaar trade.

Planned industry is grouped in the city's small industrial estates, numbered one to four. Small Industrial Estate IV covers 265 acres, and the chamber has proposed that it be included in the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor framework. Many smaller units also work outside the estates, in the older parts of the city and along the main roads to Sialkot, Pasrur and Sheikhupura.

Residential and commercial growth has moved to Satellite Town, Model Town, Peoples Colony, DC Colony and newer schemes such as Citi Housing, DHA and Master City. DC Colony is regarded as one of the main locations for commercial plots.

## Connectivity
GT Road links Gujranwala with Lahore to the south and Wazirabad and Gujrat to the north. The Lahore–Sialkot Motorway runs close to the city, and a link road from the Wando interchange connects Gujranwala to it, with commercial development planned along the route. For air travel and cargo, businesses use Sialkot International Airport or Allama Iqbal International Airport in Lahore.

## Food and local culture
Gujranwala has a strong food culture tied to its wrestling tradition. The city is known for kebabs, barbecue and other meat dishes, and for drinks such as chatti ki lassi and badam sardai. Food businesses, from small eateries to catering firms, are a common search alongside manufacturers.

## Finding businesses in Gujranwala
People searching in Gujranwala mainly look for manufacturers: fan makers, cookware and cutlery producers, ceramics and sanitary ware suppliers, auto parts workshops and rice mills. Exporters and wholesalers often want to contact factories directly. PakistanYP lists Gujranwala businesses by product and area.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the area code for Gujranwala?
Gujranwala's landline code is 055. From abroad, dial +92 55.

### What products is Gujranwala known for?
Electric fans, cookware and utensils, cutlery, ceramics and sanitary fittings, home appliances and rice processing are among the city's best-known industries.

### When was the Gujranwala Chamber of Commerce and Industry set up?
The chamber was established in 1978 and represents the city's traders and industrialists.

### Why is Gujranwala called the City of Wrestlers?
The city has a long tradition of pehlwani wrestling and has produced well-known wrestlers such as Goga Pehalwan.
